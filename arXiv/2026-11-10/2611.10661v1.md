# Black-hole ringdown at an exceptional point

## authors:
#Kyriakos_Destounis

## summary:
Black holes ring in damped oscillations. At an exceptional point, photon-sphere and near-horizon modes merge, and their response can acquire a linear time factor. We compute its amplitude from constrained Einstein-Maxwell data for a rotating, charged black hole in de Sitter space. With seven competing modes fixed, the late-time curvature error is 0.00383$\%$ for the pair and 0.01401$\%$ for a refitted exponential. We suppress the linear term by tuning the initial disturbance at fixed geometry. Hence, near coalescence, changing only the initial disturbance can shift the frequency inferred from a short ringdown fit.
