# Pfaffian sensitivity to temporal deformations of an N=5 background in the Lorentzian IIB matrix model

## authors:
#Tetsuyuki_Muramatsu

## summary:
How spacetime dimensionality and anisotropy emerge from matrix degrees of freedom is a central question in the IIB matrix model. Our previous work constructed a family of constrained classical solutions at $N=5$, including a reference configuration with vanishing temporal extent and a pre-existing $3+6$ spatial hierarchy. Here we examine how the fermionic Pfaffian responds to temporal-matrix deformations of this configuration.  The background geometry determines a distinguished real two-plane of temporal deformations. Numerical analysis of the full fermionic operator shows that the sensitivity of its lowest positive-eigenvalue doublet is strongly concentrated in this plane and dominates the first-order Pfaffian response. Under stated reflection-covariance and nonsingularity conditions, the other local one-loop contributions cancel at first order when combined, so the gradients of the logarithmic magnitudes of the total local one-loop weight and the Pfaffian agree.  We prescribe a temporal deformation along the projection of this gradient onto the two-plane and determine the remaining matrix components by conditional classical stationarity. The existing spatial hierarchy is modestly strengthened at quadratic order, while the spectrum changes little at the small amplitude considered. This quadratic response is independent of orientation within the plane, although the first-order Pfaffian response distinguishes such orientations. These results connect background geometry, fermionic spectral sensitivity, and conditional classical spatial anisotropy.
