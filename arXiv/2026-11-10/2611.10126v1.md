# Holographic warped CFTs on AdS$_3$, parity violation and unitarity

## authors:
#Kristiansen_Lara
#Ricardo_Troncoso

## summary:
We consider recently introduced boundary conditions for General Relativity on AdS$_3$, whose canonical generators span the warped conformal algebra, in the case of the Einstein-Hilbert action supplemented by parity-odd terms that do not modify the field equations in vacuum. The canonical generators are shown to acquire nonlinear contributions, and their algebra remains being the semidirect sum of Virasoro and $u(1)$ Kac-Moody, but with shifted central extensions. Thus, for suitable values of the parity-odd coupling, the Kac-Moody level can be nonnegative ($k_\text{KM}\geq 0$), admitting unitary representations.  Nevertheless, requiring the black hole entropy to be nonnegative, implies $k_\text{KM}\leq 0$, in agreement with the established understanding of holographic warped CFTs. Compatibility of both requirements (entropy and unitarity) then singles out the case $k_\text{KM}=0$. However, the field equations degenerate in this case, which can be amended by imposing vanishing torsion as a constraint. The theory then precisely becomes Topologically Massive Gravity (TMG) at the chiral point, suggesting the extension of our boundary conditions to accommodate nontrivial logarithmic branches.
