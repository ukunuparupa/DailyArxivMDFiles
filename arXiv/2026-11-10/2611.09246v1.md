# Quasi-hydrodynamics beyond the conventional paradigm

## authors:
#Lorenzo_Gavassino

## summary:
Quasi-hydrodynamics extends hydrodynamics by promoting slowly relaxing variables to additional degrees of freedom. For such a framework to be rigorously defined and parametrically controlled, the effective theory is usually assumed to retain all the slow modes of the underlying microscopic dynamics, which must be parametrically separated from the rest of the microscopic spectrum and typically arise from weakly broken symmetries. Here, we show that none of these assumptions is essential. Using the effective field theory of quasi-hydrodynamics recently derived from kinetic theory, we study three models that challenge the need for, respectively, parametric scale separation, retaining every slow mode, and a weakly broken symmetry. In all cases, the quasi-hydrodynamic expansion remains fully controlled and can be made arbitrarily accurate. This is possible because the applicability of quasi-hydrodynamics is determined not by the microscopic origin of the slow dynamics, but by the spectral properties of the kinetic operator on a suitably chosen Hilbert space.
