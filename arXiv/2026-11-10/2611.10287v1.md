# Quantum Open Effective Field Theory: anomalous decoherence

## authors:
#Gabriela_Barenboim
#Daniel_Boyanovsky

## summary:
We study decoherence of a scalar field coupled to ``anomalous'' environments with critical behavior with the Schwinger-Keldysh including the influence functional, and the quantum master equation (QME) up to second order in the coupling. The Schwinger-Keldysh formulation yields a Langevin equation and the Lindblad (QME) includes \emph{time dependent rates} allowing to study transient dynamics. We focus on three examples featuring non-Markov dynamics that cannot be described with S-matrix/Fermi's golden rule rates \textbf{i) Unparticle physics:} a conformal sector beyond the Standard Model at an infrared fixed point featuring correlation functions with anomalous scaling dimensions, \textbf{ii) Threshold and infrared enhancement:} when the mass shell of the field coincides with or is near to a kinematic threshold of the spectral density of the bath, \textbf{iii) Critical environments:} the bath is at a critical point, correlation functions scale with anomalous dimensions and long-wavelength fluctuations feature critical slowing down. In all cases the reduced density matrix undergoes decoherence in the energy basis of the probe, and off-diagonal matrix elements decay as $e^{-Φ(t)}$ with $Φ(t)>0 $ the \emph{decoherence function} and $Γ(t) = \dotΦ(t)$ the decoherence rate. In all cases we find that either transient or asymptotically $Φ(t) \propto t^{-μ}~;~ μ>0$ with $μ$, determined by the behavior of the spectral density at threshold and the anomalous scaling dimensions of the correlation functions, or $Φ(t) \propto \ln(t)$ for the infrared case. For a critical environment there is a new dynamical scaling exponent. A probe of frequency $ω_q$ is resonant with a \emph{critically slow} quasiparticle for $q_*\propto ω^{1/z}_q$ at which decoherence is strongly \emph{enhanced}.
