# The Gribov problem on the horizon: A short guide to the Gribov-Zwanziger framework and its refinement

## authors:
#Antonio_D_Pereira

## summary:
Gauge fixing in Yang-Mills theories encounters a non-perturbative obstruction: distinct gauge-equivalent configurations can satisfy the same gauge condition. These are the so-called Gribov copies and their existence challenges the global validity of the Faddeev-Popov prescription. Addressing this ambiguity motivates a framework that connects the geometry of gauge-field space to infrared dynamics. This mini-review introduces the Gribov problem in Euclidean Yang-Mills theories and traces the construction of the Gribov-Zwanziger action and its refinement through the introduction of dimension-two condensates. We discuss the resulting infrared propagators, their comparison to lattice results, and the role of BRST symmetry, before surveying selected applications and open questions.
