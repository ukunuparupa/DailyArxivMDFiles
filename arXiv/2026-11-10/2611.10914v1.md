# Untangling flow-harmonic correlations in OO and Pb--Pb collisions at the LHC using event-shape engineering

## authors:
#Suraj_Prasad

## summary:
Symmetric cumulants $\mathrm{SC}(m,n)$ measure the correlation between the event-by-event fluctuations of the flow harmonics $v_m$ and $v_n$, which, for higher harmonics, is influenced by the nonlinear response to the elliptic ($v_2$) and triangular ($v_3$) flow. Event-shape engineering, which selects events by the reduced flow vector $q_n$, is expected to control this contribution. However, its power is limited by the small forward multiplicity in light-ion collisions. In this work, we study the effect of the $q_2$ and $q_3$ selections on the symmetric cumulants in OO and Pb--Pb collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}}=5.36$~TeV using EPOS4 with an ATLAS-like acceptance. Using the law of total covariance, the symmetric cumulant is separated into a selected part, which follows the differences between the $q_n$ classes, and a residual part within them. It is observed that in OO collisions the correlations of $v_2$ with $v_3$ and $v_4$ remain almost entirely within the $q_2$ classes, unlike in Pb--Pb collisions. Nevertheless, events with the smallest $q_2$ in central OO collisions show weaker correlations of $v_2$ with $v_3$ and $v_4$, which is attributed to $v_4$ approaching its linear response. In Pb--Pb collisions, the $q_2$ and $q_3$ selections act separately on the two factors of the nonlinear coupling of $v_5$, which establishes a way to isolate them experimentally.
