# Remark on residual solutions in the $KBc$ algebra

## authors:
#Ilmar_Gahramanov

## summary:
In this paper we study residual solutions of open bosonic string field theory in the $KBc$ algebra with the auxiliary identities $(\partial^mc)^2=0$ of the ghost field. These solutions are not covered by the classification based on the defining relations alone. We show that the operator which controls the cohomology on the residual family depends only on $s=γ_1+γ_2$. At positive level $n$, cohomology can appear only on the lines $s=\pm n$. We compute it through level four and find one class at every point of each line $s=-3$ and $s=-4$. These classes give exact polynomial deformations of the residual family. At level six on $s=-6$, the cohomology instead vanishes generically and has dimension two at $(-1,-5)$ and $(-5,-1)$. We also compute the failure of the standard generator redefinition to preserve the auxiliary identities, and discuss the regularization of an explicit residual solution.
