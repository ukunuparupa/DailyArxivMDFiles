# 5d $\mathbb{Z}_2$ S-folds and Seiberg--Witten curves

## authors:
#Sung-Soo_Kim
#Song_Liu
#Mudassar_Sabir
#Futoshi_Yagi

## summary:
We compute the Seiberg--Witten curves for $\mathbb{Z}_2$ S-folds of five-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=1$ rank-1 superconformal field theories. Two complementary constructions are presented. First, we implement the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ action by identifying pairs of points on the original curve, thereby mapping them to a single point on the S-folded curve. Second, motivated by arXiv:2112.14550, we tune mass parameters to special values, corresponding to the insertion of $D_4$ 7-branes. As a nontrivial consistency check, we show that the two Seiberg--Witten curves agree with each other when expressed in the Weierstrass normal form.
