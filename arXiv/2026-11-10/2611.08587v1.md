# AdS to dS Flows Dual to Pseudo-Hermitian Theories

## authors:
#José_M_Begines
#Antón_F_Faedo
#Carlos_Hoyos
#Daniele_Musso

## summary:
We construct generalizations of ''centaur" geometries in more than two dimensions as solutions of Einstein gravity coupled to a ghost scalar. The solutions interpolate between asymptotically anti-de Sitter and asymptotically de Sitter spacetimes, with the two asymptotic regions separated by a horizon. A holographic interpretation is that they are dual to pseudo-Hermitian theories obtained from the continuation of real couplings and operators to purely imaginary values. One family of solutions is obtained from a supergravity theory that is a consistent truncation with a known embedding in string theory. We study the thermodynamic and linear stability of the solutions, and determine the uplift of the supergravity solutions to eleven dimensions.
