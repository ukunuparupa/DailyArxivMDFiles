# Large-D approach to nearly critical holographic matter and Yang-Mills deconfinement

## authors:
#Matti_Järvinen
#Leonardus_B_Putra
#Dorin_Weissman

## summary:
We study a systematic expansion of dynamical black hole solutions in a class of five-dimensional Einstein-dilaton theories that are holographically dual to nearly critical, strongly coupled four-dimensional field theories. The expansion is motivated, through dimensional reduction, by the large-D limit of pure gravity, where D is the number of spacetime dimensions. At leading nontrivial order, the Einstein equations reduce to a closed set of non-relativistic hydrodynamic equations in four dimensions, as first shown in our previous letter; these equations generalize the large-D effective theory of black branes. We derive the thermodynamics and hydrodynamics of the system from black hole thermodynamics and the holographic dictionary, and show that the energy-momentum tensor extracted from the near-boundary geometry is conserved precisely when the effective equations hold. We analyze planar, cylindrical, and spherical domain walls between coexisting phases, as well as non-equilibrium steady-state walls with nonzero momentum flux. We also show how the near-horizon geometry can be completed by a holographic flow to an asymptotically AdS5 region. Finally, as an application to the confinement-deconfinement transition of Yang-Mills theory, we construct the domain wall between the confined and deconfined phases in improved holographic QCD, compute its surface tension, and compare it with recent lattice results.
