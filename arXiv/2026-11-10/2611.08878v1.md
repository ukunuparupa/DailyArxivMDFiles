# Simplicity of the three-point stress-tensor form factor beyond the planar limit

## authors:
#Song_He

## summary:
We express the complete three-loop subleading-color finite remainder of the three-point stress-tensor-multiplet form factor in $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang--Mills theory as a permutation sum of a single $A_2$-type function. The non-product part of the seed is a combination of 35 weight-six generalized polylogarithms, while all lower-weight products and zeta-value terms belong to the same five-letter algebra. A bootstrap based on independently computed collinear data, branch-cut constraints and empirical final-entry relations determines both the symbol and the full function. The remaining function-level ambiguity is proportional to $ζ_2$ times the two-loop planar remainder and is fixed by a single final-entry relation. The weight-three left coproduct factors of the planar and subleading-color results span the same space modulo products; at weight four, the subleading-color result contains four additional nonclassical directions. Through the established maximal-transcendentality correspondence, this compact representation also describes the weight-six subleading-color sector of Higgs-plus-three-gluon amplitudes in QCD. We also construct a four-loop candidate symbol space under a conjectural extension of the three-loop constraints.
