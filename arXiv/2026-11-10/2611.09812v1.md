# The Double Copy of the Doubly Spinning Black Ring

## authors:
#Damien_A_Easson

## summary:
We establish an exact curved-background Weyl double copy for the doubly spinning Pomeransky--Sen'kov black ring. Its full Weyl tensor is a quadratic form in two source-free Maxwell fields: one generated by the stationary Killing vector, the other a flux-carrying magnetic field obtained by linearizing the dipole-ring gauge potential about its vacuum limit. The mixed Maxwell bilinear is essential: no spacetime-independent real or complex change of field basis removes it. We identify the inverse $S_{IJ}$ of the Weyl coefficient matrix as a matrix zeroth copy. The same matrix arises independently from a weighted limit of the inner products of two Killing vectors in a six-dimensional Ricci-flat parent and satisfies $\Box_g S_{IJ}=-\tfrac12 F_{I\,ab}F_J{}^{ab}$. The singly spinning limit recovers the diagonal two-field Weyl double copy of the Emparan--Reall ring.
