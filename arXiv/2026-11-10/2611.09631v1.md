# Dynamical gluon mass generation, the Schwinger mechanism, spontaneous symmetry breaking, and color confinement in the linear covariant gauges of QCD

## authors:
#Giorgio_Comitini

## summary:
We critically re-examine the Kugo-Ojima (KO) scenario for color confinement and identify several inconsistencies and a crucial error in its derivation. Within the KO framework, under customary assumptions, we show that the BRST quartet structure incorporates a unique mechanism for dynamical gluon mass generation -- namely, the Schwinger mechanism, in the formulation developed by Aguilar, Binosi, Ferreira, and Papavassiliou (ABFP). We show that the Schwinger mechanism is a Higgs mechanism, in that it occurs together with the spontaneous breaking of the global gauge charge, as it should. The ABFP bound state is the associated (unphysical) composite Goldstone boson. The zero-momentum value of the gluon propagator evaluated in the Pinch Technique--Background-Field Method (PT-BFM) naturally emerges in the derivation, setting a Renormalization-Group invariant scale for mass generation and suggesting a new nonperturbative interpretation of the PT-BFM. BRST symmetry takes over the role of the broken symmetry, while the color symmetry of the spectrum remains unbroken. We obtain a unique, well-defined, unbroken, BRST-exact asymptotic color charge operator that realizes the confinement of color in the sense of the absence of colored asymptotic states from the BRST cohomology. Still within the KO framework, we take the first fundamental step towards a proof of the nonperturbative unitarity of the linear covariant gauges of QCD, and we discuss the experimental evidence in favor of the Schwinger mechanism. We lay down the foundations for a unified theory of gauge-boson mass generation in the linear covariant gauges of Yang-Mills theories.
