# The Ghost and Its Secret Partner

## authors:
#Luca_Buoninfante

## summary:
The propagator of a ghost field acquires a pair of first-sheet complex conjugate poles above the multi-particle threshold. We show that this pole structure reveals the existence of a hidden asymptotic composite field -- the ghost's secret partner -- that remains coupled to the ghost asymptotically. This coupling induces persistent interference between the negative-norm one-particle state and the positive-norm composite-particle state. We analyze this phenomenon in four-derivative theories, in particular quadratic gravity, where the quantum dynamics prevents the spin-two ghost from appearing as an isolated asymptotic excitation. Although our results do not yet rule out observable negative probabilities, they may provide a dynamical basis for alternative amplitude prescriptions or superselection rules usually chosen or imposed ad hoc in theories with indefinite-norm vector spaces.
