# Non-Relativistic Supersymmetric Gauge Theories and Soliton Solutions

## authors:
#Yoonbai_Kim
#O-Kab_Kwon
#D_D_Tolla
#Hanwool_Song

## summary:
This paper investigates non-relativistic supersymmetric gauge theories obtained through null reduction, with particular focus on supersymmetric Abelian Higgs models and their soliton solutions. Starting from four-dimensional $\mathcal{N} = 1$ relativistic supersymmetric theories, we apply null reduction to derive Galilean-invariant non-relativistic theories in $2 + 1$ dimensions while preserving gauge symmetry and supersymmetry. The resulting symmetry structure is shown to correspond to the Bargmann extension of the super-Galilean algebra. We first analyze the massive supersymmetric Abelian Higgs model and show that, although it possesses nontrivial vacuum configurations, the mass term obstructs the existence of nontrivial BPS vortex solutions. We then study the massless supersymmetric Abelian Higgs model with multiple charged scalar fields, for which the BPS equations admit vortex-type soliton solutions under suitable charge and winding number conditions. The vacuum moduli space is identified as a weighted projective space, leading naturally to both integer and fractional vortex configurations. By imposing radial symmetry, we derive the corresponding vortex equations and examine their solutions analytically and numerically. Finally, we relate the vacuum structure of the model to the Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity, highlighting how familiar superconducting behavior can emerge from a symmetry-preserving non-relativistic supersymmetric framework.
