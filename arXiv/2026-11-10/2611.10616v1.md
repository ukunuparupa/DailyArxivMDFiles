# Scalar-tensor reconstruction of coherent quantum black holes

## authors:
#R_Casadio
#A_Giusti
#R_da_Rocha

## summary:
We construct a scalar-tensor realisation of spherically symmetric black hole geometries that effectively arise from quantum coherent states characterised by a finite core and finite extension. We determine the non-minimal coupling to the Einstein-Hilbert Lagrangian, kinetic coupling, and scalar potential and show that the kinetic term is positive only in finite domains. For fixed black hole mass, the boundaries of these domains provide a natural mechanism that relates the core size and spatial extension to the dynamics of the scalar-tensor realisation. We further show that the same geometry can also be obtained from a Horndeski action with derivative coupling and determine its domain of validity. Neither reconstruction includes the event horizon nor can it simultaneously reproduce arbitrarily large portions of the interior and exterior of the black hole, whose quantum origin therefore appears to remain partially outside the regime of applicability of semiclassical theories of gravity.
