# Thermodynamic, Optical, and Wave Signatures of Charged Letelier AdS Black Holes with PFDM and Quintessence

## authors:
#B_Eslam_Panah
#S_Gurtas_Dogan
#A_Guvendi
#O_Mustafa

## summary:
We study a four-dimensional charged Letelier--anti-de Sitter (AdS) black-hole metric containing a cloud of strings, a phenomenological perfect-fluid-dark-matter (PFDM) source, and a Kiselev-type quintessence term. We derive the curvature invariants, horizon temperature, entropy, electric potential, and horizon-based mass function, and analyze local thermal response and the associated extended-phase-space identities. A rectangular benchmark cycle gives $W=0.095000$ and $η\simeq15.39\%$ for the explicitly stated geometric-unit parameters; these values are model- and cycle-specific and are not presented as a universal efficiency enhancement. For geometric optics, we derive the photon-sphere condition, critical impact parameter, and finite-radius shadow angle. For a massless, minimally coupled scalar field, we obtain a radial-coordinate Helmholtz equation and identify frequency-dependent barriers, evanescent intervals, and candidate trapping regions; the eikonal barrier-top scale approaches the photon-sphere capture scale. Two qualifications delimit the interpretation. First, with the stated PFDM stress-tensor convention, $ρ_{\rm PFDM}=-β/(8πr^3)$, so positive $β$ describes a negative-density effective source. Second, the mass function follows from the horizon equation, but identifying it with a conserved asymptotic AdS mass requires a separate charge and boundary-normalization analysis. The potential-well analysis likewise does not determine complex quasinormal frequencies. We therefore present the results as a consistent, parameter-specific analysis of the adopted phenomenological geometry, while distinguishing algebraic thermodynamic identities from conclusions requiring asymptotic-charge or spectral calculations.
