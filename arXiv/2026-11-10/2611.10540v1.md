# Engineering a digital acoustic black hole

## authors:
#Antonino_Flachi

## summary:
We show how to design and build an analogue black hole as a synchronous digital circuit. We define the sound speed, the flow and the horizon of such a circuit in terms of its own logic, and realise one on a field-programmable gate array, where the black hole is probed electrically with a signal generator and an oscilloscope. We measure the stability of the horizon, its causal cone, the ringdown and rotational superradiance. A single flipped bit inside the horizon leaks out by an amount that falls exponentially with depth. Digital circuits thus form a reconfigurable class of gravitational analogues and realise an analogue black hole as a cellular automaton.
