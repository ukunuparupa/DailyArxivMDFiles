# Analogue Gravity: the Subcritical Case

## authors:
#F_Belgiorno
#S_L_Cacciatori
#A_Viganò

## summary:
We focus on the subcritical case in Analogue Gravity for a subluminal dispersion relation, and investigate the problem of pair creation in the absence of an horizon analytically, for the subcritical white hole case. We limit our attention to weakly dispersive media. In particular, we take into account for simplicity the Corley model and adopt an approach inspired by quantum mechanics in the so-called overbarrier scattering. The presence of complex turning points is shown to play a fundamental role in allowing a mode conversion which would be absent in a naive WKB approach. We show that there is still a pair creation phenomenon, which, in the subcritical case, is non-Planckian and without a Hawking partner. Our fully analytical results are qualitatively in agreement with the literature at hand. Furthermore we confirm the existence of a critical regime betwen the subcritical and the transcritical ones where no pair creation occurs.
