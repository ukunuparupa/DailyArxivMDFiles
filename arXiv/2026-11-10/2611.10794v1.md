# Variational Approach to the Mathisson-Papapetrou-Dixon Equations of Motion for Extended Objects in General Relativity

## authors:
#Trevor_Scheopner
#Felix_Lichtner

## summary:
The exact motion of a finite size object in curved spacetime is captured by the Mathisson-Papapetrou-Dixon equations. They associate a unique worldline, momentum, and spin to that object and determine the evolution of such in terms of its fully independent multipole moments. We provide a thorough review of this theory and show how to build it directly via a manifestly gauge invariant variational principle well-suited for effective descriptions of relativistic compact objects. We discuss conditions under which the covariant spin supplementary condition can be enforced and identify the natural basis of curvature tensors conjugate to the object's multipole moments.
