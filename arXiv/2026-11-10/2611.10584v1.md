# ERG, axions, and a PT-symmetric accelerating Universe

## authors:
#Nick_E_Mavromatos
#Sarben_Sarkar

## summary:
In conventional quantum mechanics, the Hamiltonian is required to be Hermitian, ensuring real energy eigenvalues. Certain non-Hermitian Hamiltonians can also possess entirely real spectra when they are invariant under the combined action of parity ($\mathcal P$) and time reversal ($\mathcal T$). Such Hamiltonians are called unbroken parity--time ($\mathcal{PT}$)-symmetric. We conjecture that, in a string-inspired scenario, a repulsive phase of gravity may be due to a $\mathcal{PT}$-symmetric gravitational theory, characterised by anomalous Chern-Simons terms, coupled to axion fields. The conjecture is based on the behaviour of the chiral anomalous couplings of these axions to electromagnetic potentials. Within the Exact Renormalisation Group (ERG) framework in the modified local potential approximation, we argue that singularities in the ERG beta functions for such couplings may indicate an infrared phase transition. At the energy scales of these singularities, we conjecture that the field theory transitions from the Hermitian phase to a ${\mathcal P}{\mathcal T}$-- symmetric theory, of gravity, characterised by a repulsive Netwon's ``constant''. This may induce the acceleration observed in the Universe at late eras, without the need for a dark--energy component.
