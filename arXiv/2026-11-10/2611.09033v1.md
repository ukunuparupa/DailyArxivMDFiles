# Baryon Number Susceptibilities of Hot and Dense Nuclear Matter in Relativistic Mean-Field Models

## authors:
#Deepak_Kumar
#Biswarup_Das
#Sandeep_Chatterjee
#Ujjal_Kumar_Dey

## summary:
We develop a fully analytical framework for calculating baryon number susceptibilities in relativistic mean-field (RMF) models of hot and dense nuclear matter at finite temperature and baryon chemical potential. The formalism is applied to representative nonlinear RMF parameterization sets to investigate the model dependence of baryon number fluctuations over a wide range of temperatures and baryon chemical potentials. All the considered RMF parameterizations reproduce the overall qualitative beam-energy dependence of the cumulant ratios. The observed skewness and kurtosis within the RMF models satisfy the experimental values. Specifically, the FSU-family parameterizations exhibit relatively small deviations from the measured heavy-ion observables i.e. cumulant ratios.
