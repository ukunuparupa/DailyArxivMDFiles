# Induction instead of Feynman diagrams, L-functionals instead of Hilbert space

## authors:
#Albert_Schwarz

## summary:
The main result of this paper is a construction of an inductive process that can replace Feynman diagrams. Combining this result with the notion of the L-functional, one can give an exposition of QED that does not suffer from  infrared divergences
