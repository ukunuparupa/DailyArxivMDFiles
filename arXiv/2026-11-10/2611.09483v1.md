# Thermal Field Theory for General Equilibrium Density Matrices

## authors:
#Alberto_Salvio

## summary:
Thermal field theory (TFT) is the combination of statistical mechanics and relativistic quantum field theory. This (book) chapter provides a pedagogical introduction to (and a review of) TFT for general equilibrium density matrices, which allow arbitrary values not only of temperature and chemical potentials, but also of average angular momentum. A general technique to compute ensemble averages is illustrated. Path-integral methods are developed to study the partition function and the thermal Green's functions of general operators, with an arbitrary number of points, in all field theories of physical relevance.
