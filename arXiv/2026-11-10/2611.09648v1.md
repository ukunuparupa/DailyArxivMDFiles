# The instanton sum in two-dimensional self-dual models

## authors:
#Mendel_Nguyen

## summary:
This paper considers the self-dual $CP^{N-1}$ model in two dimensions, which is defined by restricting the path integral of the ordinary two-dimensional $CP^{N-1}$ model to holomorphic configurations. We find that the instanton measure is one-loop exact and agrees with the one-loop instanton measure in the ordinary model. For the $N=2$ case, the instanton gas admits a description in terms of free massive Dirac fermions. Nevertheless, we find that the self-dual model is gapless. We also comment on the $θ$ dependence.
