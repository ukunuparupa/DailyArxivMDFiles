# The Energy-Momentum Tensor of a Compact Object in General Relativity to all Multipole Orders

## authors:
#Trevor_Scheopner
#Felix_Lichtner

## summary:
In effective field theory approaches to compact object motion in general relativity, spin and tidal effects are encoded by operators formed from the Riemann tensor and its derivatives. Many such operators contribute degenerately to observables, with a single one often affecting several multipole moments of the body. Using the Mathisson--Papapetrou--Dixon formalism, we construct a natural basis of independent tensors which are each canonically conjugate to precisely one multipole. We find the novel exact distributional formula for the energy-momentum tensor of a compact object in terms of its reduced multipole moments and these tensors.
