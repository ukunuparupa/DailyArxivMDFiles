# On Superconformal Index Degenerations and Half-Indices in S-Confining Gauge Theories

## authors:
#Melisa_Doğan
#Ilmar_Gahramanov

## summary:
We study the $p \rightarrow 0$ limit of four-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ superconformal indices and its relation to half-indices of three-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ theories. At the level of special functions, this limit reduces elliptic hypergeometric integrals to basic hypergeometric integrals. The scaling of the flavor fugacities determines the Neumann and Dirichlet boundary conditions of the chiral multiplets. The gauge multiplet has Neumann boundary conditions. We apply this prescription to s-confining theories with gauge groups $SU(2)$, $USp(2N)$, $G_2$, and $SU(N)$, and recover the half-index identities of several boundary confining dualities, expressed through the Nassrallah-Rahman and Gustafson integrals. For $SU(N)$ with a rank-two antisymmetric chiral, four fundamentals and $N$ antifundamentals, we obtain an analytic proof of the half-index identities proposed by Okazaki and Smith for all $N \geq 5$.
