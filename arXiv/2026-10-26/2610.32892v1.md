# Shear viscosity from complex light-cone expansion

## authors:
#Inês_Aniceto
#Pavel_Kovtun
#Alex_Ratcliffe
#Michał_Spaliński

## summary:
Near thermal equilibrium, the transverse momentum density in a fluid diffuses according to $ω(k) \sim - i {\cal D} k^2$ at small $k$, where the diffusion coefficient ${\cal D}$ is proportional to the shear viscosity. In field theories with a holographic dual description in terms of classical gravity, the shear-mode frequency $ω(k)$ approaches the light cone at large imaginary wave vector. We develop a WKB approach to study this limit. For black branes in AdS$_{p+2}$, we determine the leading terms of the asymptotic large-$k$ expansion of the shear-mode frequency $ω(k)$. In the case of AdS$_{5}$, we compute a large number of terms of this series and use resummation techniques to analytically continue the result to small $k$. We find strong evidence that $ω(k)$ is analytic at $k=0$ and vanishes there, which amounts to the existence of hydrodynamics encoded in the large-$k$ physics. Taking 10 terms in the large-$k$ expansion of $ω(k)$ gives an answer for ${\cal D}$ which is accurate to better than $0.5\%$.
