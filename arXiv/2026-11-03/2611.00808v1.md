# Quantum Black Holes in Non-Linear Electrodynamics

## authors:
#Jana_Menšíková
#David_Kubiznak
#Robert_B_Mann

## summary:
Techniques of AdS/CFT correspondence and braneworld holography have proven to be a useful tool for solving the problem of semi-classical gravity where quantum matter provides the back-reaction to classical spacetime geometry. In this paper, we apply this approach to Einstein gravity coupled to RegMax nonlinear electrodynamics and construct novel type of charged quantum black holes. We then discuss their thermodynamics and horizon structure.
