# Exact scalarization thresholds and onset laws for a nonminimally coupled scalar field on the Ellis wormhole

## authors:
#Abdullah_Guvendi
#Omar_Mustafa
#Claudio_Furtado

## summary:
We study a real scalar field nonminimally coupled to curvature on a fixed Ellis wormhole background. The scalar perturbation decouples at linear order, and the substitution $ x=a \tan z $ reduces its static radial equation to a trigonometric Pöschl-Teller problem. This yields the exact threshold spectrum $ ζ_{n\ell}=(n+\ell+1)^2/2 $, closed-form Gegenbauer profiles, and an exact count of negative radial eigenvalues in each angular sector. The s-wave thresholds are zero-energy resonances at which the growth rate rises linearly, whereas the $ \ell \geq1\ $ threshold states are square integrable and the growth rate rises as a square root. At the first threshold we also obtain the next-order term, $ σa=(π/4)g-(3π/16)g^2+O(g^3) $ with $g=2ζ-1 $. For a probe field with quartic self-interaction, we show that no nontrivial spherical static solution exists for $ζ\leq1/2 $ and derive local branch expansions near the s-wave thresholds. Numerical evolutions are consistent with saturation on the nodeless branch. These nonlinear results refer to the probe-field model; they do not establish the existence or stability of fully backreacted scalarized wormholes.
