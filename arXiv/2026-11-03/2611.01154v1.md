# Behavior of the zero modes in the Light-front for pseudo scalar particles: pion case

## authors:
#J_Pacheco_B_C_de_melo

## summary:
Light-front Quantum Field Theory (LFQFT) stands as an  optimal framework for  delving into the intricate substructure of hadronic  bound states. In this study, we employ a  Light-Front Constituent Quark Model (LFCQM)  to investigate the pion's internal structure. This model has previously  demonstrated significant success in describing the properties of pseudoscalar mesons.  A crucial consideration within the light-front formalism is the dependence on the  chosen reference frame, stemming from the explicit breaking of covariance. Herein, we meticulously  analyze the  contribution of zero modes to the pion's electromagnetic current.  Subsequently, we explore the impact of these zero modes on observable quantities,  including electromagnetic form factors, the pion's electromagnetic radius,  and its weak decay constant, $f_π$.  Furthermore, we comprehensively investigate the behavior of zero modes and their  dependence on both the momentum transfer, $Q^2$, and the model's intrinsic parameters,  such as constituent quark masses ($m_{(q,\bar{q})}$) and the regulator mass ($m_R$).  Our findings reveal  a substantial dependence of light-front zero modes on the specific frame employed  for the calculation of the electromagnetic current. Interestingly, we observe  that the maximal contribution of the zero mode occurs at a specific  four-momentum transfer, denoted as $Q^2 = -q^2 = q_Z^{2(Max.)}$.
