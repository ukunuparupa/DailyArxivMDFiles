# Umbilic Observatories

## authors:
#Dionysios_Anninos
#Raúl_Arias
#Damián_A_Galante

## summary:
We study gravitational dynamics for the simplest example of an umbilic boundary in four-dimensional general relativity --- a three-dimensional de Sitter hypersurface embedded in a four-dimensional Minkowski world. Generally speaking, an umbilic surface has the property that its induced metric is proportional to its extrinsic curvature. We consider the case of both conformal boundary conditions, as well as Dirichlet boundary conditions imposed at the timelike hypersurface. For both cases, we find that there are no linearised modes whose imaginary frequency grows with angular momentum. The conformal boundary conditions allow for a dynamical boundary degree of freedom, in addition to the bulk excitations. The umbilic property linearly decouples the boundary Weyl mode from the bulk dynamics, yielding linearly stable dynamics. In contrast, the Dirichlet problem has no boundary mode. The umbilic hypersurfaces may thus constitute an improved starting point for defining gravitational dynamics in the presence of a timelike boundary. Throughout our analysis, we draw parallels to the structure of spatial infinity for asymptotically Minkowski spacetimes and a more finite realisation thereof.
