# All Symmetries and Boundaries of the Two-Dimensional Free Scalar from Continuous Flat-Gauging

## authors:
#Yichul_Choi
#Ho_Tat_Lam
#Brandon_C_Rayhaun

## summary:
We propose a remote detectability criterion for two-dimensional conformal field theories, which asserts that any local operator outside of the product of the left- and right-moving Virasoro algebras can be detected by some topological line defect. We discuss its relationship to existing results in the conformal net literature. We then use this criterion to argue that the known $SU(2)$ moduli space of unitary conformal boundary conditions and the $SO(4)$ moduli space of unitary topological line defects of the $SU(2)_1$ Wess-Zumino-Witten model completely capture all unitary conformal boundaries and unitary topological lines of the theory. Invoking the fact that the non-compact boson conformal field theory can be obtained from $SU(2)_1$ by "flat-gauging" a $U(1)$ symmetry, we are also able to completely classify the unitary conformal boundaries and unitary topological line defects of the two-dimensional free scalar field. In particular, we find a 4-dimensional moduli space of topological line defects, most of which were not known previously. We support our proposed classifications by writing down concrete Lagrangian descriptions of all of the boundaries and lines of the non-compact boson. For every boundary condition, we calculate the corresponding boundary state, and for each pair of boundary conditions, we calculate the corresponding interval Hilbert space. We verify that the annulus partition functions of the theory respect open/closed duality. We also calculate the fusion rules of the topological line defects, their action on local operators, their action on the boundary conditions by parallel fusion, and their corresponding twisted-sector Hilbert spaces.
