# Zero mode, Zero influence: Spatial-derivative coupling in particle detectors

## authors:
#Eduardo_Martín-Martínez
#Ireneo_James_Membrere
#Adam_Teixidó-Bonfill

## summary:
It is common in particle detector models in cavity QED to often omit zero modes or assume their state, compromising causality or introducing ambiguity in their predictions. We show that spatial-derivative coupling eliminates both problems. For free massless scalar fields in static periodic or Neumann cavities of any spatial dimension, cavity-frame spatial derivatives annihilate the zero-mode operator while preserving microcausality. Detector dynamics and entanglement harvesting then depend only on the reduced detector-oscillator state, nonperturbatively and even with initial correlations with the zero mode. Time derivatives and moving-detector rest-frame spatial derivatives can retain zero-mode state dependence not compromising causality. Realized through current coupling in superconducting circuits, spatial-derivative coupling thus makes zero-mode state assumptions unnecessary.
