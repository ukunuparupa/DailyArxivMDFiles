# Dyeing form factors as amplitudes

## authors:
#Xinyue_Li
#Gang_Yang
#Guorui_Zhu

## summary:
The double copy of form factors has revealed a striking feature: poles that are spurious from the gauge-theory perspective become physical propagators in gravity. At the same time, form factors obey hidden factorization relations on the kinematics of these poles. We explain both phenomena by introducing a dyeing procedure, which promotes the color-singlet operator, or the Higgs particle representing it, to an adjoint massive state. The original form factor is recovered by the inverse bleaching operation, realized as a $U(1)$ decoupling of the dyed leg. In the dyed theory, these apparent spurious poles turn into ordinary physical propagators of colored amplitudes, and the hidden factorization relations follow from standard BCJ relations. Applying this framework to multiple operator insertions gives a systematic double-copy construction for multi-Higgs amplitudes and, as a byproduct, reveals scalar-ordering sectors. We also discuss higher-length scalar operators and fermionic operators, including the dyed vector construction for $\barψγ^μψ$, as well as a loop-level example.
