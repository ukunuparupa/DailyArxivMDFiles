# Landau Theory of Black-Brane Phase Transitions from Kasner Singularities

## authors:
#Hong-Da_Lyu

## summary:
We develop a Landau theory of a continuous black-brane phase transition using the geometry of the black-hole interior. The normal phase has a Reissner--Nordström Cauchy horizon, whereas the scalarized phase ends at a Kasner singularity. A conserved radial charge determines the grand potential from either endpoint and permits a direct phase comparison. At the critical point, we show analytically that the quadratic correction to the conserved radial charge vanishes. Using the inverse Kasner scalar velocity as an interior order parameter, we then construct a Landau potential whose coefficients are determined entirely from the terminal singularity. The Iyer--Wald first law relates this potential to the conventional boundary Landau theory and fixes its quartic coefficient. For two models with different scalar self-interactions, the resulting theory predicts the critical scaling of the Kasner order parameter and the grand-potential difference between the phases, in agreement with fully backreacted numerical solutions.
