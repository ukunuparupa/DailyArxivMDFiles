# Renormalized dynamical tidal response of neutron stars in binary dynamics

## authors:

#Raj_Patil

## summary:
We present the relativistic dynamical tidal response of a neutron star computed using stellar perturbation theory and matched to a worldline effective field theory via the tidal scattering phase. This phase is defined as the difference between the Compton/Raman scattering phase of gravitational-waves from a neutron star and the real part of the black hole scattering phase. The gravitational corrections to the response function are obtained using self-energy corrections up to fourth post-Minkowskian order. The response function for neutron stars obeying the BSk22 equation-of-state from Brussels-Skyrme family, is then consistently matched to an effective theory of relativistic quadrupolar harmonic oscillators to model the fundamental-mode ($f$-mode) and low-frequency gravity-modes ($g$-modes). This matching includes the frequency and tidal coupling of each mode and the post-adiabatic dynamical tidal Love number, and once matched this model is in excellent agreement with the full neutron star response. With the external tidal field up to third post-Newtonian order, we also analyse the tidal coupling of the induced quadrupole on the neutron star in a circular binary system, and the adiabatic binding energy.
