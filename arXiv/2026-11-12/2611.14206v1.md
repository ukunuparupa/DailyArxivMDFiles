# Yang-Lee edge singularity at $\mathcal{O}(\partial^4)$ from the functional renormalization group: revised analysis

## authors:
#Konrad_Kockler
#Jan_M_Pawlowski
#Vladimir_V_Skokov

## summary:
We determine the critical exponents of the Yang-Lee edge singularity as continuous functions of dimension using the functional renormalization group, retaining all operators at second and fourth order in the derivative expansion. At fourth order, we obtain $η=-0.153(7)$, $-0.352(9)$ and $-0.575(9)$ in $d=5$, $4$ and $3$, respectively, in agreement with the six-loop $ε$ expansion. In three dimensions, these results give $σ=0.076$ and $ν_c=0.359$. We locate the zero of the edge exponent at $d_σ=2.65(1)$, also consistent with the six-loop estimate. Continuing the fixed point through $d_σ$, we obtain $η=-0.795(21)$ and $ω=1.90(6)$ in $d=2$, compared with the exact values $-4/5$ and $2$.  For the complex Yang-Lee fixed point, convergence of the field expansion depends crucially on its reference point. Expansions about $φ=0$ fail to converge with the smooth regulator used here, whereas expansions about the running imaginary field value at which the mass vanishes converge down to $d=1.3$. This representation preserves the exact relation between the thermal eigenvalue and the field dimension, and hence the hyperscaling identity $θ_h+θ_t=d$, at every field-truncation order. We trace the large violations reported in earlier works to the omission of the expansion-point motion when linearizing the flow, rather than to the truncation itself.
