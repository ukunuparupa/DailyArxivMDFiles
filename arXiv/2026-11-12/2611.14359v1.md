# Multiloop Unitarity from Airy Zeros

## authors:
#Laurentiu_Rodina

## summary:
Hidden zeros are non-trivial cancellations that make amplitudes or integrands vanish on specific kinematic loci away from propagator poles. They have been shown to uniquely determine tree amplitudes and one-loop surface integrands in $\Tr(φ^3)$ theory, without assuming factorization. Here we extend both the zeros and the uniqueness results to two, three and four loops at arbitrary multiplicity. We show that two types of zeros naturally enter the all-loop problem. First, tree-level zeros extend to all loop orders and fix relative weights within families of subgraphs attached to vacuum ``loop cores.'' We then show that Kontsevich's matrix Airy function can be used to generate a rich structure of loop level zeros that fix the relative weights of the loop cores themselves. The relative factorization and cut data required by unitarity therefore follow from the combination of tree and Airy loop zeros. These results bring within reach the emergence of unitarity to all orders in perturbation theory.
