# Generalizations of Courant brackets in the T-dual description of a weakly curved string background

## authors:
#Ilija_Ivanišević

## summary:
When T-duality is realized as a canonical transformation, the standard Courant bracket is T-dual invariant, while the $B$- and $θ$-twisted Courant brackets are exchanged. We determine how these relations are modified for a closed bosonic string moving in a weakly curved background with a constant metric and a Kalb-Ramond field with linear coordinate dependence. We work to first order in its constant field strength. The T-dual background fields depend on a line integral of auxiliary worldsheet gauge fields, making the dual description nonlocal. We construct generalized charges whose parameters depend on this line integral and calculate their Poisson bracket algebra using the T-duality transformation laws and the canonical brackets of the initial theory. The resulting algebra yields a weakly curved generalization of the Courant bracket and its associated Courant algebroid. We establish an explicit isomorphism with the standard Courant algebroid by transporting the bracket, pairing, and anchor. Applying $B$- and $θ$-shifts to the dual currents, we obtain weakly curved generalizations of the respective twisted Courant brackets. We show that these brackets provide the T-dual realizations of the initial $θ$- and $B$-twisted Courant brackets, respectively, and identify the corresponding Courant algebroid isomorphisms.
