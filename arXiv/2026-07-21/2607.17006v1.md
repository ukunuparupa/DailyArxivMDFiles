# The equation of state for neutron stars

## authors:
#Veronica_Dexheimer

## summary:
This chapter is intended as an introduction to dense matter and the equation of state of neutron stars and their mergers. It begins with a brief description of neutron star interiors, followed by a historical overview of the theoretical frameworks used to describe them focusing on relativistic formalisms, including different degrees of freedom, models, symmetries, and phases. It also provides an overview of our current understanding of dense matter (including theory, experiments, and observations) and discusses the advances we expect to see in the field over the next decade.
