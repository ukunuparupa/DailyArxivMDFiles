# The next-to-next-to-leading order BFKL eigenvalue at odd conformal spin in planar N=4 super Yang-Mills: closed form, coefficient structure, and arithmetic

## authors:
#Alex_Prygarin
#Claudelle_Capasia_Madjuogang_Sandeu

## summary:
We present the three-loop (next-to-next-to-leading order) BFKL eigenvalue of planar N=4 super Yang-Mills at odd conformal spin n in closed form, as a finite combination of continued nested harmonic sums and rational functions of z=(|n|-1)/2 + i nu on an integer-shifted ladder. For each odd spin the atom table is extracted exactly, in rational arithmetic, from the Caron-Huot-Herranen three-loop integrand; a shipped command regenerates it for any odd n>=3, with n=1 a supplied boundary block. A resummed master generating function, conjectured to reproduce those integrands at every spin (verified through n=17 and at m=9,11,13,15,21,31), is not used for the per-spin tables. At nu=0 the intercepts reproduce the independent Quantum Spectral Curve values spin by spin through n=91. The coefficients are rational multiples of 1, pi^2 and zeta_3; in a fixed elementary basis, forty of the forty-seven slots close as harmonic sums of the two ladder distances (all-spin form conjectural). The residual five-kernel layer includes one grid obeying a holonomic recurrence; once the harmonic sums are separated the coefficient-weighted ladder collapses onto two depth-one polygamma transcendentals. We report an arithmetic obstruction: the exceptional-grid coefficients recruit a new denominator prime at each n=p+2 (n-2 prime) through the computed range, a finite law we prove and conjecture to continue; on that continuation their generating data cannot be algebraic or globally bounded. Even spins are conjecturally contained in the same master. At each odd spin the eigenvalue is the product-free case of the Kotikov-Lipatov hermitian-separable form. A companion Letter announces the result.
