# The next-to-next-to-leading order BFKL eigenvalue at odd conformal spin in planar N=4 super Yang-Mills

## authors:
#Alex_Prygarin
#Claudelle_Capasia_Madjuogang_Sandeu

## summary:
We present the three-loop (next-to-next-to-leading order) BFKL eigenvalue of planar N=4 super Yang-Mills at odd conformal spin n in closed form, as a finite combination of nested harmonic sums and rational functions of z=(|n|-1)/2 + i nu. For each odd n>=3 the atom table is extracted exactly, in rational arithmetic, from the Caron-Huot-Herranen three-loop integrand by direct Mellin extraction; a shipped command regenerates it on demand, with n=1 a supplied boundary block. The coefficients are rational multiples of 1, pi^2 and zeta_3; in a fixed elementary basis, forty of the forty-seven coefficient slots close as harmonic sums of the two ladder distances (all-spin form conjectural, the split a property of the basis), while the residual five-kernel layer includes one grid obeying a finite-data holonomic recurrence, an all-n rule for the rest still open. Beyond the explicit harmonic sums the coefficient-weighted ladder collapses onto depth-one digamma and trigamma functions. At nu=0 the intercepts reproduce the independent Quantum Spectral Curve values spin by spin through n=91. At each odd spin the eigenvalue is strictly product-free, the additive case of the Kotikov-Lipatov hermitian-separable form; the next-to-leading anomalous term carrying 1+(-1)^n is absent at odd n. No n-uniform form is claimed. Derivations (eleven of sixteen kernel closures proven, five verified numerically) and complete expressions are given in a companion paper.
