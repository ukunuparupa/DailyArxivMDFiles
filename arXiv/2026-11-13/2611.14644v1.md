# Gravitational observables from a post-Carrollian expansion

## authors:
#Max_Niedermaier
#Mahdi_Sedighi_Jafari

## summary:
Einstein gravity, with a few exceptions, only admits nonlocal observables. This feature is often attributed to diffeomorphism invariance, since a gravitational field can thereby locally be transformed away. We show this attribution to be incorrect by constructing infinitely many exact ultralocal Dirac observables in (velocity dominated aka electric) Carroll gravity. The latter describes Einstein gravity's ultrarelativistic limit, is fully diffeomorphism invariant, and of independent interest in a variety of contexts. Starting from these Carroll observables a systematic construction of Dirac observables for Einstein gravity is outlined. The resulting "post-Carrollian" expansion is organized by the number of spatial gradients or fractional inverse powers of Newton's constant, where each partial sum is local.
