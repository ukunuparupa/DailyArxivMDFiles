# BPS invariants from resurgent topological string

## authors:
#Jie_Gu

## summary:
We review recent progress on the conjectural relation between the resurgent structure of topological string free energies and BPS invariants of type II string theory compactified on the same Calabi-Yau threefold. In this picture, Borel singularities are located at integral periods associated with central charges of stable D-brane bound states, while Stokes constants encode their BPS indices, or generalized DT invariants. After introducing resurgence and perturbative topological strings, we explain how trans-series solutions of the holomorphic anomaly equations determine non-perturbative amplitudes and illustrate the proposed correspondence in local and compact geometries. We discuss the behavior under changes of polarization and moduli, and review supporting arguments based on exact WKB analysis, blowup equations, the Stokes automorphisms of flat coordinates and dual partition functions, and Kontsevich-Soibelman wall-crossing. We conclude with open questions concerning a general proof, the interpretation of non-perturbative amplitudes, and the construction of non-perturbative completions.
