# Galilean cosmologies in two dimensions

## authors:
#Daniel_Grumiller
#Kai-Yu_Zhang

## summary:
Inspired by Carroll black holes, we consider the most general theory of Galilean dilaton gravity in two dimensions. We find generically that the solution space describes cosmological spacetimes. From a limiting perspective, these Galilean geometries connect the white and black hole singularities through the bifurcation surface. The latter turns into a Galilean extremal surface in the limit of infinite speed of light. We also discuss aspects of Galilean tachydynamics and find that the entropy is given by the dilaton evaluated at the Galilean extremal surface.
