# When Nonlocal Total Derivatives Are Not Variationally Trivial

## authors:
#Carlos_Heredia

## summary:
We study total time derivatives in infinite-order and nonlocal Lagrangian mechanics. In the formal infinite-order setting, such terms remain variationally trivial. For nonlocal functionals on trajectories, however, exactness in the form $L=DW$ is not sufficient to ensure a vanishing nonlocal Euler-Lagrange expression. We exhibit a smooth convolution-type counterexample.
