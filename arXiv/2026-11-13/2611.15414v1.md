# Exact model of a gravitational wave and electromagnetic radiation in DHOST gravity theory: wave solutions and testable effects

## authors:
#Konstantin_E_Osetrin

## summary:
Within an analytical non-perturbative framework based on a single exact model, the coupled dynamics of gravitational waves and high-frequency electromagnetic fields is investigated in General Relativity and DHOST gravity. Using an exact gravitational-wave spacetime in an isotropic (null) wave coordinate system, Maxwell's equations are integrated analytically. This analysis provides an exact non-perturbative confirmation from the full field equations that a gravitational wave acts as a waveguide, forming a propagation channel for electromagnetic radiation co-directional with the gravitational wave and generating an effective pure-radiation source. Applying this exact electrodynamic solution to the nonlinear field equations of DHOST class~Ia gravity reveals that the algebraic structure of the DHOST constraint equations enforces a modification of the electromagnetic-wave parameters. Based on this mechanism, an observationally testable effect of scalar frequency drift is derived, providing a direct way to discriminate between General Relativity and modified gravity theories even when their metric tensors share identical functional forms.
