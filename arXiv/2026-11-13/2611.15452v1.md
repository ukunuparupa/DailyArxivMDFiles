# On JT boundary algebras

## authors:
#Feng_Xu

## summary:
We construct the canonical boundary algebras of Jackiw--Teitelboim gravity coupled to free bosonic matter. For a nonempty finite or countable family of species with lowest weights greater than one half, the two boundary algebras are mutual commutants and nonhyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_\infty$ factors. Their nonzero finite corners are nonhyperfinite type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factors. The construction uses bounded heat-regularized fields and a Hilbert algebra, which gives a faithful normal semifinite trace without a path-integral argument. The weight restriction is sharp: elementary point fields remain unbounded after positive heat regularization when $0<d\leq1/2$. For one massless scalar, specified bounded observables and normal states have a high energy limit described by a hyperfinite continuous core. We explain why this limit does not preserve the nonhyperfiniteness of the exact algebra.
