# Ernst-Potential Driven Recovery of Kerr with Neural Networks

## authors:
#Veselin_G_Filev
#Stoytcho_S_Yazadjiev

## summary:
We use artificial neural networks to recover the Kerr solution by directly minimizing a regularized Ernst sigma-model action. Reduction along the axial Killing direction avoids the divergence at the ergosphere. Compactified coordinates cover the entire black-hole exterior, while the network parametrization enforces the asymptotic data and axis regularity. For dimensionless spins $0.1 \le a/M \le 0.99$, the median maximum relative error in the recovered metric component $g_{φφ}$ remains below $7.2\times10^{-4}$. When only the horizon rod length and angular momentum are prescribed, the networks also predict the mass with median relative errors below $1.8\times10^{-3}$. These results demonstrate the practicality of direct variational training for rotating black-hole geometries.
