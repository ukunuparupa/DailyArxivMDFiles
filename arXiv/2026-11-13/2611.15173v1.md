# Canonical Commutation Relations and Poisson Brackets with Non-locality in Time

## authors:
#Yasu_Gen
#Hiroaki_Matsunaga
#Jojiro_Totsuka

## summary:
We study the canonical structure of a string-inspired model in which the kinetic term couples fields at times separated by a finite shift $σ$, with a coupling $λ$ to a fixed background. From the boundary term in the variation of the action, we construct a conserved symplectic form and derive the positive-time Poisson bracket. Using the Schwinger--Dyson relation, we show that at positive time separation $s$ the commutator equals $i\hbar$ times this bracket. The canonical normalization $\hbar$ appears as a jump of the commutator expectation value at $s=σ$, which is equal time for shifted variables with a local kinetic term; for $0<s<σ$ the commutator vanishes for every $λ>0$. Hence $λ\to0$ at fixed $σ$ does not reproduce the free commutator on this interval, whereas $σ\to0$ at fixed $λ$ recovers the local commutator at every fixed $s>0$.
