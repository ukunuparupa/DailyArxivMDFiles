# Simplicity of the Three-Loop Six-Point ABJM Amplitude

## authors:
#Hanyu_Fang
#Chia-Kai_Kuo
#Kai_Yan

## summary:
We compute the three-loop six-point amplitude in planar ABJM theory using negative geometries. At this order, only a single bipartite chain contributes to the logarithm of the amplitude, and we find that its dual-conformally invariant integrand is locally finite. The integrated chain is therefore infrared finite and, remarkably, equals exactly $-π^2$ times the one-loop amplitude. Hence the collinear anomalous dimension, like the cusp, receives no three-loop correction. Restoring the disconnected products of lower-loop contributions, which carry all the infrared divergences, we obtain the full three-loop amplitude, fixed entirely by lower-loop data and the coefficient $-π^2$. These results reveal an unexpected simplicity of the six-point ABJM amplitude and suggest an iterative relation between its odd- and even-loop terms.
