# $n$-Gon Correlators in $d$-dimensions

## authors:
#Yong-Xiang_Su
#Dongyu_Yang

## summary:
We study the analytic structure of scalar one-loop cosmological $n$-gon correlators using an integrand-level reduction and a Baikov representation adapted to linear tube-energy denominators. At spatial dimension $d=n$, we determine the algebraic factors that normalize their local leading singularities to unity and construct explicit $d\log$ forms for arbitrary $n$. For the complete box at $d=n=4$, we evaluate an external-energy discontinuity in a collinear kinematic family and find a nonvanishing complete elliptic integral of the second kind after summing all time orderings. We also compare successive tube-energy discontinuities in $d=3$ and $d=4$, and relate selected tube cuts to the cosmological dressing representation. These results illustrate the importance of integration domains in determining the analytic structure beyond local leading singularities.
