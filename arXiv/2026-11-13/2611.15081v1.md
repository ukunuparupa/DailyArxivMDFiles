# Maximally Chiral Eigenstate from Inverse Thermodynamic Bethe Ansatz

## authors:
#Tigran_A_Sedrakyan

## summary:
We show that an ordered quantum state can support waves with a finite speed in one direction and exponentially small energies in the other. We prescribe this spectrum for a spin-$1/2$ Heisenberg eigenstate and reconstruct a nonlocal Hamiltonian. The thermodynamic construction selects the ordered, current-carrying state as a ground state. Its magnetic moment remains finite while its response to a weak field has a divergent slope. The entropy per spin vanishes only inversely with a logarithm of temperature, with an exact coefficient containing the golden ratio. Our spin-response calculations reveal a bound pair whose energy can be tuned without changing its intensity. Added exchange and smoother mode selection restore quadratic excitations at the lowest energies. These results connect the design of interactions to directional dynamics and unusual thermodynamics. We propose momentum-resolved spin spectroscopy, weak-field magnetometry, and low-temperature entropy measurements to probe these counterintuitive signatures.
