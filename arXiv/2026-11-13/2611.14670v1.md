# A cubic closed string field theory

## authors:
#Harold_Erbin

## summary:
The action of closed bosonic string field theory is non-polynomial, and for more than thirty years a no-go theorem of Sonoda and Zwiebach (1990) has excluded a cubic formulation with a single string field, a symmetric vertex and the canonical propagator. We evade it with a Hubbard--Stratonovich transformation and provide an explicit construction of a cubic closed string field theory at all orders. The transformation introduces an infinite tower of auxiliary $k$-string fields $Σ_k$, entering at order $g_s^{2 k - 2}$, which turn the vertices with four or more legs into kinetic terms and cubic couplings. The action is cubic, at most quadratic in each auxiliary field, and triangular: eliminating the auxiliary fields by Gaussian integrals recovers the original action order by order at tree level, under invertibility and kernel conditions on the auxiliary kinetic operators. The original gauge symmetry lifts to the cubic action with no new parameter, and we argue that the auxiliary fields add no physical state at generic momentum, wherever their kinetic operators are invertible. On the worldsheet, $Σ_2$ is an entangled two-string state: by an operator product expansion, the quartic form reduces to a gluing of two asymmetric cubic vertices dressed by the motion of the local coordinates and defined on states without level matching. We expect this picture to generalize to higher orders, defining a gluing of patches carrying $k$ punctures each. The string vertices, read as products with several outputs, satisfy an extended form of the genus-zero $\mathrm{IBL}_\infty$ relations, including operations with no input or no output, exactly when the string products form a cyclic $L_\infty$ algebra. Finally, we compare our action with the bar--cobar construction of Chiaffrino, Mamade and Zwiebach.
