# A consistent formulation of stochastic inflation II: Reassessing its role in nonlinear cosmological perturbations

## authors:
#Diego_Cruces
#Tomotaka_Kuroda

## summary:
We question whether the conventional formulation of stochastic inflation can provide a quantitatively reliable description of cosmological perturbations. More fundamentally, we reassess the role and motivations of the stochastic approach to study cosmological perturbations, distinct from its well-founded motivation for resumming IR secular divergences. To provide a quantitative basis for this reassessment, we extend stochastic inflation beyond the linear regime by incorporating non-Markovian effects arising from infrared-ultraviolet interactions and terms quadratic in ultraviolet fluctuations, which generate non-white noise. We derive three-point correlation functions for a spectator scalar field in de Sitter space and a slow-roll inflaton including metric perturbations, demonstrating exact agreement with in-in quantum-field-theoretic predictions for superhorizon nonlinear evolution. Our results show that both non-Markovian effects and quadratic (higher order) noise terms are essential for quantitative accuracy, implying that the conventional stochastic-inflation framework is, at best, quantitatively justified only at linear order.
