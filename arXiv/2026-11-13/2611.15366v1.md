# Six-Loop Renormalization in Quantum Electrodynamics

## authors:
#Zeyu_Li
#Ming-xing_Luo
#Tong-Zhi_Yang
#Hua_Xing_Zhu

## summary:
We analytically compute the six-loop corrections to the scale evolution of the electric charge, fermion mass, and fermion field in quantum electrodynamics. The results are obtained in the $\MSbar$ scheme for an arbitrary number of equally charged fermions. They extend renormalization in quantum electrodynamics by one loop order and allow new tests of the behavior of perturbation theory at high orders. The methods developed here also open the way to six-loop computations of renormalization in quantum chromodynamics.
