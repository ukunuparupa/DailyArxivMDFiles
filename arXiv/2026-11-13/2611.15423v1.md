# D(-1)/D7 bound states in supergravity

## authors:
#Gongrui_Cheng
#Klaas_Parmentier
#Thomas_Van_Riet

## summary:
We construct D($-$1)/D7 bound states in Euclidean type IIB supergravity motivated by the string-theory setup of Billò et al., whose open-string description realizes instantons in eight-dimensional super Yang--Mills theory. Previous supergravity constructions either left unresolved issues concerning ten-dimensional Euclidean supersymmetry or relied on partial smearing to codimension one. We classify the local codimension-two solutions by showing that the supersymmetric field equations reduce to two holomorphic functions subject to a single complex constraint. The candidate bound-state solutions require complementary D3-brane fluxes, consistent with the D7 worldvolume analysis. A particularly interesting solution has constant dilaton and connects the vacuum at infinity to a controlled scaling limit in which the geometry becomes $\mathbb R\times S^1\times\mathbb T^8$.
