# A ménage à quatre without strings attached

## authors:
#Iosif_Bena
#Gongrui_Cheng
#Klaas_Parmentier
#Dimitrios_Toulikas
#Thomas_Van_Riet

## summary:
When two D-branes with eight mixed Dirichlet-Neumann directions cross, a fundamental string is created. This is the Hanany-Witten (HW) effect. In supergravity, the string shows up as an electric NSNS B-field sourced by the RR fields of the two branes. We show that two brane pairs, each of which would create an HW string on its own, can combine into a quadruple bound state with no HW strings attached. We explicitly construct a background with the fluxes of such a D0-D8-D4-D4' quadruple. Requiring no HW strings imposes a quadratic relation among the fluxes. We also realize this bound state as a supersymmetric solution of the non-Abelian DBI action of the D8-branes, whose Killing spinors match those of the bulk supergravity solution. The fluxed D8 stack acts as a domain wall between two supersymmetric D0-D8-D4-D4' backgrounds. Each background satisfies the no-string constraint, and the jumps in the fluxes give the charges carried by the stack.
