# Solving spectral flow in AdS$_3$: from covering maps to pencils

## authors:
#Sergio_Iguri
#Nicolas_Kovensky
#Julian_H_Toro

## summary:
Strings in AdS$_3$ with pure NS-NS flux are described by the exactly solvable SL(2,$\mathbb{R}$) WZW model, yet correlators of winding strings, given by spectrally flowed operators, were so far known only up to three points. We obtain all of them, for any number of insertions and windings, in terms of unflowed correlators, proving the four-point conjecture of Dei and Eberhardt. The key ingredient is a pencil of rational maps from the worldsheet to the boundary which exists for generic insertion points, unwinds all insertions at once, and collapses to the covering map when the latter exists.
