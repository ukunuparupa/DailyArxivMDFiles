# Numerical Thermodynamics of AdS Black Holes with Higher-Order Mass Corrections

## authors:
#Saad_Eddine_Baddis
#Adil_Belhaj
#Hajar_Belmahi
#Yassine_Hassouni
#Maryem_Jemri

## summary:
Inspired by noncommutative geometry and quintessence, we construct a new class of asymptotically anti-de Sitter black hole solutions characterized by a higher-order mass deformation term $\frac{aM^{n-1}}{r^n}$ involving two parameters, $a$ and $n$. Due to analytical difficulties, we approach the underlying thermodynamics through parallel programming simulations using CUDA codes. By considering models with $n=3$, $n=4$, and $n=5$, we numerically study certain thermodynamic properties, including the stability, the $P$-$v$ criticality, and the phase transitions.
