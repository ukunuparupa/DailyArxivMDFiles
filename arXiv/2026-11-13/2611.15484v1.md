# Holographic dual of the 2d Ising model

## authors:
#Kazumi_Okuyama

## summary:
We propose a holographic dual of the 2d Ising model on a torus as the Turaev-Viro TQFT on a solid torus. The bulk spacetime is divided into the ``core'' region and the ``sandwich'' region. The core region prepares a defect state $|\mathcal{D}\rangle$ on the topological boundary $\mathbb{D}$. The sandwich region computes the Turaev-Viro state sum $\langle\mathcal{D}|Π|\mathcal{F}\rangle$ between the state $|\mathcal{D}\rangle$ on $\mathbb{D}$ and the state $|\mathcal{F}\rangle$ on the physical boundary $\mathbb{B}$. The fusion diagram (or string-net) $\mathcal{F}$ on $\mathbb{B}$ is given by the Aasen-Fendley-Mong construction. There is also a ``bedding surface'' $\mathbb{S}$ between $\mathbb{D}$ and $\mathbb{B}$, and the height variables (or Ising spins inside/outside of the domain wall) are naturally living on $\mathbb{S}$. The bulk spacetime is discretized by the 2-skeleton $P$ which interpolates $\mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{F}$. When the defect $\mathcal{D}$ is absent, the 2-skeleton $P$ ends on the bedding surface $\mathbb{S}$ and $P$ is interpreted as the bulk worldsheet of the string-net $\mathcal{F}$.
