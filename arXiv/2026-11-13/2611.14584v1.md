## summary:
In $Λ$CDM, the CMB and DESI BAO upper limits on the total neutrino mass are in tension with the lower bounds from oscillation experiments, and analyses allowing negative effective masses favor negative values. We investigate whether a coupled quintessence (CQ) model in which dark matter interacts with a canonical scalar field $φ$ through an exponential mass coupling $m_{\rm c}(φ)\propto e^{-βφ/M_{\rm Pl}}$ can alleviate this tension. Combining CMB, DESI, and supernova data, we find that CQ yields neutrino-mass constraints comparable to those obtained in CPL and consistent with oscillation experiments within $2σ$ for normal ordering, without requiring the scalar-field equation of state to cross the phantom divide. We further show that it alleviates the tension more effectively than alternative interacting dark energy models that instead favor more negative effective masses. This comparison shows that whether dark sector interactions can relax neutrino mass bounds ultimately depends on the interplay of their effects on primary CMB anisotropies, geometry, growth, and lensing.
