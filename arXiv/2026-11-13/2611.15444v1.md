# Spectra and Central Charges of Gauged Curvature Squared Supergravities in Five Dimensions

## authors:
#Erol_Aksoy
#Cagatay_Okyay
#Mehmet_Ozkan
#Cemal_Berfu_Senisik

## summary:
We study the spectra and the central charges of off-shell gauged five-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ supergravity extended by the Weyl squared, Ricci tensor squared and Ricci scalar squared invariants, and by their Gauss-Bonnet combination. Working exactly in the couplings and keeping all auxiliary fields, we organize the spectra around the maximally supersymmetric AdS$_5$ vacuum into supermultiplets. With the Ricci scalar squared invariant, the spectrum gains a long vector multiplet and is free of ghosts in a finite range of the coupling, while with the Weyl squared or Ricci tensor squared invariant, it gains a long spin-2 multiplet and always contains ghosts or instabilities. The Gauss-Bonnet combination adds no new modes: around its supersymmetric vacuum, the quadratic action is a rescaled copy of that of the two-derivative theory, and this fixes the spectrum of the Ricci tensor squared extension. We also compute the central charges $a$ and $c$ and the flavor central charge exactly in the couplings. The vacuum equation is $a$-maximization, and the kinetic terms of the massless graviton and vector multiplets are proportional to $c$ and to the flavor central charge, so that the critical points, where the massive spin-2 multiplet becomes degenerate with the graviton multiplet, are zeros of $c$. Finally, around supersymmetric AdS$_3\timesΣ_2$ backgrounds of the Gauss-Bonnet extension, the two-dimensional central charges are reproduced by $c$-extremization, while the spectrum is generically not unitary and contains towers of states whose energies stay bounded at large Kaluza-Klein levels. In part of the parameter space, the tower of spin-2 multiplets has positive norm, so that the spectrum has no finite density of states.
