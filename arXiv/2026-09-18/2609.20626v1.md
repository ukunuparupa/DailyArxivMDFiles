# On the Extended Kerr-Newman-Bertotti-Robinson Spacetime: Two Black Holes and a Naked Singularity in Bertotti-Robinson Universe

## authors:
#Yu-Sen_Zhou
#Wen-Tao_Fu
#Li-Ming_Cao
#Rong-Gen_Cai

## summary:
We introduce new coordinates that extend the Kerr--Newman--Bertotti--Robinson spacetime consistently with the previous reciprocal continuation. In these coordinates, the two points at $Ω=0$ are resolved into complete Bertotti--Robinson null infinities. The extended spacetime describes two black holes sharing a common exterior of nontrivial topology, together with a naked ring singularity. In the regular static limit, the new coordinates globally identify the spacetime with the balanced Alekseev--García geometry.
