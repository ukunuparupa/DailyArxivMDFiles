# On the Gauge-Invariant Fermion

## authors:
#Kang-Sin_Choi

## summary:
We show that the Dirac dressing of the fermion is equivalent to a shift of the gauge parameter. For every gauge, the gauge-dependent part is projected out of the self-energy. After renormalization, the physical mass is the same for every dressing. The non-locality, compositeness, and path dependence associated with the dressing are therefore not physical obstructions.
