# From MHV Gravity Amplitudes and Hodges map to Pentad Varieties

## authors:
#Joris_Koefler
#Emanuele_Ventura
#Hongmiao_Yu

## summary:
We study some geometric aspects behind the Hodges matrix appearing in the context of scattering amplitudes for the $\mathcal N=8$ supergravity. Through a vector bundle construction, we show that the Hodges matrix gives rise to a canonical rational map from the two-step flag variety to symmetric matrices, called the $\textit{Hodges map}$. This construction allows to define the amplitude geometrically: its vanishing locus is a divisor - the $\textit{MHV gravity divisor}$. Inspired by the Hodges map, we introduce the notion of Hadamard ratio of two varieties. In the case relevant to gravity, this is the ratio of two Grassmannians, which we call the $\textit{pentad variety}$, connecting it to the pentad constraint from algebraic statistics. Moreover, we show that the pentad variety is closely related to the combinatorial algebraic geometry of Martin's affine slope variety, and the phirotopes of Below, Krummeck, and Richter-Gebert.
