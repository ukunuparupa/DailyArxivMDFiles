# Spectral function, OPE, and QNMs detect different singular points of black branes with unequal R-charges

## authors:
#Hyunwoo_Oh

## summary:
We consider black brane solutions in the STU model, with three R-charges labeled by the parameters $q_1,q_2,q_3$. For equal R-charges $q_1=q_2=q_3$, the solution becomes the Reissner-Nordström black brane. For unequal R-charges $q_1>q_2>q_3\geq0$, there are three curvature singular points at $R\equiv r^2=-q_i$. Only $R=-q_3$ is the physical singularity, and the other two lie beyond it. Their optical times $T_i$, the complex times of null rays from the boundary to these singular points and back, form a right triangle in the complex $T$-plane. We probe these singular points using three observables, all built from the retarded correlator of $T_{xy}$ at zero spatial momentum: spectral function, OPE coefficients, and QNMs. The exponentially small correction to the spectral function at large real frequency is dominated by $T_1$ and $T_2$, which have the smallest ${\rm Im}\,T_i$. The OPE convergence radius is determined by $T_1$, which has the smallest $|T_i|$. A line of QNMs lies along the direction of $1/T_2$, since $T_2$ has the smallest phase $\arg T_i$. Furthermore, there is a second line of QNMs along the negative imaginary axis, which carries information about $T_3$ corresponding to the physical singularity. We derive these results from a WKB analysis and check them numerically. The three observables are therefore sensitive to different singular points of the geometry.
