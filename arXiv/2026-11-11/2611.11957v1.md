# Measurements of $C\!P$ violation with charm hadrons at Belle and Belle II

## authors:
#Jaeyoung_Kim

## summary:
Recent searches for $C\!P$ violation in charm-hadron decays at Belle and Belle II are presented. The measurements cover $D^+_{(s)}\toηh^+$, $D\toππ$, $D^0\to K_S^0K_S^0$, and $D^0\toπ^+π^-π^0$, together with the singly Cabibbo-suppressed baryon decays $Ξ_c^+\toΣ^+h^+h^-$ and $Λ_c^+\to ph^+h^-$, where $h=π,K$. The Belle II analyses use the Run~1 data sample of $428\,\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$, supplemented by $980\,\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$ of Belle data for the $K_S^0K_S^0$ measurements. We report preliminary results for the $ηh^+$ modes, complementary tests of the isospin structure of $D\toππ$, and an application of inclusive charm-flavor tagging. The first individual decay-rate asymmetries in these three-body charmed-baryon modes also provide tests of $U$-spin symmetry. All reported asymmetries are consistent with $C\!P$ symmetry within the present precision.
