# Gravitational Particle Production and Locality of the Wavefunction-Squared

## authors:
#Emine_Şeyma_Kutluk

## summary:
We show that no particle production due to the background geometry implies the locality of the wavefunction-squared for free heavy masses on expanding cosmologies. On global de Sitter of odd dimensions these two stem from the same identity, whereas in the Poincare patch neither occur under any condition. These two phenomena both depend on the behaviour of the out modes at far future and we show that they almost always come together for systems with an out mode that has a phase with separated time and momentum dependency at far future. This will be true for most expanding cosmologies. We comment on how the discussion can be connected to Green's functions and on some previous results in the literature that are seemingly contrary to our conclusions.
