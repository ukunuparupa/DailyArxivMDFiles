# Nuclear geometry under external magnetic field in the Skyrme-Maxwell model

## authors:
#Christoph_Adam
#Andrés_R_Anadón
#Carlos_Naya
#Thomas_Winyard

## summary:
We find static solutions of the U(1) gauged Skyrme-Maxwell model in a constant external magnetic field, for baryon numbers $B \leq 6$. The field acts as an isospin-breaking source, stretching the skyrmion along its direction, while its size grows and then shrinks as the induction increases. We obtain the energy minimizers with a GPU implementation of the arrested Newton flow (ANF) algorithm. Gauged skyrmions have less energy and are more strongly bound than their ungauged counterpart. At weak magnetic field, the solutions are small deformations of the ungauged skyrmions, including regions of negative baryon density in sectors $B = 3, 5, 6$. For intermediate field, novel geometries appear, notably a $(B-1)$-toroidal chain. High field values lead to axially symmetric toroidal structures for all $B$.
