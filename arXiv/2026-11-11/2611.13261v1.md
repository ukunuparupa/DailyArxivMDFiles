# Expiscating the black hole singularity in holography

## authors:
#Hewei_Frederic_Jia
#Mukund_Rangamani

## summary:
We argue that the leading exponential rate in the high frequency asymptotics of holographic thermal two-point functions is independent of the conformal dimension and spin (held fixed) of the operator. We analyze the leading universal WKB spectral curve governing the asymptotics at non-vanishing momentum scaling with frequency. We show that its classical action governs time domain signatures of the bouncing geodesic singularity, highly damped quasinormal modes, and the asymptotics of multi-stress tensor OPE coefficients. Beyond the leading order, we examine linear and dimension-dependent sublinear scalings of momentum with frequency. In both regimes, we derive the connection matrix between the boundary and the horizon using exact-WKB techniques. The results show a factorization of the spectral function into a perturbative part controlled by the OPE and a non-perturbative part governed by WKB transport. For linear scaling at fixed $k/ω$ in the timelike regime, the turning points controlling physical transport remain separated from the curvature singularity. Nevertheless, a singularity-anchored representative of the same transport homology class determines the leading WKB action. In the sublinear regime, the relevant turning points approach the singularity. We match an exact solution of the near-singularity scaled equation to the exterior WKB solution to determine the connection matrix and spectral function. Our results clarify how the black hole curvature singularity is encoded in dual field theory observables at leading order in the planar expansion.
