# Cylinder spectral curve and 1-cut Matrix model

## authors:
#Bertrand_Eynard

## summary:
For a random matrix, with eigenvalues in a one-body potential $V(x)$ and a 2-body interaction $R(x,x')$, it is known that the resolvent converges in the large $N$ limit to an analytic function in the complex plane minus cuts, called the spectral curve. In the 1-cut case, a two-sheeted neighborhood of the cut is a cylinder with two simple ramification points. We describe these spectral curve data by boundary integral operators and generalized cycles. Vice-versa, given a 2-branchpoints spectral curve in a cylinder, there exists a formal random matrix model that corresponds to it. The goal of this article is to show the method to recover a formal random matrix model, with its potential and 2-body interaction, from the geometry of the spectral curve.
