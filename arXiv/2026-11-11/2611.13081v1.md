# Planckian Charge Equilibration in Fast Scramblers

## authors:
#Jaewon_Kim
#Eduardo_Fradkin

## summary:
The Planckian time, $τ_{\rm Pl}=\hbar/(k_BT)$, is conjectured to set a lower limit on thermal relaxation times in strongly interacting quantum matter. Rigorous bounds limit both how rapidly quantum information can scramble and how soon hydrodynamics can emerge, yet the relation between the scrambling and equilibration rates remains unclear. We address this question by studying the relaxation of a charge imbalance between two subsystems of the complex Sachdev--Ye--Kitaev model and its Yukawa generalizations, which exhibit maximal scrambling. In the low-temperature conformal regime, we find through a linear response analysis that the slowest decay of this imbalance occurs at rate $Γ=2π(2Δ-d)k_BT/\hbar$, where $Δ$ is the fermion scaling dimension and $d=0,1$ is the spatial dimension. These results establish Planckian charge equilibration in maximally scrambling systems and show that maximal scrambling alone does not fix its dimensionless prefactor.
