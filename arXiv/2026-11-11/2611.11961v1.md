# Semiclassical Asymptotics of Entanglement in Torus-Link States

## authors:
#Vikash_Ganwariya
#Simran_Sain
#Siddharth_Dwivedi

## summary:
We investigate the entanglement entropy of topological quantum states associated with $T_{d,\,dn}$ torus links in (2+1)-dimensional SU(2) Chern--Simons theory at level $k$. These links consist of $d$ components with pairwise linking number $n$, and the corresponding link-complement states provide a natural setting to study multipartite entanglement in topological quantum field theories. We analyze the semiclassical limit $k\to\infty$ of the entanglement entropy by deriving the asymptotic behavior of the moments of the reduced density matrix. To achieve this, we decompose the integrable-representation space into left-edge, right-edge, and bulk regions and employ the stationary-phase method to evaluate their respective contributions. We find that the leading asymptotic behavior is governed entirely by the two edge regions, while the bulk contribution is asymptotically suppressed. The resulting expression for the limiting entanglement entropy is given in terms of Hurwitz zeta functions and exhibits a dependence on the residue class of $k$ modulo $n$. We further investigate the dependence of the limiting entanglement entropy on the winding parameter $n$ and the number of link components $d$. In the large-$d$ limit, the entropy reduces to a binary entropy determined by two normalized weights associated with the left and right edges. Finally, we show that the two iterated limits, $k\to\infty$ and $d\to\infty$, commute, reproducing the result obtained previously by taking the large-party limit first.
