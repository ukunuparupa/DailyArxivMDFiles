# Gamma classes, central connection matrices, and tt* geometry

## authors:
#Martin_A_Guest

## summary:
We review the gamma class of complex projective space, but from the point of view of topological-antitopological fusion. We deconstruct the central connection matrix which appears in the work of Dubrovin, Galkin-Golyshev-Iritani, and Cotti-Dubrovin-Guzzetti.
