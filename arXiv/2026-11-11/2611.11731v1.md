# The tertiary invariant and discrete Green-Schwarz couplings in heterotic string theory

## authors:
#Hiroki_Wada
#Kazuya_Yonekura

## summary:
The tertiary invariant is a recently introduced pairing between two-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=(0,1)$ supersymmetric quantum field theories, motivated by topological modular forms. We evaluate it directly from its definition for the sigma models on $\mathrm{Sp}(2)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ with unit $H$-flux, paired with the internal theories of the non-supersymmetric $\mathfrak{so}(16)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(16)$ heterotic string and of the near-horizon region of the non-supersymmetric heterotic 6-brane, respectively. Regarding the target spaces as $S^3$ bundles over spheres, we fill in every fiber with a four-disk carrying a gauge instanton and reduce the invariant to the index of a Dirac operator on the base. We find nonzero values of order three and two, which determine the Green-Schwarz couplings of the two theories on $\mathrm{Sp}(2)$ and $\mathrm{SU}(3)$, respectively. The former, a $\mathbb{Z}_{3}$-valued discrete topological term of the $\mathfrak{so}(16)\oplus\mathfrak{so}(16)$ theory, was previously studied through anomaly inflow to NS5-branes. Our computation, which uses only the worldsheet theories, agrees exactly with that result and thus confirms the anomaly inflow.
