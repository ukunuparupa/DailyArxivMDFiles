# Holomorphic integer-graded vertex operator superalgebras are purely even

## authors:
#Chongying_Dong
#Kaiwen_Sun
#Haowu_Wang

## summary:
We prove that a simple, self-contragredient, rational, $C_2$-cofinite, integer-graded holomorphic vertex operator superalgebra of CFT-type has zero odd part. We first show that a hypothetical nonzero odd part would be a simple current of categorical dimension $-1$ over a strongly rational pointed VOA with four simple modules. We then prove that every nonvacuum simple current of a strongly rational VOA has strictly positive lowest conformal weight. This leads to a contradiction with Dong-Jiao-Xu's theorem on the positivity of categorical dimension.
