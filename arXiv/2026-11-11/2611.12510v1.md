# Real-Time Path Integral Resurgence: Which Classical Path Contributes?

## authors:
#Job_Feldbrugge

## summary:
Which complex classical paths contribute to a real-time Feynman path integral? Picard-Lefschetz theory answers this question with the intersection numbers of the thimbles, which are notoriously hard to compute in infinite dimensions. We show that resurgence provides a practical alternative. We compute the asymptotic series of the real and complex classical paths to high order, with Feynman diagrams and with a transport recursion for the cumulants, and read off the adjacency of the classical paths from the divergent tails of the series with Borel-Padé methods. The South-East rule then turns adjacency into relevance. For a particle meeting a Rosen-Morse barrier, we identify the relevant complex classical paths in front of and behind the barrier, including the complex path that carries quantum tunnelling, follow ordinary and higher-order Stokes phenomena as the final point moves, and verify the predictions with the exact propagator. The resummed series reproduce the propagator with exponential accuracy. Finally, we outline how the expansion extends to relativistic path integrals governed by a Hamiltonian constraint.
