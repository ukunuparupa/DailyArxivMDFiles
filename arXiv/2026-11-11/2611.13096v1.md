# Off-shell continuation from on-shell recursion

## authors:
#Wenqi_Ke
#Zhen_Liu
#Ishmam_Mahbub

## summary:
Starting from on-shell factorization and the polarization sum of an internal massive vector, we provide an off-shell continuation of the transverse and longitudinal contributions defined at arbitrary virtuality. The construction relies on on-shell constructibility and the three-point off-shell current constraint $p \cdot J = O(m)$. For amplitudes with external longitudinal vectors, we develop a method that isolates the mass-singular contributions associated with the longitudinal polarization, namely terms with power-law singularities in the massless limit. In the examples studied, these terms appear as contact contributions in individual factorization channels, and we identify the conditions under which they cancel. We give an independent understanding, based on massive on-shell amplitudes, of the polarization decomposition and diagram-by-diagram high-energy power counting underlying the Feynman-Diagram gauge and related five-component formulations. We further clarify how higher-point current constraints can emerge from on-shell constructibility and the cancellation of mass singularities. We illustrate the mechanism with explicit four-point examples and discuss the constraints imposed by a smooth massless limit. The work provides a systematic bottom-up method for building massive amplitudes free of mass-singular terms and opens a route toward a broader amplitude-based understanding of field decompositions, connecting massive and massless descriptions and on-shell states with off-shell descriptions.
