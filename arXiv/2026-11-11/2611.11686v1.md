# Nonlinear Scalarization of Reissner-Nordström Black Holes in Scalar-Torsion-Maxwell Teleparallel Gravity

## authors:
#Joaquín_Robledo
#Yerko_Vásquez
#P_A_González

## summary:
We investigate the nonlinear scalarization of Reissner-Nordström black holes in scalar-torsion-Maxwell teleparallel gravity, in which the scalar field is nonminimally coupled to the Weitzenböck invariant. We consider polynomial coupling functions that avoid tachyonic instabilities at the linear level, thereby exploring a scalarization mechanism driven by nonlinear effects. In the decoupling limit, we study the nonlinear evolution of finite-amplitude scalar perturbations and show that the inclusion of a sixth-order coupling term allows the scalar field to dynamically evolve toward nontrivial stationary configurations. This behavior can be understood in terms of the effective potential, which develops nontrivial local minima that provide a mechanism for nonlinear saturation. By numerically solving the fully coupled field equations, we construct asymptotically flat charged black hole solutions endowed with scalar hair. We classify these solutions according to the number of nodes in the scalar field profile, identifying multiple branches whose structure and multiplicity depend on the coupling parameters. We further show that the higher-order coupling progressively suppresses higher-node branches and characterize the scalar backreaction through deviations of the metric functions and the torsion scalar from the Reissner-Nordström background.
