# Einstein--Yang--Mills Plane Waves: Uniqueness and an Exact Curvature Double Copy

## authors:
#Metin_Gurses
#Tahsin_Cagri_Sisman
#Bayram_Tekin

## summary:
Coleman found plane waves of Yang--Mills theory that are genuinely non-abelian in four dimensions and yet obey linear field equations, and Güven showed that they remain exact when gravity is included. We study these waves in $D$ dimensions. Coleman's profile, which is linear in the transverse coordinates, is singled out twice: up to gauge, it is the only profile with bounded energy density, and the only one for which the full Riemann tensor equals the color-contracted square of the Yang--Mills field strength that sources it. In anti-de Sitter space, we construct a closed-form family for every $D\geq4$; there, the full Riemann tensor cannot equal this color square. Waves whose transverse space is a sphere are always singular. In quadratic and Gauss--Bonnet gravity, a hyperbolic transverse space avoids this only if the waves defocus light or the higher-curvature terms become comparable to the Einstein term. All solutions extend, with changed coefficients, to generic gravity and gauge theories.
