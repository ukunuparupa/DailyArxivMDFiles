# Cubic Curvature Corrections to Taub-NUT-AdS Black Holes and Their Thermodynamics

## authors:
#Yu-Qi_Chen
#Hai-Shan_Liu
#Hong_Lu

## summary:
We investigate cubic curvature corrections to four-dimensional Taub-NUT-AdS black holes and their thermodynamics. We construct the complete analytic solution to the first order in the higher-derivative couplings, and derive the corrected Hawking temperature and Wald entropy. The NUT potential remains unchanged, while reconstructing the NUT charge and Gibbs free energy through the Maxwell relation leaves just one undetermined function of the NUT parameter. We then independently evaluate the Gibbs free energy using two improved Reall-Santos methods, based on either the field redefinition or the background subtraction. After matching the thermodynamic variables, both methods yield identical results and fix the undetermined function, providing a consistent determination of the mass and NUT charge. Our work therefore establishes that the thermodynamics of Taub-NUT-AdS black hole withstands the higher-derivative corrections.
