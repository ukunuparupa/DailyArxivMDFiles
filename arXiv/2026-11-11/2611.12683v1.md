# Thermodynamics of Kerr-Newman-Bertotti-Robinson black holes from background subtraction

## authors:
#Chang-Xu_Yan
#Zili_Mou
#Yu_Tian
#Hongbao_Zhang

## summary:
The non-asymptotic flatness of charged rotating black holes immersed in the Bertotti-Robinson magnetic field causes the ambiguity in the gauge choice for the time generator and the horizon electric potential. By using the newly established form of the integrability condition together with the gauge choice for the asymptotically flat charged rotating black holes as the initial condition, we have found that the gauge can be uniquely fixed. With this fixed gauge, we further obtain the mass and angular momentum by the background subtraction method with the Minkowski spacetime as the reference spacetime. As a result, not only is the first law of black hole thermodynamics automatically satisfied, but also both the CR relation and the Smarr formula are obeyed.
