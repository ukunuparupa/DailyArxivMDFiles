# Proof of the QNEC, without assumptions and under a weight

## authors:
#Thomas_Faulkner

## summary:
We complete a proof of the quantum null energy condition (QNEC) in the mathematical framework of Half-Sided Translations and Modular Inclusions (HST/HSMI) removing an assumption that applied to previous proofs. Here we establish a theorem, which when translated to a physical setting implies that the relative entropy comparing the state of fields outside a stationary black hole with a Hartle-Hawking (HH) state is always a convex function of the horizon cut. Previous proofs only applied when a purification with finite null energy was available. We also obtain a QNEC generalization in which the HH Hilbert space is replaced with one based on a weight which is plausibly of relevance to evaporating black holes in flat space.
