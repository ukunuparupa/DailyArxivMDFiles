# Geometry of Gauge-invariant Quantum Fields

## authors:
#Andrea_Quadri

## summary:
Gauge-invariant representatives of massless gauge fields generally involve inverse differential operators. We trace this non-locality to the homotopy contracting the free gauge complex and replace it, at field level, by a local map using an independent scalar and a differential constraint. We discuss the BRST completion needed to preserve the physical content and the relation to the Stückelberg theory. We finally comment on the extension of this approach to non-Abelian gauge groups via the extension to interactions through $L_\infty$ homotopy transfer.
