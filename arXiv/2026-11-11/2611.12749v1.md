# A possible signature of neutrino spin precession in the J-PARC muon $g-2$ experiment

## authors:
#S_Carneiro
#F_C_Sobrinho

## summary:
We examine a possible signature of the neutrino angular-momentum contribution to the muon magnetic anomaly measurement in the J-PARC E34 experiment. In previous papers we argued that the angular momentum carried by the unobserved neutrino pair may leave an uncertainty in the anomaly inferred from the time modulation of detected positrons. At Fermilab the highly relativistic muons make the forward-emission approximation suitable for this estimate. The lower muon momentum at J-PARC requires the decay kinematics to be treated explicitly. We integrate the longitudinal momentum of the neutrino pair using the positron energy distribution and asymmetry derived from the theoretical weak-decay spectrum, and the same event-counting weight $N(E)A(E)$ is employed for both experiments. For positron energies between $1.5$ and $2.9$ GeV at Fermilab and between $200$ and $275$ MeV at J-PARC, the proposed correction is smaller at J-PARC by $0.415\pm0.176$ ppm, when the most optimistic E34 precision is assumed. The expected difference amounts to $2.36$ standard deviations if the future measurement is centred on this value. The calculation thus identifies an experimental comparison which may test the proposed effect independently of the hadronic contribution to the Standard Model prediction. We also examine the possibility of testing the effect by comparing measurements at two different muon momenta. With a fixed positron energy threshold of 200 MeV, the maximum sensitivity is obtained at a muon beam energy of 245 MeV. The predicted difference relative to the nominal J-PARC configuration has significance of $4.88σ$ for the most optimistic experimental sensitivity.
