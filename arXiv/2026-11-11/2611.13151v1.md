# General non-extremal rotating charged AdS black hole solutions with four independent charge parameters in $D=4$, STU gauged supergravity

## authors:
#Di_Wu
#Shuang-Qing_Wu

## summary:
We present an exact solution for generic non-extremal rotating, charged black holes with a negative cosmological constant in four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2, U(1)^4$ gauged supergravity. This general non-extremal solution contains seven independent parameters related to the mass, rotation, two electric and two magnetic charges, as well as a nonzero cosmological constant. It generalizes the four-dimensional Cvetič-Youm black hole solution to the gauged supergravity case. We anticipate that this is the only regular black hole solution in four-dimensional gauged STU model without any Misner string singularity and/or C-metric-like conical defect on the symmetry axes. We also investigate its thermodynamic property and give the Killing-Stäckel tensor responsible for the separability of Hamilton-Jacobi and massless Klein-Gordon equations. Various special cases related to the known solution are discussed, and its BPS limit is also analyzed. Our solution provides a new background to test the AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$ correspondence and to study the microscopic entropy via extremization of the BPS index.
