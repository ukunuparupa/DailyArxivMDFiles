# Non-singular cosmology from effective LQG-inspired black holes

## authors:
#V_F_C_Belo
#F_G_S_Menezes
#S_M_Rupf
#I_P_R_Baranov
#H_A_Borges

## summary:
We propose a non-singular primordial cosmology derived from matching an effective Loop Quantum Gravity-inspired black hole geometry to an expanding Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker universe via Darmois-Israel junction conditions. The resulting modified Friedmann equation replaces the singularity with a quantum bounce in the Planckian regime. Mapping the surface energy-momentum tensor of the dynamic thin shell onto a homogeneous scalar field reveals a transient phantom-like behavior. For any equation of state of the shell satisfying $ω> -1$, its effective energy density is diluted with cosmic expansion. Extending the framework to a two-scalar-field system provides a mechanism to bridge the energy gap between the Planckian bounce and the sub-Planckian scale. The pre-inflationary phase is brief, enabling the second scalar field to take over to drive the standard slow-roll inflation.
