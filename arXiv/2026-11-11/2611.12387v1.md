# On a soliton with a non-normalisable zero mode

## authors:
#Guido_Franchetti
#Stefano_Bolognesi

## summary:
We consider the holomorphic baby Skyrme model, which we propose as a simple tractable example of a soliton with a non-normalisable zero mode. The $1$-soliton has the same structure as a lump of fixed size. The size modulus of the sigma model becomes massive, while the phase modulus remains massless. The latter is non-normalisable in the $(0+1)$-dimensional sense because of the soliton tail. A suitable way to treat it is to consider the radial $(1+1)$-dimensional effective action on the half-plane. The soliton core sets the boundary condition for the effective model, which we evaluate with the WKB approximation and also numerically. We discuss the analogue of the effective moduli space approximation with a $1+1$ model and a boundary term at a certain cutoff scale. This may serve as a prototype for more complex systems featuring non-normalisable zero modes such as certain kinds of monopoles in $3+1$ dimensions.
