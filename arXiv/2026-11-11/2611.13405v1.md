# Thermal $CP$ dynamics in a four-fermion model: mean-field and functional renormalization group analyses

## authors:
#Arthur_F_Vieira
#Rudnei_O_Ramos

## summary:
We investigate a one-flavor four-fermion model with scalar, pseudoscalar, and mixed scalar-pseudoscalar interactions at finite temperature and vanishing chemical potential. Our analysis combines the mean-field approximation, which determines the equilibrium condensates, with the functional renormalization group (FRG), which describes the scale dependence of the interactions and the fermion mass phase. Within the mean-field approach, we find that, in the absence of explicit symmetry-breaking sources, the dominant interaction determines the direction of condensation. In particular, a pseudoscalar condensate spontaneously breaks the ordinary $P/CP$ symmetry and disappears through a continuous thermal phase transition. For regular high-temperature FRG trajectories, the dimensionless mixed coupling tends to zero in the infrared, while its dimensionful counterpart remains finite. Increasing temperature also suppresses the scale evolution of the fermion mass phase. Together, these results clarify the distinct roles of thermal fluctuations in suppressing spontaneous symmetry breaking and fermionic instabilities, without necessarily eliminating explicit symmetry breaking effects.
