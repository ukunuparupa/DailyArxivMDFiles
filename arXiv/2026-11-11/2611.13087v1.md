# Comment to: "Rewording Theoretical Predictions at Colliders with Vacuum Amplitudes"

## authors:
#Zeno_Capatti
#Valentin_Hirschi

## summary:
This comment highlights flaws in two central results presented by Ramírez-Uribe et al. in the titular letter, Phys. Rev. Lett. 133, 211901 (2024) [arXiv:2404.05491]. Firstly, the authors incorrectly claim that integrands of differential scattering cross-sections are obtained as single "residues" of vacuum amplitudes, although a sharp distinction of initial and final states requires to consider two iterated discontinuities. Secondly, they wrongly state that non-pinched thresholds always locally match in such a differential representation.
