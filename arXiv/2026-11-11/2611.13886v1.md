# Lattice Hamiltonians for Chiral Gauge Theories

## authors:
#Dorota_M_Grabowska

## summary:
We present a lattice Hamiltonian for chiral gauge theories on the boundary of a slab. The formulation is gauge-fixed, so the gauge variables are the physical modes of the boundary field, and Gauss's law holds exactly. The bulk gauge field is determined by the boundary field and an integer label, which is the topological charge that the boundary generates and the bulk stores as a localized instanton. The bulk enters as a dielectric, and both gauge anomaly conditions become the question of whether the boundary satisfies Gauss's law on its own. For background gauge fields, we show that sectors that differ by a unit of bulk charge are degenerate after Pauli-Villars subtraction and the mirror fermions decouple. The boundary theory has no vacuum angle. Every term in the Hamiltonian is defined in terms of the boundary gauge variables, the integer label and the fermion modes, a starting point for quantum circuit construction.
