# Resummations In Higher-Curvature Modifications Of Starobinsky Inflation

## authors:
#Marco_Piva

## summary:
We study higher-curvature corrections to Starobinsky inflation, which are expected to be generated at the quantum level. When treated perturbatively in the couplings $γ$ of the higher-dimensional operators, these terms produce inverse powers of the slow-roll parameter $α$, casting doubt on the reliability of the slow-roll expansion. If instead $γ$ is kept exact, the expansion fails to reproduce the Starobinsky model smoothly as $γ\to 0$. We show that this problem is solved by resumming certain series in the ratio $α/γ$. The resulting ``improved'' slow-roll expansion is regular in the limit $γ\to 0$ and gives the same result whether one expands first in $γ$ or in $α$. We illustrate this explicitly for an $R^3$ term, obtain the power spectra, spectral indices and tensor-to-scalar ratio at next-to-leading order in the improved expansion, and show that results in the recent literature are recovered as the first-order Taylor expansion of ours. Comparing with the latest ACT, Planck and SPT data, we find that the resummed and first-order results differ appreciably only for values of $n_s$ outside the favored regions. Finally, we comment on the origin of higher-curvature operators in UV-complete theories, such as renormalizable quantum gravity, where the predictions of the Starobinsky model are stable.
