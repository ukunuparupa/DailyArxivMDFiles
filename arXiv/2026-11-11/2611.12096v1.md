# Two-Loop $n$-Point Identical Helicity Amplitude for Gravity Minimally Coupled to a Chiral Fermion

## authors:

#_Calvin
#_Chen
#Enrico_Herrmann
#Anthony_Morales
#Radu_Roiban

## summary:
Motivated by recent developments in the two-loop identical-helicity sector of gauge theory when the one-loop self-dual integrability anomaly of Bardeen and Cangemi, or the equivalent twistor-space version of Costello and Paquette, vanishes, we compute the two-loop four-point identical-helicity amplitude in gravity coupled to a single chiral fermion, for which the corresponding self-dual anomaly also vanishes. We show that the infrared-subtracted amplitude is purely rational and proportional to the identical-helicity tree amplitude of pure gravity with a single insertion of the operator $R^3$. Using this connection, we obtain a closed-form expression for the two-loop $n$-point identical-helicity amplitude in gravity minimally coupled to a fermion. We do so by first obtaining the corresponding identical-helicity gauge-theory tree amplitude with a single $F^3$ insertion using a chiral bootstrap. We then convert these $n$-point gauge theory amplitudes using Kawai-Lewellen-Tye to obtain gravity amplitudes with an $R^3$ insertion, which are necessarily equal to the desired two-loop identical helicity amplitudes of gravity coupled to a chiral fermion. As a nontrivial check, we explicitly verify crossing symmetry and the expected soft limits of our expressions through 13 points.
