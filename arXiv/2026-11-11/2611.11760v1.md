# Proof of the Rényi QNEC

## authors:
#Thomas_Faulkner

## summary:
We give a general proof of the Rényi QNEC formulated using the Araki-Masuda weighted $L_p$ norms for all $p > 2$. This generalizes the algebraic proof of the QNEC applicable at $p=2$. Our approach utilizes a weighted version of a conditional $L_p$ norm which extrapolates the quantum expansion/ant-quantity. We elucidate some properties of the corresponding weighted conditional entropy both for half-sided modular inclusions and for general von Neumann subalgebras with a common cyclic and separating vector. The $p=2$ limit computes a conditional dynamical entropy originally defined in the context of black hole dynamics.
