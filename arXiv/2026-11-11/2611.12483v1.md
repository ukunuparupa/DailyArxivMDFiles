# Direct product geometries in $n$-dimensional linear, quadratic, and conformal gravity

## authors:
#Petr_Jizba
#Tereza_Lehečková

## summary:
Direct-product spacetimes offer a tractable route to exact solutions in gravity theories beyond general relativity. We study $n$-dimensional direct-product spacetimes of $(m_1+m_2)$, $(2+m)$, and $(2+\ldots+2)$ type in linear gravity, general pure quadratic-curvature gravity, and one of the recently proposed higher-dimensional extensions of conformal gravity, allowing the two-dimensional subspaces to have nonconstant curvature. We classify the resulting solution spaces and determine how the allowed geometries depend on the gravitational theory and, in quadratic gravity, on the curvature couplings. Metrics containing two-dimensional part(s) of nonconstant curvature are governed either by algebraic conditions on a curvature profile or, in one special class of quadratic gravity, by a differential equation. Relations governing metrics with only constant-curvature $m$-dimensional subspaces are purely algebraic. We further examine conformal relations to linear-gravity solutions, generalized Petrov types, and compatibility with electromagnetic sources. Particularly noteworthy is that standard static black-hole geometries are absent from the direct-product sector of the considered form of conformal gravity for $n>4$. Four- and six-dimensional cases are analyzed in detail, and explicit analytic solutions illustrating the resulting structure are presented whenever possible.
