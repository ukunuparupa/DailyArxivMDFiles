# Deep Inelastic Scattering and Proton Structure Functions in AdS/QCD models

## authors:
#Rafael_A_Costa-Silva
#Henrique_Boschi-Filho

## summary:
In this work we study the holographical fermionic deep inelastic scattering in a deformed AdS model. Imposing that the potentials that emerge from the Schrödinger-like equations for the fermionic chiral left and right modes are SUSY partners to preserve isospectrality, we implement a systematical truncation of such potentials. Then, we show that in the quadratic approximation of the deformed AdS model, the fermionic DIS and the proton structure functions $F_{1,2}(q^2,x)$ are identical with the results obtained from the soft wall model. Further, we obtain and compare high-order approximations for the fermionic potentials with the full solution of the deformed AdS model and show, as expected, that as greater the order of approximation, the better are the results for $F_{1,2}(q^2,x)$ when compared with experimental data explicitly for the Bjorken parameter values $x=0.85, 0.75$ and $0.65$.
