# Assessing the expressive power of a gauge-invariant PEPS ansatz across a topological phase transition

## authors:
#Etsuko_Itou
#Tsuyoshi_Okubo

## summary:
We investigate the expressive power of the gauged Gaussian fermionic projected entangled pair state (GGFPEPS) ansatz, which is a manifestly gauge-invariant tensor network ansatz for $(2+1)$-dimensional lattice gauge theories. In the GGFPEPS ansatz, the gauge field on each link is represented by coupling its configuration to the fermion number of virtual fermions placed at both ends of the link. This ansatz has been studied by variational Monte Carlo (VMC) methods in previous work~\cite{Emonts:2022yom}. However, the statistical error of VMC makes a precise determination of the variational parameters difficult, so that the expressive power of the ansatz could not be examined accurately. In this work, we reformulate the computation as a Grassmann tensor network (TN), in which the whole network is contracted deterministically, and introduce the higher-order tensor renormalization group (HOTRG), which allows us to perform calculations in large volumes up to $L=34$. We also evaluate the infinite volume system using the corner transfer matrix renormalization group (CTMRG). We find that the ansatz with only eight complex variational parameters accurately describes the pure $\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge theory including the region of the topological phase transition, not only in the energy but also in the topological entanglement entropy. The critical coupling obtained from the peaks of their susceptibilities agrees quantitatively with the one predicted by continuous-time quantum Monte Carlo simulations~\cite{wu2012phase}. Furthermore, near the transition point, we show that the ansatz represents a state outside the plaquette product family, which is a one-parameter family of states assumed in the construction of the ansatz as a simple interpolation between the strong and weak coupling limits.
