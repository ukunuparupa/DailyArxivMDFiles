# Decoupling Across Dimensions

## authors:
#Param_Gogia
#Caius_Gordon-Bruno
#Emily_Nardoni
#Peter_Weck

## summary:
We present a prescription for identifying decoupled sectors of 4d SCFTs obtained from compactifications of branes. Central to our analysis is the comparison of anomaly polynomials obtained via field-theoretic reduction versus anomaly inflow. Our flagship examples are the 4d $\mathcal{N}=1$ SCFTs obtained by compactifying the 6d $(1,0)$ theory from M5-branes probing a $\mathbb{C}^2/\mathbb{Z}_2$ orbifold singularity on a torus with flux. The anomaly polynomial of the interacting fixed point is known to decompose into the $T^2$ reduction of the 6d anomaly polynomial, plus a contribution from flip fields introduced to remove operators from the spectrum. Adopting the inflow perspective, the contribution from the flipped sector is obtained as the difference between inflow anomaly polynomials in the singular and resolved geometries, which we efficiently compute using equivariant localization. Helpfully, the 4d SCFTs admit two dual quiver gauge theory descriptions: $Y^{p,q}$ quiver gauge theories, and class $\mathcal{S}_2$ necklace quivers obtained from trinions. We study the interacting and decoupled sectors on both sides of the duality and construct an explicit map between their chiral rings, identifying a range of parameter space for which the quivers flow to apparently different interacting fixed points. Each chiral ring generator is furthermore matched to an M2-brane wrapping a 2-cycle in the resolved M-theory geometry.
