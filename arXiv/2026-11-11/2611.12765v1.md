# Quality time in extra dimensions: axion quality and radion cosmology

## authors:
#Falguni_Biswas
#Yeshan_Juberi
#Ahmed_Rakin_Kamal

## summary:
Higher-form symmetries can protect extra-dimensional axions, but additional symmetry breaking can still spoil the solution to the strong CP problem. We focus on electrically charged matter and magnetic gauging in a five-dimensional circle compactification where the axion arises from an Abelian gauge field and the radius is dynamical. We discuss two mechanisms for stabilising the radius/radion using Casimir energy from fields neutral under the Abelian gauge field, with and without an additional periodic scalar winding around the compact circle. Both admit Minkowski and de Sitter minima. At the Minkowski minima (with radion masses of $100$--$170\,\mathrm{TeV}$), electrically charged matter induces a residual strong CP angle of order $10^{-17}$ and shifts the radius negligibly. Magnetic gauging remains compatible after including its additional degree of freedom in the Casimir spectrum, provided its coupling is sufficiently small. We observe that increasing the radius suppresses charged-matter breaking relative to QCD but increases magnetic breaking contribution. We also determine the radion decay channels, including the graviphoton, and study reheating from a possible radion-dominated era without scalar winding or magnetic gauging. For a non-minimal Higgs-curvature coupling $ξ=7$, the visible-sector reheating temperature is about $85\,\mathrm{MeV}$ with a dark-radiation contribution of $ΔN_{\rm eff}\approx0.02$. The entropy released during radion decay dilutes the axion abundance, allowing axions with $f_a=10^{14}\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and an initial misalignment angle $\vartheta_i\approx0.43$ to account for the entire observed dark matter abundance. For the same decay constant, reproducing this abundance in radiation domination requires $\vartheta_i\approx0.065$.
