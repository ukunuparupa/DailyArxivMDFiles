# Gravitational waves and the exit from de Sitter in Inflation without an Inflaton

## summary:
In Inflation without an Inflaton (IWI), second-order de Sitter (dS) gravitons generate primordial curvature as the Coulomb-type potential of their gradient stress. Each pair contributes $(p/k)^2$, yielding exponential enhancement over the contributing e-folds. With exact Bunch-Davies evolution, this solution satisfies all four scalar second-order Einstein equations in pure dS. The retarded transverse-traceless solution instead has a UV kernel of order unity, $(k/p)^2$ below the scalar kernel, and a one-loop correction $Δ^2_{h,2}/Δ^2_h\simeq0.04\,Δ^2_h$. Its source acts before internal freeze-out, but oscillatory cancellations prevent tensor enhancement.  We evolve the curvature through a smooth exit into radiation domination. Conserved curvature resides on the vacuum-decay hypersurface. A global depletion clock holds each scale on zero-shear time for $N_{\rm w}$ e-folds after horizon exit before local release, giving all scales equal enhancement. Energy-momentum conservation feeds the depleted vacuum energy into a non-vacuum sector, modeled as radiation. Deep in radiation domination, $Δ^2_ζ=c_wΔ_h^4e^{N_{\rm w}}/20$, with clock-dependent $c_w$, $n_s-1=-4ε$, and $n_T=-2ε$. The spectrum is adiabatic and Gaussian; super-horizon $ζ$ is conserved to better than $10^{-4}$, and changing the adiabatic exit fluid alters it by less than $0.2\%$. A direct radiation exit requires $31\lesssim N_{\rm w}\lesssim50$, or $3\times10^{-6}\lesssim r\lesssim0.036$.  At interferometer frequencies, $Ω_{\rm GW}h^2\simeq2\times10^{-21}$--$3\times10^{-17}$, accessible to BBO for $r\gtrsim0.01$ and ultimate DECIGO for $r\gtrsim10^{-5}$; CMB B-mode searches probe $r\gtrsim10^{-3}$. Above $k_{\rm end}e^{-N_{\rm w}}$, the scalar spectrum steepens to $k^{-1}$, suppressing $μ$-distortions for sharp release when $N_{\rm w}\gtrsim45$.
