# The KLN theorem meets collinear factorisation: Local Unitarity for hadronic cross-sections

## authors:
#Zeno_Capatti
#Lucien_Huber

## summary:
We present an initial-state implementation of the Kinoshita-Lee-Nauenberg (KLN) theorem that is compatible with collinear factorisation. In this formulation, hadronic cross-sections are expressed as convolutions of parton distribution functions with partonic cross-sections obtained by summing over the number of incoming partons. By virtue of the KLN theorem, the partonic cross-sections are infrared finite by construction, without relying on dimensional regularisation as an infrared regulator. The Local Unitarity formalism provides a local realisation of this infrared finiteness, allowing partonic cross-sections to be evaluated directly in four dimensions by Monte Carlo integration. We demonstrate the framework at next-to-leading order and leading power. Using Local Unitarity, we compute the Drell-Yan and $t\bar{t}$ production cross-sections numerically; using Reverse Unitarity, we obtain the deep inelastic scattering and Drell-Yan cross-sections analytically. In all cases, a universal scheme conversion relates our results to those obtained in the $\overline{\text{MS}}$ parton model.
