# Scale separation and partial supersymmetry breaking

## authors:
#Niccolò_Cribiori
#Fotis_Farakos
#Alexandros_Zarafonitis

## summary:
We propose new candidate string theory vacua in which partial supersymmetry breaking occurs within the lower-dimensional effective theory in anti-de Sitter space, parametrically below the Kaluza--Klein scale. These vacua solve the equations of motion of massless type IIA supergravity in the smeared approximation and exhibit parametric scale separation, at both weak and strong string coupling. They are reproduced as $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetric phases of a three-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ gauged supergravity. Should these vacua be solutions of string theory, they would provide explicit setups in which partial supersymmetry breaking can be studied at energies parametrically below the compactification scale. In addition, we further comment on our previously proposed solution with scale separation and extended supersymmetry.
