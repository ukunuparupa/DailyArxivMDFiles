# Rotational instability and quadrupolar order in BFSS matrix theory

## authors:
#Jun_Liu
#Jorge_E_Santos

## summary:
We study the effect of angular momentum on thermal states of the BFSS matrix theory in its gravitational and high-temperature classical regimes. We focus on ensembles with only one non-zero angular momentum. On the gravity side, the rotating ensemble is described by the singly-spinning D$0$ black hole. Beyond a critical rotation, we find that this solution becomes unstable to a non-axisymmetric quadrupolar perturbation. Following this mode non-linearly, we find a new branch of non-axisymmetric rotating black holes that is thermodynamically favoured over the axisymmetric branch in both the microcanonical and canonical ensembles. On the other hand, in the classical matrix limit, we find no evidence for such collective quadrupolar order over the matrix sizes and angular momenta studied. Instead, rotation accelerates the separation of a single D$0$-brane from the clump of branes initially prepared. The separated brane carries a large fraction of the angular momentum by orbiting the rest of the clump at a large in-plane radius. Both the angular momentum share and the orbital radius relative to the clump size increase with the matrix size. These results distinguish a collective quadrupolar deformation in the gravity limit from angular-momentum transfer to a single brane in the classical matrix limit.
