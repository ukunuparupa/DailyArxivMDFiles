# Three Dimensional Structure of Pion with One Dynamical Gluon in the Light-Cone Quark Model

## authors:
#Satyajit_Puhan
#Shubham_Sharma
#Narinder_Kumar

## summary:
We study the three-dimensional structure of the pion beyond the leading Fock state by including a dynamical gluon, $|π\rangle=|q\bar q\rangle+|q\bar q g\rangle$. Within the light-cone quark model, we calculate the pion GPDs, TMDs, and PDFs from light-front wave function overlaps, using a modified Brodsky--Huang--Lepage wave function with the Melosh--Wigner rotation for the valence sector and the light-front quark--gluon vertex for the $q\bar q g$ sector. The quark-number and momentum sum rules are satisfied without additional constraints. We further obtain the electromagnetic form factor and the mass decomposition. After NLO evolution, we compare the PDFs with pion-induced Drell--Yan, $J/ψ$, $Υ$, and HERA leading-neutron $F_2^π$ data. The $q\bar q g$ sector contributes little to Drell--Yan but becomes important for gluon-sensitive observables such as $J/ψ$ production and the pion structure function at small $x$.
