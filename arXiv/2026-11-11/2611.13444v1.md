# Giant Graviton Correlators: From Non-maximal to Dual, Bootstrap and Hidden Symmetry

## authors:
#Qi_Chen
#Zhongjie_Huang
#Yunfeng_Jiang
#Xinan_Zhou

## summary:
We compute four-point functions of two light $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS operators and two heavy giant graviton operators in $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang--Mills theory at large $N$ and infinite 't Hooft coupling. Using bootstrap techniques, we determine the correlators for arbitrary light-operator dimensions and arbitrary allowed sizes of non-maximal sphere and dual giant gravitons. We show that these correlators exhibit a hidden ten-dimensional conformal symmetry, allowing them to be organized into compact generating functions. Remarkably, the generating functions for both types of giant gravitons can be obtained from the maximal giant case through $SO(2,10)$ rotations in the embedding space.
