# Kinematically Unrestricted Flat Space Scattering from AdS$_{d+1} \times$ S$^p$/CFT$_d$

## authors:
#Bhanu_Narra

## summary:
We construct scattering states for scalar fields with generic kinematics from CFT operators in the flat space limit of AdS/CFT. The states can have momentum along the compact $S^p$ directions of $AdS_{d+1}\times S^p$, which is achieved by scaling to large R-symmetry charge and choosing a particular parameterization for the R-symmetry polarization. These states allow for the construction of an unrestricted S-matrix from the flat space limit of CFT correlation functions, which we check for various tree level interactions. We also describe a generalization of the Mellin space formalism which incorporates the compact momentum, and we discuss some possible implications for flat space holography.
