# M-theory anomaly localization and holographic $c$-extremization

## authors:
#Carolina_Matté_Gregory

## summary:
We compute the cubic and $C_3\wedge X_8$ caracteristic class contributions to the trial anomaly polynomial of supersymmetric $AdS_3\times M_8$ backgrounds with $M_8=P(\mathcal O \oplus \mathcal L)\to B_6$. Including the Abelian currents descending from $C_3$, we identify the projective invariant $J$ as the denominator arising from their elimination by $c$-extremization and recover the central charge $c^{(0)}=-I_3+3I_+I_-/J$. The $X_8$ push-forward determines the pure R-symmetry, mixed R--Betti and gravitational anomalies. Evaluating it at the classical extremum gives the associated central-charge corrections, linear in the overall unshifted flux. The mixed term contributes equally to both central charges and is required in general. We evaluate these formulae for three flux-quantized backgrounds, including non-polynomial solutions. Direct projective fibre integration reproduces the mixed coefficient obtained by localization. On the wrapped-$M5$ $S^4$ bundle, the same couplings reproduce the independently known field-theory anomaly through orders $N^3$ and $N$. Their interpretation as full CFT central charges requires a unitary SCFT with a normalizable vacuum, suitable supersymmetric boundary conditions, a complete mixing sector of global currents and control of omitted anomaly sectors. A conditional Euclidean relative-equivariant interpretation is discussed separately.
