# BPS Bootstrap

## authors:
#Yiming_Chen

## summary:
We apply the large $N$ matrix bootstrap technique to constrain expectation values of simple operators in heavy BPS states that are dual to classical geometries. For states dual to horizonless geometries, their explicit moduli give rise to large allowable regions in the bootstrap, while for black holes, a converging bootstrap should find islands. We explore this method in $\mathcal{N}=4$ $SU(N)$ super Yang-Mills theory at large $N$, working at weak coupling and focusing on BPS states with scaling dimensions of order $N^2$. For the $1/2$, $1/4$, and $1/8$-BPS sectors, the bootstrap finds large regions, in agreement with an eigenvalue description of such states in the limit of large charges. We also present preliminary results for a BMN truncation of the $1/16$-BPS sector, which contains states dual to both horizonless geometries and black holes. Isolating the black hole states in the bootstrap remains a challenge.
