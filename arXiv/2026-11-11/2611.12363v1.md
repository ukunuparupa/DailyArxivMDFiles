# Naturalness in the Past from a Time-Dependent Supersymmetry Scale

## authors:
#Tony_Gherghetta
#Kunio_Kaneta
#Wenqi_Ke

## summary:
We consider the possibility that electroweak naturalness may be epoch dependent. In no-scale supergravity, a rolling quintessence modulus can control the supersymmetry-breaking scale, allowing superpartners to be much lighter in the early universe while remaining heavy today. Fifth-force and varying-constant effects can be suppressed via correlated modular weights in the visible-sector Kähler metric and modulus-dependent superpotential Yukawa couplings, while radiative stability of the quintessence potential requires additional tuning of the field-dependent vacuum energy. The resulting effective theory has distinctive implications for early-universe phase transitions, relic abundances, and gravitational waves.
