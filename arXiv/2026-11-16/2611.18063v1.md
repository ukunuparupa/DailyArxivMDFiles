# Spectrum of a 2D QFT analogue of the SYK model

## authors:
#Avik_Chakraborty
#Pratik_K_Das
#Manavendra_Mahato

## summary:
We study the spectrum of the two-dimensional generalisation of the SYK model proposed by Turiaci and Verlinde, in which chiral Majorana fermions with a topological kinetic term interact through random $2q$-fermion couplings. We compute the four-point function at order $1/N$ in the conformal limit. In addition to the conformal Casimir, the ladder kernels commute with the operator that exchanges a single lightcone coordinate. As a result, the four-point function splits into an even and an odd sector, which the kernels do not mix. In each sector we construct a complete eigenbasis of the Casimir, find the eigenvalues of the kernels in closed form and sum the ladder diagrams. The poles in the ladder sum lie on a spectral curve in the plane of the two conformal weights. A conformal solution in the IR limit breaks the symmetries of the model under chiral reparametrisations and local Lorentz transformations, and the kernel has eigenvalue one on the corresponding modes. The reparametrisation modes, with conformal weights $(2,0)$ and $(0,2)$, are non-normalisable, whereas the Lorentz mode of the odd sector, with weights $(1,1)$, is normalisable. We determine the region where the sum of ladders can be represented in a contour form analogous to that of the one-dimensional SYK model.
