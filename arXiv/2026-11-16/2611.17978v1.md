# General $δN$ formalism in Single Field Inflation and Graph Theory

## authors:
#Amin_Nassiri-Rad
#Hassan_Firouzjahi

## summary:
We study higher order correlation functions in single field inflation using the $δN$ formalism and methods from graph theory. By representing connected tree level contributions as graphs, we systematically classify their independent topologies, momentum shapes and amplitudes. We show that the amplitudes of arbitrary trees factorize into products of primary amplitudes, providing a general framework for relations among higher order correlators, including the Suyama-Yamaguchi equality. As the number of external legs increases, the number of distinct tree topologies grows exponentially, whereas the number of algebraically distinct amplitude products grows considerably more slowly. As an application, we consider ultra-slow-roll inflation followed by a transition to a slow-roll attractor. For transitions without a potential step, we derive an explicit all-order expression for the vertex amplitudes and establish their positivity and dependence on the sharpness of the transition. When a potential step is included, we prove that all nonlinear vertex amplitudes remain positive for upward steps, whereas downward steps allow cancellations at specific parameter values. These cancellations can eliminate entire families of tree contributions, including configurations in which the leading local bispectrum and trispectrum vanish simultaneously while higher order correlations remain nonzero.
