# What Comes Out Of Monopole-Fermion Scattering?

## authors:
#David_Tong

## summary:
We describe what happens when chiral fermions scatter off magnetic monopoles. It has long been known that, in many situations, the outgoing state cannot be constructed from any finite number of excitations. In the language of 2d CFT, it lives in a twisted sector of the Hilbert space. Here we explain what this means from the perspective of a d=3+1 observer. We show that an ingoing one-particle excitation is scattered into an outgoing state surrounded by a soft cloud which consists of an infinite number of low energy particle-anti-particle pairs. We compute energy and charge correlators, showing that this cloud fragments in any measurement. A detector will register one or more hard particles which leave tracks and carry standard quantum numbers. The remaining exotic quantum numbers live in the soft cloud, and are characterised by large charge fluctuations in the detector
