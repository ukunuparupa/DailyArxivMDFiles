# Hidden Geometric Structure Beyond Conformal Flatness

## authors:
#Xinan_Zhou

## summary:
We uncover a hidden geometric structure in holographic correlators of M-theory, despite the absence of conformal flatness. We study two-point functions of $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS local operators in the presence of a $\frac{1}{2}$-BPS surface defect in six-dimensional $(2,0)$ theory, dual to an M2-brane in $\mathrm{AdS}_7\times$S$^4$. We find that the infinite tower of tree-level supergravity correlators can be generated from a single lowest-weight correlator. After a simple reorganization of the rational terms, the generating function takes the form of a weighted average over the internal S$^4$, acting through additive shifts of the spacetime cross ratios. This reveals a remarkably simple geometric organization underlying the holographic defect correlators.
