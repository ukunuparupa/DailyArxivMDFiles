# Mirror symmetry of $\mathcal{N}=(0,4)$ boundary conditions with Nahm poles

## authors:
#Tadashi_Okazaki

## summary:
We propose mirror symmetry between $\mathcal{N}=(0,4)$ boundary conditions with Nahm poles and Neumann boundary conditions with boundary Fermi multiplets in 3d $\mathcal{N}=4$ non-Abelian gauge theories, including $U(N)$ SQCD, $T[SU(N)]$ and the ADHM theories. We obtain the half-indices of the Nahm pole boundary conditions from those of the Dirichlet boundary conditions by Higgsing, where the boundary fugacities are specialized along the principal embedding of $\mathfrak{su}(2)$. We check that they agree with the half-indices of the dual Neumann boundary conditions. In the twist limits they give closed-form formulas for the vacuum characters of the boundary vertex operator algebras of Costello and Gaiotto. We find that the unflavored vacuum characters are eta-quotients times quasimodular forms, which we write explicitly in terms of Eisenstein series for all our examples.
