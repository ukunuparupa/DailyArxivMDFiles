# Gravitational Corrections to OPE Coefficients

## authors:
#Ameya_Chavda
#Alessandro_Podo
#John_Staunton

## summary:
The short-distance singular behavior of correlation functions of quantum field theories in curved spacetime is a universal property shared by all well-behaved states (known as Hadamard states). For two-point functions this takes the form of the Hadamard tail of free scalars and its generalizations to spinning fields and interacting theories. In this work, we compute similar universal curvature corrections to curved-space OPE coefficients in a short-distance expansion for interacting quantum field theories in general dimensions. We work at leading order in the curvature but for arbitrary backgrounds. Examples include weakly coupled scalar field theories with cubic and quartic interactions as well as strongly coupled conformal field theories. The gravitationally dressed OPE coefficients allow us to test explicitly, and confirm, the validity of the smearing-in-time procedure discussed by Witten in the construction of the algebra of observables near an observer's worldline in curved spacetime.
