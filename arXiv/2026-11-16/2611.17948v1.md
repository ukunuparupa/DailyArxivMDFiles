# Local Quantum Metrology: Precision Limits for Observers in QFT

## authors:
#Markus_Fröb
#Albert_Much

## summary:
How precisely can an observer estimate a parameter related to a quantum field? This question is answered for quantum-mechanical systems using the SLD--Bures--Fisher metric and the quantum Cramér--Rao bound. However, local algebras in relativistic quantum field theory are of type III: they carry no trace and normal states have no density matrices, so the SLD quantum Fisher information and the quantum Cramér--Rao bound cannot be defined in the usual way. We define the SLD (Bures) quantum Fisher information on arbitrary von Neumann algebras through Uhlmann's transition probability and prove a quantum Cramér--Rao bound for collective measurements. We also show that the bound is sharp, and equals the largest classical Fisher information obtainable by measurements in the region.
