# Invertible parafermionic topological phases in one dimension

## authors:
#André_L_G_Mudry
#Ömer_M_Aksoy

## summary:
Invertible topological phases of matter are described by gapped ground states of local Hamiltonians that are non-degenerate on any closed spatial manifold. Classification of invertible topological phases realized by bosonic or fermionic degrees of freedom is well-understood, particularly in low dimensions. In one-dimensional space, the classification can be understood through the boundary projective representations. We extend this paradigm to one-dimensional invertible topological phases of parafermionic degrees of freedom with symmetries. We define cohomological invariants that characterize distinct boundary projective representations and compute their stacking rules. Among other results, we show (i) how these cohomological invariants can be used to compute protected boundary degeneracy and (ii) how, in contrast to bosons or fermions, invertible phases of parafermions can form a non-Abelian group under stacking. For order-$N$ parafermions with $N$ a prime power, we define representative Hamiltonians realizing invertible topological phases with $\mathbb{Z}^{3}_{N}$ symmetry. We show that under stacking they generate a non-Abelian group of order $2N^3$. When $N$ is an odd prime, this group is $\mathrm{Heis}(\mathbb{Z}_N)\rtimes \mathbb{Z}^{\,}_{2}$, where $\mathrm{Heis}(\mathbb{Z}_N)$ is the Heisenberg group of order $N^{3}$.
