# Positivity Bounds, Negative Running, and the Weak Gravity Conjecture

## authors:
#Jaime_Fernandez

## summary:
We study causality and unitarity constraints at one loop on a low-energy effective theory containing a photon, a massless scalar coupled to it through dilaton-like interactions, and a light neutral Dirac fermion with a Yukawa coupling to the scalar. In this setup, non-gravitational loop effects induced by the Yukawa interaction can drive the coefficient of the four-photon $(F_{μν}F^{μν})^2$ operator negative at low energies, apparently violating the tree-level positivity bound. Nevertheless, we find that these negative values are consistent with one-loop constraints from causality and unitarity, thanks to the low-energy contribution from the massless scalar to the relevant dispersion relation. We also examine the implications of this negative running for the Weak Gravity Conjecture in the magnetic limit of Einstein--Maxwell--dilaton black holes, since the RG-evolved Wilson coefficients can a priori shift the extremality condition towards the subextremal region. We find, however, that this shift is obstructed by the fermion mass induced by the same Yukawa coupling, which prevents the fermion from contributing to the running of the relevant operators down to the low-energy scales required to make the black holes subextremal. Finally, we discuss the relevance of negative running to loop-level positivity bounds in the SMEFT and to light-by-light scattering in the presence of a light scalar.
