# Modular Structure of the ADHM Effective Twisted Superpotential

## authors:
#Seyed_Morteza_Hosseini

## summary:
The Cardy limit of M2-brane indices retains nonperturbative information through the effective twisted superpotential, including corrections exponentially suppressed at large rank. We investigate the flavor dependence of these corrections in the three-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ $\mathrm U(N)$ ADHM theory at the universal twist. For $N_f=1,2,4$ fundamental hypermultiplets, we conjecture that the full exponential remainder of the selected Bethe critical value is an absolutely convergent divisor sum obtained by applying a first-order differential operator to an Eichler integral of a weight-four Eisenstein form. Resolving this remainder requires the additive constant, which we evaluate from its known integral representation as finite Clausen sums for every positive integer $N_f$. Expanding the integral together with the shifted-rank power determines the type-IIA coefficients of the perturbative contribution at arbitrary genus and their factorial growth. With this subtraction fixed, the proposed sums reproduce independently computed Bethe critical values at up to $800$-digit accuracy, including low ranks that test the continuation beyond the reconstructed sectors. The flavor-one expression agrees with level-one ABJM, whereas the two- and four-flavor completions have distinct divisor laws related by exact dissection identities to finite combinations of ABJM functions. The comparison supports a shared weight-four organization of nonperturbative Bethe data beyond the mirror pair, with arithmetic that distinguishes the theories. Through the finite-rank Cardy relation, the proposed completion predicts the entire $1/ω$ coefficient of the selected vacuum contribution to the superconformal index, retaining these theory-dependent corrections in its leading exponent.
