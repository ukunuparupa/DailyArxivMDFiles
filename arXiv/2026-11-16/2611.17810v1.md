# Phases of deformed sine dilaton gravity and JT limit

## authors:
#Paramesh_Mahapatra
#Hemant_Rathi
#Dibakar_Roychowdhury

## summary:
We study the implications of marginal deformation in sine dilaton gravity (sDG). We investigate the thermodynamic properties of deformed sine dilaton gravity (dsDG) and examine the phase stability of black holes in two space-time dimensions. We found that the deformation parameter ($λ$) sets a critical temperature threshold ($T_0$). Below this critical value ($T<T_0$), the system enters an unphysical phase characterized by a fractional density of states and a negative Wald entropy. Above the critical temperature ($T>T_0$), we obtain a physical black hole phase with positive entropy and negative free energy. Furthermore, we evaluate the Komar integral, derive the Casimir function for dsDG, and establish the first law of black hole thermodynamics. Finally, we explore the infrared (IR) limit of dsDG which we identify as Jackiw-Teitelboim (JT) gravity accompanied by marginal deformation. We also perform a detailed analysis of various thermodynamic entities pertinent to this deformed JT solution.
