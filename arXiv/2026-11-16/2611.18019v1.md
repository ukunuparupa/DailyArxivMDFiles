# Metastable vortex string decay in a brane picture

## authors:
#Akifumi_Chitose
#Hajime_Fukuda
#Kantaro_Ohmori

## summary:
We describe the decay of a metastable string as a bounce in a brane configuration. We consider a particular $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetric $\mathrm{U}(2)$ gauge theory with cascade Higgsing $\mathrm{U}(2)\to \mathrm{U}(1)^2 \to \mathrm{U}(1)$, where the vortex string can break by creating a pair of 't Hooft-Polyakov monopole and anti-monopole. The theory is engineered in a Type IIA brane setup, where the metastable string is realized as a D2-brane stretched between NS5- and D4-branes. The string breaks when the endpoints of the D2-brane, moving along the NS5- and D4-branes, meet so that the D2-brane shrinks to nothing. We check that the tension of the D2-brane reproduces that of the vortex string obtained numerically in the field theory, with the ratio approaching one in the limit where the vortex string becomes BPS. Within the thin-wall approximation, we derive the bounce action from the Dirac-Born-Infeld action of the D2-brane and find that it agrees with that of the field theory, with the monopole mass given by the area swept by the D2-brane. As a consistency check, we numerically construct a bounce solution of the D2-brane equation of motion under simplifying assumptions, whose action agrees with the thin-wall result in the regime where our description is reliable.
