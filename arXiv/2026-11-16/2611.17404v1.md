# Superstars, quantum wormholes and von Neumann algebras

## authors:
#Vijay_Balasubramanian
#Ryo_Nemoto
#Masaki_Shigemori

## summary:
We entangle heavy 1/2-BPS states in two copies of $\mathcal{N}=4$ SU($N$) Super-Yang-Mills (SYM) theory. Each copy is separately dual to an extremal, supersymmetric, singular black hole geometry, in which the horizon coincides with a singularity. General ideas in the AdS/CFT correspondence suggest that the entanglement between the two copies should lead to some form of connectivity between the two geometries---a kind of wormhole. We use von Neumann algebra methods in the SYM theory to diagnose the possible existence of such wormholes.
