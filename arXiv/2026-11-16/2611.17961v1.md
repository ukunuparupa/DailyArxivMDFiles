# Gravitational form factors of $ρ$ and $a_1$ mesons in V-QCD

## authors:
#Antti_Hippeläinen

## summary:
We compute the gravitational form factors and mechanical properties of the $ρ$ and $a_1$ mesons in V-QCD, a holographic model of QCD with backreacting flavor and dynamical chiral symmetry breaking. The parameters of the model are taken from an earlier fit to meson spectra and are not adjusted to form factor data. Both mesons are described by a single spin-1 field, and the six form factors are identified from the matrix element of the energy-momentum tensor with two spin-1 states, following the holographic dictionary. We find that $A_0 = J$ and $A_1 = 0$ hold identically in the model, and show that the remaining form factors $E$, $D_0$ and $D_1$ are regular at zero momentum transfer. We compare the form factors of $ρ$ with results from the Nambu--Jona-Lasinio model, a light-cone constituent quark model and the hard-wall holographic model. From the form factors, we compute the monopole and quadrupole distributions of energy, pressure and shear force in the Breit frame. The D-term, defined for spin-1 hadrons as the forward value of the combination $\mathcal{D}_0(t)$ of form factors, is found to be $-2.98$ for the $ρ$ and $-7.43$ for the $a_1$ meson. The mass and mechanical radii are $0.81$~fm and $0.89$~fm for $ρ$, and $0.73$~fm and $0.80$~fm for $a_1$.
