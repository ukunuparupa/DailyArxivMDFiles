# Entanglement-to-Stress Relation from an Effective Holographic Description of the Transverse-Field Ising Model

## authors:
#Dong-Hee_Kim
#Chanyong_Park
#Jihye_Yoo

## summary:
We investigate an effective holographic description of the transverse-field Ising model (TFIM). We reconstruct the spatial metric from its entanglement entropy and determine the temporal metric using the Einstein-scalar equations, ultraviolet time normalization, and a smooth, horizonless center. Holographic renormalization then gives an enthalpy density that can be expressed through the entropy curvature at half the system size. This relation is a prediction of the effective gravity for the microscopic theory. We test it against the exact finite-size TFIM stress, which is not used in the reconstruction. Away from criticality, the gravity prediction agrees closely with the exact stress in the near-critical scaling regime. This result suggests an entanglement-to-stress relation in the massive Ising field theory, whose field-theoretic derivation remains open. We also find that the reconstructions for different system sizes and couplings share a common scalar potential, up to finite-size deviations near the center that move away as the size increases. This supports the view that the reconstructed geometries are solutions of a single effective Einstein-scalar theory.
