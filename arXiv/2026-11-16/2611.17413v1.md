# Black Hole Probes under T-duality

## authors:
#Matilda_Delgado
#Dieter_Lüst
#Giulia_Tazzoli
#Matteo_Zatti

## summary:
Attractor black holes are finite-size probes of infinite-distance limits in the vector-multiplet moduli space of type IIA Calabi-Yau compactifications. We ask whether the hypermultiplet moduli space admits analogous probes. T-dualizing BPS black holes along a Euclidean time circle and along a spatial circle, which realizes the temporal and spatial c-map, we construct the dual type IIB D-instantons and axionic strings, on $T^6/(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2)$ and on a generic Calabi-Yau threefold. The duality maps the ADM mass of the black hole to the instanton action and to the string tension, its entropy to the instanton measure through the BPS index, and its warp factor to the four-dimensional dilaton, which therefore always runs along the dual flows. The instanton profiles are null geodesics in the complexified moduli space, and their action is bounded from below by the square root of the black-hole entropy. Hence, at fixed four-dimensional dilaton, only instantons dual to small black holes can become unsuppressed. The strings are bound states of EFT strings. Those dual to finite-size black holes, all probe the same emergent fundamental-string limit, which survives quantum corrections to the hypermultiplet moduli space. Those dual to small black holes probe other emergent-string and decompactification limits and we discuss which of these limits survive quantum corrections.
