# Anyon Proliferation at Self-Triality: From a Lattice Model to Adjoint QCD$_3$

## authors:
#Yuxuan_Guo

## summary:
We study how competitive proliferation among three types of anyons in a self-trial $\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge theory can drive symmetry breaking while preserving part of the topological order. We construct a deformation of the honeycomb color code, a spin model rooted in quantum error correction, with an $S_3$ symmetry that permutes the competing anyons, together with time-reversal and fermionic $\mathbb{Z}_2^{(1)}$ one-form symmetries. We propose two dual continuum descriptions of this transition. The bosonic description is a $\mathrm{PSU}(3)_3\times\mathrm{Spin}(8)_{-1}$ Chern-Simons-Higgs theory with a real Higgs field in the $(\mathbf{8}_{\mathrm{adj}},\mathbf{8}_v)$ representation. Fermion-boson duality gives a particularly simple fermionic dual: $\mathrm{PSU}(3)_{3/2}$ QCD$_3$ with a single adjoint Majorana fermion and gauged fermion parity. Both descriptions match the lattice model's unitary symmetries and proposed phase diagram, connecting double-toric-code order to a symmetry-broken phase with residual toric-code order. The transition is described by Higgs condensation and by a Majorana mass changing sign, respectively. The continuum theories are proposed to recover lattice time reversal at criticality. This connection to adjoint QCD$_3$ points to a possible continuous $S_3$-breaking transition from competing anyons, in contrast to the conventional first-order three-state Potts transition from competing local order parameters.
