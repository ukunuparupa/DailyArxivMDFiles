# Full-shape analysis of the Lyman-alpha forest power spectrum and bispectrum: validation on simulations

## authors:
#Roger_de_Belsunce
#James_M_Sullivan
#Mikhail_M_Ivanov

## summary:
The Lyman-$α$ (LyA) forest of neutral hydrogen provides a large-volume, high-resolution map of cosmological fluctuations from optical spectra at high redshift. To extract more information from the currently observing Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI), we derive an effective field theory (EFT) model for joint full-shape analyses of the LyA forest data. It combines the one-loop power spectrum of the LyA forest (FF), and of its cross-correlation with dark-matter halos, used as proxies for high-redshift quasars (FQ), with the tree-level bispectrum of the LyA forest (FFF). Importantly, the bispectrum breaks the LyA-specific degeneracy between the mean flux and the amplitude of the power spectrum. We validate our theory framework on idealized large-volume LyA forest mocks painted onto the AbacusSummit N-body simulation suite at redshift z=2.5. Fitting scales up to $k_\mathrm{max}^{FF\,(FQ)}=0.6\,(0.3)\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ in the power spectrum and $k_\mathrm{max}^{FFF}=0.1\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ in the bispectrum, our full-shape analysis yields unbiased constraints on the cosmological parameters in simulations. For a fully sampled flux field in a simulated volume of $V=8\,h^{-3}\,{\rm Gpc}^3$, we measure the Hubble constant, $H_0$, to $0.6\%$, the matter density, $Ω_{\rm m}$, to $1.6\%$ and the amplitude of matter clustering, $σ_8$, to $3.5\%$. Adding the bispectrum improves the constraints on $σ_8$ by $28\%$. We also demonstrate that the inclusion of the quasar one-loop power spectrum (QQ) and tree-level bispectrum (QQQ) further improves cosmological constraints by $\approx 35\%$ using $k_\mathrm{max}^{QQ,\,(QQQ)}=0.2,\,(0.08)\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$. Our theory framework enables full-shape analyses of high-redshift data from DESI and its successor DESI-II, enhancing knowledge of the growth of structure of the Universe during matter-domination.
