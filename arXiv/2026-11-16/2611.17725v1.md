# Axion quintessence with a negative cosmological constant: Bayesian constraints from current cosmological data

## authors:
#Masroor_C_Pookkillath
#Gary_Shiu
#Shinji_Tsujikawa

## summary:
We constrain canonical axion quintessence with a negative cosmological constant using DES Year 5 supernovae, DESI DR2 BAO, compressed CMB information, and geometry-corrected Gold-2017 growth data. We solve the background and linear-growth equations and sample with PolyChord. Our joint prior is logarithmic in the decay constant over $0.005\leq f/M_{\rm Pl}\leq10$ and uniform in the initial angle $θ_i=φ_i/f\in[0,π]$, conditioned on $π-θ_i\geq H_{\rm inf}/(2πf)$ with $H_{\rm inf}=10^{13}$ GeV and reduced Planck mass $M_{\rm Pl}$. The broad-prior model favors recent thawing, with median $w_{{\rm DE},0}=-0.865^{+0.068}_{-0.053}$, $m_φ\in[0.91,4.39]\times10^{-33}$ eV, and $f/M_{\rm Pl}\in[0.589,4.899]$ (central 68\% credible intervals). Bayes factors relative to flat $Λ$CDM are 0.70 and 30.1 for the normalized fluctuation-conditioned prior (Convention I) and its further conditioning on numerical acceptance (Convention II). Restricting $f\leq M_{\rm Pl}$ gives $f/M_{\rm Pl}\in[0.262,0.822]$ at 68\% credibility and Bayes factors 0.233 and 32.4. Relative to the zero-vacuum axion model, the broad-prior Bayes factors are 0.620 and 1.65, providing no compelling evidence for a negative cosmological constant. Local likelihood optimization gives an Akaike information criterion difference $Δ{\rm AIC}\simeq-10.87$ for the zero-vacuum model relative to flat $Λ$CDM. For this model, assuming that the instanton generating $m_φ^2f^2=Λ_{\rm UV}^4e^{-S_{\rm inst}}$ satisfies the axionic Weak Gravity Conjecture, the one-sided 95\% posterior lower limit on the minimum compatible coefficient $c_{\min}$ rises from 37 to 55 as the ultraviolet scale $Λ_{\rm UV}$ increases from $10^{-10}M_{\rm Pl}$ to $M_{\rm Pl}$, in tension with an order-one coefficient under these assumptions.
