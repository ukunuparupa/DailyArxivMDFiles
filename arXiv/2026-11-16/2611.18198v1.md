# Infinite Index Identities for Argyres-Douglas Theories and Graded Vector Space Isomorphisms for VOAs

## authors:
#Hongliang_Jiang

## summary:
Supersymmetric indices provide stringent tests of dualities between superconformal field theories (SCFTs). We establish infinite families of Schur-index identities among four-dimensional $\mathcal N=2$ Argyres-Douglas (AD) theories, with the logarithms of their fugacities related linearly. These identities connect theories with markedly different physical properties without requiring infrared duality, while still implying simple relations among their central charges and flavor levels. Through the SCFT/VOA correspondence, they become character identities for the associated affine and $W$-algebras. One broad family relates $D_p(G)$ and $(A_{(h+1)(p-1)},G)$ theories, corresponding to affine and principal $W$-algebras at boundary admissible levels. At the level of the underlying state spaces, these character identities suggest that the corresponding VOAs are related by graded vector space isomorphisms, which are weaker than full VOA isomorphisms. We explicitly construct such a graded isomorphism between the VOAs associated with $(A_1,A_{4p-3})$ and $(A_1,D_{2p})$. We also show the correspondence for universal affine and principal $W$-algebras and test its compatibility with their simple quotients by matching the lowest-degree null states. We establish analogous character identities for certain non-vacuum modules in concrete examples and conjecture a broader extension. Finally, beyond the SCFT/VOA correspondence, we discover similar identities between level-one affine and Virasoro algebras, whose further generalizations point toward a new spectral level-rank duality in WZW models.
