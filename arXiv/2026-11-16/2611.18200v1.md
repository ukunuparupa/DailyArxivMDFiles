# Separability of coupled gravitational perturbations in Plebański--Demiański spacetimes

## authors:
#Finnian_Gray
#Hryhorii_Ovcharenko

## summary:
Recently, a major breakthrough in the separability of gravitational perturbations of the Kerr--Newman rotating charged black hole was achieved by Hintz [arxiv:2609.33661], and extended to non-zero cosmological constant Kerr--Newman-(A)dS black holes by Yoshida [arxiv:2610.20500] and Zhao [arxiv:2610.22274]. This method removes the need for the freezing approximation, wherein coupling to the electromagnetic degrees of freedom are ignored, at the price of a coupled set of equations in the gravitational radiative degrees of freedom. The key insight is to transform the coupled system of equations into a chiral form which is amenable to a modified matrix $R$-separation of variables. In this work we extend the construction to the most general type D electro-vacuum solution to the Einstein-Maxwell equations, with aligned matter and cosmological constant. The construction makes use of the conformal properties of the equations and we find a symmetry operator underlying the decoupling of the chiral system.
