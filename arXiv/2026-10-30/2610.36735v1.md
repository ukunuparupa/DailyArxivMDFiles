# The Wess-Zumino Action of the Conformal Anomaly

## authors:
#Emil_Mottola

## summary:
The complete Wess-Zumino (WZ) action and stress-energy tensor (SET) of the conformal anomaly in both two and four dimensions is given. This local effective action is expressed in terms of a single scalar Nambu-Goldstone `conformalon' field $\varphi$, parametrizing the local abelian Weyl group of conformal transformations that act transitively on the spacetime metric. Satisfying WZ consistency, and equipped with a variational principle for $\varphi$ that determines the conformal transformation $g_{μν}\!\to\!g_{μν}e^{-\varphi}$ to an associated spacetime with no anomaly, the WZ action resolves the conformal gauge fixing ambiguity of earlier methods of `integrating the anomaly.' In $D\!=\!2$ the WZ action is the local form of the Polyakov action, whose massless $\varphi$ propagator describes a correlated two-particle scalar intermediate state in the SET polarization function $\langle TT\rangle$. The imaginary part of this polarization obeys a UV finite sum rule, becoming a Dirac $δ$-function at zero energy for conformal quantum fields, demonstrating the infrared origin of the anomaly, while the real part becomes the light cone propagator of $\varphi$. Although the $\Box R$ term in $D\!=\!4$ corresponds to a cohomologically trivial cocycle, it also arises from the IR limit of massless quantum fields, independently of UV physics, and contributes to the WZ action for low energy gravity. Analogous to the WZ action for pseudo-Goldstone mesons in low energy QCD from the axial anomaly, the WZ action for the conformal anomaly provides a Nambu-Goldstone scalar field for macroscopic gravity, in addition to the metric of classical general relativity.
