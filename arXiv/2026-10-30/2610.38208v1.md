# Covering Surfaces, Conical-Defect Continuation, and Multi-Entropy at Arbitrary Genus

## authors:
#Chen-Te_Ma

## summary:
We derive normalized geometric factors for permutation-twist correlators at arbitrary genus. For three uniformly ramified branch values, hypergeometric uniformization shows that the real part of the continued conical-defect exponent equals the covering-space anomaly factor. Its imaginary part is an Euler-characteristic Weyl phase. We extend the normalization to additional branch values, derive higher replica-index corrections to the geometric multi-entropy, and state the extra condition required for a holographic limit. A reducible conformal/topological example demonstrates why large central charge alone does not fix the complete entanglement result.
