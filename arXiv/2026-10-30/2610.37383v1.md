# Cosmological constraints on single-field inflation models from ACT/SPT data and observations at sub-Mpc scales

## authors:
#Qing-Yang_Wang
#Jing-Zhi_Zhou
#Fei-Yu_Chen
#Zhi-Chao_Li

## summary:
We investigate single-field inflation models that can produce large curvature perturbations at sub-Mpc scales while remaining consistent with cosmological observations across multiple regimes. We highlight the tension between generating enhanced small-scale perturbations and maintaining agreement with the scalar spectral index $n_s$ measured by ACT and SPT, which disfavors several conventional single-field inflation models. To address this issue, we propose three viable classes of inflationary potentials: bump/dip-modified, step-like, and double-inflection-point. Each can sufficiently amplify primordial perturbations while predicting $n_s$ that lie within the $2σ$ range of current observational bounds. In addition, we examine the primordial power spectra predicted by these models and constrain their parameter spaces using cosmological observations at different scales, including CMB, BAO, PBH, PTA, and CMB-distortion. Our results show that some of the models considered can simultaneously satisfy the ACT/SPT constraints and account for the current PTA observations, although doing so while respecting the constraints on the PBH abundance remains challenging.
