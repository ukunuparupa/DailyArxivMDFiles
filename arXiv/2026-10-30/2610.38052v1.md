# Quotient Quiver Subtraction -- Product Groups

## authors:
#Sam_Bennett
#Amihay_Hanany
#Guhesh_Kumaran

## summary:
Quotient quiver subtraction translates the gauging of Coulomb branch global symmetries into a combinatoric operation on 3d $\mathcal{N}=4$ gauge theories. This work uses brane systems to introduce a systematic method to gauge $\textrm{U}(n)\times\textrm{U}(n')$ and $\textrm{SU}(n)\times\textrm{SU}(n')$ emergent Coulomb branch global symmetries. Similar to other recently-introduced techniques, the gauging operation is a two-step process; in this case, it involves the symplectisation of unitary gauge factors into symplectic groups. A host of explicit examples are used to verify the procedure, and connections are made to Class $\mathcal{S}$ theories and three-dimensional theories realising maximal implosion spaces.
