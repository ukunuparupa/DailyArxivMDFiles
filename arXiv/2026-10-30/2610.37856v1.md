# Differential Equations for Dressed "in-in" Correlators

## authors:
#Ivo_Sachs

## summary:
We extend the formalism of differential equations and Integration-by-Parts identities, to cosmological in-in correlators of conformally coupled scalar fields, that can be formulated as dressed flat space amplitudes. These differential equations turn out to be quite powerful for extracting information about the class of functions which the corresponding amplitudes integrate to. Furthermore, they can be formulated algorithmically. Here we illustrate this for the cosmological bubble- triangle and exchange diagram.
