# Quiver Lab -- A Computing Tool for Quiver Gauge Theories

## authors:
#Zhenghao_Zhong

## summary:
Quiver Lab is a browser-based workspace for drawing quivers and exploring the moduli spaces of three-dimensional $\mathcal{N} = 4$ theories. It computes Higgs and Coulomb branch Hilbert series, plethystic logarithms, supported refinements, and Decay and Fission Hasse diagrams. This note explains the Light and Expert workflows and the main computational methods
