# Quasinormal-mode encounters: Pseudospectra near Kerr--Newman exceptional points

## authors:
#Kyriakos_Destounis

## summary:
Exceptional points (EPs), at which two quasinormal frequencies and their eigenvectors coalesce, can strongly enhance the spectral instability of black hole quasinormal modes. We investigate this behavior in Kerr--Newman spacetimes through pseudospectra and condition numbers for neutral massless scalar fields and coupled gravitoelectromagnetic (GE) perturbations. For the scalar field, we follow the $(\ell,m)=(1,1)$ spheroidal branch and derive the energy norm from the stress-energy tensor. For GE perturbations, we use a coupled four-field system equipped with a positive radiative energy. At the selected near-EP backgrounds, the condition numbers of both modes in the close pair exceed those of the two more-damped modes shown for comparison at the same background. Along a matched GE parameter path, the frequency splitting follows a square-root law and the condition numbers grow as the inverse square root of the distance to the EP. The pseudospectral contours resolve the transition from individual-mode sensitivity to the collective response of the pair. Within the discretized problems and energy norms considered here, these results quantify the amplification of spectral instability near EPs and provide a basis for studying their contribution to black-hole ringdown.
