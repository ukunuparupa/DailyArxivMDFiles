# One Method to Find Them All: Analytic Black Holes beyond Einstein

## authors:
#Dongjun_Li
#Jierui_Hu
#Nicolás_Yunes

## summary:
Rotating black holes offer powerful probes of gravity, but constructing their geometries beyond vacuum general relativity remains challenging. Here we develop a unified framework for constructing stationary, axisymmetric deformations of Kerr from their tidal curvature and physical sources. As a demonstration, we obtain closed-form solutions for six higher-curvature interactions at leading order in the couplings and for generic subextremal spin, without slow-rotation expansions, numerical fitting, or mode decomposition. The solutions pass stringent checks against the field equations, previous mode-by-mode constructions, and known analytic predictions for horizon quantities. We find that the chirality of the interactions organizes the analytic structure of these solutions, a pattern that likely extends to a large class of purely metric higher-curvature theories. Our framework opens a systematic route to black-hole geometries in beyond-Einstein theories and perturbative astrophysical environments. These geometries provide a foundation for strong-field tests with gravitational waves and other observational channels, such as black-hole images and X-ray spectroscopy, as well as theoretical studies of near-extremal black holes.
