# Connecting Regge factorization and Collins-Soper evolution

## authors:
#Andrea_Simonelli

## summary:
The relationship between Regge factorization of amplitudes and the Collins-Soper-Sterman factorization of hadronic cross sections is explored. Seeking a unified description of rapidity divergences in high-energy scattering and transverse-momentum-dependent factorization, a connection emerges between the Collins-Soper kernel and the Regge trajectory. This provides a framework for bridging high-energy and low-transverse-momentum resummation and for future extensions of $k_T$-factorization beyond leading-logarithmic accuracy.
