# M5 Instantons in ABJM

## authors:
#Jieming_Lin
#Jesse_van_Muiden
#Zihan_Wang

## summary:
We study the existence of supersymmetric M5-brane instantons in asymptotically AdS$_4 \times S^7/\mathbf{Z}_k$ geometries of M-theory, dual to the ABJM theory. The supersymmetric embeddings of these instantons are determined by the M5-brane $κ$-symmetry, which forces them to localise to the equivariant fixed points in the asymptotically AdS$_4$ geometry, and wrap $(S^3\times S^3)/\mathbf{Z}_k $. At the quantum level we find that the one-loop partition function of these instantons vanishes, suggesting that the bulk partition function is instead to be expanded in terms of supergravity modes and M2-brane instantons, in line with known results for the $S^3$ partition function of the dual ABJM theory.
