# A Toy Model for Topological Entanglement Features in 1+1D Integrable Quantum Field Theory

## authors:
#Emmalise_J_H_Aalbers
#Olalla_A_Castro-Alvaredo

## summary:
In this paper we investigate an entanglement measure, the Rényi entropy, in a 1+1D integrable quantum field theory known as the Federbush model. This is a deformation of the theory of two massive Dirac fermions by means of a bilinear term in the $U(1)$ currents that couples the two fermion species. This deformation gives rise to $S$-matrix elements which are coupling-dependent phases, distinct from $- 1$. These non-trivial phases can be seen as encoding anyon-like statistics. From this viewpoint, the Federbush model is a toy model for topological features of entanglement in one space dimension. In this paper we show that, for an infinite system, these topological features play no role when computing many known measures of entanglement at equilibrium in the ground state. This conclusion applies also to the post-quench dynamics after a small quench of the topological parameter.
