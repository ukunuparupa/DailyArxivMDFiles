# Gravity/Thermodynamics Correspondence for Generic Dynamical Spacetimes

## authors:
#Vahid_Reza_Shajiee
#Mohammad_Mehdi_Sheikh-Jabbari
#Vahid_Taghiloo

## summary:
Working within covariant phase space formalism, we establish \textit{gravity-thermodynamics correspondence} for generic dynamical spacetimes. We derive local first and second laws of thermodynamics on general null boundaries, for generic non-stationary solutions to Einstein gravity {with minimally coupled matter} for physical observers that we carefully specify. Entropy variation, which receives contribution only from the gravitational sector, is the surface charge variation associated with a vector field generating the null boundary, and temperature is its non-affinity parameter (divided by $2π$). The statement of the second law in our setting is that the entropy variation evaluated along the null vector field is strictly non-negative for the admissible positive-non-affinity clock and matter satisfying the null energy condition. Our dynamical entropy variation, which is non-integrable for generic non-stationary solutions, becomes integrable in near-stationary configurations, reproducing the Hollands--Wald--Zhang dynamical entropy formula. Strikingly, this local second law holds even when the boundary area is decreasing, disentangling thermodynamic irreversibility and the second law from standard area theorems. Our first-principles construction demonstrates that gravitational thermodynamics requires neither a stationary background, teleological event horizons, nor an exact entropy state function on individual codimension-1 spacelike slices of the null boundary (codimension two in spacetime).
