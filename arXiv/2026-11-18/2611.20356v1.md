# Triple Exchange at the Cosmological Collider in Closed Form

## authors:
#Taushif_Ahmed
#Sumit_Banik
#Syed_Mehedi_Hasan
#Andreas_Rapakoulias

## summary:
Particles with masses near the Hubble scale leave a characteristic pattern in the primordial bispectrum, turning inflation into a cosmological collider. For light fields, where this signal is largest, no closed form is known for the full shape of the quasi-single-field triple exchange. Using Feynman-integral methods, we obtain the first closed-form results, exact in the momenta, as expansions in terms of multiple polylogarithms around the massless and conformally coupled limits. We show that the template used in Planck analyses overestimates the amplitude by up to a factor of 1.8 and underestimates the mass, whereas our new template recovers both, as needed for current and upcoming CMB and galaxy surveys. We also compute the collapsed trispectrum and, combining it with the bispectrum, find that at weak mixing it bounds the self-coupling more tightly.
