# Holographic Homogeneous Black Branes from Anisotropic Scaling

## authors:
#Markus_A_G_Amano

## summary:
We construct homogeneous anisotropic black branes in Einstein gravity and Einstein--Maxwell--dilaton theory in arbitrary spacetime dimensions. By imposing compatibility between radial scaling and the spatial Lie algebra, we reduce the field equations to radial equations and algebraic constraints on the scaling exponents, curvature, and matter fields. We construct neutral, electric, magnetic, and dyonic solutions, including explicit five-dimensional Bianchi geometries and higher-dimensional families supported by Ricci nilsolitons. We investigate the thermodynamics of these solutions, obtaining their Euclidean action, mass, and entropy scaling. We also study the dynamics of a massive probe scalar, deriving an asymptotic mass bound and an exact purely damped quasinormal spectrum for a particular class of scaling geometries. Numerical calculations further explore the mass, dynamical exponent, and momentum dependence of scalar relaxation in Nil, Solv, and Bianchi type VI geometries. Our results establish a framework for studying thermodynamic and dynamical properties of homogeneous anisotropic scaling geometries that are generally not asymptotically locally anti-de Sitter.
