# On $ι$Quiver (BPS) Algebras and (Strict) Partitions

## authors:
#Jiakang_Bao

## summary:
We consider the $ι$quiver algebras of split type associated with the quiver BPS algebras of affine type A. They are supposed to give twisted affine Yangians and (elliptic) $ι$quantum toroidal algebras that could possibly arise from D-branes on $\mathbb{C}\times\mathbb{C}^2/\mathbb{Z}_n$ with orientifolds. We mention some properties of these algebras, such as the minimalistic presentations in the rational cases and the coideal structures. We also investigate how one might identify these algebras from the quiver theories with orientifold actions. Moreover, we discuss some representations with combinatorial bases of these algebras. In particular, the algebras with $n=1,2$ in certain limits of the parameters have representations labelled by (coloured) strict/symmetric partitions, which in the case of $n=1$ recovers the known BPS counting of certain theories.
