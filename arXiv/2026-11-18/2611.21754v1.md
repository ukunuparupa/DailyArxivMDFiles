# Giant gravitons without giant gravitons

## authors:
#Blake_J_Bottesi
#James_T_Liu

## summary:
We demonstrate that the partition function of a free bosonic $U(N)$ multi-matrix model admits a giant graviton expansion. For $d$ matrices, there are $d$ distinct giant gravitons, and the expansion can be characterized by a $d$-node quiver with bifundamentals connecting the nodes. This suggests the intriguing possibility of a more generalized holography where models without any obvious gravitational dual can nevertheless be given a dual brane interpretation. The giant graviton expansion of this multi-matrix model is somewhat unconventional though, as the giant graviton partition functions will necessarily have intrinsic $N$-dependence.
