# Wilsonian renormalization group flow in QED with the Pauli term

## authors:
#Sorato_Nagao
#Hiroshi_Suzuki

## summary:
We study the renormalization group (RG) flow of quantum electrodynamics (QED) with a Pauli term, $κ_τ\Barψσ_{μν}F_{μν}ψ$, by using the gradient flow exact renormalization group (GFERG). In the GFERG, the gauge Ward--Takahashi identity takes the conventional form and is exactly preserved, and the gauge coupling flows only through the photon anomalous dimension~$γ_A$. In the leading large-$N_f$ approximation, the vacuum polarization induced by the Pauli coupling yields a nontrivial fixed point with~$e_*=0$, $κ_*\neq0$, and~$γ_A=-1$. This fixed point has three relevant directions, as does one of the fixed points of the asymptotically safe QED proposed by Gies and Ziebell. At finite~$N_f$, however, the $N_f$-independent one-loop effects on the fermion line, in particular the additive mass renormalization proportional to~$κ^2$, eliminate this fixed point unless $N_f$ is at least several tens; for~$N_f=1$ there is no fixed point at weak Pauli coupling. Along the full flow for~$N_f=1$, $κ_τ$ is irrelevant and is attracted to an induced value~$κ_{\text{ind}}(e_τ)$, which is proportional to~$e_τ^3$ at weak coupling, while $e_τ^2$ is marginally irrelevant as in QED without the Pauli term.
