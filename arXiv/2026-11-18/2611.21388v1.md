# Carroll Double Copy

## authors:
#Sucheta_Majumdar
#Silvia_Nagy
#Giorgio_Pizzolo

## summary:
We construct a double copy for Carroll gravity as the ``square'' of Carroll Yang-Mills theory at the linearised level. Starting from the convolutional double copy prescription in Lorentzian spacetime, we take the Carrollian limit $c \rightarrow 0$ to establish a double copy dictionary compatible both with symmetries and the corresponding equations of motion. Since this limit yields two distinct sectors, namely the electric and magnetic Carroll theories, we systematically investigate double copy constructions involving all possible combinations of these parent theories. To accommodate the time-independent on-shell configurations of the magnetic Carroll fields, we introduce a suitable regularisation procedure for the convolutional double copy. In addition, the covariance of the double copy under Carroll boosts requires an appropriate dressing of the fields, leading to the inclusion of a Goldstone-type object in the construction. We first develop the formalism using toy models involving scalar fields and then apply it to the double copy between linearised Carroll Yang-Mills theory and Carroll gravity.
