# Fate of chiral and $U(1)_A$ symmetries in hot QCD matter under background magnetic fields

## authors:
#Dan_Zhang
#Heng-Tong_Ding
#José_Javier_Hernández_Hernández

## summary:
We summarize our recent lattice QCD study of chiral and singlet $U(1)_A$ symmetry breaking in background magnetic fields. The magnetic field explicitly reduces the non-singlet flavor symmetry through the unequal light-quark electric charges. We therefore study two neutral-channel susceptibility differences: $χ_{π^0}-χ_σ$ for chiral symmetry breaking and $χ_{π^0}-χ_{δ^0}$ for singlet $U(1)_A$ symmetry breaking. The calculation uses fixed-scale $(2+1)$-flavor HISQ ensembles with $m_l=m_s^{\mathrm{phys}}/10$, corresponding to a zero-field pion mass of $M_π\simeq220\,\mathrm{MeV}$. Magnetic fields enhance both susceptibility differences at low temperatures. Near the crossover, sufficiently strong fields instead suppress both differences. The suppression sets in at larger magnetic fields and is milder for the singlet-axial susceptibility difference than for the neutral chiral susceptibility difference.
