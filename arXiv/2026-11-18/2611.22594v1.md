# Higher Spin Lagrangians for Black Holes in Arbitrary Dimension

## authors:
#Francesco_Campanella

## summary:
In this paper, we investigate the classical gravitational multipolar structure of rotating objects studying higher-spin Lagrangians coupled to gravity in arbitrary dimensions $D=d+1$. We first consider fields described by totally symmetric tensors with $s$ indices. At finite spin, the minimal coupling depends on both the spin and spacetime dimension. We show that, in the high-spin limit $s\gg 1$, the minimal multipolar expansion becomes dimension independent and it corresponds to the one which reproduces the Kerr solution in four dimensions. Furthermore, we identify a dimension independent relation among the non-minimal coupling coefficients that allows us to reproduce the Myers-Perry multipolar structure in arbitrary dimensions. We then extend our analysis to Lagrangians involving fields with different Young representations, which are allowed in higher dimensions.
