# Cosmological Collider Signals from Fermion Bubble Loops with a Chemical Potential

## authors:
#Chengcheng_Han
#Zhanhong_Lei
#Zhehan_Qin
#Wuzhou_Yin

## summary:
We present the first systematic analytical calculation of the local and nonlocal cosmological collider (CC) signals generated by massive fermion bubble loops in the presence of an effective chemical potential during inflation. Working in the de Sitter approximation, we investigate the four-point correlator of inflaton perturbations in the single-squeezed limit. By combining CC cutting rules, late-time expansions, and partial Mellin-Barnes (MB) techniques, we derive analytical expressions for both local and nonlocal signals. In particular, we identify two distinct contributions to the local signal, arising from spectrum and integer poles and from ultraviolet poles of the loop-momentum integral. We further investigate the three-point limit, where the separately extracted local and nonlocal contributions develop apparent folded singularities. By reorganizing the MB representation, we demonstrate that these singularities cancel in the complete signal and obtain a convergent double-MB integral suitable for numerical evaluation. Independent calculations using complementary MB representations provide nontrivial consistency checks of our results. We find that the chemical potential can significantly enhance the oscillatory signal while increasing its logarithmic oscillation frequency. Our analytical results provide a foundation for further studies of chemical-potential-enhanced fermionic CC signals, including fermion triangle loops.
