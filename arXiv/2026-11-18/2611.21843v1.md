# Structure Constants of Planar N=4 SYM at Any Coupling

## authors:
#Simon_Ekhammar
#Alessandro_Georgoudis
#Nikolay_Gromov

## summary:
We recast a recent conjecture for structure constants in planar N=4 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory in a separation-of-variables-inspired form suitable for non-perturbative analysis. All state-dependent information enters through the quantum spectral curve. As a showcase, we compute at finite coupling the three-point functions of two half-BPS operators and the spin-two member of the Konishi multiplet. Our results span weak to strong coupling, reproduce the known perturbative and string-theory limits, and are consistent with conformal-bootstrap bounds. We also obtain new six- and seven-loop perturbative coefficients and extract new strong-coupling data from high-precision numerical fits.
