# Non-Canonical Q-balls in Polyattractor Inflation Models

## authors:
#Kimberley_Siret
#Rhys_Abercromby
#Kit_Lloyd-Stubbs
#John_McDonald

## summary:
We show that Q-balls can exist in supergravity-based polyattractor inflation models that interpolate between $α$-attractor T-models and polynomial attractor models. We consider both the original polyattractor potential and a natural generalisation. We find that Q-balls solutions exist when the model deviates significantly from the T-model $α$-attractor, whereas in the T-model limit no Q-balls exist. These are non-canonical Q-balls due to the non-canonical kinetic term of the complex inflaton field. Such Q-balls could have significant cosmological consequences if they are produced at the end of inflation. In particular, if the global $\mathrm{U}(1)$ symmetry of the supergravity-based polyattractor model is unbroken, the Q-balls can be absolutely stable and could account for dark matter.
