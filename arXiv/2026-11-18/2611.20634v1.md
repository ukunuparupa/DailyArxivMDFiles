# Boson Stars with Ayón--Beato--García nonlinear electrodynamics

## authors:
#Long-Xing_Huang
#Yong-Qiang_Wang

## summary:
In this paper, we study spherically symmetric boson star solutions in Einstein gravity minimally coupled to Ayón--Beato--García nonlinear electrodynamics and a massive complex scalar field. We find that these solutions exist only when the electric charge $q$ is below a critical value. Within this range, solutions with relatively small charges can exhibit a gravitational blueshift, and the radial mass function becomes negative in some regions while the ADM mass remains positive. For larger charges, frozen boson-star solutions emerge as the scalar-field frequency $ω$ approaches zero. In these configurations, the metric function $1/g_{rr}$ and $-g_{tt}$ become very small near a special position $r_{\mathrm{ch}}$, and the spacetime resembles an extremal black hole from the perspective of an observer at infinity. Moreover, timelike trajectories in these frozen boson stars are strongly deflected near $r_{\mathrm{ch}}$, and particles require a longer coordinate time to traverse the same radial distance than they do in ordinary boson stars.
