# Not All Holographic Entropy Inequalities Are Free of Area Law Ultraviolet Divergences

## authors:
#Xin-Xiang_Ju
#Bartlomiej_Czech

## summary:
In the AdS/CFT correspondence, linear constraints on subsystem entropies, which are necessary conditions for the validity of the Ryu-Takayanagi proposal, define the holographic entropy cone. We disprove a prior claim that all holographic constraints of this type are free of area law divergences (superbalanced). We analyze one explicit counterexample and explain how to easily find more; we report signatures of $\mathcal{O}(10^5)$ counterexamples. Our findings invalidate multiple prior claims and conjectures.
