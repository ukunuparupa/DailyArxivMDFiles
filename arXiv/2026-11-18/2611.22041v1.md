# Quantum Born Geometries from Modular Quantization

## authors:
#Paolo_Bertozzini
#Roberto_Conti

## summary:
A structural link between quantization and "thermal-doubled" non-commutative Born geometry (with its split-quaternionic structure) is uncovered via Tomita-Takesaki first-quantized modular theory for complexified and polarized ortho-symplectic spaces.  Implications for the modular algebraic quantum gravity program (arXiv:1007.4094) hint at further deep modifications of the notions of space and geometry (thermal quantum space-time hypothesis) waiting to be further explored.  Speculative suggestions in the direction of A.Sakharov and L.Boyle-K.Finn-N.Turok CPT-mirror cosmologies will be mentioned, among several others.  The present work is based on the manuscript "Modular Quantization and Non-commutative Space-time" (now on alphaXiv) that has been shared, with a selected number of researchers, since end of April 2026.
