# Localization identities for spectral determinants: two-dimensional QCD and Ising field theory

## authors:
#Bercel_Boldis
#Nikolay_Gromov
#Gregory_P_Korchemsky
#Alessandro_Testa

## summary:
Spectral determinants encode the meson spectra of planar two-dimensional QCD and of Ising field theory in the two-particle Bethe-Salpeter approximation. We derive localization identities for these determinants which express their logarithmic derivatives with respect to the spectral parameter in terms of Baxter $Q-$functions evaluated at special points, proving previously conjectured relations. Our derivation uses finite-rank commutation relations and virial identities of the underlying integral spectral operators. We identify a sufficient rational closure condition on the symbol of these operators that extends localization to a broader class of spectral problems and determines the evaluation points. Applications include generalized two-dimensional QCD with a quartic gauge-field potential and symbols interpolating between the QCD and Ising cases. We also give a general construction of the spectral operators and establish conditions for the finite-rank reduction on which localization relies.
