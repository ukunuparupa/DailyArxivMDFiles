# Polynomial twisted periods and affine vertex algebras

## authors:
#Tomoyuki_Arakawa
#Wenbin_Yan

## summary:
We prove the conjecture of Kang, Lawrie, and Song that Zhu's $C_2$-algebra of a simply-laced boundary admissible affine vertex algebra is isomorphic to the Jacobian ring of an invariant potential obtained by lifting a polynomial twisted period from a Cartan subalgebra via the Chevalley isomorphism. We further identify the $C_2$-algebras of the corresponding Drinfeld-Sokolov reductions with the Jacobian rings of the restrictions of this potential to Slodowy slices. We also extend the Jacobian presentation to non-simply-laced principal boundary admissible affine vertex algebras, where the restriction of the potential to the Cartan subalgebra satisfies a Dunkl-singularity equation with root-length-dependent multiplicities.
