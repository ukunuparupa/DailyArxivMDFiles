# Maximum entropy reconstruction of the black hole greybody factors from its Korteweg-de Vries integrals

## authors:
#Noah_Alberca
#Michele_Lenzi
#Carlos_F_Sopuerta

## summary:
The dynamics of perturbed non-rotating black holes (BHs) can be described by master wave equations with a potential barrier. Recent work has shown that these equations possess an infinite number of symmetries generated by the Korteweg-de Vries (KdV) hierarchy, leading to conserved quantities (KdV integrals) that fully determine the BH greybody factors via a moment problem. In this paper, we introduce and validate a new method to solve this moment problem using the maximum entropy principle. The method reconstructs, at each order of truncation, the least-biased transmission probability compatible with a finite set of KdV integrals, guaranteeing positivity by construction. We first validate the technique against the exactly solvable Pöschl-Teller potential. We then apply it to the Regge-Wheeler potential governing axial perturbations of a Schwarzschild BH, where no exact solution is available; the resulting reconstruction agrees closely with the fourth-order Wentzel-Kramers-Brillouin (WKB) approximation across several multipoles. This is the first application of the maximum entropy method to the reconstruction of BH greybody factors, demonstrating that a modest number of KdV integrals is sufficient to recover them accurately.
