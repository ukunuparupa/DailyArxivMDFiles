# Center anomalies without twists: torsion, exact degeneracies, and condensates

## authors:
#Mohamed_M_Anber

## summary:
Mixed center anomalies are usually exposed by 't Hooft's twisted boundary conditions in a Hamiltonian formalism. On $\mathbb {RP}^3$, torsion makes the twist unnecessary: a center operator on the untwisted $SU(2)$ Hilbert space anticommutes with time reversal in Yang-Mills theory at $θ=π$ and with the chiral symmetry of super Yang-Mills theory, so every level is at least doubly degenerate. The relevant semiclassical object is the BPST instanton, which descends to a charge-$\tfrac12$ saddle on the orbifold $\mathbb R^4/\mathbb Z_2$. We complete it in two ways: applying a conformal transformation maps the orbifold to $\mathbb R\times\mathbb {RP}^3$, with the saddle describing the tunneling event between the two center sectors; by resolving the orbifold singularity, we obtain the Eguchi-Hanson space, which gives the gaugino condensate at its weak-coupling value.
