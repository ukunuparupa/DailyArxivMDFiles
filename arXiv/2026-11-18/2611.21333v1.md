# Non-factorisable Yangian Lax matrices through oscillators

## authors:
#Rouven_Frassek
#Henrik_Jürgens

## summary:
We construct oscillator realisations of Yangian Lax operators in type $A$, with particular interest in non-factorisable operators associated with prime snake modules. Our construction applies to finite-dimensional skew representations of $Y(\mathfrak{gl}_n)$. We prove that these representations are generated by the tensor vacuum in suitably ordered tensor products of oscillator representations. Their Lax operators are obtained by restricting the corresponding products of linear oscillator Lax operators to these submodules. We determine the vacuum eigenvalues and Drinfeld polynomials, and give a determinant formula for the dimensions directly in terms of the highest loop weights. A $21$-dimensional example illustrates the construction, including an explicit basis and a transfer-matrix identity.
