# Lie-Poisson Formulation of Dissipative Spin Hydrodynamics

## authors:
#Zi-Hao_Liu
#Mei_Huang

## summary:
Covariance and the second law cannot fix how fluid flow couples to spin, since reversible couplings produce no entropy. We show that the spin commutation relations and the Poincaré algebra fix these couplings. Reversibly, a flow exerts no torque on the spin: its magnitude per particle is conserved, its direction is Fermi--Walker transported, and spin--shear couplings reflect a deforming triad. The magnitude changes only through higher multipoles, as in spinor condensates, or dissipation, whose first-order spin--shear coupling reorients the spin away from the vorticity above a critical shear rate.
