# Exceptional cubics on 7-manifolds

## authors:
#Sergey_Grigorian

## summary:
A $G_2$-structure $\varphi$ on a $7$-manifold $\mathcal M$ determines a two-dimensional space of invariant totally symmetric cubic forms on each fiber of the rank-$27$ bundle $S^2_0T^*\mathcal M$ of traceless symmetric $2$-tensors. This space is spanned by the trace cubic $C_0$ and a cubic $C_\varphi$ that, up to a constant factor, is the pointwise integrand of the Yukawa coupling on the $G_2$ moduli space. We determine the exact infinitesimal stabilizers over $\mathbb C$ of all nonzero cubics in this family. For $C_0+λC_\varphi$, the stabilizer has dimension $78$ precisely at the two non-real values $λ_\pm=(1\pm i\sqrt7)/8$. Its dimension is $21$ for $C_0$ and $14$ in all other cases. At $λ_\pm$, the stabilizer is simple of type $E_6$, and the corresponding cubic is linearly equivalent to the complex Albert algebra cubic norm.  The two cubics at $λ_\pm$, which we call critical, define complex-conjugate $E_6$-reductions of the complexified bundle. At any element with nonzero critical norm, the corresponding cubic determines a complex Albert algebra with basepoint stabilizer $F_4$; a line subbundle of the tangent bundle selects a smooth bundle of these algebras with tensorial operations and a Euclidean real form. The induced Levi-Civita connection preserves either critical cubic exactly when the $G_2$-structure is torsion-free. Its orthogonal projections onto the two exceptional reductions coincide with the connection induced by the minimal $G_2$-connection.
