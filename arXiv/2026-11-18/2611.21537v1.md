# The Skyrme-Dilaton and Nucleon Observables

## authors:
#Thomas_Fischbacher
#Roman_Zwicky

## summary:
We extend the Skyrme model by incorporating the $σ/f_0(500)$-meson as a dilaton. The motivation is twofold: the $σ$-meson is the lightest scalar resonance in QCD and is therefore the natural extensions to the pions of the classical Skyrme model, while its interpretation as a pseudo-Goldstone boson of spontaneously broken scale symmetry is supported by recent studies of gravitational form factors and related developments. Although the $σ$-meson is a broad resonance, for static observables it can be treated as real particle since its line-shape is not expected to be essential. A combined fit to static nucleon observables yields $χ^2/\mathrm{dof} $ close to unity, with substantial improvement over the original Skyrme model, while simultaneously reproducing the physical value of $F_π$, the overall scale of the model.
