# Covariant Wigner--Weyl calculus, non-dissipative transport and the chiral anomaly in inhomogeneous systems with nontrivial momentum-space topology

## authors:
#M_A_Zubkov

## summary:
We review, systematize and extend the covariant Wigner--Weyl calculus for fermions in non-Abelian gauge fields and its applications to systems inhomogeneous in coordinate space with nontrivial momentum-space topology: the non-Abelian chiral separation and quantum Hall effects, their stability under interactions, the Fermi-surface invariant of inhomogeneous systems and the non-elliptic models that carry it, the factorization of the chiral anomaly into the instanton number and the momentum-space invariant $N_3$, and the noncommutative-geometric form of the invariants.
