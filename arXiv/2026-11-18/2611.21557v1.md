# On the Asymptotic Expansion of Massive Fields in Spherically Symmetric Systems

## authors:
#Robin_Fynn_Diedrichs
#Iris_van_Gemeren

## summary:
The behavior of massive real scalar fields, when sourced by a central object, is usually assumed to follow a Yukawa falloff towards radial infinity. In this paper, we show that this is generically not the case, even for minimally coupled fields. Further, while the point-particle action provides a coordinate-invariant definition of the central object's scalar charge, we show that the coefficient one usually extracts at asymptotically large radii in the case of massive scalar fields is neither coordinate invariant nor identical to the scalar charge appearing in the point-particle action. We resolve this issue perturbatively by constructing matched asymptotic expansions and investigate the non-perturbative structure using numerical methods.
