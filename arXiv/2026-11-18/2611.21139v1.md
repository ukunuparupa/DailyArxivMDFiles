# Entropy of Noncommutative Spaces

## authors:
#Cisca_Kalmijn
#Walter_D_van_Suijlekom

## summary:
We give an overview of the second quantisation of spectral triples and compute the entropy of the noncommutative space described by it in terms of a spectral action. This reviews earlier work by Chamseddine, Connes and the second author, but presented here concisely, including the procedure of second quantisation using infinite-dimensional Clifford algebras. We exemplify the theory by considering noncommutative metric spaces consisting of $N$ points. The entropy of these discrete spaces is explicitly computed, and we study the resulting entropic force as well as the evolution of the spaces in time.
