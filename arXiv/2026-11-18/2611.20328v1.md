# Phase Structure and Thermodynamic Geometry of STU Black Holes

## authors:
#Fil_Simovic
#Kenan_Scavennec
#David_Kubiznak
#Robert_B_Mann

## summary:
We study the thermodynamics of four-dimensional STU black holes, a family of charged solutions of gauged N = 8, SO(8) supergravity, from the perspective of geometric thermodynamics. We focus on non-rotating solutions carrying pairwise-equal charges, obtaining closed-form expressions for the thermodynamic quantities directly in terms of the physical charges, and use them to determine the equation of state and associated phase structure. We also construct the Ruppeiner geometry for the STU black hole, taking the temperature and thermodynamic volume as fluctuation variables, and study its renormalized curvature as a probe of the black hole microstructure. We find a rich phase structure with two critical points present whenever the charge ratio is sufficiently large, and identify regions in parameter space associated with attractive and repulsive microscopic interactions.
