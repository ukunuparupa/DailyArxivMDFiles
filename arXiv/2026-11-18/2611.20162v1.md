# On Thermodynamics of AdS Black Holes in Square-Root Nonlinear Electrodynamics

## authors:
#A_Belhaj
#S_E_Ennadifi
#M_Jemri
#M_Nach

## summary:
Inspired by exponential Maxwell models, we propose a new black hole solution in square root nonlinear electrodynamics. Using the analogy between black hole mechanics and thermodynamics, we investigate the corresponding stability, the P-v criticality and the phase transitions. As a result, we find that the obtained black hole exhibits Van der Waals behaviors without additional corrections which typically appear in universal ratio computations.
