# Detectability of Eccentric Intermediate Mass-Ratio Inspiral in the Decihertz Band

## authors:
#Devesh_Giri
#Sajal_Mukherjee
#Apratim_Ganguly
#Sanjit_Mitra

## summary:
The proposed decihertz gravitational-wave (GW) band $(\sim 10^{-2}-1\,\Hz)$ will observe the late inspiral of intermediate mass-ratio inspirals (IMRIs), binaries with mass ratios $10^2 \lesssim q \lesssim 10^4$ that assemble dynamically in dense stellar environments and are expected to retain measurable eccentricity. Search and parameter-estimation pipelines are predominantly built around circular templates, and we test this assumption. Using black-hole perturbation theory waveforms from \textsc{FastEMRIWaveforms} for a representative IMRI ($M=10^4\,\modot$, $q=100$, observed for its final $10\,\hr$), we compute fitting factors between eccentric signals and circular templates, maximizing over the template's total mass and mass ratio with a differential-evolution search, for the B-DECIGO, LGWA-Si, and IndIGO-D noise curves and a flat PSD. The mismatch grows with initial eccentricity $e_0$ and crosses the standard $3\%$ threshold at $e_0\simeq3\times10^{-2}$, well within the range allowed by the waveform model and expected from dynamical formation. The best-fitting circular templates also bias the total mass and mass ratio by several percent at $e_0=0.1$. Combining the mismatch with the signal-to-noise-dependent distinguishability criterion, we find that for a source at $1\,\mathrm{Gpc}$, eccentricities as small as $e_0\simeq5\times10^{-4}$ (B-DECIGO and IndIGO-D) and $5\times10^{-3}$ (LGWA-Si) are resolvable, one to two orders of magnitude below where detection efficiency degrades. The loss of match arises from the excitation of radial Teukolsky harmonics, which spread power into sidebands that circular templates cannot represent, and from the slower orbital decay of eccentric binaries at fixed initial semi-latus rectum. Robust detection and parameter estimation of IMRIs with DECIGO, LGWA, and IndIGO-D will require eccentric waveform models and template banks.
