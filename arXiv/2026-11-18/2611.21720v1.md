# Constraints on the volume enclosed by Fermi surfaces

## authors:
#Subir_Sachdev

## summary:
A fundamental property of a Fermi liquid (FL) is that the volume enclosed by the Fermi surface is determined by $ρ$, the total density of electrons. In a fractionalized Fermi liquid (FL*), the volume enclosed by the Fermi surface is determined by the same expression after $ρ$ has been shifted by a quantized amount, most commonly to $ρ-1$ (we assume spin rotation invariance, for simplicity). The FL* shift is related to the quantized symmetry fractionalization data of a quantum spin liquid, whose fractionalized spinon excitations are present alongside the Fermi surface quasiparticles. In this review article, we establish the FL* Fermi volume by generalizing the original Luttinger proof for FL to parton gauge theories of the quantum spin liquid and FL*. We also discuss Oshikawa's flux insertion arguments for FL, and their extension to FL*, including a discussion of gapless spin liquids.
