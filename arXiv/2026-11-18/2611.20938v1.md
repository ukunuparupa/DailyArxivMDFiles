# A consistent (3D) EFT expansion of the bubble nucleation rate. Part I: scalar theories

## authors:
#Luis_Gil
#Javier_López_Miras

## summary:
Precise predictions of bubble nucleation are essential for connecting cosmological phase transitions to their potential observational signatures. In this first paper of a two-part series, we develop a general, fully perturbative framework for incorporating higher-dimensional operators (HDOs) into nucleation calculations in scalar high-temperature effective field theories. Our approach consistently includes their effects on the bounce and the fluctuations around it, including interactions with higher derivatives. We prove that the resulting statistical rate is invariant under field redefinitions, and establish sufficient conditions for the existence of the perturbative bounce solution in the presence of HDOs. We illustrate the framework by applying it to a model containing a scalar and a fermion, where we find modest HDO corrections in its regime of moderately strong transitions. We thus extend the systematic study of HDO effects beyond critical thermodynamics and provide a controlled method for determining their impact on bubble nucleation.
