# A note on Padmanabhan's path-integral duality

## authors:
#P_Fernandez_de_Cordoba
#J_M_Isidro

## summary:
We develop a field-theoretic implementation of Padmanabhan's principle of invariance under the duality $\mathcal S \rightarrow {L_0^2}/{\mathcal S}$, where ${\mathcal S}$ is the proper length (the action integral along a particle's trajectory) and $L_0$ is a quantum of length. Starting from the particle propagator and its Schwinger proper-time representation, we derive the wave operator associated with the duality transformation in terms of a scalar field, rather than a point particle. The resulting theory is governed by a nonlocal operator involving modified Bessel functions. We analyse the infrared and ultraviolet regimes, demonstrate the recovery of standard Klein-Gordon theory at low energies, and show the appearance of exponential ultraviolet suppression. Our analysis provides a natural extension, to quantum field theory, of Padmanabhan's duality for point particles.
