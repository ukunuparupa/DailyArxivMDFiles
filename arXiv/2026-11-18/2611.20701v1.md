# Doubly-spinning Black Saturns

## authors:
#Jay_Armas

## summary:
We construct a six-parameter family of stationary, asymptotically flat solutions of five-dimensional vacuum Einstein gravity describing a spherical black hole surrounded by a black ring, with rotation in both independent planes. In a specific regime both horizons can become extremal, thereby providing the first example of a regular asymptotically flat vacuum Einstein solution with two extremal disconnected horizons. We also show that while non-extremal black Saturns can be in thermodynamic equilibrium, the doubly extremal Saturn cannot. The solution admits the doubly spinning Myers-Perry, the Pomeransky-Sen'kov black ring, and the singly spinning black Saturn as regular limits. We also show agreement with earlier blackfold predictions for the phase diagram of these solutions.
