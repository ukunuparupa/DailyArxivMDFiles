# Non-integrability of open strings via Pohlmeyer reduction

## authors:
#Divania_Carelse
#Laila_Tribelhorn
#Konstantinos_Zoubos

## summary:
We study integrable and non-integrable boundary conditions for classical strings from the point of view of Pohlmeyer reduction. We first consider the integrable case of strings ending on Y=0 non-maximal giant gravitons. Pohlmeyer reduction of such strings leads to a sine-Gordon model, for which a criterion for boundary integrability was provided by Ghoshal and Zamolodchikov. We confirm that the Pohlmeyer field satisfies this condition. We then turn to the main focus of our work, which is open strings on AdS3. By evaluating the Ghoshal-Zamolodchikov criterion for the corresponding Pohlmeyer-reduced models, we are able to distinguish between integrable and non-integrable cases, in line with expectations from recent analytic and numerical studies. We conclude that Pohlmeyer reduction can provide a useful perspective on open-string non-integrability.
