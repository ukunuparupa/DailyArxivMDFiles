# FLZ integrability in the 't Hooft model

## authors:
#Alexey_Litvinov
#Egor_Shestopalov
#Nikita_Slavnov

## summary:
We study the 't Hooft equation describing mesons in large-$N_c$ two-dimensional QCD with arbitrary unequal quark masses. Using its Fredholm formulation and the associated Baxter finite-difference equation, we investigate the relations between spectral determinants and canonical Baxter $Q$-functions. We derive special-point identities for the Baxter solutions by exploiting finite-rank commutator relations of the underlying integral operators. We also determine the spectral residues of the inhomogeneous solutions and obtain exact expressions for eigenfunction normalization factors in terms of values at distinguished complex points. These results establish the unit-residue property of the Baxter expressions entering the Fateev-Lukyanov-Zamolodchikov (FLZ) spectral-determinant identities. As a further application, we prove the nondegeneracy of the meson spectrum for arbitrary unequal quark masses. We further identify a broader class of integral operators exhibiting the same mechanism, which we refer to as FLZ integrability, and illustrate its applicability to the meson spectral problem in Ising field theory. Our approach reveals a connection between the analytic structure of Baxter equations and the theory of completely integrable Fredholm operators.
