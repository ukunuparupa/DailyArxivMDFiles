# Regular/Singular Black Hole Thermodynamics from Renormalised Volume/Area

## authors:
#Gabriel_Arenas-Henriquez
#Rodrigo_Olea
#David_Rivera-Betancour

## summary:
We study the finite mismatch between the holographically and topologically renormalised Euclidean actions of the four-dimensional AdS C-metric. Starting from the relation between Renormalised Volume and the Einstein sector of Conformal Gravity, we separate the regular curvature contribution from the contributions supported on the two conical axes. After introducing the usual Euler normalisation for smooth manifolds, the residual difference between the regular action evaluations is determined by the tension-weighted Euler terms of the renormalised worldsheet area. We identify this difference with the Gauss--Bonnet entropy contribution at the polar conical points of the horizon. The ordinary Euler characteristic remains fixed, while the deficits determine how its curvature representation is divided between regular and singular contributions. At fixed total string tension, the difference of the renormalised worldsheet areas per unit Euclidean time determines the relative tension work, giving a geometric interpretation of the relative thermodynamic length.
