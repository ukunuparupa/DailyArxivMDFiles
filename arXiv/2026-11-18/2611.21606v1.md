# The Shape of the Strongly Coupled MQM Ground State

## authors:
#Alexander_Frenkel

## summary:
We study a surprisingly efficient family of variational ansatze for the ground state of bosonic matrix quantum mechanics at large $N$. These are ansatze whose norm-squared probability distributions are themselves matrix integrals with single-trace actions, and so have their own independent `t Hooft expansions. They are efficient in the sense that optimizing over a small number of parameters produces many digits of accuracy for the energy, single-trace moments, and connected correlators. For infinite-$N$ planar data our results rapidly converge to within published bootstrap intervals and stabilize to a few digits of precision beyond them. We also demonstrate that we can efficiently capture $1/N$ corrections by computing connected two-point functions. These are matched against resummed quantum perturbation theory and Monte Carlo simulations.
