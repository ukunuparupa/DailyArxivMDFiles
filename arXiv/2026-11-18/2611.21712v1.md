# Hidden wormholes from an action principle in $D=4$

## authors:
#Francesco_Del_Porro
#Alessia_Platania
#Samuele_Silveravalle

## summary:
We present new black hole solutions for the class of gravitational effective field theories in four spacetime dimensions that extend General Relativity while retaining the graviton as the only degree of freedom of gravitational origin. We consider terms up to cubic order in curvature, solve the resulting field equations, and find one-way hidden wormholes as numerical solutions: these are regular black holes with an interior wormhole throat that is crossable in one direction and leads to a cosmological spacetime in its interior. They are regular and have no inner horizon, thus evading the mass inflation instability that afflicts conventional regular black hole geometries.
