# Modular conjugations for thermal states and second worlds

## authors:
#Alan_Garbarz
#Gabriel_Palau
#Guillem_Pérez-Nadal

## summary:
The modular conjugation $J$ maps an algebra of observables onto its commutant. For the vacuum on a Rindler wedge it is a spacetime reflection, but for thermal states in two-dimensional CFTs the known expressions involve chiral fields at the complex points $x\pm iβ/2$, interpreted as belonging to a ``second world''. Here we compute $J$ for the geometric thermal state in explicit cyclic purifications, using either the vacuum vector, through the conformal map to a Rindler wedge, or a vector in the natural cone of a local algebra. The modular images are then fields at real points, and complex coordinates arise only when operators of the commutant outside the image of the thermal representation are written as fields on the original spacetime. For the chiral $U(1)$ current we also purify by doubling the Fock space, obtain explicit formulas for $J$, show that this purification coincides with Sorce's canonical purification, and treat all primary thermal states.
