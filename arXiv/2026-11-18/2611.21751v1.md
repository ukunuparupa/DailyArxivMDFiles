# From Top to Bottom: Quark Mass Hierarchies in Heterotic String Theory

## authors:
#Yuri_Chervonyi
#Fabian_Ruehle
#Nenad_Tomasev

## summary:
Standard Model fermion masses exhibit large hierarchies, spanning more than five orders of magnitude in the quark sector alone. In this paper, we study whether these hierarchies can be generated perturbatively at tree level in smooth heterotic Calabi-Yau compactifications, where the supergravity approximation remains under computational control. In such backgrounds, physical Yukawa couplings combine holomorphic superpotential integrals with matter field Kahler metrics. The former can be computed algebraically, while the latter require solving a coupled system of partial differential equations for the Ricci-flat metric, Hermitian Yang-Mills connections, and harmonic bundle-valued forms. Within this framework, we identify two new tree-level mechanisms by which complex structure moduli can generate fermion mass hierarchies and show analytically and numerically that both mechanisms are obstructed for previously studied tetra-quadric models. Motivated by these obstructions, we scan the existing databases of tens of thousands of complete intersection Calabi-Yau line bundle models and find a unique smooth quotient admitting a rank-three up-quark Yukawa matrix at tree level. Evaluating the physical couplings on this manifold and evolving them under the renormalization group, we obtain realistic mass hierarchies for the top, charm, up, and bottom quarks in the smooth supergravity regime.
