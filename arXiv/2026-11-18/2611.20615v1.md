# Hyperbolic string vertices as geometric chains

## authors:
#Hao_Yu

## summary:
For 0<L<=2arcsinh(1), the hyperbolic string vertices of Costello and Zwiebach consist of the bordered hyperbolic surfaces with geodesic boundary of length L whose interior simple closed geodesics have length at least L. We show that the corresponding regions in moduli space are compact oriented orbifolds with corners whose boundaries are, including orientations and automorphism groups, the spaces of twisted sewings. Hence their generating chain satisfies the master equation without the bracket term exactly in a complex of geometric chains. The usual connected form cannot hold exactly for geometric chains; we determine its exact form, show that it holds modulo gluing and covering relations in a complex computing the homology of the moduli spaces, and that the vertex currents are the unique top-dimensional solution. Finally we compare with Costello's homotopical framework. In a strict model given by hyperbolic surfaces with marked boundary points, every solution of his master equation with his initial condition integrates exactly to the vertex currents, up to the sign (-1)^(3g-3+n); such solutions exist, are unique up to homotopy, and correspond to the hyperbolic vertices. Under a continuity assumption on the comparison map and granting the inputs of Costello's homology computation, the hyperbolic vertices represent his homotopy class of solutions, for a completed notion of homotopy.
