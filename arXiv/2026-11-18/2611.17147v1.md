# Gauge aspects of teleparallel gravity and Noether's second theorem

## authors:
#Sebastian_Brezina
#Martin_Krššák

## summary:
We examine the gauge structure of the Teleparallel Equivalent of General Relativity (TEGR) from the perspective of Noether's second theorem. We show that diffeomorphisms and local Lorentz transformations are the gauge symmetries of TEGR, as these are the only variational symmetries that give rise to Noether identities. We then discuss the distinct roles of these two symmetries and their implications for the gauge-theoretic interpretation of gravity. Finally, we discuss consequences of these observations for the role of translations in gauge theories of gravity and the coupling of matter in TEGR. In particular, we demonstrate that the matter coupling prescription is uniquely determined and coincides with that of general relativity, including in the case of spinor fields.
