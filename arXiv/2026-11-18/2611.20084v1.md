# On reflection positivity of lattice chiral fermions

## authors:
#Zhiyao_Lu
#Shu-Heng_Shao

## summary:
We point out that the overlap Weyl fermion, the spacetime lattice realization of a Weyl fermion underlying Lüscher's construction of lattice chiral gauge theories, is not reflection positive in two and four dimensions at finite lattice spacing. Consequently, the standard Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction does not yield a Hilbert space with a self-adjoint Hamiltonian. In two dimensions, we further show that the overlap Weyl fermion cannot be cut open: the lattice action becomes nonlocal in the presence of a boundary, as expected from the gravitational anomaly of the continuum field theory. By contrast, the overlap Dirac fermion is known to be reflection positive. However, its compact axial symmetry is not invariant under the reflection, whereas a reflection-invariant axial symmetry is noncompact, and both are part of an Onsager algebra.
