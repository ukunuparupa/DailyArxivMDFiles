# Isomorphisms of Class S Theories with IndexLab

## authors:
#Zhihao_Duan
#Zhenghao_Zhong

## summary:
We use IndexLab to scan untwisted and twisted class $\mathcal{S}$ constructions based on ADE Lie algebras of rank up to seven, as well as $A_8$ and $E_8$. We consider surfaces of genus at most three with up to five punctures, covering 167,244,647 constructions in total. To find candidate pairs of isomorphic theories, we compare Schur and Hall-Littlewood indices, Weyl anomalies, Coulomb branch spectra, and flavor data. We set aside pairs explained by known mechanisms, leaving a catalogue of 5,278 candidate pairs. Recurring changes in rank, puncture labels, and twists suggest families of candidate isomorphisms. We group 99.58% of the pairs into 25 families and 6 finite families. IndexLab is available at superconformal.com.
