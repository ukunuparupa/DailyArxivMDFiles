# Renormalization-Group Foundation of Critical Modes in Correlation Matrices

## authors:
#Yanhua_Zhang
#Mingmei_Xu
#Jinghua_Fu
#Yuanfang_Wu

## summary:
Based on the relevant scaling operators of renormalization-group theory, we establish a theoretical framework for constructing complete families of critical-sensitive fluctuation observables and their connected correlation matrices. The corresponding eigenstates of the correlation matrices reveal a hierarchy of multiscale critical correlation patterns and the leading normalized eigenvalues and their ratios show the finite-size scaling behavior. Applying this framework to the two-dimensional Ising model, the correlation matrices associated with both spin and energy operators are constructed. The resulting energy-sector eigenstate patterns, together with the scaling behavior of the leading eigenvalues, provide further support for the proposed framework. Finally, we discuss its potential extension to systems with unknown Hamiltonians.
