# Soft momentum expansion about the Stokes topological limit of Bogoliubov coefficients

## authors:
#Daniel_J_H_Chung
#Nidhi_Sudhir
#Junqi_Zhang

## summary:
It is known that particle production in time-dependent cosmological backgrounds can be computed using the Stokes phenomena occurring in Bogoliubov transformations with an analytically continued time coordinate. We analytically compute the soft momentum expansion of the particle production amplitude about the Stokes topological limit that was recently discovered in this context. The computational technique relies on an exponentially improved asymptotic expansion that involves automorphisms in the space of functions defined partly by the angular step function intrinsic to the Stokes topological sectors. We combine this with special discrete symmetries tied to the time dependence of the dispersion relation $ω_k^2(z) = C(k) + A z^n$ for even $n$ to explicitly compute the leading momentum dependence of the amplitude which is nonperturbative in $A$. We also use this result to give a semi-analytic estimate of the number density of particles in this class of scenarios.
