# Off-shell one-loop counterterms of Palatini gravity with an independent background connection

## authors:
#F_T_Brandt
#G_A_Menezes

## summary:
We compute the one-loop divergences of pure gravity in the first-order (Palatini) formulation, using the background field method in Goldberg variables, with a background connection independent of the background metric. Background Ward-Takahashi identities are verified at one loop. The divergences of the two-point functions, obtained in arbitrary dimension and gauge, require, besides the curvature-squared terms, invariants built from the nonmetricity of the background connection; in particular, the Gauss-Bonnet combination, which is not topological for a nonmetric connection, does not suffice. Three- and four-point functions in the Feynman-de Donder gauge complete the counterterm Lagrangian up to fourth order in the nonmetricity and provide parameter-free tests of it. All the counterterms vanish on shell, as in the metric formulation, and off shell they can be removed by field redefinitions.
