# Modular Hamiltonian and modular flow of a massive scalar in an interval

## authors:
#Raúl_E_Arias
#Daniel_Fondevila

## summary:
We obtain the vacuum modular Hamiltonian of a free massive scalar in an interval, for any $mL$. Its eigenvectors are superpositions of massive plane waves emitted from the two end points, with amplitudes given by a linear integral equation whose Neumann series is an expansion in reflections, and a normalization fixed by a Painlevé V transcendent. We follow the modular flow of initially localized wave packets inside the causal diamond. The flow is not geometric: the commutator profiles spread and can develop peaks ahead of the conformal trajectory.
