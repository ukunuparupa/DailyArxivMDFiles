# Ricci-flat metrics from gauged linear $σ$-models without RG flow

## authors:
#Dmitri_Bykov
#Ondrej_Hulik
#Ulf_Lindström
#Martin_Roček
#Rikard_von_Unge

## summary:
We investigate a class of Ricci-flat Kähler metrics on generalized conifolds constructed via gauged linear sigma models (GLSMs) with indefinite signature. By introducing shadow coordinates (superfields) entering the sigma model with negative signature kinetic term, we show that these GLSMs yield explicit Ricci-flat metrics on complex cones over products of projective spaces. We provide a general formula for the resulting Kähler potentials, along with detailed examples. Our results suggest new directions for the study of Calabi-Yau metrics and toric geometry, and raise interesting questions about the geometric meaning of indefinite signature models. We also give an interpretation in terms of a novel generalized Kähler gauging.
