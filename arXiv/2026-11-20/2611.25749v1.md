# Second-order formulation of chiral fermions with a local boundary condition

## authors:
#Kazuya_Yonekura

## summary:
Chiral fermions in $d$ dimensions arise naturally as boundary modes of a massive fermion in $d+1$ dimensions subject to a local boundary condition. When anomalies are absent, this construction can be used to define an intrinsically $d$-dimensional theory of chiral fermions. However, defining the path integral using the first-order Dirac operator with this boundary condition is technically inconvenient, even in the continuum. We present a formulation in terms of second-order differential operators that is completely equivalent to the original formulation in the continuum. Its natural lattice discretization generates Wilson's term and avoids fermion doubling. Our lattice formulation closely resembles the boundary-fermion construction of Shamir, and provides some insight into the case of a general Wilson parameter.
