# Entanglement entropy from fundamental strings

## authors:
#Houwen_Wu
#Shuxuan_Ying

## summary:
In previous work, we derived the local entropy capacity from off-shell string theory and combined it with the gravitational replica construction and flow-cut duality to normalize the entropy flow carried by physical fundamental strings. In this paper, we apply this framework to interval entanglement in holographic CFT$_2$. We construct explicit on-shell F1 worldsheets in the AdS$_3$ vacuum and planar BTZ, derive their Kalb-Ramond source currents, and show that the normalized spatial currents saturate the corresponding RT surface. Their flux reproduces both the vacuum single interval entropy and the finite temperature CFT$_2$ result. We then vary the NS-NS flux fraction. Although the total maximal flux remains unchanged, the F1 equations continuously redistribute the strings among different parts of the complement. In planar BTZ, the resulting signed flux through the bifurcation line reproduces the extensive thermal entropy and can vanish in the pure NS-NS limit without changing the interval entropy. Thus the on-shell string dynamics additionally determines its physical routing and boundary contact structure.
