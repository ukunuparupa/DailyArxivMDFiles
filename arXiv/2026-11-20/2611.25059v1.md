# Nonzero cubic Love numbers of Schwarzschild black holes

## authors:
#Bruno_Bucciotti

## summary:
Static Love numbers of Schwarzschild black holes vanish at linear and quadratic order in General Relativity, while generic nonlinear tides remain less understood. We solve a specific non-axisymmetric octupolar tide at cubic order and, building on the symmetry of static General Relativity exploited by Parra-Martinez and Podo, match the result to a worldline effective field theory (EFT) using dimensional regularization. Surprisingly, we find a Love number that neither vanishes nor runs.
