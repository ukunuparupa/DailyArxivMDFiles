# $C_T$ and $C_J$ of general scalar--fermion conformal field theories in $d=4-ε$

## authors:
#Shiroman_Prakash
#Shubham_Kumar_Sinha

## summary:
We compute $C_T$ and $C_J$, the coefficients of the stress-tensor and conserved-current two-point functions, to order $ε^3$ at any fixed point of the general renormalizable theory of scalars and fermions in $d=4-ε$. We give explicit results for the chiral Ising, XY and Heisenberg models and for fixed points with emergent supersymmetry in $d=3$. In theories without a four-dimensional counterpart, fermion loops with an odd number of Yukawa vertices contribute at order $ε^3$ through traces whose continuation away from $d=3$ is ambiguous; we fix it with $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetry, by computing the two-point functions of the supercurrent and of the fermionic partner of a flavor current to four loops. In $d=3$, Padé approximants closely reproduce the exact $C_T$ of the $\mathcal N=2$ super-Ising model. We estimate the universal conductivity at the charge-density-wave, Kekulé and Néel transitions of graphene to be $σ/σ_{\rm free}\approx0.91$, $0.79$--$0.83$ and $0.64$--$0.74$, and show that the exact conductivity at the supersymmetric transition on the surface of a topological insulator is $σ/σ_{\rm free}=\frac{9}{10}\,C_T/C_{T,\rm free}\approx0.654$, $10\%$ below the value previously reported.
