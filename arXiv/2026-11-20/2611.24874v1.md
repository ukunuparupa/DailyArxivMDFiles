# Rotating black hole thermodynamics at NNLO in higher-curvature gravity

## authors:
#Cristobal_Laporte

## summary:
We investigate higher-curvature corrections to black-hole thermodynamics in quasitopological gravity using perturbative Euclidean-action methods. Static, spherically symmetric black holes provide a benchmark for the action expansion, while our main application concerns five-dimensional, asymptotically flat, rotating black holes with equal angular momenta. For Einstein gravity supplemented by Gauss--Bonnet and cubic quasitopological interactions, we compute the Gibbs free energy through next-to-next-to-leading order, retaining both quadratic and cubic corrections. The result retains the full dependence on the background spin parameter, without a slow-rotation expansion, and extends to arbitrary couplings of quadratic and cubic curvature invariants. We derive the corrected mass, entropy and angular momenta, and show how rotation affects the size and sign of the corrections.
