# Black hole binary mergers in dynamical Chern-Simons gravity with regularisation

## authors:
#Llibert_Aresté_Saló
#Maxence_Corman
#Pau_Figueras
#Áron_D_Kovács
#Shunhui_Yao

## summary:
We study the non-linear dynamics of Chern-Simons gravity with numerical relativity for the first time. We use the regularisation prescription to obtain a well-posed initial-value formulation in the gauges commonly used in numerical relativity, and we simulate head-on collisions and binary black hole mergers in the regime of validity of effective field theory, extracting the corresponding scalar and gravitational waves. This opens up the possibility to test general effective field theories of gravity, including those with higher-than-second-order equations of motion, with gravitational waves.
