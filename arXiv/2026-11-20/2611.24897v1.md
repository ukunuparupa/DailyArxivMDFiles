# Beyond hidden zeros and uniqueness in Tr$(φ^3)$: Block and rod zeros in any Tr$(φ^m)$

## authors:
#Laurentiu_Rodina

## summary:
Hidden zeros have proven powerful tools for reconstructing amplitudes in Tr$(φ^3)$, a theory of colored scalars with cubic interactions. We show that these structures extend to any Tr$(φ^m)$ theory. For odd $m$, we construct \emph{block zeros} from orthogonal sums of consecutive momenta, generalizing the original cubic hidden zeros. For every $m\geq4$, including even $m$, we describe a second family of nonlinear \emph{rod zeros} through a rod packing problem. In Tr$(φ^4)$ this construction reduces to a simple rank-one condition on a matrix of momentum contractions. We connect this new zero to the classic threshold nullification zeros of Voloshin, where certain amplitudes in massive $φ^4$ theory vanish when all produced particles are at rest. Finally, we demonstrate the reconstruction power of these zeros. Assuming locality, we prove that the rod zeros uniquely fix all Tr$(φ^m)$ tree amplitudes for $m\geq4$ up to normalization. We also conjecture that the block zeros enforce both locality and unitarity for any Tr$(φ^m)$ theory with odd $m$.
