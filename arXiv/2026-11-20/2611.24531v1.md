# $1/N$ corrections to $\langle TJJ\rangle$ and $\langle TTT\rangle$ in QED$_3$, QED$_3$--GNY and scalar QED$_3$

## authors:
#Yongwei_Guo
#Zhijin_Li
#Ke_Ren

## summary:
Three-dimensional Abelian gauge theories have broad applications in deconfined quantum criticality, Dirac spin liquids and cuprate superconductivity. Correlation functions of conserved currents $J$ and the stress tensor $T$ carry fundamental information about these theories. We compute the first subleading large-flavor corrections to the parity-even three-point functions $\langle TJJ\rangle$ and $\langle TTT\rangle$ in QED$_3$, QED$_3$--Gross--Neveu--Yukawa, and critical scalar QED$_3$. For $\langle TJJ\rangle$, we study both global flavor currents and the topological $U(1)$ current, which measures the magnetic flux of the dynamical gauge field. We evaluate the flat-space energy flux in directions where the leading free-field profile vanishes. At first subleading order the flux in these directions is determined by four-particle production, yielding analytic corrections to the conformal collider parameters. We reproduce the corrections independently from squashed-sphere free-energy derivatives. Together with known two-point normalizations, the collider parameters fix the coefficients of both independent tensor structures in each correlator. Our results are useful for studying conductivity and shear response near quantum critical points and for conformal bootstrap studies of conserved-current and stress-tensor correlators in strongly coupled gauge theories.
