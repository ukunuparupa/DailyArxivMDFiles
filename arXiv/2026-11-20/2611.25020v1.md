# Asymmetric Orbifolds and 4-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ String Islands

## authors:
#Gerardo_Aldazabal
#Eduardo_C_Andrés
#Anamaría_Font
#Elisa_I_Marieni
#Kumar_Narain
#Ida_G_Zadeh

## summary:
We formulate heterotic and type II $\mathbb T^6/\mathbb Z_n$ asymmetric orbifolds preserving all 16 right-moving supersymmetries and leading to 4-dimensional islands without massless moduli other than the dilaton. We establish the consistency of the orbifold conformal field theory, including the validity of operator interpretation. In the heterotic case we classify all islands based on the $Γ(22,6)$ lattice constructed via the Leech lattice. In the type II case we also provide a classification of allowed $Γ(6,6)$ lattices and orbifold generators. In both cases we further examine whether a particular orbifold admits an archipelago of different islands arising from inequivalent realizations of the orbifold action.
