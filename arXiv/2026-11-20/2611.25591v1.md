# A full-color four-point form factor

## authors:
#Song_He
#Xuhang_Jiang

## summary:
We determine the symbol and complete functions of the full-color two-loop four-point maximally helicity violating stress-tensor form factor in $\mathcal N=4$ super Yang-Mills theory, including $N^1$ double traces and $N^0$ single traces. Numerical unitarity and a master-integral bootstrap fix the result. A direct form-factor bootstrap reproduces the symbol. Permuting external legs in the planar 88-letter alphabet yields all 79 letters, while the $N^0$ terms contain four new nonclassical contributions. We also obtain the complete $N^0$ two-loop triple-collinear hard splitting functions.
