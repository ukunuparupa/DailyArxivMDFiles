# Scherk-Schwarz Compactifications: From Circles to Strings

## authors:
#Karim_Benakli

## summary:
This review is written with a simple question in mind: what does one need to know to enter the literature on Scherk-Schwarz compactifications? It is organized as a sequence of questions that a graduate student might naturally ask, together with the concepts, calculations, and formal tools needed to work through the literature. The discussion starts at an elementary field-theory level and gradually develops toward more advanced aspects of supergravity, orbifolds, quantum effects, and string theory. Phenomenological applications are deliberately left aside.
