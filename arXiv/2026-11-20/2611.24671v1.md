# A New GUT

## authors:
#Prateek_Agrawal
#Michael_Nee
#Mario_Reig

## summary:
We present a new class of 4-dimensional Grand Unified Theories where the gauge sectors of the standard model have a novel embedding in the unified group that does not factor through an $SU(5)$ subgroup. The smallest example has gauge algebra $\frak{su}(12)$ and an anomaly-free spectrum that produces the three standard model generations with no additional chiral matter. The embedding predicts the axion-photon anomaly ratio $E/N=2/3$ and the weak mixing angle $\sin^2θ_W=1/2$ at the unification scale. Gauge-mediated proton decay proceeds through flipped-$SU(5)$-like operators and the minimally charged smooth monopole carries two Dirac charge units. The global gauge group can be $SU(12)$ or $SU(12)/\mathbb{Z}_2$, with the former allowing color singlets of half-integer electric charge. We develop a GUT bootstrap to constrain $SU(n)$ embeddings with three generations and no additional chiral matter. For unified fermions in up to two-index representations, we prove that all new embeddings arise from the $SU(8m+4)$ family, including flavor unified models for $m \geq 2$.
