# Holographic Duals of $SL^{(q)}(2, \mathbb{R})$ Deformed CFTs

## authors:
#Somnath_Porey
#Baishali_Roy
#Rahul_Roy
#Bobby_Ezhuthachan

## summary:
We study the holographic bulk dual of 2D CFTs evolved by $sl^{(q)}(2,R)$-deformed Hamiltonians. Working in the Hamiltonian-adapted frame, in which the $sl^{(q)}(2,R)$-deformed effective Floquet Hamiltonian generates time translations, we construct the dual Bañados geometries for the three distinct classes of dynamics: the heating phase, the non-heating phase and the phase boundary. Specifically, we show that in the heating phase, the bulk patch dual to the region between two neighbouring fixed points has a time-dependent causal horizon and asymptotes to a static AdS Rindler geometry at late times, while the non-heating phase has no horizon. We discuss how the late-time behaviour of the entanglement entropy, particularly its linear growth in the heating phase, can be understood from the bulk in two different ways: in global AdS, from the portion of the geodesic behind the horizons, and in a quotient description, from an effective shockwave produced by a boosted conical defect. Finally, for ($q=1$), we complete the holographic analysis of Ref. [1] by reproducing the exact CFT expression of the OTOC from the bulk and explaining why it exhibits an exponential growth {\it only} when the operators lie on the side of the two fixed points.
