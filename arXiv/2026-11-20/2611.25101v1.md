# Calabi-Yau linear models with abelian bundles

## authors:
#Yann_Proto

## summary:
We introduce a construction of (0,2) gauged linear sigma models whose large radius phases realize Calabi-Yau sigma models with line bundles. We study a family of such models admitting Landau-Ginzburg orbifold phases, described as asymmetric orbifolds of (2,2) Gepner models and free left-moving fermions. These orbifolds extend the Gepner construction to a broader class of (0,2) SCFTs, and reveal unexpected features, including the emergence of free fermions in twisted sectors and substantial enhancements of the left-moving current algebra. The gauged linear sigma model connects the Calabi-Yau geometry to these rational points, where the volume modulus becomes charged under the enhanced gauge symmetry and freezes at the string scale.
