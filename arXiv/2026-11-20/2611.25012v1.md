# Coupling of TQFTs to QFTs and SymTFT interfaces from bordisms

## authors:
#Max_Hübner
#Ling_Lin
#Daniel_Robbins
#Subham_Roy

## summary:
M-theory local models may exhibit torsional cycles and other non-trivial topology which interpolate between quantum field theory sectors localized at singularities and the asymptotic regions of the local model. We show that such structures give rise, in standard decoupling limits, to additional topological sectors which can couple non-trivially to the localized QFT. From a symmetry topological field theory (SymTFT) perspective, the physical boundary conditions associated to such local models are modified. We give a construction of SymTFT interfaces from geometric bordisms interpolating between the local singularity and the asymptotic boundary. We apply this construction to the circle compactification of a frozen $D_5$ singularity, identifying a topological interface between the $\mathbb{Z}_4$ SymTFT of the full theory and the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ SymTFT of its local $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ gauge sector. We show that the resulting coupling between the gauge theory and an additional topological quantum field theory accounts for the apparent mismatch between electric and magnetic symmetries of the frozen theory.
