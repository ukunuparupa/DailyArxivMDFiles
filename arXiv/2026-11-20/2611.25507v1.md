# Pairwise fermionic mode correlations in electric and magnetic backgrounds: Landau-level structure and Schwinger production

## authors:
#Sahil_Chetan_Patnekar
#Sristi_Sanjivanee_Goswami
#Sanjay_Pant
#Shagun_Kaushal

## summary:
We investigate mutual information and logarithmic negativity in fermionic Schwinger pair production in parallel electric and magnetic fields. Using spin-dependent Bogoliubov transformations, we construct the in-vacuum and derive the particle--antiparticle correlations for selected Landau modes. We also consider an initially entangled two-particle state and evaluate the correlations for six bipartitions. The physical Landau-level structure is treated explicitly by distinguishing modes with the same oscillator index from the spin representatives of a common physical Landau level. For matched higher levels, the pairwise redistribution relations retain the electric-only form, with the magnetic-field dependence entering through the Landau-level pair-production probabilities. When the two particle groups occupy different physical levels, the cross particle--antiparticle correlations become unequal, and we derive analytic relations characterizing this asymmetry. We further obtain the local logarithmic negativity from the ordinary partial transpose in a fixed occupation-space basis. Finally, we extend the analysis to a Sauter-type electric field in a constant magnetic background and examine the dependence of the pairwise correlations on the field parameters and Landau-level structure.
