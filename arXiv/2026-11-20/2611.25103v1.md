# From Instantons to AdS Boundaries: Tracing the Geometric Path from Quantum Anomalies to Massive Gravity

## authors:
#Leonardo_Sanhueza

## summary:
This thesis studies how boundary data enter two gravitational observables: the Dirac index on gravitational instantons and conserved charges in three-dimensional New Massive Gravity. In Einstein gravity coupled to ModMax electrodynamics, we compute the index of charged Dirac fermions on Taub-NUT and Eguchi-Hanson instantons using the Atiyah-Patodi-Singer theorem, which includes the spectral asymmetry of the boundary Dirac operator. The Taub-NUT index agrees with the Maxwell result. For Eguchi-Hanson, the gauge-field contribution to the axial anomaly depends explicitly on the ModMax parameter when expressed in terms of the solution parameters. For asymptotically anti-de Sitter solutions of New Massive Gravity with Brown-Henneaux boundary conditions, a term linear in the extrinsic curvature reproduces the holographic stress tensor of the auxiliary-field formulation. At the degenerate point, relaxed boundary conditions admit an additional constrained Fefferman-Graham coefficient. A quadratic extrinsic counterterm cancels the residual power divergence. We derive the boundary Ward identity and show that the holographic and Noether-Wald charges agree for the solutions studied.
