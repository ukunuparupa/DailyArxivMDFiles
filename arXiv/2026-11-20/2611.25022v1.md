# Stringy Corrections from Gravity: Predictions for BFSS and ABJM

## authors:
#Aidan_Herderschee
#Victor_Ivo
#Henry_W_Lin
#Yifan_Wang

## summary:
Despite enormous progress in string theory, the leading stringy corrections to many holographic observables remain unknown. String/M-theory dualities nonetheless allow them to be determined from gravity alone. From the winding modes of one-loop 11d supergravity on a circle, we derive the leading tree-level stringy terms in the type IIA effective action for the metric, dilaton, and Ramond--Ramond one-form, in closed form as a finite supertrace over Lorentz generators. On the thermal D0-brane geometry, this gives the first correction to the low-temperature thermodynamics of the BFSS matrix model. Its coefficient differs from published lattice Monte Carlo estimates by $\approx 20\%$. With the four-form flux included, the same method reproduces the $ζ(3)k^{2}$ term of the ABJM free energy on the three-sphere obtained by localization.
