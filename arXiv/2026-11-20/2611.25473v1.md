# Upper Bounds on the Radion Mass in a Warped Extra Dimension

## authors:
#Leandro_Da_Rold
#Eugenio_Megias
#Mariano_Quiros
#Lindber_Salas

## summary:
We study absolute upper bounds on the mass ratio $R_{11}=m_{r_1}/m_{h_1}$ between the radion ($r_1$) and graviton ($h_1$) lightest modes in five dimensional warped models. We consider warped models with an admissible singular behavior in the infrared: i.e.~models with power-like ($\sim \barφ^{2n}$) and with exponential ($\sim e^{2ν\barφ}$) bulk potentials, where $\barφ$ is the stabilizing bulk field. We find a correlation between $R_{11}$ and the singular behavior of the Ricci scalar, which allows to establish an absolute upper bound on the former. For the power-like models, the maximum value of $R_{11}$ increases with $n$. In the limit $n\to\infty$, the power-like model reproduces the exponential model and reaches the same upper bound as the latter. For the exponential model, we derive analytically the bound $R_{11}<1$, with the largest value approached for the case $ν\to 1^+$ (where $ν=1$ corresponds to the linear dilaton model) and consistent with unitarity sum rules. For exponential models we also obtain the absolute upper bound on the second radion mode ratio $R_{21}=m_{r_2}/m_{h_1}\lesssim 1.374$. This value is reached for $ν=2$ when the IR brane approaches the singularity. For this configuration, we verify that the scalar contribution satisfies the unitarity sum rule when the complete radion Kaluza-Klein tower is included.
