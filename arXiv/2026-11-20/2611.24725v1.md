# An Atiyah--Hirzebruch to Adams map and the hastened Adams spectral sequence for supercohomology

## authors:
#Arun_Debray
#Weicheng_Ye
#Matthew_Yu

## summary:
We construct a map of filtered spectra inducing a map from the Atiyah--Hirzebruch spectral sequence (AHSS) to the Baker--Lazarev Adams spectral sequence of a $\mathit{ko}$-module $M$. Using this and a theorem of Bruner--Rognes, we construct a hastened Adams spectral sequence (HASS) for the Postnikov truncations of $\mathit{ko}$ and the connective Adams summand. Then, motivated by questions in quantum field theory, topological phases of matter, and tensor category theory, we apply the HASS to supercohomology, the cohomology theory $I_{\mathbb{C}^\times}(τ_{\le 2}\mathit{ko})$, and observe that in examples of interest it is easier to calculate with than either the AHSS or the usual Adams spectral sequence. We include a User's Guide to the HASS and several worked examples, as well as hastened Adams charts for many commonly encountered $\mathcal{A}(1)$-modules.
