# Worldsheet Effective Theory of Abelian-Higgs Cosmic Strings beyond Nambu--Goto

## authors:
#Sergio_Alameda-Calvo
#Josu_C_Aurrekoetxea
#Jose_J_Blanco-Pillado
#Alberto_García_Martín-Caro
#J_M_Queiruga

## summary:
Cosmic strings can support long-lived excitations confined to their cores, opening channels for energy exchange with the transverse Goldstone modes that describe the string's motion. We derive a covariant worldsheet theory of their coupled dynamics directly from the Abelian-Higgs model at critical coupling, with all interaction coefficients fixed by the microscopic theory. The resulting action, which we validate against numerical lattice field theory simulations, extends the Nambu--Goto description to include a massive scalar shape mode and a massive vector mode. Dualizing the vector reveals a geometric distinction: the shape scalar mode couples to intrinsic worldsheet curvature, while the pseudoscalar couples to normal bundle curvature. These interactions explain the distinct parametric instabilities observed in lattice simulations and predict how the relative polarization of colliding transverse waves controls the excitation of the two internal modes. Our results connect microscopic string structure to the nonlinear worldsheet dynamics, providing a predictive framework for cosmic strings beyond the Nambu--Goto approximation.
