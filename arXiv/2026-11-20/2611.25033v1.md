# Pure integral formula of spinfoam amplitude and curvature corrections in effective theory

## authors:
#Muxin_Han

## summary:
We develop a new analytic method for the semiclassical analysis of the Lorentzian EPRL spinfoam amplitude on a fixed triangulation. We derive a pure integral formula for the spinfoam amplitude that trades the discrete sum over internal spins for integrals over continuous variables. This reformulation is free of discrete sums and provides a well-behaved action that is well-adapted to the stationary phase approximation in the large-spin regime. Integrating out the non-geometric variables yields an effective action for the edge lengths of the Regge geometry. Applying the stationary phase approximation and expanding this effective action to second order in perturbations around a flat background, we obtain the classical Regge action at leading order, supplemented by a quadratic correction in the deficit angles $\varepsilon$. This curvature correction originates from two distinct sources: a holonomy correction resulting from the new action and the fluctuations of non-Regge areas. We show that the quadratic $\varepsilon$ correction appears at a higher order in the Barbero-Immirzi parameter $γ$ relative to the Regge action. Together with the large-spin expansion, this yields a two-parameter expansion for the spinfoam effective action, whose leading order is the quadratic Regge action. In the long wavelength regime, the latter approaches the action of a massless spin-2 graviton.
