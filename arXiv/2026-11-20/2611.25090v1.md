# Soft Factorisation of WQFT and the Spinning 4PM Magnusian

## authors:
#Riccardo_Gonzo
#Gustav_Mogull
#Benjamin_Sauer

## summary:
We derive the soft divergences of $\hat{N}=-i\log\hat{S}$ in the Worldline Quantum Field Theory (WQFT) formalism, focusing on poles due to single massive-massive (Coulomb) and massive-massless (tail) graviton exchanges. Our derivation is inspired by the Faddeev-Kulish procedure, switching the interaction picture to one with an infrared-finite $\hat{N}$-matrix. Soft divergences emerging from the worldline are correlators of Wilson lines. As confirmation of this structure, we demonstrate explicitly how divergences of inclusive observables including the momentum impulse $Δp_i^μ$ and spin kick $ΔS_i^μ$ cancel, and we reproduce the infrared divergence of the one-loop scattering waveform. Using the Magnus series representation of $\hat{N}$ and Murua coefficients, we then calculate the Magnusian $N=\langle0|\hat{N}|0\rangle$ at fourth post-Minkowskian (4PM) order, including linear-in-spin corrections. For aligned spins, $-\partial N/\partial L$ reproduces the full scattering angle through 3PM order; at 4PM order, $N$ develops a soft divergence as predicted by our soft factorisation. Using the Feynman $i0^+$ prescription, we also derive an infrared-finite 4PM Magnusian which compactly encodes existing results for the conservative momentum impulse and spin kick via nested iterations of Poisson brackets.
