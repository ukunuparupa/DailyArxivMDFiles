# Localization of a 3-form gauge field in a braneworld

## authors:
#Pratik_Chattopadhyay
#M_J_Neves

## summary:
The localization of a $3$-form gauge field is investigated in a extra dimension attached to an AdS metric in $4+1$ dimensions. We propose the study of a model for the scalar field and a $3$-form gauge field coupled to the gravitational field in $5D$. The spacetime is set by an AdS metric whose geometry introduces a warped function of the fifth spatial coordinate. Considering that the scalar field also depends only on the extra coordinate, we obtain the equation of motions for the set of fields of the model. Using the technique of variable separation in the $3$-rank gauge sector, we explore the localization of the dynamical in terms of the warped function. We also extend the initial model for a complex scalar field that couples minimally with the dual strength field tensor associated with the $3$-rank gauge field. Thus, the coupling constant has a fundamental role in the localization of new $p$-form gauge field on the brane domain.
