# Quiver-Invariant Triality between Brane Brick Models

## authors:
#Dongwook_Ghim
#Juno_Kwon
#Rak-Kyeong_Seong

## summary:
We study quiver-invariant triality between 2d (0,2) supersymmetric gauge theories described by brane brick models associated with toric Calabi-Yau 4-folds. The dual theories share the same gauge groups, field content and E-terms but have distinct J-terms. We present an explicit pair of such brane brick models and identify the sequence of triality mutations relating them. This sequence returns the quiver to its original form while transforming the J- and E-terms. We explain the relation between quiver-invariant triality and the orbifold reduction of quiver-invariant brane tilings.
