# Equation of state of hot and dense QCD at the physical point via the complex Langevin equation

## authors:
#Michael_Mandl
#Dénes_Sexty
#Daniel_Unterhuber

## summary:
This study presents the first results for the equation of state of 2+1+1-flavor QCD at the physical point, obtained using the complex Langevin equation. We calculate continuum-extrapolated thermodynamic quantities, such as the baryon density, pressure, the speed of sound and the adiabatic compressibility. The simulations are performed in the isospin-symmetric, strangeness- and charm-neutral setting above the crossover temperature and at unprecedentedly large baryon chemical potentials.
