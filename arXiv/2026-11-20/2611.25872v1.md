# Majorana dark matter from a thick-to-thin brane junction: Modal stability, real-mediator coannihilation, and a dark-radiation imprint

## authors:
#F_A_Brito

## summary:
We show that the localized spectrum of a $D=7$ thick-to-thin brane junction can host a thermal Majorana dark-matter candidate. The anisotropy of the wall widths isolates a light junction fermion, which a four-dimensional Majorana mass splits into two nearby states. The factorized transverse profiles impose a modal selection rule on all cubic couplings, and together with the relaxation of the softly deformed background this makes the lighter state stable without an imposed dark parity. Because the localized mediator is a real scalar, identical Majorana particles annihilate in the $s$ wave, and the relic coupling becomes nearly independent of the Majorana mass. The junction sector is a Standard-Model singlet whose light chiral mode is a source of dark radiation. Current CMB data restrict its decoupling temperature and imply $0.05\lesssim\dNeff\lesssim0.076$ for dark-matter masses between $10\GeV$ and $1.5\TeV$, a range accessible to next-generation CMB surveys.
