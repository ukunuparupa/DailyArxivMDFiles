# UV/IR scale dependence in a four-derivative scalar field theory

## authors:
#Bob_Holdom

## summary:
An analog of the standard renormalization procedure may be useful for four-derivative theories, and we illustrate it with the four-derivative scalar field theory in the $m\to0$ limit. The renormalization scale $μ$ appears in momentum-dependent logarithms associated with both UV and IR divergences. At one loop, the on-shell scattering amplitude satisfies an analog Callan--Symanzik equation involving the analog beta functions. These functions are determined from the combined UV and IR pole parts of the 1PI functions.
