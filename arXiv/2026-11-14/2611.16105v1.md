# Baxter $\mathbb{Q}$-operator and Separation of Variables for BC-type open $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$ spin chain

## authors:
#I_Kotikov
#P_Antonenko

## summary:
We construct two-parameter Baxter $\mathbb{Q}$-operators for the transfer matrices of noncompact open $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$ BC-type spin chains with general boundary interactions, treating both one-boundary and two-boundary models. The $\mathbb{Q}$-operators are defined as traces of general monodromy matrices over infinite-dimensional auxiliary spaces carrying principal series representations of $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$.  At specific values of parameters the two-parameter $\mathbb{Q}$-operators degenerate into simpler one-parameter Baxter operators. We prove that the general transfer matrices factorize into products of these one-parameter $Q$-operators. We provide both an algebraic derivation based on local operator identities and a diagrammatic proof using Feynman diagrammatic techniques.  Finally, we apply the quantum Separation of Variables (SoV) method to the spectral problem of the one-boundary transfer matrix. We derive the system of separated difference equations for the transfer matrix eigenfunctions and show that, under the assumption of kernel factorization, it reduces to a one-dimensional difference equation coinciding precisely with the Baxter equation for eigenvalues of appropriately normalized one-parameter Baxter $Q$-operator.
