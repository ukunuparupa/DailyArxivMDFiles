# Infinite spin superparticles

## authors:
#Eugenia_Boffo
#Martin_Cederwall

## summary:
We obtain infinite spin representations of the super-Poincaré algebra from a higher-derivative superparticle action on a super-Minkowski background in 3, 4, 6 and 10 dimensions. Super-twistor transforms are performed, and used to deduce first-quantised spectra.
