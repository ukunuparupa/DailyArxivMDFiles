# A Universal Law for Horizon Formation in Black Hole Mergers

## authors:
#Yu-Cun_Xie
#Vaishak_Prasad

## summary:
When two black holes merge, a common horizon appears around them as a single surface, which immediately splits into an outer and an inner one. We show that this event has a universal local form. Without any symmetry assumption, the two surfaces separate as $(t-t_*)^{1/2}$ along a single, everywhere positive mode and share a drift linear in $t-t_*$. Three numerical simulations, including an eccentric, precessing one, confirm the exponent to half a percent, and the reconstructed formation surface is marginally stable, as predicted.
