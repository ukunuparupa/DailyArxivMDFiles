# Entropy and charge transport limit Mpemba preparation in holography

## authors:
#Yu_Shi
#Sheng_Long

## summary:
We study Mpemba relaxation and its preparation cost in a holographic superconductor with full backreaction. At matched final energy and charge, boundary drives can reverse the ordering of the remaining horizon entropy after source removal. In separate numerical comparisons, some protocols reach a given tolerance earlier even when driving and waiting times are included. To examine the limits of preparation, we consider critical states that already carry a slow scalar field and exterior charge. Along the critical slow branch at fixed energy and charge, horizon displacement cancels the quadratic area response, leaving an entropy reserve of $O(\varepsilon^4)$ for an initial scalar amplitude $\varepsilon$. The same coefficient connects this reserve to the entropy advantage of the nearby superconducting equilibrium and the entropy weight of the slow tail on the normal side. This limited reserve constrains how quickly the existing field can be removed. For releases that suppress the leading horizon field and exterior charge, the preparation time grows at least as $\varepsilon^{-2}$ under horizon regularity and uniform radial control. Charge transport constrains the same covariant scalar flux that produces horizon entropy, raising the minimum flux cost and strengthening the time bound.
