# Discrete anomaly inflow from SymTFT of $U(1)$ global symmetry: the modified Villain resolution

## authors:
#Napat_Poovuttikul
#K_Sapmee

## summary:
We construct a lattice SymTFT for a $U(1)$ global symmetry with anomaly coefficient $k$ using the modified Villain formulation, in which the spin structure required for odd $k$ is realised explicitly by lattice Majorana fermions. We demonstrate that the same Majorana fermions sit at the junctions of the symmetry lines, exactly where the fusion of two $U(1)$ elements wraps around the circle. From the lattice algebra of lines and junctions we derive the $R$- and $F$-symbols in the bulk and on the gapped boundary, and show that for odd $k$ the boundary $F$-symbol obeys the Gu-Wen relation for the $U(1)$ symmetry defect, without passing through a bosonic shadow. Restricting to $\mathbb{Z}_2\subset U(1)$, we reproduce the mod-4 anomalous phase from a mapping torus and the fermionic stacking rule. As a simple application of this SymTFT, we also determine which subgroups $\mathbb{Z}_d\subset U(1)$ can be gauged and the symmetry of the resulting theories. This is a first step towards deriving, from the anomaly inflow of a continuous symmetry, the junction data of global anomalies beyond group cohomology.
