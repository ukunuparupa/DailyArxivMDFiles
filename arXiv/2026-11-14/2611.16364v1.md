# Thermodynamics within and across AdS Gauss--Bonnet holographic CFTs

## authors:
#Limin_Zeng

## summary:
We study thermal competition within individual holographic conformal field theories (CFTs) and across theories with different anomaly coefficients in five-dimensional AdS Gauss--Bonnet gravity. We derive the work term conjugate to the dimensionless coupling from the boundary first law and use a common boundary clock to compare charged spherical saddles at fixed external source. Varying this source drives a first-order switch between the CFTs at the endpoints of the chosen coupling interval. In the unit-lapse cylinder clock with the zero-type-D vacuum energy, strict concavity at fixed radius reduces the global comparison to these two endpoints. The preferred coupling jumps across the coexistence line, whose slope is related to the entropy difference by a generalized Clapeyron relation. Where the Einstein endpoint itself undergoes small--large coexistence, a third saddle joins the competition and produces a kink in the cross-theory boundary. A compensating change of the external control preserves saddle ordering under finite energy shifts. Within fixed CFTs, we determine the neutral Hawking--Page line and the charged small--large black hole coexistence surface. The critical charge tends to zero at a finite coupling. The entropy gap closes with exponent one half, and the heat capacity diverges with exponent two thirds along a temperature sweep at the critical charge.
