# Proton Spin Fluctuation Decomposition

## authors:
#Tomoya_Uji

## summary:
We formulate a fluctuation decomposition of proton spin in terms of the variances and covariances of the spin and orbital angular momentum of quarks and gluons. We derive their representations in terms of single- and double-parton distributions. The definite proton spin imposes sum rules requiring the variances of its spin and orbital contributions to be compensated by their covariances. This framework reveals correlations and fluctuations that are invisible to the conventional proton spin decomposition.
