# Probing the Molecular Nature of $D_{sJ}(2860)$ and $D_{s3}(2860)$ through Their Decays

## authors:
#Qing_Lu
#Dan_Dan_Feng
#Cai_Cheng
#Yin_Huang

## summary:
In this work, we investigate the molecular interpretations of $D_{sJ}(2860)$ and $D_{s3}(2860)$ by studying their strong decays within the effective Lagrangian approach. We consider two possible configurations for $D_{sJ}(2860)$: an $S$-wave $D_1K$ molecule and a $P$-wave $D^*K^*$ molecule, while treating $D_{s3}(2860)$ as a $P$-wave $D^*K^*$ molecule. By fitting the LHCb-measured angular distribution and decay widths in $B_s^0\to\bar{D}^0K^-π^+$, we determine the molecular coupling constants and cutoff parameter $Λ$. Using these parameters, we calculate the total decay widths and the ratios $R$ of the partial widths into the $D^*K$ and $DK$ channels. For $D_{sJ}(2860)$, the $S$-wave $D_1K$ assignment provides a slightly better description of the measured total width than the $P$-wave $D^*K^*$ assignment. However, the combined $D_1K$ and $D^*K^*$ assignments for $D_{sJ}(2860)$ and $D_{s3}(2860)$, respectively, are disfavored by the predicted $R_{D_{s3}(2860)}$, which exceeds the experimental upper limit. In contrast, assigning both states as $P$-wave $D^*K^*$ molecules yields good agreement with the measured $R_{D_{s3}(2860)}$ and predicts $R_{D_{sJ}(2860)}\simeq 1.26$--$1.29$. These results favor the $P$-wave $D^*K^*$ molecular interpretation for both states, which can be further tested through future measurements of $R_{D_{sJ}(2860)}$.
