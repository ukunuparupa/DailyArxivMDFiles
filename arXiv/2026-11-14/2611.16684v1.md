# Is Spin Slow in Hot QCD?

## authors:
#Zi-Hao_Liu
#Mei_Huang

## summary:
Promoting spin to a hydrodynamic variable requires slow relaxation, as mass-suppressed helicity flips in hot QCD suggest. Using the full leading-order collision operator, we show instead that light-quark spin damps at the transport rate $g^4T\ln(1/g)$, nearly as fast as vector currents. Since spatial spin locks to momentum, helicity-conserving soft, small-angle scatterings readily rotate it. Damping involves multiple kinetic modes beyond a single relaxation time, global rotation adds only reversible precession, and stronger coupling further disfavors a slow spin mode in quark-gluon plasma.
