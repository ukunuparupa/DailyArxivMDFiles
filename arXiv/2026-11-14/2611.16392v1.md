# Poles Are Not Enough: Zeros of Dyson-Schwinger Gluon Propagators in Yang-Mills Thermodynamics

## authors:
#Marco_Frasca
#Anish_Ghoshal
#Stefan_Groote

## summary:
A normalized two-point function with several poles does not, on its own, fix the source-free thermal partition function. For the Gaussian measure of the original gluon field, a positive-residue rational propagator contributes determinants from its poles and from zeros interlaced between them. The zero factors distinguish this completion from a gas of independent pole fields and restore two ultraviolet polarizations per adjoint eigenvalue at every finite truncation. We apply the construction to a background-covariant completion of the elliptic Dyson-Schwinger gluon kernel. The first-order SU(3) transition is already present with a single pole, and the saturated six-pole result shifts $T_c/M_g$ by only $1.48\%$, where $T_c$ is the coexistence temperature and $M_g$ is the lowest pole mass. At six poles, the zero determinants raise $T_c/M_g$ by $2.31\%$ and lower $L/T_c^4$ by $12.4\%$, where $L$ is the latent heat and each prescription uses its own $T_c$. At fixed $M_g$ the dimensional latent heat decreases by $4.0\%$. The fixed-holonomy free-energy difference gives an ultraviolet test independent of the additive pressure normalization. At fixed pole number $P$, its asymptotic value agrees with the two-polarization Weiss result when the zeros are retained and is $(3P-1)/2$ times as large in the poles-only prescription.
