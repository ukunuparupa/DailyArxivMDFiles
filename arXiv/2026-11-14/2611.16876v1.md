# Numerical tests of chiral fermion weights with $η$-invariant phases in the 3450 model

## authors:
#Tatsuhiro_Misumi

## summary:
We test numerically a recently proposed prescription for coupling continuum chiral fermions to interpolated lattice gauge fields, in which the fermion weight has a Pauli-Villars (PV) regularized magnitude and a phase given by an Atiyah-Patodi-Singer (APS) $η$-invariant in one higher dimension. We use the anomaly-free two-dimensional $U(1)$ theory with left-handed charges $3,4$ and right-handed charges $5,0$, whose torus determinant is known exactly. At zero magnetic flux, with gauge fields interpolated from lattice links, the Polyakov loops evaluated on a $16\times16$ grid of flat connections agree with the exact values within $0.04\%$, whereas omitting the APS phase substantially reduces them to about $3\%$ of the exact result. The vacuum-polarization response yields an induced mass $m/e=2.82089$, close to $5/\sqrtπ=2.82095$. At unit flux, where zero modes make the determinant vanish, we calculate a twelve-point correlator on a cylinder with the generalized APS boundary condition. For three smooth backgrounds its magnitude agrees with the exact result within $0.8\%$. Without the $η$-invariant its phase deviates by $1.8$-$3.0$ mrad, and with it by $0.2$-$0.5$ mrad. The phase of each charged fermion changes by $15$-$46$ mrad when the gauge field is extended differently into the third dimension, but the change cancels to below $0.2$ mrad in the anomaly-free combination.
