# Holographic viscous transport in scalar-coupled Gauss-Bonnet gravity

## authors:
#Tao_Ma
#Song_He
#Li_Li

## summary:
We test horizon formulas for the shear and bulk viscosities of a holographic plasma against viscosities extracted directly from retarded stress-tensor correlators. We work in a five-dimensional Einstein-dilaton-Gauss-Bonnet model calibrated to the lattice QCD equation of state at zero baryon density. UV-finite renormalized correlators are obtained by including the required curvature and scalar counterterms, and the fluctuation equations are solved while retaining the full dependence on the Gauss-Bonnet coupling $α$, without using a horizon conservation law or an entropy-production formula. For constant coupling, the horizon conserved-current calculation reproduces the boundary Kubo results in both channels. For the scalar-dependent coupling studied here, both methods find a minimum in the shear-viscosity-to-entropy-density ratio $η/s$. In the bulk channel, the conserved-current calculation truncated at first order in $α$ yields a lower peak height in $ζ/s$, with the peak temperature nearly unchanged relative to the boundary result. For both couplings, the unmodified Eling-Oz (EO) formula, $ζ/s=(4π)^{-1}(\partialφ_h/\partial\ln s)^2$, reproduces the boundary result for $ζ/s$, where $φ_h$ is the horizon value of the scalar. The prefactor $1/(4π)$ is thus retained even when the Gauss-Bonnet interaction shifts $η/s$ from this value. These results provide a direct boundary benchmark for horizon prescriptions and support the unmodified EO formula beyond constant coupling.
