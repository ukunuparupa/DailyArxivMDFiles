# Extension of Poincare superalgebra via hidden symmetries

## authors:
#Ümit_Ertem

## summary:
Isometries of a manifold and special types of spinors called Killing spinors which are related to Killing vector fields constitute a symmetry superalgebra structure corresponding to Poincare superalgebra. The antisymmetric generalization of Killing vector fields to higher degree forms which are called Killing-Yano forms are considered. The odd part of the Poincare superalgebra is extended from the space of Killing vector fields and their rotational representation to the space of special odd Killing-Yano forms and special even closed conformal Killing-Yano forms. The even part is again corresponds to the space of Killing spinors. By defining the relevant brackets, we show that hidden symmetries and Killing spinors together define an extension of the Poincare superalgebra.
