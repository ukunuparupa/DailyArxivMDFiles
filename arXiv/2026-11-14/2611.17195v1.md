# Spinorial geometry of Bismut--Hermitian--Einstein manifolds

## authors:
#Beatrice_Brienza
#Matteo_Kevin_Crisafio
#Anna_Fino
#Dario_Martelli

## summary:
We present a pair of spinorial equations, generalizing the internal Killing spinor equations of Type I supergravity, and show that they characterize several classes of Bismut-Hermitian-Einstein structures on six-dimensional Riemannian spin manifolds. We show that in this framework the local reduction to a four-dimensional transverse Hermitian geometry is provided by two canonical, commuting, Bismut-parallel Killing vector fields arising as bilinears in the Killing spinors, without assuming compactness. In one class, the transverse geometry is conformally Kahler and both compact and non-compact solutions exist. We also discuss variants that do not admit compact solutions, one of which has hyper-Hermitian transverse geometry. We discuss the relation of these structures to the Hull-Strominger system with closed three-form and to five-dimensional Gauntlett-Kim geometries and provide explicit embeddings in Type I, Type IIB and massive Type IIA supergravity, as well as in generalized supergravity. We illustrate our results with a number of examples, both compact and non-compact.
