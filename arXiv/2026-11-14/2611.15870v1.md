# Cosmological Dressing Rules for Massive Scalars

## authors:
#Jonathan_Gräfe
#Matthias_Nowinski
#Ivo_Sachs
#Siva_Pawan_Venna

## summary:
The Schwinger-Keldysh formulation of de Sitter correlation functions comes in different guises including harmonic analysis and dressed flat space correlators. We observe that these are equivalent spectral representations of the same Sturm-Liouville problem. Starting from the latter we derive dressing rules for scalar fields of arbitrary mass with auxiliary propagators in the form of Euler integrals that are accessible to flat space diagrammatic tools.
