# Triple-Massive-Exchange Bispectrum of Quasi-Single-Field Inflation

## authors:
#Xingang_Chen
#Zhong-Zhi_Xianyu

## summary:
The triple-exchange bispectrum of the scalar quasi-single-field inflation model has resisted analytic evaluation across its full parameter space since its advent, despite rapid progress in cosmological collider physics. In this Letter, we solve it for general mass and mixing strength as a convergent double series, by applying the Mellin-Barnes representation directly to the mixed propagator between the massless and massive modes. The result is exact, non-perturbative, and physically transparent; the method is broadly applicable.
