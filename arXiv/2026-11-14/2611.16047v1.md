# Anti-periodic Gravitino and Dirac Effective Operators

## authors:
#Karim_Benakli

## summary:
Introducing a preserved $U(1)_R$ symmetry forbids gravitini Majorana masses while allowing a Dirac mass. We investigate how a Dirac mass for gravitini can be described by an effective operator in $N=1$ superspace, in analogy with the well-known Dirac gaugino mass. We find that the corresponding spin-$3/2$ bilinear operator can be expressed in terms of gauge-invariant gravitino curvature superfields coupled through a nonlocal kernel involving an inverse d'Alembertian. We study this construction in minimal five-dimensional supergravity compactified on $S^1/\mathbb Z_2$. The gravitino satisfies anti-periodic boundary conditions induced by a Scherk-Schwarz twist. The physical Kaluza-Klein Dirac masses then appear as poles in the two-point functions of the curvature superfields associated with sources localized at the endpoints. Separation of physical and gauge modes relates these propagators to a quadratic fermionic kernel represented by a $16\times16$ matrix. Its elements match the fermionic components of two invariants written in $N=1$ superspace, including their spinor contractions and normalization. This matching is carried out on a fixed background with a nonzero radion auxiliary field.
