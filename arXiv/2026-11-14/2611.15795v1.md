# How to find regular black-hole islands in the swamp

## authors:
#Astrid_Eichhorn
#Pedro_G_S_Fernandes
#Verena_Koeder

## summary:
An infinite tower of higher-curvature corrections is expected to lead to regular black-hole solutions. We assume that this regular behavior does not abruptly arise, but gradually builds up with each successive order of the effective field theory of gravity. In addition, we assume that the effective field theory comes with a single scale of singularity-resolving new physics. We translate this assumption into a monotonicity requirement for deviations of the metric functions from a Schwarzschild black hole. Under these assumptions, conditions can be formulated which trace the increase of ``less singular'' black-hole behavior on and outside of the black-hole horizon within the regime of validity of the effective field theory. These conditions constrain the effective field theory coefficients. To exemplify this construction, we motivate five specific conditions and find a corresponding island of black hole regularity conditions at order eight in the effective field theory. We also explore whether string theory as well as asymptotically safe gravity lie on this island.
