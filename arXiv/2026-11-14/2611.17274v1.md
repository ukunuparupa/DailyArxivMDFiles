# A precision bound for gravitational transseries

## authors:
#Ji_Hoon_Lee

## summary:
We propose that the large $N$ transseries of a holographic observable above the black hole threshold admits a precision bound, a limit on how precisely the transseries can determine the observable at finite $N$, set by the data of its D-brane instanton sectors. In doing so, we develop methods in large $N$ resurgence, based on the work of Écalle, that capture the $e^{-N^2}$ asymptotics of gravitational saddles beyond the $e^{-N}$ instantons. Under an assumption on the growth of the D-brane instanton sums that we expect in the black hole phase, we find an analytic obstruction to the resummation of the large $N$ transseries. This obstruction sets the precision bound. As our main example and consistency check, we study the $\frac{1}{16}$-BPS index of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang-Mills and its black hole. We derive relations between the D-brane instanton data on thermal AdS and on the black hole, in the form of a duality that exchanges the numbers of D-brane instantons on the two backgrounds. In the index, these relations reproduce the actions of D3-branes wrapped on the black hole. We conclude with our expectations for generic situations and their relation to the double-cone wormhole.
