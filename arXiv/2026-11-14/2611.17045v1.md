# De Sitter Gravity and Emergent Time from Fusion Matrix Product States and SYK

## authors:
#Shinsei_Ryu
#Yu-ki_Suzuki

## summary:
We propose a quantum-mechanical model dual to two-dimensional de Sitter (dS) gravity. It is a quantum channel, defined by a matrix product state that fuses one spin at each step, and is related to a limit of the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev model without chord crossings. Its simplest part is a random walk with an emergent $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ symmetry, which we identify with the symmetry of the future boundary of dS. It selects a time variable in which the walk obeys the Schwarzian action and yields the disk amplitude of dS Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity. As the simplest case, we consider free massless scalar fields in the fixed background of dS$_2$. We find the agreement of the two-point function in the Bunch-Davies vacuum and the matching of symmetry. Our construction provides a concrete model for the dS/CFT correspondence for two-dimensional de Sitter gravity.
