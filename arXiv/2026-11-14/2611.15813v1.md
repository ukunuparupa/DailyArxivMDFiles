# Integrable Singularity Black Holes without Inner Horizons in Asymptotically Safe Inspired Gravity and NFW Dark Matter Effects

## authors:
#Milko_Estrada

## summary:
We investigate an alternative ultraviolet (UV) structure for Renormalization Group improved (RG) black holes within a phenomenological framework inspired by asymptotically safe gravity. In several regular RG constructions, regularity at the origin is associated with a dS core and an additional inner horizon, raising issues of spacetime predictability and possible mass inflation instabilities. Motivated by this problem, we relate the RG scale to the radially weighted effective quantum gravitational source entering the volume integrand of the quantum corrected Ricci scalar. This prescription yields a Bessel type running Newton coupling with $G_I(r)\sim r$ in the UV, rather than the usual $G(r)\sim r^3$ behavior, and supports an integrable central singularity. The inner branch is matched through the Israel Darmois conditions to an exterior RG solution with $G_{II}\to G_0$ in the infrared, where $G_0$ is the Newton constant. Within the numerical range investigated, the resulting geometry has a single event horizon and no additional inner horizon. We then incorporate an NFW dark matter distribution coupled to the same running coupling. Since $ρ_{\rm NFW}\sim r^{-1}$ while $G_I(r)\sim r$ near the origin, the effective matter contribution entering the field equations remains finite, producing a cored effective behavior without modifying the NFW profile by hand. The gravitational decoupling algorithm keeps the quantum gravitational and dark matter sectors separately conserved within the same geometry. For the configurations considered, the NFW sector shifts the event horizon while preserving the single horizon structure. Thus, the same running coupling governs both the integrable UV black hole geometry and the softening of the central gravitational response of the NFW source.
