# Toward the abelian category of positive Lagrangians

## authors:
#Asaf_Kapota
#Jake_P_Solomon

## summary:
We introduce a geometric construction whose terminal configuration determines the kernel and cokernel of a morphism between positive Lagrangian branes in the derived Fukaya category of a Calabi-Yau manifold. Thus, if the construction always terminates, the full subcategory of positive Lagrangian branes is abelian, as conjectured by Joyce. We show that this abelian category admits Harder-Narasimhan filtrations with respect to the central charge defined by the holomorphic volume form. Under appropriate split-generation assumptions, we construct a Bridgeland stability condition whose stable objects are isomorphic to special Lagrangian branes, as predicted by the Thomas-Yau conjecture.  The geometric construction is based on a one-parameter family of special Lagrangian submanifolds with boundary. One part of the boundary is contained in the Lagrangian underlying the domain of the morphism and the other in the Lagrangian underlying the codomain. Surgeries occur as the family encounters singularities or obstruction walls. We prove that the construction always terminates for the two-torus and provide higher dimensional examples.
