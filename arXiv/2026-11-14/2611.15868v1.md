# Cosmological correlators and kinematic flow on complex Hartle-Hawking saddles

## authors:
#Vikramaditya_Mondal
#Shinji_Mukohyama

## summary:
The Lorentzian gravitational path integral over homogeneous and isotropic metrics in a closed universe with positive cosmological constant is dominated by complex saddle points, such as the Hartle-Hawking saddles. We ask how to compute cosmological correlators on such complex backgrounds, and how they respond to the shifts of the kinematic variables---the kinematic flow---without tracking the bulk time evolution explicitly. We, specifically, study a conformally coupled scalar test field with a cubic interaction around these saddles. We find that the wave function coefficients can be written as discrete spectral sums with the summands containing the corresponding coefficients in the Einstein static universe, which are obtainable in analogy with the polytopes program for flat spacetime. Whereas the wave function coefficients of the flat FLRW case are given by integrals over shifted kinematics, the discrete spectrum on the three-sphere for Hartle-Hawking saddles produces sums. We identify the coefficients, which depend on the final hypersurface through a phase fixed by its size, with Srivastava-Daoust multi-variable hypergeometric functions. They satisfy recursion relations for any number of sites, under which the sums collapse into rational functions. For the one- and two-site Feynman-Witten tree diagrams, we derive difference equations in the kinematic variables which close on a finite set of sums, giving a discrete kinematic flow. The transformation matrices are upper triangular with rational elements, and satisfy a consistency condition which makes the flow path independent. We then obtain the two-, three- and four-point cosmological correlators. At the late-time conformal boundary, we derive an explicit difference equation for the four-point function.
