# Multiparticle residues of open-string tachyon amplitudes from DDF sewing

## authors:
#Dripto_Biswas
#Qu_Cao

## summary:
We compute the maximal simultaneous residues of color-ordered tachyon disk amplitudes in the critical open bosonic string by summing all physical intermediate states. Sewing cubic Del Giudice--Di Vecchia--Fubini (DDF) vertices reduces this sum to Gaussian contractions for any planar cubic tree, with the internal levels specified independently. Each internal level fixes an exact finite mode cutoff. The residue then follows by coefficient extraction in the sewing variables from a determinant prefactor times the exponential of a quadratic form in the sources. With consistent sewing conventions, the local-coordinate scales cancel between the vertices and the sewing metric. At four points, cubic local maps and a one-boson stress-tensor Ward identity give an all-order resummation that recovers the Veneziano pole coefficients. The same construction gives explicit matrices for the five-point double residue and the six-point comb and snowflake, with low-level checks against independent Koba--Nielsen expansions. The determinant is the vacuum sewing factor of the transverse bosons. We formulate the operator, coordinate-map and measure identities needed to relate this construction to Grunsky operators and the full higher-point Koba--Nielsen top forms.
