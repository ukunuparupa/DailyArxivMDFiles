# Love numbers for the Myers-Perry geometry

## authors:
#Rhucha_Deshpande
#Oleg_Lunin

## summary:
We analyze Love numbers for scalar fields in the Myers-Perry geometry in arbitrary dimensions. The tidal responses of black holes are specified by two constants which encode a potential logarithmic running and the Love number. We argue that the parameter specifying the running is not sensitive to the structure of the horizon, and we explicitly compute this parameter for Myers-Perry black holes in arbitrary dimensions by utilizing the asymptotic expansion of the wave equation. The Love numbers rely on the details of the boundary conditions at the horizon, and we compute them using several distinct approximations.
