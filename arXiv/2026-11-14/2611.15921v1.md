# Exact Infrared Triangle in Gravity with Long-Range Interactions

## authors:
#Sangmin_Choi
#Ameya_Kadhe
#Prahar_Mitra
#Andrea_Puhm

## summary:
Einstein gravity coupled to massless matter exhibits universality in the infrared. In the presence of long-range interactions we show that a novel superrotation charge conservation is equivalent to the massless limit of the classical logarithmic soft graviton theorem, and we propose a formula for the corresponding tail to the displacement memory. These results, which extend earlier work on gravity coupled to massive matter in a qualitatively different way, are exact in the gravitational coupling and reveal that long-range gravitational dressings resum. We also compute higher-order corrections to the known superrotation charge which are exact in the gravitational coupling and would correspond to a 1-loop exact correction to the Cachazo-Strominger subleading soft graviton theorem.
