# Harmonic superspace and Kaluza-Klein reduction

## authors:
#Nikita_Zaigraev

## summary:
We extend the Kaluza-Klein reduction procedure to the framework of harmonic superspace and apply it to the construction of off-shell superfield formulations of supersymmetric gauge multiplets. The reduction is implemented by a deformation of the harmonic derivatives by mass terms, while ensuring that manifest off-shell supersymmetry and Grassmann analyticity are preserved at all stages. As a first application, we reduce the free \(5D,\mathcal{N}=1\) Maxwell theory and obtain an off-shell superfield description of the BPS \(4D,\mathcal{N}=2\) massive vector supermultiplet. The resulting formulation naturally incorporates an analytic Stückelberg-type gauge symmetry. We then reduce linearized \(5D,\mathcal{N}=1\) Poincaré supergravity and derive the unconstrained harmonic-superspace action for the BPS \(4D,\mathcal{N}=2\) massive supergravity multiplet. The technical simplicity of the procedure stems from the structure of gauge theories in harmonic superspace, where the gauge symmetry is naturally encoded in analytic prepotentials and harmonic derivatives. The approach presented in this paper opens a direct route to gauge-invariant off-shell superfield formulations of massive higher-spin $4D,\mathcal{N}=2$ BPS multiplets. Furthermore, the resulting theories can be reformulated in $4D,\mathcal{N}=1$ superspace, opening the way to gauge-invariant off-shell descriptions of $4D,\mathcal{N}=1$ massive higher-spin theories.
