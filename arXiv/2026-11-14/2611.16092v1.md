# Critical behavior of the bosonized Schwinger model with uniform matrix product states

## authors:
#Takuya_Okuda

## summary:
Quantum electrodynamics in two spacetime dimensions, also known as the Schwinger model, admits a dual formulation in terms of a non-compact boson field. Using a Hamiltonian lattice discretization of the bosonized model, we compute the critical mass of the continuum Schwinger model at $θ=π$. We numerically obtain the ground state on an infinite chain by the variational uniform matrix product state (VUMPS) algorithm, preserving charge conjugation exactly. As a truncated basis for the local Hilbert space, we employ the oscillator basis that diagonalizes the one-site reduced density matrix of the free limit. We constrain the fitting functions for the continuum extrapolation using the Symanzik effective theory for lattice corrections. Our result for the critical mass, $(m/g)_c = 0.333\,555(5)$, agrees with recent staggered-fermion determinations at comparable precision.
