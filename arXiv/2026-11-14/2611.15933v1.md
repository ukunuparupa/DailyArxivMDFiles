# Imaging the two-sided black hole interior with four-point correlators

## authors:
#Joydeep_Chakravarty

## summary:
We propose a boundary four-point function smeared with wavepackets, which probes local physics behind the horizon of a two-sided AdS black hole. At high frequencies, boundary wavepackets aimed at an interior bulk point travel along geodesics and scatter locally about the bulk point, factorizing the correlator into a flat-space like scattering amplitude. To obtain this configuration, we utilize a non-trivial time ordering of operators, and also use one state-dependent operator insertion moved halfway across the thermal circle. An interesting property of this observable is that it also probes features of nontrivial states: null shocks and matter shells behind the horizon show up as jumps in the observed signal as the bulk point crosses them, thereby imaging the black hole interior.
