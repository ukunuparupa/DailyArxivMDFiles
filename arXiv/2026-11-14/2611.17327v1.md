# Decoupling Dynamics: A Tale of Two Limits

## authors:
#Andrea_Antinucci
#Oscar_Lewis

## summary:
The symmetries of a $d$-dimensional QFT are known to be captured by a topological theory in one dimension higher, the Symmetry Topological Field Theory (SymTFT), which is invariant under gauging finite symmetries of the QFT, but not under dynamical gauging of continuous symmetries. We show that the distinct SymTFTs of a $d$-dimensional QFT before and after dynamical gauging of a continuous Abelian symmetry arise as different topological limits of the same dynamical holographic gauge theory in $(d+1)$-dimensions. Gauging changes the boundary condition of this dynamical theory and thereby selects a different topological limit, explaining why the associated SymTFT changes. We give a fully quantum definition of these SymTFTs at the level of Hilbert spaces and operators by combining suitable limits of the bulk holographic theory with photon truncation. This construction clarifies how topological symmetry defects, charges, and anomalies survive while the dynamical information carried by currents is discarded. We study non-anomalous and anomalous examples, providing a complete quantum analysis for $(2+1)$-dimensional bulks. For a four-dimensional boundary theory with a mixed axial-vector anomaly, we recover the SymTFT of the non-invertible chiral symmetry that remains after gauging the vector symmetry.
