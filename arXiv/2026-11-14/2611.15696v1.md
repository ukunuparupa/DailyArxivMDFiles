# Critical L-values and genus-zero Gromov--Witten invariants

## authors:
#Wu-yen_Chuang
#Yi-Jing_Tseng

## summary:
We establish a period comparison for a two-dimensional rational Hodge summand of type $(3,0)+(0,3)$ in a smooth projective threefold over $\mathbb Q$ with $h^{3,0}=1$. Given an algebraic projector onto the summand and a correspondence whose action on the motive of a rational weight-four newform is nonzero after projection to the summand, restriction to the holomorphic de Rham lines identifies motivic morphisms with maps between these lines. Fixing rational generators of the holomorphic de Rham lines determines a unique isomorphism of homological motives. Its Betti realization then yields an exact relation between geometric periods and two critical modular $L$-values. For the Fermat sextic, we construct the correspondence over $\mathbb Q$ and compute its Betti and de Rham actions, determining the scale from algebraic traces and cup-product pairings. Critical-value evaluations and, under mirror comparison and explicit continuation hypotheses, a continued genus-zero Gromov--Witten partition identity follow as corollaries.
