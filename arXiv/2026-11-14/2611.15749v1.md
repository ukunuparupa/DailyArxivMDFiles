# Quantum and classical thermal equilibrium for probabilistic classical fields

## authors:
#C_Wetterich

## summary:
The time evolution of the probability distribution for classical fields can be described by the quantum formalism. For free classical fields the free quantum field theory arises as a subsystem. Different probability distributions for classical fields correspond to the classical thermal equilibrium with the Rayleigh-Jeans divergence or the quantum thermal equilibrium with the Planck spectrum. The selection of the Rayleigh-Jeans or Planck spectrum is not a question of fundamental concepts. It becomes an issue of dynamical selection once interactions are present. We compare the momentum dependence of the classical energy and field correlations for the classical and quantum equilibrium. This permits to decide which equilibrium is selected as a result of asymptotic dynamics by numerical simulations of the probabilistic classical fields including interactions. For free probabilistic classical fields we construct a general ``classical-quantum map'' between the classical probability distribution and the density matrix of the quantum field theory.
