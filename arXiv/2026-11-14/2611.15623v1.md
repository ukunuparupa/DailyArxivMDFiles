# Schwinger-Unruh Interplay and Mode-Resolved Entanglement of Charged Scalar Fields in Rindler Spacetime

## authors:
#Shagun_Kaushal
#Himanshu_Parihar

## summary:
We study pairwise and cross-horizon correlations of a charged scalar field in Rindler spacetime in the presence of parallel electric and magnetic fields. The transverse magnetic field produces Landau levels, while the longitudinal mode problem follows the charged-scalar Rindler construction with the effective mass $M_n^2=m^2+(2n+1)|qB|$. For a specified global vacuum in the negative-frequency sector, we obtain the outgoing four-mode Gaussian state and calculate logarithmic negativity and quantum mutual information for selected mode pairs. We find that the cross-wedge antiparticle-particle pair is entangled only when $q_2<\tanh r_U$. It can therefore become separable although its mutual information remains nonzero. The intra-wedge particle-antiparticle pair remains entangled for nonzero Schwinger squeezing. Using the Gaussian Schmidt decomposition, we show that the entanglement across the complete right--left wedge partition is carried by a pair of collective modes, even when the selected outgoing cross-wedge pair becomes separable.
