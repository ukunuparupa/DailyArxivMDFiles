# A constructive computer-assisted existence proof for a Calabi--Yau metric

## authors:
#Michael_R_Douglas
#Javier_Gómez-Serrano
#Fabian_Lehmann
#Daniel_Platt
#Yidi_Qi

## summary:
We give a rigorous, constructive proof that there exists a Ricci-flat Kähler metric on the Fermat quartic hypersurface, independently of Yau's famous theorem, along with an explicit and accurate analytic approximation to the metric. The proof combines numerical techniques to obtain the approximately Ricci flat metric, a fixed point theorem to prove existence, and verified numerics to get the bounds used by the fixed point theorem. The method is general and we discuss the computational issues in applying it to other manifolds.
