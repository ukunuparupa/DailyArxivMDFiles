# Gauge Theory Bootstrap with varying $N_c$

## authors:
#Rafael_Cordoba
#Yifei_He
#Martin_Kruczenski
#Derek_Ping
#Jignesh_Mohanty
#Emil_Alegria

## summary:
We use the Gauge Theory Bootstrap to study asymptotically free gauge theories with a varying number of colors $N_c$. The parameter $N_c$ enters the computation through the sum rules and asymptotic form factors, allowing us to tune this microscopic parameter directly and examine the corresponding low-energy strongly coupled dynamics. For $3\le N_c\le 12$, we extract the scattering lengths, effective ranges, and low-energy constants $\bar{l}_i$, and study their $N_c$ dependence. We also obtain the $σ$, $ρ$, and $f_2$ resonances for the same range of $N_c$. The $ρ$ and $f_2$ masses depend only weakly on $N_c$, and their widths decrease as $N_c$ increases, consistent with the $1/N_c$ scaling expected for conventional $q\bar{q}$ mesons. In contrast, the $σ$ meson mass and width both increase with $N_c$, in agreement with a previous unitarized $χ$PT analysis. These results provide a further test of the Gauge Theory Bootstrap for studying asymptotically free gauge theories.
