# Cubic Curvature Deformations to the Kerr Black Hole Shadow

## authors:
#Alireza_Allahyari
#Shinji_Mukohyama
#Yi_Pang

## summary:
We investigate the effects of cubic curvature corrections on the shadows of rotating black holes within the effective field theory of gravity. Starting from a perturbatively corrected Kerr metric incorporating two independent cubic curvature couplings, we construct black hole shadows through numerical integration of null geodesics, without assuming separability of the geodesic equations. Using a Hamiltonian ray-tracing approach, we systematically examine the dependence of the shadow geometry on the higher-curvature couplings, black hole spin, and observer inclination. We find that cubic curvature interactions can produce discernible deviations from the Kerr shadow, modifying its apparent size and shape. In particular, the two cubic interactions exhibit distinct effects on the shadow boundary, with the magnitude of the deviations depending sensitively on the coupling strengths and black hole spin. We validate our numerical results through independent convergence tests of the geodesic integration and shadow-boundary determination, and recover the analytical Kerr shadow in the vanishing-coupling limit. Our results provide a complementary probe of higher-curvature gravity in the strong-field regime and illustrate the potential of black hole shadow observations to constrain departures from general relativity.
