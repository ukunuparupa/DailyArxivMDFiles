# Self-Force and Bremsstrahlung in Finite Electrodynamics

## authors:
#Kyungsun_Lee
#Piljin_Yi

## summary:
We study the self-force and the accompanying Bremsstrahlung for a moving point charge in nonlinear electrodynamics with finite self-field and self-energy. The self-force in the Maxwell theory has been the subject of many studies, often by renowned physicists. After reviewing Coleman's lucid treatise for Maxwell among them, we wish to explore the same question in the Born--Infeld theory, our prototype. We independently compute the local self-force and the global radiative dissipation, and show how the two are mutually consistent. Despite the nonlinearity turning off for the asymptotic radiative field, its residual effect survives via the effective multipole moments of a moving charge, allowing deviation from Maxwell. We close with a detailed discussion about two different manifestations of the self-force in the literature and how these enter our computation. One of the appendices explores Born--Infeld ModMax as a generalization.
