# Lattice response theory for subsystem symmetry-protected topological phases

## authors:
#Yuki_Furukawa
#Takuya_Okuda

## summary:
We develop a framework to study subsystem symmetry-protected topological (SSPT) phases using lattice response theory. We begin with a systematic construction of spacetime lattice gauge fields from general microscopic $X$-type symmetry actions on qudit systems, including global symmetries, subsystem symmetries, and more general modulated symmetries. Given a gauge-invariant response action, we then construct a corresponding entangler and explicitly show that it implements stacking with the SSPT phase described by the response action. We show that a broad class of SSPT phases can be described by intersection pairings and realized by cluster entanglers. We also study SSPT phases beyond intersection pairings, including those described by subsystem analogues of type-II group cocycles involving the Bockstein homomorphism. We construct a new 3+1d SSPT phase protected by two 2-foliated planar subsystem symmetries and a global 0-form symmetry, whose response action is a subsystem analogue of a type-III cocycle. On its boundary, the 0-form symmetry acts by stacking with a 2+1d linear SSPT phase, signaling a mixed 't Hooft anomaly.
