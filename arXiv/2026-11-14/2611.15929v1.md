# Magnetic-slope crossovers in the QCD bulk-to-shear viscosity ratio

## authors:
#Aalok_Misra
#Shivam_S_Kushwah
#Keshav_Dasgupta
#Charles_Gale
#Sangyong_Jeon

## summary:
How a magnetic field alters dissipation in quark--gluon matter is almost unconstrained from first principles, since real-time transport is not directly accessible to lattice simulation. Using a top-down MQGP-inspired M/string-theory construction of a QCD-like thermal plasma, together with a kinetic-theory identity within a specified single-mode quasiparticle truncation and a compatibility construction using available lattice equation-of-state data, we propose a normalization-insensitive sign prediction that the field slope of the longitudinal bulk-to-shear viscosity ratio ζ_\parallel/η_\parallel reverses sign twice with temperature---below and above the deconfinement pseudo-critical temperature T_0, at T_1<T_0<T_2 (the lower reversal T_1 conditional on a stated pion-gas rate inequality). A kinetic-theory identity with phase-appropriate carriers, a quark--gluon plasma above T_0 and a chiral pion gas below, reproduces the same pattern, and lattice speed-of-sound data can be shown to be consistent as well. The bottom-up VQCD in the deconfined phase supports a negative slope for large temperatures; the slope crossover is not resolvable from the available ζ_\parallel/s, η_\parallel/s plots.
