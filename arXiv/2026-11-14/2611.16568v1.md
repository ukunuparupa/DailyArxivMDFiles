# Accelerating solitons in Rindler space

## authors:
#Huiquan_Li
#Jianyong_Wang

## summary:
Relativistic nonlinear field theories that are integrable in two-dimensional Minkowski spacetime remain integrable in Rindler space. We investigate the properties of classical sine-Gordon (SG) solitons in Rindler space. Accelerating observers in each of the two Rindler wedges can see only a portion of the full soliton in Minkowski spacetime; this portion itself forms a soliton carrying a partial topological charge. The soliton solution admits the following interpretation: a soliton incoming from the horizon decelerates to stop at some point, then falls back and accelerates to the speed of light toward the horizon. Energy can flow into and out of the Rindler horizon. The higher energy soliton gains energy from the horizon while the lower one loses energy.
