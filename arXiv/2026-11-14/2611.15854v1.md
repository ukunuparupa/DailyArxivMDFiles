# Swing Surfaces in AdS/CFT

## authors:
#Jacqueline_Caminiti
#Sabrina_Pasterski

## summary:
We study holographic entanglement entropy in the flat limit of AdS$_3$/CFT$_2$, and clarify the relation between Ryu-Takayanagi surfaces and the swing surfaces proposed for flat-space holography. We show that the ordinary RT formula does not admit a well-defined flat-space limit and connect this to subtleties of the state-operator map in the flat limit. We then construct an AdS$_3$ uplift of the swing prescription and develop its corresponding information-theoretic interpretation through two complementary approaches. First, we show that the swing surface length computes the mutual information between suitably chosen nearly null boundary intervals in a special class of pure CFT states. The gravity interpretation of this result involves scattering processes which take place deep inside of the AdS$_3$ bulk. Second, we reduce the problem of finding an information-theoretic interpretation for swing surfaces to the more general problem of finding an information-theoretic interpretation of black hole inner horizons in holography.
