# Silent Heavy Fields: An Exclusion Rule for the Cosmological Collider

## authors:
#Shuntaro_Aoki
#Zhehan_Qin
#Masahide_Yamaguchi
#Yuhang_Zhu

## summary:
The cosmological collider uses oscillatory signatures in primordial correlators to probe heavy fields during inflation. This probe has blind spots, as heavy fields can keep ticking in time while remaining silent in the correlators. In this letter, we derive an exclusion rule under which cosmological collider signals vanish. The rule covers two classes of diagrams where a de Sitter invariant heavy sector couples covariantly to a single external field at one vertex. We first prove it for exchanges mediated by the two-point function of the heavy sector to all loop orders, using a spectral decomposition followed by a field redefinition. The result then extends to one-loop polygons by matching their oscillatory contributions onto those of the first class. The approximate signals of previously studied processes that fall into these classes must therefore be revisited. As a nonvanishing example, we derive the leading signal of a triangle loop for the first time in a toy model motivated by chemical potentials.
