# The three-loop QCD helicity amplitudes for $V$+jet production at hadron colliders in full color

## authors:
#Thomas_Gehrmann
#Petr_Jakubčík
#Andreas_von_Manteuffel
#Cesare_Carlo_Mella
#Lorenzo_Tancredi

## summary:
We compute the three-loop Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) corrections to the production of a vector boson $V=Z,W^\pm,γ^*$ and a jet in quark-antiquark annihilation and its kinematic crossings, retaining full dependence on the number of colors. By expressing the infrared-finite remainders in terms of a small set of rational and special functions, we find that the amplitudes live in a strongly restricted function space, compared to the full space of the related Feynman integrals. We provide fast and reliable evaluation in all kinematic regions using numerical transport.
