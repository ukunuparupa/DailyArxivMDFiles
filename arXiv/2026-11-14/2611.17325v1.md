# Bound States of Two D0-Branes and the Three-Graviton Coupling

## authors:
#Sergio_Sanjurjo
#Themistocles_Zikopoulos

## summary:
We numerically solve for the ground state of the $SU(2)$ BFSS matrix model for M-theory. This state corresponds to the zero-energy bound state of two D0-branes or, by the BFSS conjecture, to the eleven-dimensional graviton with two units of momentum along the M-theory circle. The wavefunction exhibits a strongly coupled core, as well as an asymptotic power-law tail where the D0-branes are well separated. The coefficient of this power-law decay is the three-graviton coupling, previously computed by Herderschee and Maldacena, which our numerical solution reproduces with high accuracy. We also show that this coupling is protected by supersymmetry in the matrix model, and derive its value from the non-renormalized one-loop interaction of the two D0-branes. Lastly, we provide a simple representation-theoretic argument regarding the recently proposed excited bound states of the model.
