# $α'^2$-corrections to the bosonic string from an action-level double copy map

## authors:
#Eric_Lescano
#Agustina_Pereyra_Grau
#Jesús_A_Rodríguez

## summary:
The low-energy effective action of the bosonic string contains higher-derivative corrections of the form Riem$^2$ and Riem$^3$ to the Einstein--Hilbert action, together with the associated Kalb--Ramond couplings. We construct an action-level double copy description of these corrections up to cubic order in the fields. Two ingredients are required: a higher-derivative gauge-theory Lagrangian, which up to field redefinitions is controlled by a single $F^3$ coupling, and a deformation of the color-to-kinematics replacement of the structure constants by the corresponding three-momentum tensor. We show that no higher-derivative gauge-theory operator can produce the Riem$^3$ vertex with the standard replacement rule, and that the matching conditions for the gauge-theory coupling and the deformation of the map admit a unique solution, a double root, which renders the cubic action left--right symmetric, of the form $(\mathrm{YM}+F^3)\otimes(\mathrm{YM}+F^3)$. Without further input, the resulting map reproduces the cubic $\mathrm{Riem}\,H^2$ coupling of the bosonic string at order $α'$, the absence of six-derivative cubic Kalb--Ramond couplings at order $α'^2$, and the absence of interactions odd in the Kalb--Ramond field. The comparison is performed in transverse-traceless gauge, up to field redefinitions.
