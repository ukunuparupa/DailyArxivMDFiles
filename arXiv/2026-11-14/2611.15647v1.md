# Enhanced Early Structure from Axiodilatons & 21cm Observations

## authors:
#Chan_Youn_Weon
#Carsten_van_de_Bruck
#C_P_Burgess
#Jeong_Han_Kim
#Adam_Smith

## summary:
Axiodilatons provide a promising unified model of the Dark Sector whose properties are motivated by the use of accidental approximate symmetries to embed the light fields needed for observations into well-motivated UV physics in a technically natural way. These models turn out to help ameliorate many current puzzles in $Λ$CDM phenomenology (e.g. the Hubble tension and the evidence for evolving Dark Energy) `for free' despite not being designed for that purpose. We here explore in more detail another serendipitous feature of these models: their tendency to enhance early structure formation, in a way that seems suggestive given the accumulating evidence for structure arising earlier than expected in $Λ$CDM. Since early structure growth is the cleanest to study we explore in detail the expected 21cm signal produced by axiodilaton models, using the same parameter choices that improve agreement with observations elsewhere in cosmology. For fixed astrophysics, we forecast that SKA1-Low could distinguish dilaton couplings as small as a few times $10^{-2}$ from $Λ$CDM at $2σ$, mainly because of the earlier formation of structure. We provide complete derivations of the mass dependence of the relevant cosmological observables, including how dilaton-dependent baryon and electron masses affect the hyperfine transition, the Ly$α$ coupling, X-ray heating and ionization during cosmic dawn and the epoch of reionization. We provide details of radiative transfer, atomic physics and the equations governing the thermal and ionization state of the intergalactic medium.
