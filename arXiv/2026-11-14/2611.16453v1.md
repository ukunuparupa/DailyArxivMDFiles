# Affine Vertex Operator Algebras and Correlation Functions on Elliptic Curves

## authors:
#Linfang_Hou
#Si_Li
#Yongchang_Zhu

## summary:
Let $V^{\Bbbk}(\mathfrak{g})$ be the universal affine vertex algebra attached to a finite-dimensional complex simple Lie algebra at arbitrary level, and let $M=\bigoplus_{N=0}^{\infty}M_N$ be a $V^{\Bbbk}(\mathfrak{g})$ -module. We give an explicit formula for every genus-one trace of affine currents as a finite sum over cycle partitions. We then show the convergence of all fixed zero-mode trace series, hence absolute locally uniform convergence of the current traces and their meromorphic continuation on the elliptic configuration space. The same conclusion is obtained for arbitrary affine-state insertions. Finally, we compute the cases of at most four currents and the nodal limit $q\to 0$, in which the type-1 and type-2 elliptic cycle coefficients degenerate to rational genus-zero cycle coefficients and the module trace reduces to the ground-state space.
