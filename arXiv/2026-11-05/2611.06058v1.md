# On phases of 4d $Spin/SO$ Yang-Mills theories

## authors:
#Ryo_Hamachika
#Takahiro_Nishinaka

## summary:
We study the low-temperature phase diagrams of $Spin(N)/SO(N)_\pm$ Yang-Mills theories as functions of the $θ$-parameter. In particular, for every even integer $N\geq 4$, we find a phase diagram consistent with the center/CP mixed anomaly, via the technique of mixed gauge theory. The resulting phase diagrams are in perfect agreement with the phase diagrams of $Spin(N)/SO(N)_\pm$ super Yang-Mills theory softly broken by a small gaugino mass. In the case of $Spin(4k)$ Yang-Mills for an integer $k\geq 2$, only two phases appear in the phase diagram, while in the $Spin(4)$ case the phase diagram contains four SPT phases protected by the $\mathbb{Z}_2\times \mathbb{Z}_2$ center symmetry. In the case of $Spin(4k+2)$ Yang-Mills for a positive integer $k$, the phase diagram contains four SPT phases protected by the $\mathbb{Z}_4$ center symmetry.
