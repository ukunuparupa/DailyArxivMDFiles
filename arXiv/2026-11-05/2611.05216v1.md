# Two examples in amplituhedron tilings

## authors:
#Evgeniya_Akhmedova
#Thomas_Lam
#Calvin_Yost-Wolff

## summary:
We give an example of a tiling of the $m=4$ amplituhedron that has fewer cells than that of the magic number conjecture. The tiling uses an injective degree two cell.
