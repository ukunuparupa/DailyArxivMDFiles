# Carroll fermions of arbitrary spin

## authors:
#Andrea_Campoleoni
#Lea_Mele

## summary:
Electric and magnetic Carrollian actions for fermions of arbitrary spin in any spacetime dimension are defined as inequivalent $c \to 0$ limits of the relativistic Fang-Fronsdal actions.
