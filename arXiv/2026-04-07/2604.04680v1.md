# Twin hypercharges

## authors:
#Nguyen_Huy_Thao
#Nguyen_Thi_Nguyet_Nga
#Tran_Dinh_Tham
#Phung_Van_Dong

## summary:
It is shown that a duplication of the hypercharge, which is identical for the normal sector but different for the dark sector, may manifestly address neutrino mass and dark matter.
