# Maximally symmetric black holes with torsion in Lovelock gravity

## authors:
#B_Cvetković
#D_Simić

## summary:
We analyze the existence of static black hole solutions with maximally symmetric horizons and nontrivial torsion in five-dimensional Lovelock gravity. For each symmetry class, we obtain a black hole solution characterized by two independent constants. We also determine the conserved charges associated with these solutions.
