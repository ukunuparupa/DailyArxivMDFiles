# The Komar charges of type II supergravity and supersymmetric localization

## authors:
#Gabriele_Barbagallo
#Tomás_Ortín

## summary:
We compute the on-shell closed, generalized Komar charges associated to an arbitrary Killing vector of the two 10-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity theories which are the low-energy effective field theories of the type~IIA and IIB superstrings. We study different rewritings of this charge in terms of Page charges and forms with special properties that are used in the derivation of thermodynamical relations in black $p$-brane spacetimes.
