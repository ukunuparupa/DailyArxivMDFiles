# The origin of amplitude chaos and strings

## authors:
#Pallab_Basu
#Anurag_Sarkar

## summary:
We analytically study the peak statistics of an amplitude ensemble to identify the local mechanism responsible for peak repulsion. Our analytical approaches show that a smooth, nondegenerate ensemble of sufficiently differentiable amplitudes exhibits quartic nearest-neighbor repulsion, $p_{\rm nn}(S)\sim S^4$, where $S$ is the spacing between adjacent peaks. We verify this prediction numerically for the tree-level highly excited string scattering amplitudes. Interestingly, we find the quartic repulsion to be robust by varying both the underlying ensemble and the amplitude functions, whereas a tunable cusp model shows that weakening differentiability changes the strength of the repulsion. Peak repulsion therefore defines a local universality class that is not unique to string theory and may occur in amplitude ensembles in other theories.
