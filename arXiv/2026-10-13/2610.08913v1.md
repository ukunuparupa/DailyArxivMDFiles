# Worldsheets reconstructed: Duals to two-cut matrix models

## authors:
#Sounak_Pal

## summary:
We compute the higher-rank translation ($\mathcal{T}$) and rotation ($\mathcal{R}$) matrices associated with a genus-one spectral curve. Using these data, we reconstruct the worldsheet correlators of vertex operators in the corresponding supersymmetric Landau--Ginzburg (LG) theory. This provides a generalization of the genus-zero, one-cut spectral-curve construction to higher-genus geometries. The matrix-model correlators are mapped to integrals over the moduli space of Riemann surfaces for the symmetric sextic double-well potential, which has a double-cut solution. We further identify the rational and elliptic contributions to the worldsheet integrated vertex operators and show that each of the sector precisely matches the corresponding correlators of single-trace operators in the matrix model. Altogether, our construction showcases an explicit dictionary between the higher-genus spectral-curve data, the associated Cohomological field theory (CohFT), and the worldsheet description of the corresponding matrix-model observables.
