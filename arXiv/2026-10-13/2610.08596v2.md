# Connected Geometries in Gravitational Path Integrals, and Normalized No-Boundary Probabilities

## authors:
#Jean-Luc_Lehners

## summary:
We argue that some of the puzzling features associated with the normalization of gravitational wave functions can be substantially alleviated by restricting the path integral to a sum over connected, allowable geometries. This prescription gives sensible results when applied to classical transitions and it provides non-trivial no-boundary probabilities, in line with old expectations. We highlight the additional impact of both boundary conditions and integration contours on these issues.
