# Reflected entropy and islands in JT gravity from partial dimensional reduction

## authors:
#Debarshi_Basu
#Himanshu_Chourasiya

## summary:
We study how mixed-state correlations reorganize across island transitions in Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity coupled to a non-gravitating bath. Using the partial-dimensional-reduction realization of double holography, we compute reflected entropy for adjacent and disjoint radiation regions in the vacuum and in the thermofield-double state of an eternal black hole. Reflected entropy probes the internal partition of the entanglement wedge and therefore resolves structure that is invisible to the ordinary Page curve: its dominant saddle can change even when the entropy saddle of the union remains fixed, and it vanishes at leading order when the entanglement wedge disconnects. We show that the lower-dimensional reflected-island description agrees, to leading order in the dynamical brane fluctuation, with the higher-dimensional defect-extremal-surface picture. The finite-angle comparison further suggests a projection-covariant identification of island and brane endpoints at linear order. The resulting Page-like curves and Markov gaps provide a geometric probe of the reorganization of the purification and of non-Markovian, multipartite correlation structure during island transitions.
