# Null string holography in AdS from local BRST descent

## authors:
#Sarthak_Duary
#Sourav_Maji

## summary:
We show that the radial sector of a Carroll-Weyl gauged null string in Poincaré $\mathrm{AdS}_D$ is locally trivial in BRST cohomology. In a quotient-adapted BFV basis, the radial coordinate, its conjugate momentum, and the associated ghosts form a contractible quartet. An explicit contracting homotopy extends this reduction to the full algebra of local spatial jets, leaving precisely the $(D-1)$-dimensional ILST BRST complex. The same quartet carries zero net Virasoro central charge, so that the anomaly condition of the parent theory, $D=27$, descends to the ILST condition $d=26$.
