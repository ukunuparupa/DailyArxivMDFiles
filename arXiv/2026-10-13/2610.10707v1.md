# Large Charge Jets in $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM Energy Correlators

## authors:
#Alba_Grassi
#Cristoforo_Iossa
#Luigi_Tizzano
#Alexander_Zhiboedov

## summary:
We compute the energy-energy correlator in a state created by a heavy canonical operator of scaling dimension $Δ_H$ in $\mathcal{N}=4$ $SU(N)$ SYM, at fixed $N$ and arbitrary values of the large charge 't Hooft coupling $λ\sim Δ_H g_{\rm YM}^2$. The state created by the canonical operator triggers spontaneous breaking of conformal symmetry and the Higgs mechanism, with squared W-boson masses proportional to $λ$. We start with the known finite-coupling result for the underlying four-point function of local operators, perform the detector and Fourier transforms and express the resulting energy-energy correlator as an infinite OPE sum in the source-detector channel, which we then analyze both numerically and analytically. We show that the finite-coupling energy-energy correlator exhibits a hierarchy of distinct physical regimes as a function of $z=(1-\cosθ)/2$, where $θ$ is the angle between the detectors. A small-angle vacuum-dominated behavior at $z\llλ^{-1}$ is followed by a non-monotonic medium-induced transient behavior at $λz \sim1$. A large charge jet regime follows, where the energy-energy correlator displays a power-law behavior $\sim1/(λz^3)$ for $λ^{-1}\lesssim z \lesssim λ^{-1/3}$, before approaching an angle-independent plateau at larger angles.
