# An Algorithm for Generating All Berglund-Hubsch-Type Calabi-Yau Orbifolds and Their Mirrors. Classification of Maximal Admissible Groups

## authors:
#Sergei_Aleshin

## summary:
In this paper, we present an algorithm for constructing all Calabi-Yau orbifolds and their mirror pairs associated with an arbitrary Berglund-Hubsch-type model, and prove its correctness. In particular, the proposed method yields explicit generators for the groups defining the corresponding orbifolds. The method for classifying maximal admissible groups is proposed. We illustrate the application of the classification approach to an arbitrary Fermat-type Calabi-Yau manifold. In addition, as an illustrative example, we classify all maximal admissible groups of Fermat-type Calabi-Yau threefolds.
