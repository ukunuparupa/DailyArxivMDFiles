# Derivation of color projectors on multi-quark states using the Casimir operator

## authors:
#Stéphane_Peigné
#Clément_Liesse

## summary:
We present an elementary recursive construction of Hermitian projectors on multi-quark ${\rm SU}(N)$ color states, based on the characterization of these states by their quadratic Casimir, and using the birdtrack pictorial technique.
