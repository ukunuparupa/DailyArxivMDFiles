# Absence of Continuous Spin Particles in Superstring Theory

## authors:
#Arwa_Alabbasi
#Fernando_Quevedo

## summary:
The study of continuous spin particles, as general low-energy implications of relativity and quantum mechanics, has intensified in recent years with potential experimental signatures. In this note we show that these particles are absent in perturbative super-string theories, generalising a result in arXiv:1302.4771 to the supersymmetric case. This stands out as a general low-energy prediction of all perturbative superstring constructions.
