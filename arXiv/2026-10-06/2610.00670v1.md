# Monogamous quantum corrections to holographic mutual information

## authors:
#César_A_Agón
#Javier_Molina-Vilaplana
#Javier_Moreno

## summary:
We construct an extensive model of bulk mutual information (MI) in anti-de Sitter spacetime and use it to describe the leading quantum corrections to the MI of certain holographic theories. The equivalence between bulk and boundary MI follows from the Faulkner--Lewkowycz--Maldacena prescription. We show that the extensivity of the bulk MI is sufficient for the quantum-corrected boundary entropy vector to satisfy monogamy of MI and all other holographic entropy cone inequalities. Our model is built from a covariantly conserved current-current kernel in AdS and is realized explicitly by a free massive Dirac fermion, whose boundary MI at long distances is that of a fermionic generalized free field (GFF). The construction also reproduces the expected short-distance divergence structure of a GFF, and the current-current correlator defines a positive and monotonically decreasing function along the renormalization-group flow. We illustrate this explicitly for disjoint half-disks at the conformal fixed point of the model in AdS$_3$, where we present the exact result.
