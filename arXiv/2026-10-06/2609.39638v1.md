# Neutrino Oscillations without Mass: A Re-analysis of KamLAND Data following the Dirac Equation in Curved Spacetime

## authors:
#Golam_Mortuza_Hossain
#Pushpit_Kumar

## summary:
The Dirac equation in stationary curved spacetime implies that describing two-flavor neutrino oscillations requires treating the invariant mass and conserved energy of each propagating state distinctly, as gravity affects energy differently than mass. By re-analysing the publicly released KamLAND dataset, we show that modeling flavor states of massless neutrinos as two-level quantum states with energy-dependent level splitting, analogous to modified Jaynes-Cummings models, results in a higher-likelihood fit to the observed data than the standard massive-neutrino paradigm.
