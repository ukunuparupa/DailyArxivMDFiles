# Quasi-Einstein metrics from Horava gravity

## authors:
#Jorge_Bellorin

## summary:
In this note we present a direct relation between the geometrical definition of a quasi-Einstein metric and Horava gravity: the solutions of the large-distance effective action of Horava gravity that are static and orthogonal are quasi-Einstein metrics. The converse is also true if the function over the quasi-Einstein manifold satisfies a given equation.
