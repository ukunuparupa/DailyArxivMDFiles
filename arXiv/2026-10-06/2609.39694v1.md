# Gravitational Memory: an approximate description

## authors:
#Q_-L_Zhao
#P_-M_Zhang
#M_Elbistan
#J_Balog
#P_A_Horvathy

## summary:
The large-distance behaviour of a sandwich gravitational wave can be approximated by a continuous but not necessarily smooth profile, providing us with a simplified description of particle motion. Our approximate model is consistent with the Carroll symmetry. Our strategy is illustrated by the Poschl-Teller profile.
