# Soliton like solutions of $λφ^4$ theory in de Sitter space

## authors:
#D_V_Diakonov

## summary:
We study classical solutions of a scalar field with quartic potential in de Sitter space-time. Using the ansatz $φ=F(X\cdotξ)$, via embeding coordinate $X_α$ of the ambiend space we find several families of analytic solutions classified by the causal character of the constant vector $ξ$. For time-like $ξ$, the solutions are globally regular and describe either global oscillations around a minimum or global vacuum transition; for space-like $ξ$, they are singular in the global patch but regular in the open slicing. In the Poincaré patch they correspond to contracting bubbles. We compute the classical actions of these solition which are finite only in spacetime dimensions $D \le 5$. We show that the $D=4$ global vacuum transition solution has the radiation equation of state $w=1/3$. In $D=4$ global vacuum transition solution, the retarded Green's function acquires a growing tail as compared to the Green's function on top of vacuum, implying that the transition amplifies the signal at late times.
