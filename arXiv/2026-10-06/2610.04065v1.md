# Wilson line defect indices for M2-brane SCFTs

## authors:
#Hirotaka_Hayashi
#Tomoki_Nosaka
#Tadashi_Okazaki

## summary:
We investigate line defect Coulomb and Higgs indices in 3d $\mathcal{N}\ge 4$ supersymmetric gauge theories. The normalized line defect indices defined by ratios of the decorated defect indices to the ordinary indices yield well-defined integer values, the line defect Witten indices in the unit R-charge fugacity limit. We propose general formulae for these Witten indices in both Coulomb and Higgs limits for the theories without Chern-Simons terms, and we show that both count the states invariant under the residual gauge group left unbroken at a generic point of the branch, which we read off from the vacuum equations. Furthermore, we find powerful algebraic recursion relations for Wilson line defect Coulomb indices in the 3d SCFTs describing M2-branes, including ADHM-type theories and ABJ(M) theories. These relations imply that higher rank line defect indices are recursively determined by lower rank data across $U(N)$, $USp(2N)$, $SO(2N)$ and $SO(2N+1)$ ADHM-type theories and ABJ(M) theories.
