# Evolution of multihadron fragmentation functions with nonperturbative behavior

## authors:
#K_Makino
#T_C_Rogers

## summary:
QCD calculations that use multihadron fragmentation functions usually involve multiple invariant mass scales simultaneously, with only one being the hard scale associated with an overall hard process. The other scales are associated with relative internal motion intrinsic to the fragmenting parton and the multihadron system. Nonperturbative contributions to this later type of dependence attract interest for their association with intrinsic partonic structures, but care is needed in disentangling the truly nonperturbative effects from perturbative behavior and relating the former to universal nonperturbative matrix elements. To this end, we show how to evolve collinear multihadron fragmentation functions in a way that accounts for the simultaneous presence of these extra scales. The general multihadron fragmentation functions evolve in the same way as ordinary single hadron fragmentation functions, with the dependence on the extra scales separated out. We use this to unify treatments of perturbative and nonperturbative effects in multihadron systems in a way that optimizes for interpretations focused on partonic structure.
