# 6d SCFTs and terminal singularities

## authors:
#Andrea_Sangiovanni

## summary:
The classification of 6d $\mathcal{N}=(1,0)$ SCFTs has been carried out with great success, enumerating consistent elliptically fibered threefolds in F-theory. In this note, we observe that the E-string 6d SCFT can be decorated with extra matter sectors. This decoration exploits an opening allowed by the current classification, implementing terminal singularities nested on the curve of the base of the elliptic fibration that supplies the tensor branch. Additionally, via dimensional reduction on a circle, we identify versions of the 5d $E_n$ theories decorated by extra matter sectors. As a bonus, the end of the resulting RG flow cascade reproduces a 5d theory recently constructed in the literature, explaining its 6d origin. We remain agnostic as to whether the decorated 6d and 5d theories constitute novel SCFTs.
