# Liénard--Wiechert fields in AdS and flat-space antipodal matching from geodesic-centered Coulombic data

## authors:
#Sarthak_Duary

## summary:
We give a geometric derivation of Li'enard--Wiechert fields in flat space and in global AdS, emphasizing the origin of antipodal matching. In flat space, the field of a uniformly moving charge is obtained by using coordinates centered on the source timelike geodesic, where the charge is at rest and the solution is the ordinary Coulomb field. The nontrivial angular dependence of the leading data at null infinity arises only after rewriting this static solution in a non-centered frame, making the antipodal matching condition transparent. We extend the same construction to global AdS, where uniform motion is replaced by motion along a timelike geodesic. Starting from the static Maxwell solution at the AdS center, we use geodesic-adapted coordinates and embedding-space invariants to reconstruct the field in arbitrary global coordinates. This yields a closed-form AdS analogue of the Coulombic Li'enard--Wiechert field, including the gauge-invariant component $F_{ρτ}$. We show that the bulk field satisfies exact antipodal covariance, and that its null-fringe limit reproduces the standard flat-space antipodal matching relation between the Coulombic data at $\mathscr I^+*-$ and $\mathscr I^-*+$. We also describe an image-charge interpretation of the Coulomb seed, giving a unified picture of Coulombic fields, antipodal matching, and the AdS-to-flat-space limit.
