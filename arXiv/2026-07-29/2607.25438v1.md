# Calabi-Yau Deformation Quantization

## authors:
#Jakob_Ulmer

## summary:
We record the Calabi-Yau version of Kontsevich's formality morphism from deformation quantization. As a special case we find that any unimodular holomorphic Poisson Calabi-Yau has a canonical closed deformation quantization of its resolved algebra of functions. A broader motivation is to find an explanation of Kontsevich's deformation quantization in the setting of Calabi-Yau categories via Sen-Zwiebach's string vertices.
