# Worldline representation of multiloop amplitudes in quantum electrodynamics

## authors:
#Victor_M_Banda_Guzmán
#Uwe_Müller
#Cristofer_Nava_Jacuinde
#Christian_Schubert
#Carlos_J_Servin_Tomas

## summary:
The worldline approach to quantum electrodynamics allows one to construct integral representations combining large numbers of Feynman diagrams. Here I review the state-of-the-art of a long-term effort to make this fact useful for actual multiloop calculations, such as of the scalar and spinor QED vacuum polarization functions and the electron anomalous magnetic moment. After a short historical introduction, and a discussion of the non-standard mathematical challenges involved, I focus on a recent calculation of the two-loop vacuum polarisation in scalar QED and some master integral formulas obtained in this context.
