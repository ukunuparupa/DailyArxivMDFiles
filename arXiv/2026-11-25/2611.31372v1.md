# Bootstrapping two-loop $β$-functions for bosonic and heterotic strings

## authors:
#Achilleas_Gitsis
#Falk_Hassler

## summary:
Beyond one loop, $β$-functions of non-linear $σ$-models are hard to compute directly. We obtain them instead as a gradient flow of the low-energy effective action, whose coupling-space metric is fixed exclusively by symmetry. This gives a two-parameter family of O$(d,d)$-covariant, two-loop $β$-functions that covers the bosonic and heterotic string at once. Because only symmetry enters, the method opens a route to higher loops. As an application, we derive the two-loop RG flow of the integrable $η$- and $λ$-deformations; for the heterotic string, the correction is half of the bosonic one.
