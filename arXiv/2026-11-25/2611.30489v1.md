# Non-invertible Duality Defects in Four-Dimensional $\mathbb{Z}_N$ Lattice Gauge Theories

## authors:
#Satoshi_Yamaguchi

## summary:
We consider four-dimensional $\mathbb{Z}_N$ lattice gauge theories with generalized plaquette actions. These theories are known to exhibit dualities. We construct topological interfaces implementing these dualities by assigning a weight to each building block and proving topological invariance. At self-dual couplings, these interfaces become duality defects representing non-invertible symmetries. We also study center symmetry defects and their junctions with the duality interfaces and derive several crossing relations.
