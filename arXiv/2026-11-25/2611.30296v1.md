# Matter-decorated trumpets in double-scaled SYK

## authors:
#Matteo_Beccaria

## summary:
In sine-dilaton gravity, the double trumpet, the wormhole amplitude without matter, is obtained by gluing two trumpets along a closed geodesic of discrete length and summing over this length. When the wormhole carries the propagator of a matter field of dimension $Δ$, joining the two asymptotic boundaries and crossing the neck, such a gluing is known only in the Jackiw--Teitelboim (JT) limit. We construct the gluing beyond JT in double-scaled SYK (DSSYK), the proposed boundary dual of sine-dilaton gravity, whose disk amplitudes it reproduces. In this context, we adopt the definition of the matter-decorated wormhole in terms of the ETH two-matrix model with Gaussian matter. We build matter-decorated trumpets from an end-of-the-world brane trace with a matter insertion, and prove that the wormhole is glued from them exactly, sector by sector in the number of chords through the neck, with a positive weight that accounts for the twists and windings of these chords and for the closed loops around the neck. Trumpets and weight are obtained in closed form, the former as transforms of Rogers polynomials, and we discuss the uniqueness of the construction. In the JT limit, both reproduce the known gluing separately, and not only in combination. In the classical limit, each trumpet takes the form expected in the bulk: a pure-gravity sine-dilaton trumpet dressed by the factor that a probe matter geodesic contributes at its boundary.
