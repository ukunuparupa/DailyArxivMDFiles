# Tensionless M5-brane in light-cone gauge. The spinor moving frame method is wanted

## authors:
#Igor_Bandos

## summary:
We perform the complete light-cone gauge fixing for the tensionless limit of the M-theory 5-brane the action for which was recently obtained in [1]. The problem of gauge fixing of local fermionic $κ$-symmetry becomes quite nontrivial because the projector of that is constructed from the gauge field strength which in its turn is required to be nonvanaishing and nontrivial in this limit. To resolve this problem the spinor moving frame method has been adapted. The corresponding moving frame is constructed on a vector quadratic in the gauge field strength, which is required to be nonvanishing and nondegenerate in the tensionless limit, and the derivative of the PST (Pasti-Sorokin-Tonin) scalar. The symmetries of the gauge fixed action and equations of motion are briefly discussed. To make a comparison with the case of usual tensionless p-branes (null-p-branes), those which do not carry worldvolume gauge fields, we also elaborate their spinor moving frame formulation.
