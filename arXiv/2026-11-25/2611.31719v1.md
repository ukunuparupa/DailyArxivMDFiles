# BPS baryonic tubes in chiral perturbation theory minimally coupled with electrodynamics and a novel crossover

## authors:
#Fabrizio_Canfora
#Martín_Garrido
#Pablo_Pais

## summary:
We derive a novel first-order BPS system (whose solutions are automatically solutions of the second-order field equations) in chiral perturbation theory (CPT) in $(3+1)$ dimensions. These BPS configurations describe baryonic tubes carrying a chiral current along the tubes themselves. Such baryonic tubes can be either homogeneous or modulated along the $z$-axis. We analyze the leading term in CPT, which is the $SU(2)$ non-linear sigma model minimally coupled to electrodynamics. These exact solutions are qualitatively different from the available analytic description of baryonic tubes since, due to their BPS nature, the positions of the energy-density peaks can be arbitrary. In contrast (in the case of the known solutions in the literature), the energy-density peaks' positions are fixed. We compare these two types of baryonic configurations; the relevant parameter that determines which one is energetically favored is the baryonic charge density: for low densities, the old solutions are favorable, while for high densities these novel BPS tubes are energetically favored. The inclusion of the electromagnetic field (together with the corresponding back-reaction on the baryonic tubes) can also be treated analytically. We discuss the differences in the electromagnetic field distribution between the two regions (low and high baryonic density). The analysis of potential terms for the chiral field is detailed in Appendix B.
