# Parton Physics in the Schwinger Model from Matrix Product States

## authors:
#Mari_Carmen_Bañuls
#Krzysztof_Cichy
#C_-J_David_Lin
#Manuel_Schneider

## summary:
Parton distribution functions (PDFs) and light-cone distribution amplitudes (LCDAs) encode the non-perturbative structure of hadrons in terms of their partonic constituents. They are defined through matrix elements containing parton operators that are connected by a Wilson line along a light-front direction, which makes them difficult to access in Euclidean lattice calculations. We demonstrate how parton functions can be directly obtained in Minkowski space using the Hamiltonian framework. We compute fermion and antifermion PDFs, LCDAs, and decay constants of the vector meson in the Schwinger model for several fermion masses. States in Hilbert space are efficiently represented by tensor networks, with an expressivity that is systematically controlled by a bond dimension. The light-front Wilson line is implemented as a stepwise evolution on the lattice that alternates spatial shifts of an external charge with Trotterized time-evolution steps acting on a matrix product state. All systematic uncertainties are quantified by varying and extrapolating in the lattice spacing, volume, bond dimension, gauge-field truncation, and Trotter step width. The slow growth of the entanglement entropy makes moderate bond dimensions sufficient, which supports the feasibility of using tensor networks for computing light-front matrix elements in more general settings. We confirm that the computed distributions fulfill the expected normalizations, momentum sum rules, and symmetry properties.
