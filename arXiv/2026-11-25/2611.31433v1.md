# Topological Phase Transition of $ F_{α}{}^{γ} F^{αβ} F_{β}{}^{η} \ F^{λμ} R_{γηλμ} $ AdS Black Holes

## authors:
#Mehdi_Sadeghi
#Faramarz_Rahmani

## summary:
We investigate the thermodynamic criticality and topological properties of four-dimensional charged anti-de Sitter black holes in the presence of a nonminimal electromagnetic-curvature interaction of the form \(F_α{}^γF^{αβ}F_β{}^ηF^{λμ}R_{γηλμ}\). Treating the coupling perturbatively to first order, we construct the corrected black hole solutions and derive their thermodynamic quantities in the extended phase space. Conventional critical behavior is examined through the criticality conditions for the Hawking temperature in both canonical and grand canonical ensembles. We then use thermodynamic topology to investigate the global structure of equilibrium configurations and its dependence on the thermodynamic parameters. In the canonical ensemble, two distinct regimes emerge: a noncritical region characterized by a total winding number \(W=+1\), and a critical region with \(W=0\). In the grand canonical ensemble, three distinct regimes are identified according to the electric potential. Interestingly, the low- and intermediate-potential regions share the same topological number, \(W=+1\), although conventional critical points occur only in the intermediate-potential region. The high-potential region, by contrast, is characterized by \(W=0\) and exhibits no conventional critical behavior. Our findings show that thermodynamic topology provides a global characterization of equilibrium configurations that complements conventional temperature-based criticality analysis, and that the global winding number alone does not uniquely determine the existence of conventional critical points.
