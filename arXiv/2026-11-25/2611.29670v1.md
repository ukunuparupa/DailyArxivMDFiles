# Quadratic-in-spin scattering of compact bodies at fourth post-Minkowskian order

## authors:
#Taushif_Ahmed
#Syed_Mehedi_Hasan

## summary:
We present the fourth-post-Minkowskian scattering angle of aligned spinning compact bodies through quadratic spin at arbitrary velocity and mass ratio. We include spin-induced quadrupoles and radiation reaction, supplemented by curvature-squared spin-tidal terms. Three-loop worldline integrals yield closed forms in polylogarithms and elliptic integrals. Infrared poles cancel exactly, fixing the tail logarithm. We obtain a new post-Newtonian coefficient and third-order angular-momentum loss for general quadrupoles. The new input shifts resummed predictions for black-hole scattering.
