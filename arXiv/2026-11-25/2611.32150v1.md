# Resolvent reconstruction of minimal strings beyond KdV II: D series models

## authors:
#Wasif_Ahmed
#Joydeep_Naskar

## summary:
Three-state Potts gravity and tetracritical Ising gravity have the same central charge and the same conformal spectral curve. What distinguishes their amplitudes once a handle or a boundary is added? We answer this question by extending Johnson's differential-equation approach, in the reconstruction form developed in Part I for the reduced KP hierarchies, to D-series minimal strings. Their spatial operator is a pseudo-differential pencil with a nonlocal Pfaffian term, and the classical energy acquires a second pole at zero momentum. Regularity at both poles fixes the quantum corrections uniquely, ordinary string equations supply them, and the ordinary D flows add boundaries in a way that matches the tau function. At genus one the closed free energy follows from the pencil as a sum of logarithms of critical-value velocities. The two theories differ through the origin of the curve, which the D theory counts as two canonical directions, and through the pairing of every point with its reflection. We follow the origin contribution through the free energies to genus three, the one-holed torus and the torus one-point amplitudes, and in the primary range the latter reproduce the formulas conjectured from worldsheet numerics for both series.
