# Second-order localization and intersecting O6-planes in scale-separated AdS vacua

## authors:
#Aravind_Aikot
#Timm_Wrase

## summary:
We construct explicit periodic second-order localization corrections to scale-separated AdS$_4$ vacua of massive type IIA supergravity on $T^6/(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2)$. At first order, the corrections from different O6-plane families simply add. The interactions between intersecting families first enter at second order and could obstruct the continuation of the solution. We find explicit solutions for the warp factor, dilaton, full internal metric and required form fields, including the terms generated by these interactions. The metric at second order contains off-diagonal components. With a common regularization of the singular source terms, we verify the full field equations and the conditions required for a solution on a compact space. Using the pure-spinor formalism, we find that the second-order fields satisfy the supersymmetry conditions accessible at the retained orders.
