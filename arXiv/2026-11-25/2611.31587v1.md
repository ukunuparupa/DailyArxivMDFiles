# All the Positive Energy Correlators

## authors:
#Lorenzo_Ricci
#Marc_Riembau
#Minho_Son

## summary:
We develop a framework to systematically obtain the full set of positivity bounds on multi-point energy correlators for sources of arbitrary spin. The constraints are organized in a family of positive operators $\mathscr{E}^{(N,k)}$, with $k=0,\ldots,\lfloor N/2\rfloor$. Our approach provides outer bounds, which exclude correlators that violate the positivity of $\mathscr{E}^{(N,k)}$, and inner bounds, obtained by constructing ensembles of consistent collider events via linear programming. Both methods converge to each other as numerical precision increases. This approach, in particular, characterizes the allowed higher-point correlators once lower-point correlators are determined. We illustrate this through several examples, including bounds on multipoles of the three-point energy correlator in QCD derived from measurements of one- and two-point correlators at LEP.
