# Charged spin-$\frac32$ in a chiral Feshbach--Villars model: hyperbolic propagation, Landau-level instability, and exceptional points

## authors:
#Haryanto_M_Siahaan

## summary:
We study a Pauli-coupled second-order wave equation on $(\frac32,0)\oplus(0,\frac32)$ with a free gyromagnetic ratio $g$. Its Feshbach--Villars form is a sixteen-component pseudo-Hermitian Hamiltonian system with lightlike covariant characteristics in smooth electromagnetic backgrounds. The solution space is chirality doubled; a covariant physical single-species reduction is not assumed. The exact spin-$j$ Landau spectrum remains real at every field strength precisely when $|g|j\le1$. At $g=2$, the critical spin-$\frac32$ channel reaches zero at $|qB|=m^2/2$, where each chiral block develops a second-order exceptional point. This threshold also marks the loss of a time-independent positive-definite conserved quadratic form for the channel. We also include a bounded, Gaussian-damped harmonic electrostatic potential and its associated electric field. The complete first-order problem on a degenerate manifold reduces to $V\pm iC$, with $V$ and $C$ the projected potential and spin--electric coupling. On the lowest two-state spin-$\frac32$ manifold, nonreal eigenvalue branches arise for $m^2/6<|qB|<m^2/2$ at sufficiently small nonzero perturbation amplitude, except at a special well range where the first-order splitting vanishes. The spin-$\frac12$, $g=2$ limit satisfies the Dirac spectral-reality check, whereas $g=1/j$ has no imaginary first-order correction for this perturbation. These results separate spectral instability from loss of hyperbolicity; they do not establish a physical fermionic quantization.
