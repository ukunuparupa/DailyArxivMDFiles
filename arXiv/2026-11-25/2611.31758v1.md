# A cubic theory of gravity with quasitopological-like properties for compactified cosmologies

## authors:
#Vicente_Aguilar
#Fabrizio_Canfora
#Vicente_García
#Alex_Giacomini
#Julio_Oliva

## summary:
We identify a cubic combination of curvature invariants in dimension $D=7$ that when added to the Einstein-Hilbert action, yields a healthy cosmological evolution in four dimensions after compactification on a constant-curvature internal space of dimension three. For generic backgrounds it leads to fourth-order field equations, nevertheless, on a warped product of a spatially flat, homogeneous and isotropic four-dimensional universe with a constant-curvature space of radius $b(t)$, the constraint is of first order in the scale factor $a(t)$ and in $b(t)$, while the remaining equations are of second order. Requiring this property uniquely singles out the cubic theory. We study the solutions describing a four-dimensional de Sitter Universe in direct product with an internal space of stabilized radius and evaluate its linear stability under time-dependent perturbations, showing that for a given sign of the curvature of the internal space, stability of the static solution fixes the sign of the higher curvature coupling. We also argue that linearized scalar perturbations depending on the spatial coordinates obey equations with at most two time derivatives. Finally, we numerically evolve the homogeneous and isotropic system non-linearly and present evidence that for a positive bare cosmological term $Λ_0$ and a hyperbolic compact space, the singularity formation present in GR in this ansatz is pushed into the future by increasing the higher-curvature coupling.
