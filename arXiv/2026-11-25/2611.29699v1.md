# Bounds on entanglement fluctuations at holographic interfaces

## authors:
#Pallab_Basu
#Vishnu_Jejjala

## summary:
How small can entanglement fluctuations be at fixed entropy? For a class of holographic conformal interfaces supported by a scalar with a potential, we prove $c_\text{eff}\leq c_\text{cap}\leq c_\text{eff}^2/c_{LR}$, relating entropy, capacity, and energy transmission. The coupled gravitational response is a transport term minus a positive scalar contribution; the background Einstein equation bounds that subtraction. A long Janus throat allows unbounded capacity at a finite entropy coefficient while the scalar response remains gapped.
