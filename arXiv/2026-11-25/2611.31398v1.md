# Refined Rényi Entropy from Replica-Geometry Tensor Networks

## authors:
#Qiang_Wen
#Haocheng_Zhong
#Yiwei_Zhong

## summary:
Holographic tensor networks are a central route to understanding the mechanism behind the holographic duality, but standard fixed-graph constructions with maximally entangled links face a structural obstacle. Their leading entanglement spectra are flat, so their Rényi entropies do not depend on the replica index. This is in sharp contrast with holographic conformal field theories, where a tensionful cosmic brane makes the refined Rényi entropy $n$-dependent. Capturing this replica dependence is therefore an important test of tensor-network descriptions of holographic entanglement. We construct an explicit family of replica-dependent geodesic graphs that reproduces this behavior while keeping the links maximally entangled. For each replica index $n$, geodesics are emitted from the replicated boundary with the same local, isotropic intersection measure. They perfectly tessellate the selected replica geometry, so the $n$-dependence of their intersection counts comes from the geometry. Tensor-network models on these graphs reproduce the cosmic-brane prescription for refined Rényi entropy by counting crossing threads. Their cross-copy endpoint density also agrees, in CFT$_2$, with a boundary reconstruction within the additive linear combination proposal, providing a check beyond the total entropy. In higher dimensions, we further obtain an analytic large-separation decay law for this density.
