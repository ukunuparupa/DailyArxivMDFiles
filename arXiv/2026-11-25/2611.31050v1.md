# Gradient formula from the sphere for perturbative RG flows near critical dimensions

## authors:
#Marten_Reehorst

## summary:
We study the gradient property of perturbative renormalisation group flows in $d=d_c-\varepsilon$, with a potential and a metric regular at $\varepsilon=0$ up to logarithms of gauge couplings. We state properties of the renormalised functional with local couplings on the sphere $S^d$: finiteness, a local Weyl anomaly, the Ward identity of a conformal Killing vector, rotation properties and statements on the free theory. From them we derive a Ward identity without anomaly for a combination of the functional and its anomaly. Its first variation is a gradient formula whose symmetric metric contains the two-point function on the sphere and whose potential at fixed points is, in an extended form of the properties, proportional to the sphere free energy $\tilde F$ up to a constant. Sources of relevant operators enter through their Weyl variation, and in a scheme which we construct the gradient formula persists, with a nondegenerate metric if a matrix of the free theory is invertible. For multiscalar $φ^4$ theory and for scalars and Dirac fermions with Yukawa and quartic couplings in $d=4-\varepsilon$, for sextic scalars in $d=3-\varepsilon$ and for cubic scalars in $d=6-\varepsilon$, we derive all these properties from a finiteness hypothesis. The gradient formula then holds to all orders for the beta function defined on the sphere. For interacting gauge theories the properties remain assumptions.
