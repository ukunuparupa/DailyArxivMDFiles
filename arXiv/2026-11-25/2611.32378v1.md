# The DBI critical electric field in stationary non-diagonal geometries

## authors:
#Leila_Shahkarami

## summary:
We investigate the critical electric field for the holographic Schwinger effect in general stationary non-diagonal geometries. We show that, in such backgrounds, the DBI reality condition must be formulated in terms of the electric field measured in a local orthonormal frame, since the coordinate components of the field strength need not represent a purely electric field in that frame. The critical field measured in the orthonormal frame takes the universal value \(E_{\mathrm{proper},c}=T_F\), while its coordinate expression depends on the background geometry. The resulting critical field is independently confirmed by the holographic potential analysis, through the disappearance of the potential barrier. We further illustrate the general prescription using the planar limit and the global rotating Myers--Perry--AdS geometry, which exhibit different types of metric mixing.
