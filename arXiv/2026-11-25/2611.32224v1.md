# Holographic Entanglement Entropy of Network CFT

## authors:
#Zhi-Rong_Deng
#Jie_Yang

## summary:
Miao et al. \cite{Guo:2025sbm, Guo:2026prm} proposed the AdS/NCFT correspondence, which explores the gravity dual of conformal field theories defined on networks. While previous studies investigated this model within Einstein and Gauss-Bonnet gravity (first- and second-order Lovelock gravity), in this paper, we generalize the framework to higher-order Lovelock gravity and more complex network structures. We derive the extremal and junction conditions for generalized Ryu-Takayanagi (RT) surfaces in third-order Lovelock gravity, which can be naturally extended to arbitrary higher-order Lovelock theories. Focusing on a network model comprising one node and three branches, we numerically investigate the relationship between the network holographic entanglement entropy (HEE) and gravitational coupling coefficients. Furthermore, by analyzing the HEE across various network configurations, we find that the primary structural feature affecting the network HEE is the presence of internal nodes within line-shaped networks. Our results indicate that the network HEE decreases as the number or depth of internal branches increases.
