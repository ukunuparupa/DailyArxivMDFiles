# Void inside the horizon: connecting the third-quantized vacuum to a classical spacetime

## authors:
#Dong-han_Yeom

## summary:
We investigate the emergence of a classical spacetime from a quantum-gravitational vacuum within the framework of third quantization applied to the black hole interior. Following the canonical quantization of the Kantowski--Sachs spacetime, one can construct a wave packet that explains the classical interior of a Schwarzschild black hole. This wave packet should be extended to the classical singularity. To avoid this, one can introduce an analytic continuation and the Euclidean wave function. This is not an analytic continuation of the spacetime, but of the superspace. To give this a suitable meaning, it is reasonable to introduce the concept of the third quantization. In this aspect, the Euclidean analytic continuation of the superspace explains the on-shell trajectory connecting the third-quantized vacuum state to a coherent classical geometry. Finally, we discuss how nonlinear self-interactions and baby-universe wormhole couplings must be incorporated into the third-quantized action to dynamically drive this transition.
