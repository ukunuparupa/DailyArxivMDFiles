# The Matrix in Pieces: Complete BFSS Scattering at Finite Rank

## authors:
#Mai_Qi

## summary:
As two matrix blocks separate in Banks-Fischler-Shenker-Susskind matrix quantum mechanics, the modes connecting them develop a growing excitation gap. Supersymmetry cancels their leading vacuum energy, so blocks in internal bound states can scatter as composite particles. We prove that these particle channels are asymptotically complete for the physical relative Hamiltonian at each fixed finite rank $N\ge 2$. Because a block can itself break up as its center moves away from the others, the proof must control motion on both scales. Inverse powers of the matrix radius control derivatives along internal flat directions, while the growing gap suppresses excitations between blocks. Propagation estimates then allow us to resolve successive breakups by induction on the block rank. The incoming and outgoing wave operators each have range equal to the absolutely continuous subspace, and the singular-continuous subspace vanishes. The scattering operator is thus unitary when each constituent can occupy any of its internal bound states. For a normalized incoming state of zero-energy constituents, the outgoing probability missing from the zero-energy channels equals the inclusive probability of producing at least one constituent with positive internal energy.
