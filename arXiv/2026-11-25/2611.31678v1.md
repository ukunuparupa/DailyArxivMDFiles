# $AdS$ Leading Regge Trajectory from String Field Theory

## authors:
#Minjae_Cho
#João_Gomide
#Jaroslav_Scheinpflug
#Xi_Yin

## summary:
We determine the $AdS$ mass of the leading Regge trajectory in superstring backgrounds $AdS_5\times S^5$, $AdS_4\times\mathbb{CP}^3$, and $AdS_3\times S^3\times{\cal M}_4$ with pure Ramond-Ramond (RR) flux for both ${\cal M}_4=S^3\times S^1$ and ${\cal M}_4=T^4$, to the second order in the expansion with respect to the background RR flux by solving the linearized equations of motion in closed superstring field theory. The $AdS_5$ and $AdS_4$ results are in a complete agreement with those obtained from the integrability approach to dual gauge theories, thereby providing a nontrivial check of the holographic duality beyond supersymmetry-protected quantities.
