# Hadron structure in $1+1$ dimensions from the infinite Hamiltonian lattice

## authors:
#Ross_Dempsey

## summary:
The internal structure of a hadron is encoded in matrix elements of operators between hadron states of definite momentum. In this paper, we develop a framework for computing such matrix elements in $(1+1)$-dimensional gauge theories by working on an infinite spatial lattice. We apply the method to the massive Schwinger model. We benchmark our method against discretized lightcone quantization (DLCQ) at $θ= 0$, and then use the lattice to study hadrons at $θ\neq 0$, where DLCQ does not apply. We compute parton distribution functions and gravitational form factors, and both show signatures of hadrons becoming large, loosely-bound states when we set $m$ greater than the critical mass $m_c$ and approach $θ= π$ from below. Moreover, we see the gravitational form factor change from that of a free boson to that of a free Majorana fermion when we set $θ= π$ and approach the 2D Ising critical point at $m=m_c$ from below.
