# Quark and gluon gravitational form factors of the nucleon at next-to-leading order

## authors:
#Ulaş_Özdem

## summary:
We compute the quark and gluon gravitational form factors $A$, $J$ and $D$ of the nucleon in three-point QCD sum rules at next-to-leading order in $α_s$ for the quark and gluon energy-momentum tensors (EMT), treating both at the same order in the $\overline{\rm MS}$ scheme. The nucleon EMT also tests the method, showing where the sum rules are reliable and why. Protected estimators of $D$ and of the trace form factor $\bar c$ require antisymmetric Borel moments, since none with equal Borel masses alone is protected. The $\mathcal{O}(α_s)$ corrections reduce $A_{\rm tot}$ by $32$--$40\%$, $J_{\rm tot}$ by $22$--$27\%$ and $|D_{\rm tot}|$ by $21$--$27\%$, since the perturbative share of the two-point function is much larger. The total form factors agree with lattice QCD for $1\le Q^2\le2.5$~GeV$^2$. At $Q^2=1.5$~GeV$^2$, $A_{\rm tot}=0.337\pm0.113$, $J_{\rm tot}=0.206\pm0.100$ and $D_{\rm tot}=-0.206\pm0.121$. $D$ is the most reliable result: its correlator is mostly perturbative, and its quark and gluon parts agree with the lattice. In $A$ and $J$, a local gluon-condensate term that does not fall with $Q^2$ is the largest, and their gluon shares are well below the lattice, possibly because the gluon EMT couples at this order only through gluon exchange and the condensate. A working region exists only near $β=1$, and the constant part of transitions cannot be separated within the Borel windows. The individual conservation identities deviate at one point by two standard deviations, consistent with a missing finite term in the $g_{μν}$ structure, and $\bar c$ is therefore given at leading order with condensates.
