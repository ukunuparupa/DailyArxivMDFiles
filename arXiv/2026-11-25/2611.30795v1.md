# Multipartite entanglement structure of crystal quenches

## authors:
#Paul_Gaudin
#Clément_Berthière

## summary:
Understanding how entanglement emerges and spreads in out-of-equilibrium quantum many-body systems is a fundamental problem. In this paper, we investigate multipartite entanglement after quantum quenches. In particular, we study the entanglement structure and dynamics of quasiparticle multiplets generated by crystal quenches in the tight-binding chain. We identify zero-configurations for which a multiplet shared between three parties has no irreducible tripartite entanglement---these configurations consist entirely of pairwise distributed bipartite-entangled states. The existence of zero-configurations has important implications on the entanglement structure and dynamics of crystal quenches. For example, we show that it can further delay the production of tripartite entanglement. We build the quasiparticle picture for the Markov gap, and prove that it is a faithful witness of irreducible tripartite entanglement for crystal quenches. We discuss several key aspects of their tripartite entanglement dynamics through the lens of the Markov gap. We confront our results with exact lattice numerics and find perfect agreement in the hydrodynamic regime.
