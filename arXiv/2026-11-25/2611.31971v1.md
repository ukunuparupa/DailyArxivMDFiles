# Field-Dependent Kinetics, Exact Kinks, and a Moduli-Space Route from Self-Dual Yang-Mills Theory

## authors:
#Ji_Wang

## summary:
We study scalar-field models in 1+1 dimensions with field-dependent kinetic coefficients, motivated by self-dual Yang-Mills (SDYM) theory as a generator of integrable equations. We derive the Euler-Lagrange equation, traveling-wave reduction, and first integral, and solve the inverse problem: given a kinetic structure and a prescribed tanh kink, which potential makes it exact? A quadratic spatial deformation with a canonical temporal sector yields a sextic potential. We obtain: (i) a closed-form kink energy with a Bogomolny-type formulation, independent of the odd parameter, which nonetheless shapes the energy density asymmetrically; (ii) vacuum curvature relations tying the spectrum to the kinetic function at the vacua; (iii) two propositions on speed families, plus a lemma showing single-speed data probe only one combination of the kinetic functions; (iv) a unified admissibility analysis in which positivity of the spatial kinetic function controls hyperbolicity, the potential bound along the kink, and the vacuum masses; (v) a self-adjoint fluctuation operator with a closed-form potential whose asymptotes are the vacuum masses, an exact translation zero mode, and numerical spectra with no negative mode on the admissible region, evidence for spectral stability. Exact SDYM reductions yield integrable equations, whereas the deformed model is generically non-integrable and cannot be an exact reduction. The correct bridge is the moduli-space route: SDYM reduces to the Bogomolny equations, whose BPS monopole and dyon dynamics produce field-dependent kinetic terms; any smooth one-dimensional moduli metric expands, to second order, to the quadratic model studied here, and the Taub-NUT metric of well-separated dyons gives an explicit realization. Full identification of the sextic potential within a specific gauge-theory moduli dynamics remains open and is formulated as a research program.
