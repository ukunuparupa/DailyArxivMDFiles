# Frobenius $2$-algebras and $R$-matrices

## authors:
#Hibiki_Shibuya
#Yuji_Terashima

## summary:
In this paper, introducing Frobenius $2$-algebras, which are categorical generalizations of Frobenius algebras, we show that every Frobenius $2$-algebra determines an $R$-matrix satisfying the Yang-Baxter equation in the monoidal category of endomorphisms of its underlying object. Moreover, we illustrate this construction through three examples: a pair of objects in a braided monoidal category, a Hopf algebra with invertible antipode, and representations of the Heisenberg double of a finite-dimensional Hopf algebra. In particular, in the third example, we recover Kashaev's construction of the universal $R$-matrix. Thus, our construction provides a categorical generalization of Kashaev's construction.
