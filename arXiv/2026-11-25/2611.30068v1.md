# Reconstructing Pure-State OTOCs from Forward-Time Linear Response

## authors:
#Yu-Hang_Jin
#Xudong_He
#Yu_Chen

## summary:
We show that the connected out-of-time-ordered correlator (OTOC) of any pure state can be reconstructed exactly from commutator linear responses alone. The protocol requires no time reversal, no ancilla, no double copy, and no weak measurement: a unitary string pulse, forward many-body evolution, and a single local readout suffice. An average over a complete basis of pulse generators yields the OTOC exactly. We further show that the measurement can be made sparse by restricting the pulses to a fixed operator weight, which turns the truncation into an analytically controlled spectral filter: the deterministic bias can be computed before the experiment and cleanly separated from the statistical sampling variance. For Gaussian states, the finite-weight response is exactly proportional to the full OTOC after a known combinatorial rescaling. For quantum chaotic systems, the typical residual is suppressed as a power law in the sector size. Exact-diagonalization benchmarks in the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev model and a nonintegrable Ising chain confirm these predictions.
