# Thermodynamics of QCD+QED from the lattice

## authors:
#G_Endrődi
#E_Garnacho-Velasco
#G_Markó
#L_Pethő

## summary:
QED corrections to observables in QCD are by now well-understood at zero temperature, where QED effects are perturbative and required for precision physics observables, such as the neutron-proton mass difference or the muon anomalous magnetic moment, among many others. However, there are indications in the literature that at finite temperature the introduction of QED can lead to non-perturbative effects, affecting the structure of the phase diagram, since the combined theory is exactly center-symmetric. In this proceedings article, we analyze the structure of the combined theory and the different strategies to simulate it on the lattice, as well as presenting novel results of lattice QCD+QED simulations at finite temperature, focusing on understanding the role of the electromagnetic coupling in the thermodynamics of this system. We also discuss new features that arise in the combined theory and the challenges that these simulations entail, which require the development of new algorithms in order to understand the phase diagram of QCD+QED from first principles.
