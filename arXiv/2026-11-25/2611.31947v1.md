# M5-branes wrapped on topological disks from maximal 7D gauged supergravity

## authors:
#Patharadanai_Nuchino
#Parinya_Karndumri

## summary:
We study new supersymmetric $AdS_5 \times Σ$ solutions for $Σ$ being a topological disk from the maximal gauged supergravity in seven dimensions with $CSO(p,q,5-p-q)$ gauge groups. These gauged supergravities can be obtained from consistent truncations of eleven-dimensional supergravity on $H^{p,q} \circ T^{5-p-q}$. We find a number of solutions with $SO(2)\times SO(2)$ and $SO(2)\times SO(3)$ symmetries from $SO(5)$ and $SO(3,2)$ gauge groups as well as $SO(2)_{\textrm{diag}}$ symmetric solutions from $SO(5)$ and $SO(4,1)$ gauge groups. Solutions with $SO(2)\times SO(3)$ symmetry preserve $16$ supercharges while the other solutions preserve only $8$ supercharges. Although some of the solutions give a holographic description of the dual $N=1$ SCFTs in four dimensions with finite central charges, most of the solutions describe codimension-2 defects within $N=(2,0)$ theories in six dimensions. For solutions with $SO(2)\times SO(2)$ and $SO(2)_{\textrm{diag}}$ symmetries from $SO(5)$ gauge group for which a complete truncation ansatz of eleven-dimensional supergravity on $S^4$ is known, we also uplift the solutions to eleven dimensions and compute the holographic central charge of the dual four-dimensional $N=1$ SCFT with $SO(2)_{\textrm{diag}}$ symmetry.
