# D0-brane bound states in Matrix theory: nonperturbative bounds on matrix extent, noncommutativity and spatial shape

## authors:
#Tetsuyuki_Muramatsu

## summary:
In Matrix theory, a single eleven-dimensional supergraviton is associated with a BPS bound state of D0-branes. Assuming the existence of a normalized physical BPS state, we derive nonperturbative bounds on its matrix extent, noncommutativity and spatial shape from supersymmetry in the BFSS model.  The probability of matrix configurations smaller than a fixed fraction of an explicit extent scale is exponentially suppressed in the square of the number $N$ of D0-branes. In Yang-Mills variables, this scale for the trace-normalized extent is of order the cube root of the 't Hooft coupling. Relative noncommutativity, measured by a dimensionless commutator ratio, has an expectation bounded by a quantity of order the inverse of $N$, and its probability of exceeding a sufficiently large fixed multiple of the inverse of $N$ vanishes at large $N$.  Concentration of the matrix extent along a fixed number of principal directions fewer than nine at large $N$ requires the total extent relative to a corresponding reference scale to diverge in probability. It also requires relative noncommutativity to be suppressed more strongly than the inverse of $N$, in every fixed finite moment of order at least one.  These bounds apply to the full quantum wave function, including off-diagonal matrix degrees of freedom, and constrain the quantum structure of D0-brane bound states and the possible large-$N$ matrix geometries in Matrix theory.
