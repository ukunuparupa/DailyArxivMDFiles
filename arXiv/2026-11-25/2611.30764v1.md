# Fate of Vainshtein Screening for UV/IR Mixing with a $P(X)$ Higgs Classicalizer

## authors:
#Eléana_Berteaux
#Florian_Nortier

## summary:
Classicalization is a non-Wilsonian framework in which an effective field theory self-completes above its strong-coupling scale by generating semiclassical objects that decay into many soft quanta, thereby realizing ultraviolet-infrared mixing. A key requirement of this mechanism is that the force carrier be lighter than the strong-coupling scale. A natural application would be to embed classicalization in the electroweak Higgs sector to address the little hierarchy problem. In this work, we investigate whether Higgs classicalization can occur within the $P(X)$ class of gauged k-essence and k-chameleon models. For classicalization to occur, the theory must support Vainshtein screening around a sharply localized source. Our results show that it fails to do so, even when one attempts to engineer a symbiosis with chameleon screening through a gauge kinetic function, owing to a strong-coupling breakdown inside the screening region. These $P(X)$ theories therefore fail to provide a suitable framework for Higgs classicalization. Nevertheless, we emphasize that our results, obtained for a specific class of models, should not be interpreted as a no-go theorem for Higgs classicalization.
