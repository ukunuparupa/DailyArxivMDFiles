# Imprints of quantum space in the ringdown phase of Kerr black holes

## authors:
#Nikola_Herceg
#Tajron_Jurić
#A_Naveena_Kumara
#Andjelo_Samsarov
#Ivica_Smolić

## summary:
We investigate quantum-space corrections to the gravitational quasinormal modes (QNMs) of Kerr black holes within the framework of noncommutative geometry. The analysis employs a pseudo-semi-Killing Drinfeld twist, which preserves the classical Kerr geometry as an exact background solution while introducing nontrivial quantum corrections to linearized gravitational perturbations. Working to first order in the black hole spin and the quantum deformation parameter, including their mixed contribution, we derive a Schrödinger-like master equation for axial gravitational perturbations with a frequency- and spin-dependent effective potential. We compute the fundamental QNM frequencies using the WKB approximation and Leaver's continued-fraction method. Our results reveal corrections to the QNM oscillation frequencies and damping rates arising from rotation, pure quantum effects, and their interplay. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first systematic analysis of gravitational metric perturbations in a noncommutative Kerr black hole spacetime using a Drinfeld twist, extending prior work beyond the Schwarzschild case. Our findings demonstrate that quantum deformation effects alter both the oscillation frequencies and damping rates of quasinormal modes, with spin-quantum coupling introducing additional spin-dependent corrections to the Kerr QNM spectrum. Using a Kerr-anchored phenomenological prescription, we estimate the fractional frequency and damping-time shifts for selected merger remnants from the GWTC-3 and GWTC-5.0 catalogs. These estimates illustrate the potential signatures of quantum corrections in gravitational-wave ringdown.
