# Central charges as black-hole hair in weakly coupled trace-anomaly gravity

## authors:
#Francisco_Tello-Ortiz
#A_Vásquez
#Kazuharu_Bamba
#Ghulam_Mustafa

## summary:
We study black holes in Gabadadze's weakly coupled effective theory of the gravitational trace anomaly, keeping both the Euler and the Weyl central charges. We prove that a constant anomalyon on any Ricci-flat geometry, the Kerr family included, solves the full theory if and only if the two central charges coincide. Otherwise, the black holes continuously connected to this branch carry scalar hair whose charge is fixed by the surface gravity, the horizon topology and the difference of central charges alone: maximally supersymmetric matter leaves black holes bald, the Standard Model and photon-dominated sectors produce hair of definite sign, and conformal collider bounds confine it to a universal window. Electric charge separates the two anomaly invariants: the hair reverses sign at a critical charge-to-mass ratio set by the ratio of central charges, its extremal value retains only the Weyl charge, and the Euler charge governs the near-horizon growth of the anomalyon. For every unitary matter sector, the hair regular at the event horizon diverges at the Cauchy horizon. Finally, once the higher-derivative Weyl operator is order-reduced, the background, the anomalyon-graviton mixing and all propagation speeds depend only on the difference of central charges, which extends the no-ghost and Laplacian stability conditions to the full anomaly.
