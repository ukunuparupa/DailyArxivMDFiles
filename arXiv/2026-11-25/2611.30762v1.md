# Conservative tidal effects to third post-Minkowskian order in scalar-tensor theories

## authors:
#Zhengwen_Liu
#Hongbin_Wang

## summary:
Using worldline effective field theory, we compute the conservative finite-size contributions to the impulse and scattering angle of nonspinning compact binaries in massless scalar-tensor theories with a scalar-Gauss-Bonnet coupling through third post-Minkowskian (3PM) order. We retain the full velocity dependence at linear order in the tidal response coefficients. The calculation covers the scalar dipole and quadrupole and the electric, magnetic, and mixed quadrupoles and octupoles. Among them, the scalar dipole enters the dynamics already at third post-Newtonian (3PN) order, two orders before the gravitational quadrupole. At 3PM, the tidal correction to the angle also depends on the first scalar derivatives of the responses and contains terms linear in the Gauss-Bonnet coupling. The results reproduce the general-relativistic tidal angle and the post-Newtonian tidal dynamics through 5PN, and a test-body calculation confirms their leading order in the mass ratio, including the Gauss-Bonnet terms for a companion with vanishing sensitivity.
