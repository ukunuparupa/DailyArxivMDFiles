# Elliptic spin Ruijsenaars--Schneider model via the Poisson reduction of the affine Heisenberg double

## authors:
#Gleb_Arutyunov
#Lukas_Hardi
#Davide_Polvara

## summary:
We construct the Hamiltonian structure of the elliptic spin Ruijsenaars--Schneider model with dynamical inhomogeneities by Poisson reduction of the affine Heisenberg double extended by an affine oscillator manifold. This construction extends the Heisenberg double approach to spin Ruijsenaars--Schneider models from the hyperbolic to the elliptic case. We solve the moment map constraint and derive the Poisson algebras of the invariant Lax operator and spin variables. A realization with finitely many degrees of freedom is obtained by evaluating and localizing the invariant oscillators at distinct points of the affine parameter. Consistency requires these points to be dynamical so that their nontrivial Poisson brackets with the spin variables cancel derivative terms that would otherwise obstruct closure of the Poisson bracket on finitely many degrees of freedom. The resulting Poisson brackets and Hamiltonian coincide with those recently obtained from the elliptic Coulomb branch of the necklace quiver. Our construction thus provides an independent derivation of this Hamiltonian structure and establishes a direct link between Poisson reductions and Coulomb branches.
