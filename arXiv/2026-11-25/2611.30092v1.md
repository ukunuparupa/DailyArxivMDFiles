# Boundary spectroscopy of hidden near-horizon reflection

## authors:
#Yiling_Chen
#Jiayue_Yang
#Zhen_Zhong
#Robert_B_Mann
#Jie_Jiang
#Ming_Zhang

## summary:
A classical black hole horizon absorbs completely, and any deviation would be hidden in the bulk. For a scalar field in a rotating Bañados-Teitelboim-Zanelli exterior, we derive an exact boundary response of a corotating passive wall near the horizon. An Airy filter gives its spectrum, and positive energy excludes growing modes. On any frequency band the response identifies a constant wall impedance and the wall position, which enter orthogonally with weights determined by the spectral function. The boundary thus sees a reflecting wall, whereas a perfect absorber is exponentially hidden.
