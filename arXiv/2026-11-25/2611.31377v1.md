# A Kerr-black-hole worldline action to $\mathcal{O}(G^5)$

## authors:
#Yilber_Fabian_Bautista
#Mathias_Driesse
#Kays_Haddad
#Gustav_Uhre_Jakobsen

## summary:
We establish a worldline action which correctly reproduces Kerr-black-hole dynamics up to fifth order in Newton's constant in a physical post-Minkowskian counting. This encompasses operators up to second order in the Riemann curvature tensor and up to fourth order in the spin tensor. Doing so entails performing the full effective-field-theory computation of the amplitude for a gravitational-wave scattering off of a spinning compact object up to four-loop order, and matching the result to the predictions from linear black hole perturbation theory. Quadratic-in-curvature operators which must be added to match successfully include time-reversal anti-symmetric ones at linear and cubic spin orders, capturing absorption across the black hole horizon, and conservative quadratic-in-spin ones. As an immediate application of our action, we compute the leading-order change in spin magnitude, and reproduce the leading change in mass, for a Kerr black hole in a scattering event with a second compact body, considering a generic orientation of the black hole's spin.
