# Superdaisy Resummation in Theories with Broken Gauge Symmetries

## authors:
#Martin_Münzenberg
#Carlos_Tamarit

## summary:
We extend the partial dressing resummation scheme for the finite-temperature effective potential to the gauge and ghost sectors of spontaneously broken gauge theories and apply the resulting framework to the Standard Model. Going beyond the usual high-temperature treatment, we retain the full mass dependence of the thermal self-energies and determine the scalar, gauge-boson and ghost masses self-consistently from a coupled system of gap equations. The solutions show that corrections beyond the leading $\mathcal{O}(T^2)$ approximation are particularly relevant for the gauge-boson thermal masses, while they induce a comparatively small shift in the scalar spectrum for the benchmark studied. The self-consistent gauge-boson dressing in the longitudinal sector reduces the barrier in the finite-temperature Higgs effective potential. Implementing the resummation in the transverse sector removes the barrier and would seem to exclude a first-order phase transition in accordance with lattice results. While such resummation is not quantitatively reliable, due to the transverse excitations becoming strongly coupled, the results showcase the crucial role played by transverse modes in determining the nature of the electroweak phase transition.
