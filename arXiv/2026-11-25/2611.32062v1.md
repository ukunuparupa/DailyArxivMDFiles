# Entanglement structure in conformal Y-junctions

## authors:
#Yan_Liu
#You-Jie_Zeng

## summary:
We study how entanglement constrains energy transmission in conformal Y-junctions using holographic and free-field models. We define pairwise effective central charges from mutual information as a natural extension of the interface entanglement coefficient. For holographic junctions, we formulate a branched RT prescription and show that the resulting entropies satisfy monogamy of mutual information. We find that the pairwise effective central charge does not always bound the corresponding energy transmission, but a correction involving finite tripartite information restores the bound, with a sharp coefficient determined by limiting saturation. Free-boson and Dirac-fermion junctions also violate the pairwise bound, but the tripartite correction fails because the tripartite information is positive or vanishing, respectively. Analytic limits and numerical results instead support a candidate square-root correction based on L-entropy, which vanishes in the holographic complete-leg prescription considered here. We discuss the possibility of a common multipartite information bound in general conformal junctions.
