# Radiation from periods: Bremsstrahlung functions, wound loops, latitudes and integral constraints in ABJM

## authors:
#Michelangelo_Preti

## summary:
The Bremsstrahlung functions of the 1/2BPS and 1/6BPS Wilson lines of ABJM theory measure their response to small deformations. We show that a single geometric object, the spectral curve of the localisation matrix model, captures both of them in closed form, together with the planar wound and latitude Wilson loops at any complex winding and framing. The fermionic function turns out to be a period of the second kind: a single hypergeometric function, which solves a second-order differential equation and is quasimodular. The bosonic one is not a period but a ratio of periods. At every coupling it is the smaller of the two, so radiation grows along the defect flow from the bosonic to the fermionic line. The key role is played by a new observable, the winding velocity, which turns out to be the 't Hooft coupling shifted by four times the fermionic function. It is at the same time the winding derivative of the wound loop and the curvature of the latitude, and the curve captures it in both incarnations through its logarithmic periods and through its periods with two charges. It is also a datum of the defect conformal field theory on the 1/2BPS line, since it fixes a new integrated four-point function of the tilt operators. All results extend to ABJ theory when the ranks of the gauge groups are not equal.
