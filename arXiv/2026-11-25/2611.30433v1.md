# Darboux transformation for the ASDYM equations: Solitons and instantons

## authors:
#Shangshuai_Li

## summary:
Using a one-fold Darboux transformation for the anti-self-dual Yang--Mills equations, we express the Yang--Mills Lagrangian density in terms of $τ$-functions. For solitons constructed from exponential functions, we show that the associated $τ$-functions admit a parametrization by arbitrary points in the Grassmannian Gr(N,M;$\mathbb{C}$), and construct the corresponding Yang--Mills fields. For instantons arising from the Atiyah--Drinfeld--Hitchin--Manin (ADHM) construction, we reinterpret the ADHM construction in terms of a one-fold Darboux transformation, recover Osborn's formula for the Yang--Mills Lagrangian density, and obtain the corresponding gauge connection.
