# A note on Kaluza-Klein theories

## authors:
#Fr{é}d{é}ric_H{é}lein

## summary:
We present a variant of the Kaluza-Klein theory that was introduced in previous papers (ATMP, 2020; LMP 2022; Ann. Fac. Sci. Toulouse, 2025), based on an action functional whose critical points, in the presence of point-wise constraints, lead-under reasonable assumptions-to the existence of a principal fiber bundle and solutions to the Einstein-Yang-Mills system. The difference is that it is not necessary to impose a point-wise constraint.
