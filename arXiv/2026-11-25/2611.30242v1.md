# The type-II double copy for relativistic Rindler fluids

## authors:
#Yu-Cheng_Wang
#Jing-Rui_Zhang
#Yun-Long_Zhang

## summary:
Within the Weyl double copy framework, we study a mixed single-copy construction for the Petrov type-II geometry of first-order relativistic Rindler fluids. Working on a common fixed flat Rindler background with consistent spinor conventions and a real field strength representation, we algebraically factorize the Weyl spinor into two symmetric rank-two Maxwell spinors and test the Maxwell equations and the zeroth copy scalar equation up to the first order in the relativistic gradient expansion. We separate the degeneration parameter from the gradient expansion parameter and analyze the continuous degeneration to the Petrov type-D and type-N branches as the complex shear and vorticity tend to zero, together with the preservation of the accuracy of the Maxwell and scalar field equations along each path. We also compare the relativistic construction with the nonrelativistic case.
