# Reproducing kernel and matrix representation of the deformed quantum optical density operator of $\mathcal{R}(p,q)-$super-coherent states on $\mathcal{B}_0\oplus\mathcal{B}_1$ fermionic quantum algebra

## authors:
#Kawèyim_Lankpetre
#Isiaka_Aremua
#Joseph_Désiré_Bukweli_Kyemba
#Bienvenu_Gnim_Adewi

## summary:
We study the $\mathcal{R}(p,q)-$super-coherent states with two Grassmann variables $(ζ_1,ζ_2)$ built on the superalgebra $\mathcal{B}_0\oplus\mathcal{B}_1$ associated with the $\mathcal{R}(p,q)-$deformed quantum algebras of Hounkonnou and Bukweli-Kyemba, the two dilation parameters $φ_1(p,q)$ and $φ_2(p,q)$ being meromorphic functions of two variables evaluated at $(p,q)$. The graded super-Fock space splits into four sectors: bosonic, $φ_1-$fermionic, $φ_2-$fermionic and bi-fermionic. In each of them we establish the diagonal representation of the density operator by means of the $\mathcal{R}(p,q)-$derivative, angular integration and Berezin integration, and we give a unified compact form in terms of sectorial parametric functions. The total density operator is obtained in explicit form as the sum of the four sectorial contributions, and can be read as a mixture of the four sectors. We define the sectorial reproducing kernels, obtained from the resolution of the identity, we compute the matrix elements of the density operator in the super-coherent state basis, and we prove the hermiticity, positivity and matrix multiplication properties of the kernels, together with the series expansion of their diagonal values. An integral formulation relates the density operator, its density function and the reproducing kernel, the latter being the function associated with the identity operator. Berezin quantization in the bosonic sector yields the quantized operators associated with $z$, $\bar z$, $|z|^2$, $z^2$ and $\bar z^2$ and their mean values; the dispersions of the super-position and super-momentum operators show that the super-coherent states are intelligent states.
