# Beyond the Thermodynamic-Volume Limit in Holographic Complexity

## authors:
#Di_Wu
#Robert_B_Mann

## summary:
In the large black hole regime, holographic complexity obeys a thermodynamic-volume scaling law, while the finite-size approach to this limit remains unresolved. In this Letter, we develop an analytic description of finite-size corrections to this scaling for complexity of formation in the complexity=volume (CV) proposal of odd-dimensional equal-spinning Myers-Perry-AdS black holes. We find that the leading finite-size correction retains state dependence that is absent from the limiting thermodynamic-volume law. This correction changes sign at a distinguished Critical Horizon Ratio (CHR), where the generic leading finite-size contribution vanishes and convergence to the thermodynamic-volume law is correspondingly accelerated. We further determine the dimension dependence of the CHR analytically, showing that it approaches a finite nonextremal value at large dimension with a calculable leading finite-dimensional correction. These results show that the finite-size approach can provide a new probe of holographic structure, motivating its exploration across different realizations of holographic complexity and its eventual characterization in the dual field theory.
