# The Schwarzschild-Kerr crossover of high overtone quasinormal modes

## authors:
#Yasuyuki_Hatsuda

## summary:
A slowly rotating black hole is close to a nonrotating one, but its highly damped quasinormal spectrum is not. The Schwarzschild approximation holds only up to overtone numbers of order $χ^{-4/3}$, where $χ$ is the dimensionless spin. We find that the frequency shift at this scale is a universal function, derived from the wave boundary conditions with an error estimate. It is given by the spectrum of a sextic oscillator, via the correspondence between ordinary differential equations and integrable models. It recovers the Schwarzschild $\log 3$ and organizes the singular Kerr coefficients.
