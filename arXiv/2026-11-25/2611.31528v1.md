# Exact Scalar and Fermionic Propagators in a Uniform Electric Background

## authors:
#Emerson_B_S_Corrêa

## summary:
By implementing an analytical hybrid framework that combines the Ritus eigenfunction technique with Schwinger's proper-time formalism, we construct exact propagators for charged scalar and fermionic fields in a constant, homogeneous, external electric field in four-dimensional Euclidean space. A central advantage of this approach is that the resulting propagators retain a four-dimensional momentum representation after the spectral summation, connecting the structure of the Ritus basis with the operator formulation of the proper-time method, without requiring the full traditional derivation of Schwinger's approach and the coordinate restrictions originally associated with the Ritus method for fermions. The translationally non-invariant component emerging after the Laguerre-polynomial summation is isolated through a suitable change of variables, yielding translationally invariant kernels and maintaining the background field strength unchanged. As a consistency check, we successfully rederive Schwinger's seminal expression for the fermionic pair production rate.
