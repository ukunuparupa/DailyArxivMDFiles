# A colored look at anyons and parastatistics

## authors:
#Francesco_Toppan

## summary:
Color Lie (super)algebras were introduced in 1978 by Rittenberg and Wyler as generalizations of ordinary Lie (super)algebras. These color structures, graded by abelian groups, are defined by brackets which satisfy generalized Jacobi identities. Only recently the relevance of color Lie (super)algebras to physical applications has been pointed out and proved. The color framework not only describes, but also provides physical signatures for two broad types of parastatistics (particles beyond ordinary bosons/fermions): i) paraparticles which are exchanged via the permutation group and can exist in any space dimension D and ii) anyons (exchanged via the braid group), which exist up to D=2 space dimensions. This paper presents an update of the recent advancements which motivate a brand new "colored look" at questions involving parastatistics and braid statistics.
