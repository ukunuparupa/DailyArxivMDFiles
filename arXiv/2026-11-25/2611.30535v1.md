# Chiral crossover responses in a magnetic field: Finite-$μ_5$ matching in the NJL model

## authors:
#Jingyi_Chao

## summary:
We investigate how independent information at finite chiral chemical potential $μ_5$ constrains pseudocritical-temperature responses in the Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model under a magnetic field. Vacuum calibration alone leaves the coefficient $c_r$ of the additional interaction $δΩ_r=c_rμ_5^2(M-m_0)^2$ undetermined. We compare soft-cutoff (SC) and Pauli-Villars (PV) vacuum regularizations, each combined with the direct treatment (D) or the magnetic finite-difference scheme (MFDS). The reference SC and PV treatments exhibit opposite finite-$μ_5$ responses already at $eB=0$. Matching the $eB=0$ scalar-susceptibility peak-temperature ratio predicted by unitarized chiral perturbation theory gives $c_{\mathrm{SC}}=-0.07112$ and $c_{\mathrm{PV}}=0.22479$. Holding these coefficients fixed at $eB=0.1,0.2,0.4$ GeV$^2$ reduces the four-treatment spread of the normalized finite-$μ_5$-induced mass-inflection temperature change by factors of approximately $7.5$-$15.3$ at $μ_5=0.15$ GeV and $18.1$-$32.6$ at $μ_5=0.30$ GeV. For the distinct scalar-susceptibility peak-temperature ratio at $μ_5=0.15$ GeV, the spread decreases by factors of approximately $74$, $27$, and $14$ at the same magnetic fields, respectively. The reduction in regularization-dependent finite-$μ_5$ responses thus persists at the sampled nonzero magnetic fields without further matching.
