# Rotating Black Holes from Scaling Transformations in Horava Gravity

## authors:
#Deniz_O_Devecioglu
#Mu-In_Park

## summary:
We study the scaling transformations that change the physical parameters of solutions in low-energy Horava gravity. In particular, we present a simple scaling transformation that generates rotating black-hole solutions in low-energy Horava gravity from known relativistic solutions. As explicit examples, we obtain "Doran-like" and "Kerr-Schild-like" rotating solutions in Horava gravity. We find that both solutions are horizon-penetrating, but their null horizons are not Killing horizons. We also find that, although their singularity structures differ from each other, chronology is protected in both solutions, as in the previously obtained Kerr-like solutions in Boyer-Lindquist coordinates.
