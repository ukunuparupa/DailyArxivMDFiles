# Symmetry resolved spread complexity for BMN matrix model

## authors:
#Dibakar_Roychowdhury

## summary:
We investigate the symmetry-resolved spread complexity of the Berenstein-Maldacena-Nastase (BMN) matrix model under a global $U(1)$ symmetry acting within the transverse $SO(6)$ scalar sector. Decomposing the time-evolved charged thermofield double (cTFD) state into orthogonal fixed-charge sectors, we examine the return amplitude, spectral moments, tridiagonal Lanczos coefficients, and early-time spread complexity across the low-temperature confined ($βμ\gg 1$) and finite-temperature deconfined ($T \gg μ$) phases. In the low-temperature gapped regime, effective sector dynamics map onto decoupled, displaced harmonic oscillators with a Casimir-like zero-point offset, yielding factorized moments, linear drift in diagonal Lanczos coefficients $a_n^{(q)}$, and sub-linear $\sqrt{n}$ off-diagonal growth with thermally suppressed initial hopping. Charge-dependent Boltzmann weights, vacuum shifts, and degeneracies break complexity equipartition, while inter-sector coherence and charge dispersion enforce an early-time positivity bound $ΔC(t) = C_{\text{total}}(t) - \bar{C}(t) \ge 0$. In the \emph{finite}-temperature continuum phase, modeling the planar large-$N$ spectral density as a thermal Gaussian gives constant diagonal coefficients $a_n^{(q)} = \bar{E}_q$, universal square-root scaling $b_n^{(q)} = σ_E \sqrt{n}$, and a quadratic spread launch $\mathcal{C}^{(q)}(t) = σ_E^2(q,T)\,t^2 + \mathcal{O}(t^4)$ governed by the backreacted sector energy variance. Holographically, these regimes distinguish coherent string fluctuations in horizonless Lin-Maldacena (LM) droplet geometries from dissipative scrambling near charged ten-dimensional black D0-brane horizons.
