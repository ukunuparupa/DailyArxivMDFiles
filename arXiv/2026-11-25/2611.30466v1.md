# Dissipative Tidal Love Numbers of Neutron Stars from Viscous Heating

## authors:
#Nikola_Savić
#Filippo_Vernizzi

## summary:
We compute the leading low-frequency dissipative tidal response of nonrotating relativistic neutron stars. We formulate the response in a worldline effective field theory. We determine the linear dissipative Love number in two complementary ways: from the redshifted viscous entropy production inside the star and from the imaginary, frequency-odd part of the exterior metric. The first method reduces the calculation to an integral over the static equilibrium tide; the second provides an independent check. We apply our approach to publicly available equations of state and assess the corresponding correction to the gravitational-wave phase of neutron-star binaries. For an equal-mass $1.4+1.4M_\odot$ binary with both stars at a redshifted temperature $T_\infty=10^5\,\mathrm K$ and a standard electron shear viscosity, tidal dissipation shifts the gravitational-wave phase by less than $10^{-3}$ cycles between $20\,{\rm Hz}$ and $1\,{\rm kHz}$ for the four equations of state considered. Including the resulting rise in the stars' temperature in a simple thermal model reduces the shift below $10^{-6}$ cycles. For the viscosity model considered here, shear-viscous tidal dissipation in cold neutron stars is negligible for gravitational-wave phasing. This suggests that a measurable dissipative tidal signature would have to come from a different mechanism.
