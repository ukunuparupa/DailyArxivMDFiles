# Classification of Chern-Simons Couplings and Deformed Pull-Backs on D-Branes

## authors:
#Armin_Hatefi
#Ehsan_Hatefi

## summary:
We establish a geometric framework for classifying higher-derivative Ramond-Ramond (RR) couplings on Dp-branes, focusing on the complete scattering amplitude of a closed-string RR state, a gauge field, and two transverse scalars. The low-energy expansion reveals infinite massless poles and local interaction terms that are beyond standard Myers and Taylor expansions. We propose a higher-class deformation of the pullback using mixed Chern-Simons transgression operators to address these complexities. We also propose a generalized class Chern character and three conjectures: (i) the finite dimensionality of independent couplings at fixed derivative order, (ii) the representation of all-order mixed RR-gauge-scalar interactions through finitely many effective even forms, and (iii) the generation of higher-derivative contact terms from finitely many primitive tensor structures. Our framework provides a systematic mathematical foundation for reconstructing D-brane effective actions and exploring their underlying geometric and cohomological structures. We also introduce a higher-class pullback operator \( P_{\infty} \) and mixed transgression operators \( \mathcal{T}_{\infty} \), emphasizing amplitude-derived pole residues and local contact terms as key physical data. This formalism distinguishes admissible tensor structures, presenting characteristic-form aspects as conjectures alongside their validity and their constraints.
