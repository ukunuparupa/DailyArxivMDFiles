# New analytic constraints for the soft anomalous dimension bootstrap at three loops

## authors:
#Einan_Gardi
#Luke_Lippstreu
#Andrew_J_McLeod
#Laura_Walsh
#Zehao_Zhu
#Enrico_Zunino

## summary:
The soft anomalous dimension governs the universal long-distance singularities of gauge theory amplitudes. It exhibits a highly constrained analytic structure and has been shown to be amenable to bootstrap methods. In this paper we revisit the relation between unitarity cuts and sequential discontinuities of Feynman integrals and use it to derive new constraints on the soft anomalous dimension. At three loops, we show that certain sequential discontinuities in non-overlapping two-particle channels vanish for every web contributing to the quadrupole function that correlates four coloured partons. These constraints hold for arbitrary parton masses, and we explicitly verify them for the known massless and one-mass cases. We then demonstrate their constraining power by substantially strengthening the massless bootstrap and carrying out the first bootstrap construction of the one-mass quadrupole function. The new constraints are largely complementary to those arising from the absence of unphysical branch points, symmetries, and collinear and Regge limits. With their help, we determine each quadrupole function within the assumed function space up to a single coefficient, which we fix by matching to existing diagrammatic results. These results open the way to bootstrapping the three-loop soft anomalous dimension for amplitudes with several massive partons.
