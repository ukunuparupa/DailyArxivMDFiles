# Analytic $k$-essence Inflation

## authors:
#V_K_Oikonomou

## summary:
In this work we develop a fully analytic $k$-essence inflation solution which is compatible with the observational constraints on inflation, without the need for the slow-roll assumption being imposed dynamically in the field equations. The analytic solution is obtained for a kinetic scalar sector of the form $K(X)= \frac{X^n}{X_s^{n-1}}$ and for the scalar field satisfying a tracking condition $\dotφ^2=γH^{-m}$. For this class of theories, the sound speed of the primordial curvature perturbations is constant, so no gradient instabilities occur in the theory. The Hubble rate, the scale factor and the scalar field can be analytically expressed as functions of the cosmic time, and the observational indices of inflation depend solely on the parameters $n$ and $m$. The resulting evolution is inflationary and it develops a pressure singularity a few $e$-foldings after the end of inflation. However, using qualitative and quantitative numerical arguments, we show that the pressure singularity is classically avoided when the matter fluids of radiation and cold dark matter are taken into account.
