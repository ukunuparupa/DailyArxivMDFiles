# The Eigenfunction Method to Calculate the Klein--Gordon Propa-gator in an Inhomogeneous Magnetic Field

## authors:
#Emerson_B_S_Corrê
#Mateus_G_Lima
#Suellen_C_Q_Arruda

## summary:
In this paper, we use the Eigenfunction method (Ritus' method) to calculate the Klein--Gordon propagator under an external magnetic field with an exponential damping factor $b$, in a four-dimensional Euclidean space. We write the propagator of scalar field in terms of plane waves and Laguerre polynomials. Also the eigenvalues associated to differential operator show the quantization in the $\mathrm{xy}$-plane through the Landau levels. We apply the calculated propagator to the mass parameter of an interacting boson system under an exponentially decaying magnetic field.
