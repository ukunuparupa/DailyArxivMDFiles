# New relations of tree-level string amplitudes from the massless prescription

## authors:
#Chen_Huang
#Yi-Xiao_Tao

## summary:
We discover new relations between the string corrections to $(m+1)$-point field-theory amplitudes and $m$-point string amplitudes at tree level from the massless prescription of vertex operators.
