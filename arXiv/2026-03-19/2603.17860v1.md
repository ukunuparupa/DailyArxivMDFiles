# Discrete Dyson-Schwinger equations

## authors:
#Marco_Frasca

## summary:
We develop the discrete set of Dyson-Schwinger equations for scalar fields. We show that their solution is Gaussian as expected from the theorems of Aizenman and of Aizenman and Duminil-Copin for $d\ge 4$. Extension to lower dimensionality fails, as it should, by observing the the triviality theorems used in our proof are not applicable in such cases.
