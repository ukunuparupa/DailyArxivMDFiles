# Probing the small-scale primordial power spectrum via relic neutrinos and acoustic reheating

## authors:
#Giovanni_Piccoli
#Sunny_Vagnozzi
#Joseph_Silk

## summary:
We show that the dissipation of small-scale perturbations through diffusion damping after neutrino decoupling lowers the present-day neutrino temperature compared to the expected value of $1.96\,{\text{K}}$. This reduces the relic neutrino abundance by an amount controlled by the integral of the primordial curvature power spectrum $Δ_{\cal R}^2(k)$. We find that a relic neutrino detection by PTOLEMY can set limits $Δ_{\cal R}^2(k) \lesssim {\cal O}(0.1)$ on scales $k \lesssim 3 \times 10^5\,{\text{Mpc}^{-1}}$, complementary to limits from Big Bang Nucleosynthesis, spectral distortions, pulsar timing arrays, and future dark ages 21-cm observations.
