# Making the Great Desert Bloom

## authors:
#Jacques_Distler
#Andreas_Karch

## summary:
The absence of new physics beyond neutrino masses between the weak scale and the Planck scale is referred to as the great desert. It is often seen as a nightmare scenario for particle physics. Here we argue, by contrast, that the great desert assumption in fact has dramatic implications: it predicts that the Standard Model has a semi-classical AdS$_4$ vacuum with CFT$_3$ dual and, under reasonable assumptions, must be accompanied by at least one extra dimension significantly larger than Planck length. We also present one plausible candidate CFT$_3$ to be ``the" dual to the Standard Model.
