# The Pfaffian sector of $\mathrm{SO}(2N)$ BPS cohomology: brane excitations, triality and fortuity

## authors:
#Zheng_Miao
#Yuezhang_Tang

## summary:
We study the classical $\frac{1}{16}$-BPS cohomology of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super-Yang-Mills theory with gauge group $\mathrm{SO}(2N)$ in the BMN sector, including the operators built with one $ε$-tensor. These operators are odd under the reflection in $\mathrm{O}(2N)$, carry the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ charge of a D3-brane wrapped on $\mathbb{RP}^3\subset\mathbb{RP}^5$, and form the Pfaffian sector. For $\mathrm{SO}(8)$ we determine this sector through level $j=18$, where the level is twice the total charge of an operator. All but one of its $106$ $\mathrm{SU}(3)_R$ multiplets are products of trace gravitons and excited Pfaffians. The exception is a singlet, represented by the singlet projection of the rank-four member of a four-term family of $ε$-tensor operators. We construct a covering space for the Pfaffian sector in which this family is closed at every rank, and the family defines a non-graviton class for every $N\ge 4$. Triality pairs the singlet with a reflection-even class that is fortuitous with respect to the trace covering. The two classes restrict, along the spinor and the vector embedding of $\mathfrak{so}(7)$ respectively, to the known non-graviton singlet of $\mathrm{SO}(7)$, which is lifted at first order. We show that the two $\mathrm{SO}(8)$ classes, and the family for every $N\ge 4$, are protected to all orders of perturbation theory. Their protection is compatible with the lifting in $\mathrm{SO}(7)$ because restriction to a subalgebra does not commute with the quantum corrections to the supercharge.
