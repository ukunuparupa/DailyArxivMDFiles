# Only the disk: topological obstructions for warped AdS$_6\times S^2$ in Type IIB

## authors:
#Nakwoo_Kim

## summary:
Supersymmetric AdS$_6\times S^2$ solutions of Type IIB supergravity are warped products over a Riemann surface $Σ$. They are determined by two holomorphic differentials on $Σ$, with five-branes at poles on its boundary. We derive restrictions on the genus and the number of boundary components of $Σ$. Without seven-branes or orientifolds, the differentials are taken to be single-valued on $Σ$. Regularity implies that the number of interior zeros of one differential equals the total rotation of the phase of the ratio of the two differentials along the boundary. It also bounds this rotation on each boundary component by the number of poles on that component. Combined with the known count of zeros on the double of $Σ$, these two statements force $Σ$ to be a disk. With mutually local seven-branes and orientifolds as interior punctures, the same conclusion follows under additional assumptions on the global structure of the differentials and on their behavior at the punctures. The disk then admits at most one orientifold puncture. On a disk without punctures, the bound applied between successive poles also recovers the known cyclic ordering of the five-brane charges along the boundary.
