# Scalar/axion Wormholes and Boundary Conditions in Gravitational Path Integrals

## authors:
#Jason_Hallsten
#Jesse_Held
#Zhenyu_Heng
#Donald_Marolf
#Zhencheng_Wang

## summary:
Recent work noted that whether or not a given class of spacetime wormholes contributes to the gravitational path integral can depend strongly on the choice of boundary conditions. In particular, for asymptotically-AdS scalar/axion wormholes with spherical boundaries it was suggested that there are regimes where the same wormhole contributes to a Euclidean low-energy effective field theory computation of the partition function $Z_N$ defined by imposing Neumann boundary conditions on the scalar/axion field, while it fails to contribute to a corresponding partition function $Z_D$ defined by imposing Dirichlet boundary conditions on this field. Here we investigate toy models of related wormholes associated with JT gravity coupled to a scalar field, asymptotically-AdS gravity coupled to an axion/scalar with toroidal boundary conditions, and asymptotically flat gravity coupled to an axion/scalar. In all cases we compare $Z_D$ and $Z_N$ as computed in a truncated low-energy effective theory by an appropriate integral over constrained wormholes. In each case we find that, even though $Z_D$ and $Z_N$ are related by an integral transform, the two generally receive contributions from differing sets of saddles. Along the way, we uncover a variety of additional interesting subtleties associated with semiclassical contributions to our $Z_D$ and $Z_N$.
