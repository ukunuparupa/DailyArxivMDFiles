# Dynamics of a spinning body with scalar multipoles

## authors:
#Jacopo_Lestingi
#Thomas_P_Sotiriou
#Andrew_Spiers
#Andrea_Maselli

## summary:
We construct an effective worldline action for a spinning compact object in theories of gravity with massless, non-minimally coupled scalar fields. To this end, we generalize the standard skeletonized approach used in General Relativity to model the stress-energy tensor of a small body in a background spacetime, dressing its multipolar structure with the scalar field. Within a pole-dipole approximation, we derive a modified set of Mathisson-Papapetrou-Dixon equations of motion for the spinning body, together with the field equations for the scalar field and the metric. We consider both parity-even and parity-odd scalars. We tailor this formalism to model the evolution of asymmetric binaries, such as Extreme Mass Ratio Inspirals (EMRIs), by systematically expanding the relevant equations in powers of the binary mass ratio and deriving the dynamics up to first post-adiabatic (1PA) order, along with the self-force equations needed to determine the particle's acceleration. For a parity-even scalar, the 1PA dynamics depends only on the mass, spin, and scalar charge of the secondary, plus a single self-coupling constant. For a pseudoscalar, we find that the binary dynamics coincides with General Relativity through 1PA.
