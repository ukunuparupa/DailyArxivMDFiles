# Melvin-type Electromagnetized Kerr Black Hole in Kaluza-Klein and Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton-axion Theories

## authors:
#Liang_Ma
#Zhi-Chao_Li
#H_Lu

## summary:
A naive application of solution-generating techniques to construct Melvin-type rotating black holes in the Kaluza-Klein (KK) theory in the presence of both electric and magnetic external fields generically introduces an undesirable Taub-swirling twist term. We investigate its origin and modify the electromagnetization procedure, thereby constructing an exact solution of the Melvin-type electromagnetized Kerr black hole in the KK theory without the swirling term. We also obtain the analogous solution in the Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton-axion (EMDA) theory. The modified solution-generating techniques allow one to introduce both the dyonic charges and external electromagnetic fields independently into Ricci-flat seed metrics in both theories, without generating either Taub-NUT or Taub-swirling twists.
