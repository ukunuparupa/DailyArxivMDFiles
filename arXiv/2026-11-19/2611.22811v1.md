# 5d $\mathbb{Z}_{n}$ S-folds, quotient curves, and isogenies

## authors:
#Sung-Soo_Kim
#Song_Liu
#Mudassar_Sabir
#Futoshi_Yagi

## summary:
We construct Seiberg--Witten curves for the $\mathbb{Z}_3$, $\mathbb{Z}_4$, and $\mathbb{Z}_6$ S-folds of 5d rank-1 superconformal theories. Compared with the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ case arXiv:2611.09804, $\mathbb{Z}_{3,4,6}$ S-folds require stronger constraints on the mass parameters. We provide a systematic procedure of realizing the S-foldings at the mirror curve level. We obtain the same elliptic curve families by specializing the complexified mass parameters of the rank-1 $E_6$, $E_7$, and $E_8$ theories, with holonomies motivated by the 7-brane insertions required by the S-fold prescription. The two constructions agree in Weierstrass form. The families with continuous mass deformations organize into characters of $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ and $\mathfrak{g}_2$, consistently with their singular fibers. Finally, we discuss that the Seiberg--Witten curves for $\mathbb{Z}_n$ S-folds can be obtained through cyclic $n$-isogenies, which may provide a uniform quotient curve description of rank-1 S-folds.
