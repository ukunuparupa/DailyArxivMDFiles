# Scalar inelastic scattering of pseudo-Majorana dark matter through a radion portal

## authors:
#Ruifeng_Leng
#Kirtimaan_A_Mohan
#Keping_Xie
#Hong-Hao_Zhang

## summary:
We investigate a radion portal for pseudo-Majorana dark matter as an interpretation of the high-recoil event LZ230616. In contrast to the familiar pseudo-Dirac regime, the pseudo-Majorana structure allows a dominant scalar transition while suppressing elastic scattering. Endothermic kinematics remove low-energy recoils, while the nuclear response shapes the surviving high-recoil spectrum. Combining the accepted LZ event count with the Born-level thermal relic abundance yields a reference solution with $m_χ=1$ TeV, $δ=340$ keV, and $m_r=30$ GeV. This solution lies within the published LZ spin-independent $\mathcal{O}_1^s$ interval and remains compatible with existing OPAL and CMS constraints, while a simplified HL-LHC diphoton projection could probe it. Solar neutrino searches offer a complementary test. The benchmark's energetic-daughter proxy rates are comparable in magnitude to the rate scales reconstructed from IceCube's direct $b\bar{b}$ and $τ^+τ^-$ templates. An illustrative IceCube-Gen2 rescaling suggests future sensitivity, while a model-specific assessment requires the radion-cascade neutrino spectra and detector response.
