# Folded out-of-time-order correlators in double-scaled SYK

## authors:
#Joseph_D_Lykken
#Madeline_Mooney
#Maria_Spiropulu

## summary:
Harlow and Zhao arXiv:2607.14215 have identified ``anti-scrambling'' as a characteristic signature of cosmological horizons in de Sitter space, and proposed that it can be realized in a quantum system with bounded energy by Euclidean time ``folding" of out-of-time-order correlators (OTOCs). We implement this prescription in the DSSYK model. We use arXiv:2303.03474 to define an infinite-temperature ($β=0$) DSSYK OTOC that in the semiclassical limit of small values of the double-scaling parameter $λ$ reproduces the usual AdS eikonal shockwave behavior with its characteristic time delay, and then apply the Harlow-Zhao fold with the ``fake'' DSSYK inverse temperature $β_f$ playing the role of $β$. We demonstrate both numerically and analytically that the resulting folded OTOC displays anti-scrambling. The exact DSSYK evaluation tracks the semiclassical de Sitter eikonal formula in the scrambling regime, including a time advance instead of a time delay.The direction of the fold selects the side from which the continued eikonal amplitude approaches its branch cut. We show how our results are consistent with recent ``no-go" constraints. We compare the folded correlator with the two-shell analysis of arXiv:2607.14215: the growing term of the two-shell dilaton invariant appears in DSSYK with the same exponent, but as an imaginary part, while its real part, which would signal the backreaction that ends in a crunch, only moves by $O(λ)$ for thermal-scale probes. We also generalize to finite $β$; we find reasonable agreement with the de Sitter eikonal form for sufficiently high temperatures, but at lower temperatures the folded correlator is dominated by a pair of complex conjugate saddles, an anti-Stokes crossover that prevents retrieval of the eikonal behavior from the folded OTOC.
