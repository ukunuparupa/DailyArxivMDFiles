# Cohomological Foundations of Entropic Monotonicity: The Relative Cyclic Complex, the Transgression Map, and the Entropic $c$-Theorem in $d = 2$

## authors:
#R_C_Rashkov

## summary:
We develop a cohomological framework for the entropic $c$-theorem in two dimensions, based on the relative cyclic complex of a pair of von Neumann algebras and on the transgression map associated with a faithful normal state. The framework identifies the positivity underlying the entropic proof as Borchers positivity of the zero-frequency component of the relative modular cocycle, which is the operator-algebraic origin of the quantum null energy condition (QNEC). The positivity results are dimension-independent: they depend only on the split property, modular $p$-nuclearity, and the half-sided modular inclusion structure.  The main results of the paper are: (i) the identification of the entanglement cohomology with the kernel of the restriction map (Theorem 2.6); (ii) Borchers positivity of the relative modular cocycle (Proposition 3.8}); (iii) the finite-dimensional positivity of the entropy flux (Corollary 3.9); and (iv) the entropic $c$-theorem in $d = 2$ (Theorem 4.3), which follows from (iii) and the one-variable analysis. The $c$-theorem proof uses no ultraviolet regulator at any intermediate step.  We also formulate two open problems: the cochain-level positivity of the transgression (Conjecture 3.5) and the cohomological interpretation of the QNEC (Conjecture 3.11). Both are stated as research directions, not as results of the paper; in particular, the $c$-theorem proof does not depend on either of them.  The extension of the framework to the $F$-theorem in $d = 3$ and the general-$d$ program is the subject of the companion paper \cite{Rashkov:2026b}.
