# Gopakumar-Vafa invariants and Mordell-Weil lattices

## authors:
#Lutian_Zhao

## summary:
Let $X\to B$ be a Weierstrass fibration with integral fibers from a smooth Calabi--Yau threefold to a del Pezzo surface. For a smooth curve $D\subset B$ with smooth inverse image $Y$, we decompose the moduli fiber of stable sheaves with Euler characteristic one and projected cycle $D$. In fiber degree $k$, its components are indexed by all sections $C$ of $Y\to D$ with $\tfrac12\langle C,C\rangle\leq k$, where $\langle\, ,\,\rangle$ is the Mordell--Weil height pairing. For infinitesimally rigid sections we compute the GV contributions, recovering the $E_8$ theta product over a $(-1)$-curve. We formulate the corresponding conjecture for families of sections.
