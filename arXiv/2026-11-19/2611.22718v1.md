# Fluid/Gravity Correspondence in 3d Flat Gravity with Non-Compact Boundaries

## authors:
#H_Adami
#R_Espíndola
#K_Lara
#A_Latifi

## summary:
We study 3d flat gravity on a restricted phase space with a non-compact spatial boundary. The charge algebra splits into two commuting chiral current algebras, which a fluid/gravity dictionary maps to the stress tensor of a two-dimensional conformal fluid, with energy bounded below by the momentum. On the line, finiteness of the charges selects integrable, square-integrable currents, and the global data are carried by the scattering variables of a Schrödinger operator rather than by a monodromy. We analyze Heisenberg-like and Dirichlet-like boundary conditions; the latter give independently propagating chiral kinks associated with the reflectionless Pöschl-Teller operator. Both admit first-law relations. We outline the quantization as a Weyl $C^*$-algebra with quasi-free states. Retaining the radial dependence, the quasi-local stress tensor obeys an exact, non-perturbative flow of square-root $T\bar T$ type, which integrates to a finite boost in the radial position.
