# Quantum phase transition and superselection sectors in two-color one-flavor matrix QCD at weak coupling

## authors:
#Nirmalendu_Acharyya
#Arkajyoti_Bandyopadhyay
#Parthsarathi_Kondapure
#Sachindeo_Vaidya

## summary:
We numerically investigate the weak-coupling (small $g$) regime of the matrix model of $SU(2)$ gauge theory coupled to one flavor of massless fundamental quarks. The theory possesses a global Pauli-Gürsey $SU(2)_B$ symmetry, which organizes physical states of spin $J$ into sectors labelled by its representation $B$. We find localization-delocalization quantum phase transitions (QPTs) in multiple $(B,J)$ sectors at sector-dependent critical couplings $g_\ast^{BJ}$. Direct evaluation of the localization probability reveals that the ground states are predominantly localized near $A_i=0$ for $g<g_\ast^{BJ}$, but become almost entirely expelled from this region for $g>g_*^{BJ}$. The QPTs in different sectors exhibit distinct features, and we establish that quark-glue interactions are essential for these transitions. The transitions are singular: the ground state expectation value of $\text{Tr}(A_iA_i)$ remains finite for $g< g_\ast^{BJ}$, whereas it diverges as $g$ approaches $g_\ast^{BJ}$ from above. We show that the two limiting ground states on either side of $g_\ast^{BJ}$ belong to distinct superselection sectors. Furthermore, as $g$ approaches $g_\ast^{BJ}$ from above, the ground state localizes on a submanifold (of codimension 2) of the gauge configuration space, with the spin and color of its glue components becoming locked.
