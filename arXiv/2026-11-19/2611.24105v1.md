# Stabilizer models of achiral abelian topological orders

## authors:
#Marvin_Qi
#Wilbur_Shirley

## summary:
We construct continuous-variable stabilizer codes realizing every achiral 2D abelian topological order, both bosonic and fermionic. Starting from an arbitrary signature-zero integer $K$-matrix, we show that the corresponding topological order can be obtained by condensing a lattice of dyons with trivial pairwise mutual braiding in an $\mathbb{R}^N$ gauge theory. We implement this condensation microscopically by gauging a non-on-site 1-form symmetry of the $\mathbb{R}^N$ toric code.
