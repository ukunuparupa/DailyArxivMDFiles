# Holomorphic Feynman diagrams in four dimensions and the quantum BPS ring

## authors:
#Botao_Li
#Kangning_Liu
#Chi-Ming_Chang

## summary:
We compute, at all loop orders, the kernels of the holomorphic Feynman graphs that carry the loop corrections to supersymmetry transformations and to regularized products of local operators in the holomorphic twist of four-dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ supersymmetric gauge theories. The current equations of these holomorphic graph integrals are those of an electrical network. Schwinger parameters are resistances and holomorphic momenta are injected currents. Iterating series, parallel and star--triangle reductions on Schwinger space gives the fan, spider and cubic product-forest kernels at all loop orders. Assuming that twisting commutes with quantization for these composite operators, we identify the twisted differential with their loop-corrected supersymmetry transformation. We give every perturbative correction to the supersymmetry transformation in $\mathcal N=4$ Yang--Mills theory, including its action on operators with arbitrary holomorphic derivatives. Using the marked higher operations of Gaiotto, Kulp and Wu, we also construct and compute the quantum product of operators and prove that, on $\mathbf Q$-cohomology, it is associative, graded commutative and unital, supplying the multiplication of the quantum BPS ring. Every multi-graviton operator thus has an explicit finite completion to a cocycle of the full quantum supercharge. Our computer census finds new fortuitous classes and one-loop lifts in $\mathcal N=4$ Yang--Mills theory with several gauge groups. We have found no two-loop lift in the sectors tested. We also find two-loop lifts in the Leigh--Strassler and $β$-deformations of $\mathcal N=4$ Yang--Mills theory. In other theories, we find two-, three- and four-loop lifts in abelian gauge theories, and two- and three-loop lifts in $\mathcal N=2$ $SU(2)$ and $SU(3)$ gauge theories and their singlet deformations.
