# Gauge and scale dependence of the quadratic backreaction source during inflation: Uniform-expansion gauge

## authors:
#Inyong_Cho

## summary:
Cosmological backreaction is most directly expressed through the local expansion of the spacetime foliation, so uniform-expansion (UE) slicing provides a natural geometrical setting in which to examine the quadratic effective energy-momentum tensor (2EMT) of scalar perturbations during slow-roll inflation. We impose the complete scalar gauge conditions $κ=0$ and $E=0$. The uniform-expansion condition gives a \textit{differential} constraint for the shift $Q$ in configuration space and satisfies $Q_{\mathrm{UE}}(k)=-3(Ψ'+\mathcal HΨ)/(k^2+3ε\mathcal H^2)$ for each Fourier mode. In the strict infrared, the UE source vanishes at zero gradient, while its first nonzero terms are $\widehatτ_{00}^{\mathrm{UE}}/\mathcal F_{\mathrm{LW}} \simeq8σ_1^2/ε$ and $\widehatτ_{ii}^{\mathrm{UE}}/\mathcal F_{\mathrm{LW}} \simeq-8σ_1^2/(3ε)$ at leading slow-roll order. Thus $w_{\rm raw}^{\mathrm{UE}}\simeq-1/3$, but the first finite-gradient coefficients differ from the comoving and uniform-density gauges. Sufficiently deep in the gradient-dominated intermediate infrared, the leading UE behavior approaches the spatially-flat result, whereas the crossover region between the strict and intermediate infrared regimes requires the full expressions for the 2EMT. In the ultraviolet, we have $\widehatτ_{00}^{\mathrm{UE}}\simeq2\mathcal F_{\mathrm{SW}}$ and $\widehatτ_{ii}^{\mathrm{UE}}\simeq(2/3)\mathcal F_{\mathrm{SW}}$, yielding $w_{\mathrm{raw}}\simeq1/3$. Separately, we demonstrate the gauge dependence of the 2EMT under general scalar gauge transformations and discuss the complete fixing of the UE gauge.
