# Competing Magnetic Orders and Continuously Varying Exponents in $U(1)$ Spin Liquids

## authors:
#Vira_Shyta
#Flavio_S_Nogueira

## summary:
Unequal couplings to a common emergent gauge field provide a continuous way to tune competing magnetic instabilities in an effective description of $U(1)$ spin-liquid mixtures. We study this mechanism in quantum electrodynamics in 2+1 dimensions with two families of four-component Dirac fermions. Combining a leading large-$N$ treatment with Schwinger--Dyson equations, we obtain chiral condensates representing Néel order in the two sectors. At fixed fermion number, the coupling ratio $κ=e_2^2/e_1^2$ controls which condensates develop. Depending on $N$, the phases with a single condensate are connected through either coexistence or a finite interval in which both fermion families remain gapless. The mass-generation thresholds are reciprocal, and the fermion gaps close with Miransky essential singularities. The renormalization group flow has a circular line of fixed points parametrized by the invariant ratio $κ$. At the calculated order, gauge-invariant staggered magnetization correlations have continuously varying exponents in the gapless regime, while the exponent for correlations between the two sectors is independent of $κ$. These results connect the competition between magnetic orders to a continuous family of critical theories and identify magnetic correlation signatures of the intervening gapless regime.
