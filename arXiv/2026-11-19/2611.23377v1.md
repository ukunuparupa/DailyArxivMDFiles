# The Holomorphic-Topological Poisson Sigma Model and Quantization of Affine W-algebras

## authors:
#Niklas_Garner
#Brian_R_Williams
#Paul-Hermann_Balduf

## summary:
We construct explicit deformation quantizations of classical principal affine $W$-algebras for every simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak g$ using the holomorphic-topological Poisson sigma model of A. Khan and K. Zeng. A linear differential-graded presentation identifies the universal bulk with Chern--Simons theory near a principal nilpotent background, where holomorphic gauge gives a finite, 1-loop exact quantization. The quantum oper boundary observables form the principal affine $W$-algebra at level $k-h^\vee\hbar$; we also obtain this level shift directly for affine current algebras. For $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, we prove a perturbative quantum equivalence between Chern--Simons theory and three-dimensional chiral gravity, including their oper and Dirichlet boundary conditions, and determine the quantum relation between their parameters.
