# An Effective Theory for Hot Lines

## authors:
#Julien_Barrat
#Alessio_Miscioscia
#Fedor_K_Popov

## summary:
We develop a high-temperature effective theory for spatial conformal line defects in theories with thermal screening. The defect partition function admits a local geometric expansion, whose leading terms describe the effective tension and bending response of the line. Using a convergent bulk OPE for heavy-state defect matrix elements, we derive asymptotic relations for averaged products of defect one-point coefficients and bulk OPE coefficients in the regime $1 \ll Δ_χ\ll Δ^{1/d}$. We compute the leading EFT coefficients for a twisted free scalar and for the magnetic line at the $\mathrm{O}(N)$ Wilson-Fisher fixed point, large-$N$ Gross--Neveu model and $\mathcal N = 2$ Chern-Simons-matter theory. For the three-dimensional Ising model, Monte Carlo simulations yield a perimeter coefficient in good agreement with a Padé interpolation of our $\varepsilon$-expansion result.
