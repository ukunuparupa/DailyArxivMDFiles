# Localizing the M-Theory Path Integral: Membrane Instantons

## authors:
#Pietro_Benetti_Genolini
#Jerome_P_Gauntlett
#Jaeha_Park
#James_Sparks

## summary:
We propose a formula for M2-instanton contributions to the M-theory path integral, incorporating quantum corrections to all orders in the Planck length. The M2-brane action is determined by relative equivariant localization, while an equivariant Dolbeault character computes the one-loop determinant. Continuous families of M2-branes are treated by equivariant localization on their moduli spaces. For M-theory backgrounds dual to ABJM theory, we compute single instanton contributions from linear holomorphic fillings to the sphere partition function and the topologically twisted index associated with supersymmetric $AdS_4$ black holes. Our results reproduce known field theory instanton coefficients and give new analytic predictions for mass and squashing deformations at all Chern-Simons levels. For the $V^{5,2}$ theory, we compute new instanton contributions and establish an exact relation between its partition function at special squashing and that of ABJM theory on the round three-sphere.
