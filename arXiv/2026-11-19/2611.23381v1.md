# Non-Perturbative Moduli Stabilization with Sp$(4,\mathbb Z)$ Modular Symmetry

## authors:
#Miguel_Hernandez-Segura
#Xiang-Gan_Liu
#Hans_Peter_Nilles
#Saul_Ramos-Sanchez

## summary:
We study the stabilization of the three complex moduli associated with Sp$(4,\mathbb Z)$ modular symmetry by gaugino condensation in $\mathcal N=1$ supergravity models from heterotic string theory, where one-loop threshold corrections fix both the modular weight and the singularity structure of the superpotential. We determine, for each threshold class, the complete parametrization of the consistent superpotential relying on Sp$(4,\mathbb Z)$ basic modular-invariant ratios and their syzygies, generalizing the insufficient SL$(2,\mathbb Z)$-inspired techniques based solely on polynomials of its absolute invariants. This structure supports a rich landscape of supersymmetric Minkowski vacua. Unlike in the SL$(2,\mathbb Z)$ case, higher-order zeros produce continuous surfaces and curves of supersymmetric Minkowski vacua, while, among the six isolated fixed points, only those with $S_4$ and $D_{12}$ residual symmetries can stabilize all three moduli. We also construct isolated vacua away from fixed points, some of which lie at the moduli values preferred by genus-two modular flavor fits, and examine the suppression of the geometric moduli masses near an enhanced-symmetry point.
