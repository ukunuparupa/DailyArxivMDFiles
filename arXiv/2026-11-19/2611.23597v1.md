# Comment on "Exact, nonsingular black holes from a phantom DBI field as primordial dark matter"

## authors:
#Mustapha_Azreg-Aïnou
#Marco_A_A_de_Paula

## summary:
We demonstrate that the metric and scalar configuration proposed in Ref.~[1] do not satisfy the complete set of field equations of the Dirac-Born-Infeld (DBI) field theory minimally coupled to general relativity on a radial domain containing the event horizon. The physical DBI branch for this gravitational setup terminates outside the event horizon. Thus, the proposed configuration does not describe a black hole solution of that theory.
