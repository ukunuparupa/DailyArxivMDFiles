# Axion electrodynamics and the advection-diffusion structure of TEM modes in hollow-conductor waveguides

## authors:
#André_H_Gomes

## summary:
We present a systematic investigation of transverse electromagnetic (TEM) modes in hollow-conductor waveguides filled with a dielectric medium described by axion electrodynamics. While such modes do not propagate in conventional single-conductor waveguides, the coupling of the axion field to an external magnetic field enables their propagation. We show that the resulting TEM modes are governed by an advection-diffusion-type equation for the transverse electrostatic potential, establishing a direct mathematical connection between axion electrodynamics and advection-diffusion phenomena. We illustrate this connection through explicit solutions and field profiles for cylindrical waveguides, considering uniform and rigid rotational drift fields, both individually and in combination, as well as different values of the parameter controlling the advection-to-diffusion ratio (Péclet number). These results establish a transport-based framework for understanding how spatial variations of the axion-electrodynamics background shape the transverse electromagnetic fields in hollow-conductor waveguides.
