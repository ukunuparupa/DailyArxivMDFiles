# Spectrum of the parity operator for parafermions and parabosons from character formulas

## authors:
#N_I_Stoilova
#J_Van_der_Jeugt

## summary:
In a recent paper [J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. 59 175201 (2026)] Green's triple relations for parafermions and parabosons were extended by including a new operator $P$, referred to as the parity operator. The spectrum of $P$ in Fock space representations was computed, making use of the action of parafermion (resp.\ paraboson) creation and annihilation operators. In the current paper, we adopt a different method to compute the spectrum of $P$, namely using character formulas. For a set of $n$ parafermions, the action of $P$ is deduced from character formulas of $so(2n+2)$. For a set of $n$ parabosons, the known spectrum of $P$ yields new character formulas of particular $osp(2|2n)$ representations.
