# Zero mode surface of Hermitian two-by-two matrices

## authors:
#Andreas_Sykora

## summary:
We classify the zero mode surfaces, also called Clifford spectra, of Hermitian $2\times 2$ matrices. Using the covariance of the Dirac operator under the Euclidean group and its invariance under unitary conjugation, together with a singular value decomposition, every $m$-tuple of Hermitian $2\times 2$ matrices, after adjoining zero matrices if $m<3$, is reduced to the normal form $(b_1σ_1,b_2σ_2,b_3σ_3,0,\dots,0)$ with $b_1\geq b_2\geq b_3\geq 0$. Since adding a vanishing matrix only embeds the Clifford spectrum into a higher-dimensional space, it suffices to consider three matrices. In this normal form the determinant of the Dirac operator is given in closed form and its critical points are determined explicitly. The zero mode surface consists of one or two points if $b_3=0$. Otherwise it consists of two disjoint topological spheres if $b_1>b_2+b_3$, of two topological spheres touching at one point if $b_1=b_2+b_3$, and of a single topological sphere if $b_1<b_2+b_3$. The topology is thus governed by the triangle inequality for $(b_1,b_2,b_3)$, which can be interpreted with Heron's formula. In particular, the Clifford spectrum of any tuple of Hermitian $2\times 2$ matrices is never empty.
