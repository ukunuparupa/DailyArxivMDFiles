# Probing the QCD Conformal Window with Four-Quark Operators

## authors:
#Junchen_Rong
#Emmanuel_Stamou
#Tom_Steudtner

## summary:
The infrared fate of QCD-like theories and their conformal window for different numbers or flavours and colour remains an open question with applications ranging from high-energy to condensed-matter systems. A hallmark of phase transitions from a conformal to symmetry breaking phase can be the presence of classically irrelevant operators that provide relevant deformation in the infrared. We investigate this scenario by computing anomalous dimensions of all four-quark operators, with or without chiral symmetry, at four-loop order as well as the dimension-six gluonic operator at three loops in Veneziano QCD. Scaling dimensions are derived in a systematic conformal expansion. These results are improved with various resummation techniques and we estimate their uncertainties. We find no concrete evidence for a composite operator effectively describing a merger with the QCD fixed point.
