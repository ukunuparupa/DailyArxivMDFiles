# Self-Dual Yang-Mills in AdS$_4$

## authors:
#Simon_Heuveline
#Romain_Ruzziconi
#Ahmed_Sheta
#Andrew_Strominger

## summary:
Quantum Yang-Mills theory in Euclidean AdS$_4$ with Neumann boundary conditions is studied as a function of the complex couplings $\frac{1}{g_\pm^2} \equiv \frac{1}{g^2}\mp \frac{i θ}{8 π^2}$. We define a "self-dual limit" by $g_- \to 0$ with $g_+ ~{\rm fixed}$. We argue that the limiting theory is well-defined perturbatively and equivalent to the BF formulation of self-dual Yang-Mills theory with a particular boundary condition relating $B$ and $F$ at the boundary $\partial$AdS$_4$. $g_+$ is the loop-counting parameter of the self-dual theory. This is in stark contrast to flat space, where $θ$ has no effect on perturbative dynamics. In the self-dual limit, it is shown that all tree-level zero-plus and single-plus boundary correlators vanish. A closed form expression is given for any number of gluons for the double-plus tree correlators. Finally, we show that in the flat space limit of AdS$_4$, the total energy poles in the tree boundary correlators correctly reduce to the single-minus gluon amplitudes.
