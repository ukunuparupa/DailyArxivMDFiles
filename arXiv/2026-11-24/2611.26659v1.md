# Gravitational Wave Detection via Massive-Particle Interferometry

## authors:
#Alireza_Maleki

## summary:
Gravitational-wave (GW) observations provide a powerful means of testing general relativity and exploring the physics of black holes and the early universe, motivating the development of complementary detection techniques. Here, we propose and theoretically investigate a GW detector based on massive-particle interferometry, in which the signal is encoded in the relative phase accumulated by a quantum particle traversing a closed-loop trajectory and a reference path. We derive the frequency-dependent GW-induced phase response and examine the parameters on which it depends. These parameters provide a means of tuning the detector response over a broad frequency range, motivating the exploration of tabletop detectors for GW signals at frequencies comparable to and below the LIGO band, in the idealized limit. By exploiting the trajectory-dependent gravitationally induced phase, the proposed scheme offers a distinct and potentially complementary approach to GW detection and motivates further investigation of its experimental feasibility and practical sensitivity.
