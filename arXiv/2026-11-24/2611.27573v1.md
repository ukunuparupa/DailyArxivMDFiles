# The Kerr Metric in Canonical Harmonic Coordinates to All Orders

## authors:
#Amina_Sadu
#Pierre_Vanhove

## summary:
We give in closed form the transformation from Boyer--Lindquist coordinates to the canonical harmonic coordinates of the Kerr metric, to all orders in Newton's constant and in the spin. This is the canonical harmonic chart selected by the MPM prescription and by the harmonic-gauge Einstein recursion sourced by the Kerr multipoles, with the residual homogeneous freedom fixed by the boundary conditions. The chart is the real, time-reversal-covariant combination of the two radial harmonic functions underlying the Cook--Scheel coordinates, which differ from it by a definite residual harmonic transformation. The proof rests on a classification of the residual harmonic freedom. Rotational covariance, parity, time reversal and analyticity in the spin allow a decaying homogeneous solution beyond the linear field only in the spatial components at third post-Minkowskian order, with angular degree $\ell=p+2$ at spin order $a^p$. This sector is a pure gauge mode, equivalent to an MPM gauge moment. The exact chart selects the representative in which this third-PM homogeneous mode vanishes; imposing the same condition on the recursion removes the only residual ambiguity and fixes its solution uniquely. As a consequence, the canonical MPM iteration needs no finite-part regularisation and no harmonicity correction at any order. We give the exact gothic metric and the post-Minkowskian coefficients in closed form through fifth order, where an arctangent function first appears at fourth order. We find agreement with recent canonical MPM and amplitude-based results at fourth order, and we verify the construction in exact arithmetic through fifth order and twelfth order in the spin, with code made publicly available.
