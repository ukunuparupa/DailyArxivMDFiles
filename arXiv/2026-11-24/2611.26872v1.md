# Majorana quasiparticles, mass hierarchies, and the phase diagram of the Gross-Neveu model in D=2+1

## authors:
#Igor_F_Herbut
#Mathew_Schneider

## summary:
We show that the canonical $2+1$-dimensional $O(2N)$-symmetric Gross-Neveu model with the inversion-breaking mass-term features a sequence of $O(2N-k) \times O(k)$ - symmetric ground states in its zero-temperature phase diagram, with $k=1,...,N$. For odd $k$ the quasiparticle excitations are itinerant, massive, two-real-component Majorana fermions, i.e. half of Dirac fermions. The solutions of the mean-field equations symmetric under $O(2N-k_1-k_2) \times O(k_1) \times O(k_2)$ with $k_1, k_2 \geq 1$ also exist, but are found to be unstable. Some manifestations of Majorana quasiparticle in the particular ``uniaxial nematic" state with $k=1$ are outlined, and analogies to other physical systems are offered.
