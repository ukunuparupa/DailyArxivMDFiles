# A covariant analysis of massless Dirac fields in LRS spacetimes

## authors:
#Eduardo_Bittencourt
#Sante_Carloni
#Giuseppe_De_Maria
#Peter_K_S_Dunsby

## summary:
We investigate the massless Dirac field in locally rotationally symmetric (LRS) spacetimes using a covariant spinorial-fluid representation, with the spinor current and spin direction aligned with the LRS frame. When the current has both vorticity and sheet twist, characterized by a constant ratio $λ$, the LRS integrability condition relates evolution and propagation derivatives, reducing the field equations to an ordinary differential system that can be integrated exactly. The integrability of the chiral angle forces the heat flux to vanish and the source to saturate the null energy condition along the two radial null directions. A constant boost in the radial Lorentzian two-plane maps the spinor current to a canonical congruence, which is stationary (LRS~I) for $|λ|>1$ and irrotational and geodesic (LRS~III) for $|λ|<1$. In this frame, the local metric is reconstructed in the Goode--Wainwright--Van den Bergh canonical form. For $|λ|>1$ the energy density is positive, and the metric is locally isometric to the horizonless charged Taub--NUT (Brill) metric: in the polar representation, the massless Dirac field supplies a stress--energy tensor algebraically identical to that of the Brill electromagnetic field, with no Maxwell field present. For $|λ|<1$, the energy density is negative and the metric is the hyperbolic ($k=-1$) charged NUT metric with a formal negative squared-charge parameter, whose homogeneous slices are of LRS Bianchi type~VIII.
