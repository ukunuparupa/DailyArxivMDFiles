# Calabi-Yau at 50: mathematical discovery in the age of AI

## authors:
#Yang-Hui_He

## summary:
Using the golden jubilee of Yau's proof of the Calabi conjecture as a spring-board, we discuss how the dataset of Calabi-Yau manifolds brought together mathematicians and physicists since the 1980s, and then further brought in the world of machine-learning and AI since 2017. We give a historical account on the growth of the field of ``AI-assisted mathematics'' in the last decade, and comment on what culminated in 2026 as perhaps the most dramatic year in the history of mathematics since Euclid.
