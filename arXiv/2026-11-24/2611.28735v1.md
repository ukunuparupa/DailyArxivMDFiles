# Relativistic Hydrodynamics as an Intersection Problem

## authors:
#Nikita_Nekrasov
#Paul_Wiegmann

## summary:
Ideal relativistic hydrodynamics admits a covariant description as an intersection problem in a symplectic space associated with spacetime. The independent fields are differential forms of degrees 0,1,3,4. Spacetime diffeomorphisms and shifts of the momentum one-form act Hamiltonianly; the zero locus of their moment map is a coisotropic subvariety encoding the transport equations. The spacetime metric and equation of state determine a Lagrangian submanifold, whose intersection with the coisotropic subvariety is precisely the Lichnerowicz--Carter system. We explain the geometric meaning of the canonical flow parameter, derive circulation and Ertel invariants directly from the constraints, and identify energy--momentum conservation with covariance of the constitutive Lagrangian. The nonrelativistic limit and a relation of entropy-level helicity to split Calabi--Yau geometry are briefly indicated.
