# Thermodynamics of hard wall model of AdS/QCD at finite chemical potential with Gauss-Bonnet corrections

## authors:
#Dharm_Veer_Singh
#Shobhit_Sachan

## summary:
We study the confinement/deconfinement transition of the hard wall model of AdS/QCD at finite quark density in five-dimensional Einstein--Maxwell--Gauss--Bonnet gravity, with the back-reaction of the quark matter on the geometry included. The deconfined phase is a charged Gauss--Bonnet black hole, and the confined phase is thermally charged AdS cut off in the infrared. The infrared value of the gauge field, which Lee, Park and Sin fixed by requiring a consistent quark number, becomes a function of the Gauss--Bonnet coupling. We obtain the transition line in closed form at fixed quark chemical potential and at fixed quark number density. At fixed density the transition passes through a region where the two phases coexist. A negative Gauss--Bonnet coupling raises the transition temperature and extends the confined phase to higher density. A value inside the causality bound gives a transition temperature at zero density close to the lattice value. In the dense confined phase we compute the masses and decay constants of the $ρ$ meson and its radial excitations. All masses increase with density. A WKB analysis relates the increase to a shorter effective size of the confining region, and at nuclear density the $ρ$ mass rises by about one percent.
