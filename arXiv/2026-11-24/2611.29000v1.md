# Finite-Temperature Stabilization of a KKLT Modulus in Three-Dimensional Lattice Simulations

## authors:
#Saya_Mahmoudi
#Furkan_Utku_Biber
#Hyukjung_Kim
#Heeseung_Zoe

## summary:
We investigate the cosmological stabilization of a KKLT modulus through three-dimensional Langevin lattice simulations incorporating an evolving thermal bath, dissipation, spatial gradients, and stochastic fluctuations. While the spatially averaged modulus closely follows homogeneous evolution away from the capture threshold, near-critical trajectories linger near the unstable potential barrier for approximately one e-fold, allowing long-wavelength fluctuations to undergo tachyonic amplification and drive localized regions across the barrier even as the mean field turns back toward the stabilized vacuum. Linear Langevin theory reproduces the fluctuation growth, while convergence tests identify a narrow stochastic layer in which the barrier-crossing fraction shows no systematic dependence on the discretization at a fixed effective distance from the threshold. These results demonstrate that capture of the spatially averaged modulus does not guarantee that every spatial region remains within the stabilizing barrier.
