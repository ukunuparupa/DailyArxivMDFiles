# The $a$-function in fractional dimensions

## authors:
#I_Jack
#D_R_T_Jones

## summary:
We consider the $β$-functions for both scalar, and supersymmetric chiral scalar, field theories with a general $n$th-order self-interaction, in their critical dimensions, which are in general non-integer. We perform a full computation up to fourth order in the coupling in the supersymmetric case, and a partial one in the scalar case. We then demonstrate the existence of an $a$-function, related to the $β$-functions by a gradient flow with a positive-definite metric, through fourth order in the coupling in the supersymmetric case; and show that all our fourth-order results in the scalar case are consistent with a similar property holding there too.
