# Banana D-modules

## authors:
#Giacomo_Brunello
#Wojciech_Flieger
#Yoshiaki_Goto
#Saiei-Jaeyeong_Matsubara-Heo
#Nobuki_Takayama

## summary:
We study the holonomic $D$-module whose solutions are generic-mass banana Feynman integrals with complex propagator powers and spacetime dimension. We call this the banana $D$-module. Two properties of the banana $D$-module are studied: its holonomic rank and the irreducibility of the monodromy representation. This is achieved by relating the banana $D$-module to Lauricella $F_{C}$ hypergeometric systems. Further, we expand the generic-mass banana Feynman integral and the associated discontinuity integral in terms of series solutions. Finally, we analyze the Pfaffian system arising from the banana $D$-module and construct an explicit $d\log$ representation in the equal-mass specialization.
