# Gauging background isometries of the ILST null string

## authors:
#Ulf_Lindström

## summary:
We present a general gauging of a subalgebra, $\mathfrak h$, of the isometries $\mathfrak g$ of the background geometry for the ILST string (e-Print: hep-th/9307108 [hep-th]). We discuss which of the isometries in $\mathfrak g$ survive the gauging as symmetries. The gauged symmetry does not in general involve transformations of the vector density V. There is thus a corresponding contraction of the algebra of constraints as compared to the flat background and it no longer represent the full Carroll-Weyl algebra. This algebra of constraints is derived and discussed, partly in an appendix. The gauging of isometries sets the stage for a reduction, or quotient construction, leading to a new null string on the space of orbits of the gauge group. We illustrate this by deriving the ILST string in $AdS$ background as a quotient of a model in SO(d,2) background by a SO(d,1) isometry, in group space
