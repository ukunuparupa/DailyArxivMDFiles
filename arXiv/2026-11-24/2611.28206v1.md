# An all-orders proof of gradient flow for multiscalar $φ^4$ theory in four dimensions

## authors:
#Marten_Reehorst

## summary:
We prove that the renormalisation group flow of multiscalar $φ^4$ theory in four dimensions is a gradient flow to all orders in perturbation theory. The proof uses the Weyl consistency conditions of Osborn and Jack, and it assumes two properties of perturbation theory: that the generating functional with local couplings is finite (also assumed by Osborn and Jack), and that the known expansion of massive Feynman integrals at large momenta holds with a bound on its remainder at each momentum. The consistency conditions give a gradient formula in which the tensor that multiplies the beta function is allowed to have an antisymmetric part, determined by coefficients of the Weyl anomaly. We derive a Ward identity for the two-point function of the marginal operators at constant couplings away from fixed points, with sources for the currents of field rotations and for the mass operator. It re-expresses one of the anti-symmetric contributions in terms of two-point functions and the first moment of the three-point function. We show that, after this rewrite, each of the resulting terms is equivalent to a symmetric contribution to the metric and a contribution to the potential, and we give both explicitly. The metric is positive definite to all orders in perturbation theory. The proof implies in particular the four relations among five- and six-loop coefficients of the field rotation, which Pannell and Stergiou derived as the conditions for a gradient flow at six loops. We check by explicit computation that these relations indeed hold. A companion paper treats $d=4-\varepsilon$ and other critical dimensions with a Ward identity on the sphere.
