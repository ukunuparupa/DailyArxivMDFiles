# String breaking in a (1+1)-dimensional $e^+e^-$ collider

## authors:
#Ross_Dempsey
#Anna-Maria_E_Glück
#Silviu_S_Pufu
#Benjamin_T_Søgaard

## summary:
In this paper, we develop a framework for studying solitons in $(1+1)$-dimensional gauge theories using tensor networks, and we apply it to simulate $e^+e^-$ collisions in the massive Schwinger model. We work at $θ= π$ and at a bare fermion mass exceeding the critical value; in this regime, there are two degenerate vacua, and the $e^+$ and $e^-$ particles are realized as soliton states interpolating between them. We carefully explain how to find and characterize such soliton states using infinite tensor network methods, and then exhibit real-time simulations of their collisions. These collisions involve the formation of an energetic flux string, and we demonstrate that our final states are superpositions of elastic and string-breaking trajectories.
