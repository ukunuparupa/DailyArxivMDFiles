# The Gradient Structure of Perturbative RG Flows

## authors:
#Lorenzo_Benfatto
#William_H_Pannell
#Andreas_Stergiou

## summary:
Weyl consistency conditions show that perturbative renormalisation group flows in two, four and six dimensions are almost gradient flows. The obstruction is an antisymmetric contribution to the metric on the space of couplings, which has resisted removal since the work of Jack and Osborn more than 35 years ago. We show that a conformal Ward identity on the sphere allows it to be removed by a choice of scheme. The beta function is then the gradient of a potential $A$ with respect to a symmetric metric, to all orders in perturbation theory. The Ward identity gives this result in any dimension $d$, in theories with and without a Weyl anomaly. At leading order the metric is fixed by the two-point functions of the perturbing operators, so unitarity makes it positive definite at weak coupling. At fixed points $A$ equals $\widetilde{F}=\sin\tfrac{πd}{2}\,\ln Z_{S^{d}}$, so the $\widetilde{F}$-theorem follows for perturbative flows to all loop orders. We show that sources of relevant operators, which on the sphere mix with the curvature, do not affect these results. In unitary theories the only exception is the source of a free scalar field near $d=6,10,\ldots\,$, so that for $φ^3$ theory near six dimensions the gradient property remains open.
