# Moment bounds and UV reconstruction from curved positivity cones

## authors:
#Fu-Ming_Chang
#Shi-Lin_Wan
#Shuang-Yong_Zhou

## summary:
The fundamental principles of the S-matrix impose positivity bounds on the Wilson coefficients of EFTs, including the SMEFT. These bounds form convex cones that often have curved boundaries when there are degeneracies among the irreducible two-particle low-energy states. Numerical approximations have previously been used to evaluate these curved cones. We develop a moment-based framework to analytically carve out these cones, particularly those associated with anomalous quartic gauge couplings in the SMEFT. The framework also allows us to reverse engineer UV models from the curved boundaries of the positivity cones.
