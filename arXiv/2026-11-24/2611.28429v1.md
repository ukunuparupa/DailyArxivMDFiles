# Chiral and deconfinement thermodynamics of the Polyakov-quark linear sigma model with and without charm quark: an SU(3) versus SU(4) comparison

## summary:
Using the Polyakov-loop extended linear-sigma model (PLSM), we investigate strongly interacting matter at finite temperature and baryon density by comparing the three-flavor (u,d,s) and four-flavor (u,d,s,c) formulations on equal footing, with the aim of isolating the thermal contribution of the charm quark. The model describes chiral dynamics through explicit and spontaneous symmetry breaking and reproduces the vacuum spectra of scalar and pseudoscalar mesons. Confinement is incorporated through a logarithmic Polyakov-loop potential calibrated to the continuum lattice-QCD equation of state (EoS). Within the mean-field approximation, we calculate light-, strange-, and charm-quark condensates, the Polyakov loop, and bulk thermodynamic observables, including pressure, trace anomaly, and entropy density, over broad ranges of temperature and baryon chemical potential. We also determine the chiral and deconfinement pseudocritical boundaries and represent them as phase boundaries and three-dimensional order-parameter surfaces. After tuning the Polyakov sector to lattice-QCD pressure and trace-anomaly data, the resulting entropy density agrees with HotQCD 2+1(+1)-flavor results throughout the crossover region. At zero baryon density, the non-strange condensate melts at Tc = 210 MeV in SU(3) and 218 MeV in SU(4), with curvature parameters kappa=0.018 and 0.017. Because mc>>Tc, charm remains thermally suppressed across the crossover. After normalization to the corresponding Stefan-Boltzmann limits, the SU(3) and SU(4) EoS nearly coincide below T~2Tc. Charm mainly contributes through a higher ideal-gas limit, a gradual high-temperature tail, and the flavor ordering u,d->s->c. For all baryon chemical potentials accessible within mean-field theory, the hadron-parton transition remains a smooth crossover.
