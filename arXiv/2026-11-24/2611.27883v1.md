# Soft-photon resummation for low-energy $e^+e^-$ colliders

## authors:
#Thomas_Dave
#Lois_Flower
#Jérémy_Paltrinieri
#William_J_Torres_Bobadilla

## summary:
We present resummed predictions for $e^+e^- \to μ^+μ^-(γ)$ and $π^+π^-(γ)$ in the multi-photon soft limit, matched to exact NLO. The resummation is performed within the Yennie-Frautschi-Suura framework at the amplitude level. The initial and final-state contributions are included coherently, and NLO accuracy with finite electron, muon and pion masses is achieved. We present our calculation, its implementation in a fast Fortran Monte Carlo integrator, and compare our predictions with the generators BabaYaga, KKMC, Phokhara and McMule, in scenarios inspired by the energy-scan (CMD) and radiative-return (KLOE, BES III, B factories) experiments. We find agreement with BabaYaga, which includes higher-order corrections through a parton shower, at the per-mille level in the cross-sections, and differences of up to two percent with the soft-photon resummation of KKMC. This work represents an important step towards the validation of Monte Carlo generators for low-energy experiments, and will be released in the upcoming new version of Phokhara.
