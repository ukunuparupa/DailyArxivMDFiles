# Spin-Torsion Coupling: Vector and Scalar Torsional Potentials of Chiral Spinor Currents

## authors:
#Elisa_Varani

## summary:
We investigate integral representations of torsional fields sourced by chiral spinor currents within a weak-field, Ricci-flow-inspired geometric framework. Starting from the coupling between spinorial currents and axial torsion, we construct screened Green-kernel solutions and examine their relation to antisymmetric tetradic perturbations and contorsion.  Particular attention is devoted to stationary toroidal configurations, for which the magnetic-type dipole moment vanishes while a nonzero toroidal moment and effective circulation remain. The dependence of these quantities on Yukawa screening is analyzed, together with their implications for an Aharonov-Bohm-like geometric structure.  We further introduce a mixed-chirality source describing an effective connection between oppositely polarized domains and investigate the corresponding flip-sector integral. The dynamics of massive Majorana neutrinos in an axial background is then examined, distinguishing chiral mixing from helicity oscillations and identifying the role of transverse torsional components.  The resulting framework connects spinorial sources, finite-range torsional potentials, toroidal geometry, and neutrino spin dynamics, while providing a mathematical basis for further investigations of torsional transport and observable effects.
