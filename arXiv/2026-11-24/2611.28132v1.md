# Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet gravity corrections in Dark Dimension scenario

## authors:
#George_K_Leontaris
#George_Prampromis

## summary:
We investigate the thermodynamics, dynamical stability, and phenomenological implications of Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet (EGB) black holes in Dark Dimension scenarios with one or two micron-sized extra dimensions. For the asymptotically flat, neutral Boulware-Deser solution with positive Gauss-Bonnet coupling $α$, we derive the mass, Hawking temperature, Wald entropy, and heat capacity in arbitrary dimension $D\ge 5$. A positive-heat-capacity branch arises only in $D=5$, where the temperature peaks at $T_{\max}=1/(8π\sqrtα)$ at $r_h=2\sqrtα$ and vanishes in the zero-radius limit at finite mass $M_{\rm lim}=3πα/(4G_5)$. For $G_5=(8πM_*^3)^{-1}$, $α=M_*^{-2}$, and $M_*\sim 10^{10}$ GeV, this yields $M_{\rm lim}\simeq 1.06\times 10^{-12}$ g. While this branch formally overlaps with classical instability zones, the string ultraviolet cutoff safely suppresses the responsible high-harmonic modes. Assuming a UV completion stabilizes these relics, we show they can constitute all of cold dark matter. We map their observational signatures, highlighting a scalar-induced gravitational-wave peak at 33 THz and the suppression of Hawking bursts. In $D=6$, the heat capacity is negative at all radii. Within a bulk blackbody emission model, we derive the leading large-mass lifetime correction and the small-radius scaling $τ\propto M_0^3$, compared to $τ_{\rm GR}\propto M_0^{5/3}$. For $M_*\sim 10$ TeV, we identify distinct collider benchmarks: a steeper energy scaling for the production cross section and a suppressed decay multiplicity ($\langle N \rangle \sim 4$). These provide clear experimental signatures distinguishing 6D EGB gravity from classical General Relativity, mapping the transition toward the string scale.
