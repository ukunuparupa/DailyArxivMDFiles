# ACT Excited: Reconciling Starobinsky and Quadratic Chaotic Inflationary Models with ACT Results via Excited States

## authors:
#Amjad_Ashoorioon
#Abdolreza_Yousefi-Sostani

## summary:
Data Release 6 of the Atacama Cosmology Telescope, combined with Planck, CMB lensing and DESI BAO data, favours a higher scalar spectral index $n_{s}$ than Planck and a mildly positive running $α_{s}$, putting the Starobinsky model in tension with the data, while the quadratic chaotic model is already excluded by its large tensor-to-scalar ratio $r$. We show that relaxing the Bunch--Davies assumption for the initial state of the scalar perturbations can restore agreement without modifying the inflaton potential. Parametrising the Bogoliubov coefficients by an excitation parameter $χ_{S}$ and a relative phase $2\varphi_{S}$, we derive the shifts of $n_{s}$ and $α_{s}$ induced by their scale dependence and map the regions of the $(\varphi_{S},χ_{S})$ plane compatible with the $1σ$ ACT DR6 ranges, the bound $r<0.038$, and the requirement that the scale of new physics $Λ$ exceed the Hubble scale $H$. For the Starobinsky model, a scale-dependent $χ_{S}$ or $\varphi_{S}$ works almost everywhere in this plane: only small neighbourhoods of $\varphi_{S}=0,π$ with $χ_{S}\gtrsim1.22$ are excluded, and a scale-dependent phase fails at $\varphi_{S}=0,π/2,π$ and at $χ_{S}=0$, where the required ${\rm d}\varphi_{S}/{\rm d}\ln k$ and ${\rm d}^2\varphi_{S}/{\rm d}(\ln k)^2$ diverge. For the QC model, the excited state must also suppress $r$, which requires $χ_{S}\gtrsim0.63$ and $Λ\lesssim6H$. A scale-dependent $χ_{S}$ must satisfy $|{\rm d}χ_{S}/{\rm d}\ln k|\gtrsim0.0021$ in both models, and in the QC model we show analytically that $0.0021\lesssim{\rm d}χ_{S}/{\rm d}\ln k\lesssim0.0058$ and $0.0008\lesssim{\rm d}^2χ_{S}/{\rm d}(\ln k)^2\lesssim0.0062$. If the tensor modes remain in the BD state, the scenario also predicts a violation of the consistency relation $r=-8n_{T}$, testable once the tensor power spectrum and its tilt are measured.
