# Agent-guided construction of Feynman integral bases

## authors:
#Pau_Petit_Rosas
#William_J_Torres_Bobadilla

## summary:
Finding bases of Feynman integrals whose differential equations are suitable for phenomenology is a remarkably verifiable task. We exploit this to design two \emph{skills} with which Claude Opus~5.5 produces novel results autonomously, working for several days. For a two-loop five-point family (with two masses and 18 elliptic sectors) and a two-loop superfamily for the radiative jet function with a K3 sector, the agent finds bases whose differential equations are free of poles in the dimensional regulator $\varepsilon$ and at most quadratic in it, and takes the jet-function one to $\varepsilon$-factorized form.
