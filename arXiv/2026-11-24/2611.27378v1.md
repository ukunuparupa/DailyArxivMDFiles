# Pairing and modular: Generalized electrons and quasiholes inside the $\boxtimes$ of topological orders

## authors:
#Yoshiki_Fukusumi
#Yuma_Furuta

## summary:
We study the relationship between the pair creation mechanism in physics and the pairing ($\boxtimes_{\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}}$) in mathematics, focusing on the modular and algebraic properties. We provide a way to couple two topologically ordered systems, $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{A'}$ sharing the common (non-group-like) structure $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}=\overline{\mathbf{A'}_{\text{ub}}}$, through the nonabelian anyon analog of the pair creation mechanism denoted as $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub},\boxtimes}=\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}\boxtimes \mathbf{A'}_{\text{ub}}$. We identify $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub},\boxtimes}$ as the generalized version of electron operators in general topological orders or hidden current with (half-)integer spin involving the conformal embeddings. We provide a way to construct $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub},\boxtimes}$ as the hidden topological operator and identify $\mathbf{A}\boxtimes_{\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub}}}\mathbf{A'}$, a general class of topoloical orders, as the $\mathbf{A}_{\text{ub},\boxtimes}$-extended symmetry topological field theory. We also conjecture a series of underlying (unexplored) modular invariants, connecting the conformal embedding and the pairing.
