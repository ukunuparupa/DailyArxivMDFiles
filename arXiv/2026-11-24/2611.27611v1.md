# Cosmological Correlators with Triple Massive Exchanges

## authors:
#Shuntaro_Aoki
#Lucas_Pinol
#Zhehan_Qin
#Fumiya_Sano
#Masahide_Yamaguchi
#Yuhang_Zhu

## summary:
Among all tree-level contributions to the primordial bispectrum, the triple exchange of massive scalars is the most challenging. In this paper we present an analytical formula for this bispectrum in the form of a double series, which allows the three exchanged fields to have different masses and converges throughout the whole physical region. We obtain it by expanding the mixed propagator with the softest momentum, so that the triple exchange becomes a sum of integrals over two mixed propagators. Each of these integrals satisfies a differential equation and is fixed by its value at equal momentum. We further show that the squeezed limit follows directly from this formula and takes a simple and compact form.
