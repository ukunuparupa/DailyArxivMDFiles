# Unruh-like effects and correlation harvesting between particle detectors in uniform motion in (2+1)-dimensional Minkowski spacetime

## authors:
#Siwei_Han
#Zeinab_Imani
#Jun_Feng
#Robert_B_Mann

## summary:
We study Gaussian-switched Unruh-DeWitt detectors coupled to a massless scalar field in the (2+1)-dimensional Minkowski vacuum and following four classes of uniformly accelerated trajectories: linear, catenary, cusped, and circular motion. We first determine how the single-detector transition probability and effective temperature depend on acceleration and the torsion-to-acceleration ratio $\bar{b}$, whose continuous variation connects these four trajectory classes. We find that both quantities generally increase with acceleration, while their dependence on $\bar{b}$ can become nonmonotonic depending on the energy gap. We also examine a correlation-harvesting protocol by two detectors following horizontally displaced copies of the same trajectory. Increasing acceleration or separation generally suppresses the harvested concurrence and quantum mutual information, but the extent of suppression at fixed acceleration depends strongly on the trajectory class. For each of the four trajectory classes, we identify regimes of genuine entanglement harvesting in which vacuum correlations alone suffice to entangle the detectors, while the communication contribution alone cannot overcome local noise.
