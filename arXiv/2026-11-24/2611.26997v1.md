# On a Supersymmetric Generalization of the Matytsin Formula

## authors:
#Evgeny_Porubov

## summary:
We derive the leading asymptotics of the supersymmetric analogue of the Itzykson-Zuber integral for the supergroup $U(n|m)$ in the limit $n,m\to\infty$ when $n/m$ is kept finite. This generalizes the Matytsin result for $U(N)$. We show that it is described by the classical action of two independent Hamiltonian systems corresponding to the bosonic and fermionic sectors.
