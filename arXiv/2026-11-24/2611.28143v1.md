# Imprints of perfect fluid dark matter distributions and topological defects on Born--Infeld AdS black hole signatures

## authors:
#Faizuddin_Ahmed
#Ahmad_Al-Badawi
#İzzet_Sakallı

## summary:
We construct a static, spherically symmetric \BI{} \AdS{} \BH{} that carries a topological defect, either a \GM{} or a \CS{}, and is immersed in \PFDM{}. The metric function collects four independent deformations of the Schwarzschild geometry: a constant solid-angle deficit $(1-ξ)$ from the defect, the nonlinear charge sector controlled by the \BI{} scale $β$, the logarithmic \PFDM{} term set by the intensity $λ$, and the \AdS{} curvature $Λ$. We verify that the solution reduces to the charged \AdS{}--\PFDM{} defect geometry as $β\to \infty$ and to Schwarzschild when all deformations switch off. The horizon, photon sphere, and shadow all grow monotonically with the defect strength $ξ$. We compute the innermost stable circular orbit and the epicyclic frequencies, read off the relativistic-precession predictions for twin-peak quasi-periodic oscillations, and confront them with five accreting sources through a Markov chain Monte Carlo fit. For spin-0 perturbations we obtain the scalar potential, the higher-order WKB \QNM{} spectrum, and the \GBF{}, all of which soften as $ξ$ rises. The extended phase space analysis yields a \VdW{}-type $P$--$V$ structure whose critical point shifts with the defect, the \BI{} scale, and the halo intensity. Throughout, the four sectors leave distinct imprints on imaging, timing, ringdown, and thermal channels, {\color{black} which we read as parameter sensitivities rather than as an observational separation of the four, a distinction a joint multichannel fit would be needed to settle}.
