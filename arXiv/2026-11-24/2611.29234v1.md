# Möbius Fusion Algebra

## authors:
#Ippo_Orii
#Yuji_Tachikawa

## summary:
Given a pseudo-unitary modular tensor category $\mathcal{C}$ with an anti-monoidal involution $\mathsf{P}$, we define a generally non-commutative algebra from this data, which we call the Möbius fusion algebra $\mathfrak{M}$ of $(\mathcal{C},\mathsf{P})$, in terms of ribbon graphs drawn on the Möbius band, where the orientation-reversal of the Möbius band acts on the graphs by $\mathsf{P}$. We show that this algebra is semisimple, and give a formula for the dimensions of its simple modules, in terms of the modular $S$-matrix of $\mathcal{C}$ and a certain set of quantities extracted from the action of $\mathsf{P}$. This formula is expected to play the role of a non-orientable analogue of the Verlinde formula, in the context of Reshetikhin-Turaev-type topological quantum field theories on non-orientable manifolds.
