# Scotogenic dark matter from gauged $L_μ+L_τ$

## authors:

## summary:
In this work, we introduce the first scotogenic model based on a gauged $L_μ + L_τ$ symmetry whose spontaneous breaking by two units leaves a remnant matter parity $M_P$. The resulting light active neutrinos are of Majorana nature and the lightest electrically neutral state mediating the scotogenic loop is stabilized by $M_P$ and can be identified as a weakly interacting massive particle dark matter candidate. We analyze the phenomenology of a predominantly singlet-like CP-even scalar dark matter candidate and the representative regions of parameter space compatible with the observed thermal relic abundance. The $Z'$ boson is compatible with a broad range of parameters relevant for dark matter. In particular, electroweak precision constraints do not lead to significant tensions for kinetic mixing values up to $ε\sim 10^{-2}$. We discuss the implications of existing searches for leptophilic gauge bosons and the conditions under which visible and invisible collider signatures become relevant.
