# Numerical tests of 5D APS $η$-invariant phases for 4D chiral fermions

## authors:
#Tatsuhiro_Misumi

## summary:
We calculate the phase of a 4D chiral fermion determinant from the 5D Atiyah--Patodi--Singer (APS) $η$-invariant. We study the anomaly-free $SU(5)$ representation $\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf 5}$ on a four-torus, using gauge fields independent of one coordinate. For these backgrounds, the 4D phase can be calculated independently from a 3D Dirac spectrum. For an Abelian background embedded in $SU(5)$, two different 5D bulk extensions reproduce the 4D phase within $0.03\%$ in the tested heat-kernel extrapolations. One extension changes the spatial gauge field along the fifth direction and requires a coupled 5D spectral calculation. An independent single-charge check reproduces the Chern--Simons anomaly coefficient within $0.3\%$ using quadratic extrapolations. We also calculate 5D APS phases for non-Abelian backgrounds in the $SU(4)\times U(1)$ subgroup. For a background generating the full $\mathfrak{su}(4)$ algebra, the 5D phase with a fixed spatial field agrees with the independent 4D phase within $0.5\%$ over the six heat-kernel parameters examined. We further study a varying-field 5D extension for an embedded $SU(2)$ background. These results show that the 5D APS calculation reproduces a nonzero chiral phase after anomaly cancellation.
