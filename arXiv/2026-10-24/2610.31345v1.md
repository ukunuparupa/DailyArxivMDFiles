# Unitarity methods for Magnus amplitudes

## authors:
#Jung-Wook_Kim
#Sungsoo_Kim
#Jian-Rong_Li

## summary:
Magnus amplitudes refer to the matrix elements of the logarithm of the $S$-matrix. Modern unitarity methods construct cut-constructible parts of scattering amplitudes without directly invoking Feynman diagrams, offering an efficient way of obtaining loop integrands for scattering amplitudes. A procedure is presented that adapts unitarity methods to the computation of Magnus amplitudes.
