# Nonlinear Response of Schwarzschild Black Holes at Arbitrary Perturbative Order

## authors:
#Jiaqi_Chen
#Yu-Cun_Xie

## summary:
We establish recursive integration-by-parts identities that symbolically reduce the inhomogeneous solutions of the Regge-Wheeler-Zerilli equation to a small set of master integrals, yielding the nonlinear response kernels of Schwarzschild black holes at arbitrary perturbative order. We further derive a closed ODE system that enables rapid, accurate, and stable numerical evaluation of these master integrals. To demonstrate our result, we compute quadratic, cubic, and quartic quasinormal-mode coupling coefficients as special cases of the response kernels.
