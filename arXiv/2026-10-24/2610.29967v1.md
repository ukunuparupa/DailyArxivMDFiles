# Gravitational Waves from New Inflation

## authors:
#Naoya_Kitajima
#Kamil_Mudrunka
#Kazunori_Nakayama

## summary:
Gravitational waves provide one of the most powerful observational probes for discriminating among inflationary models. In particular, it has recently become clear that the high-frequency gravitational-wave spectrum encodes a wealth of information about the properties of the inflaton and the post-inflationary dynamics. In this paper, we present the first comprehensive prediction of the complete gravitational-wave spectrum in the new inflation scenario, a prototypical realization of small-field inflation, incorporating all known production mechanisms including both the classical and quantum sources.
