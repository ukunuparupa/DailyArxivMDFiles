# Higher-Loop Vacuum Integrals on $S^D$

## authors:
#Varun_Lochab
#Frederik_Denef

## summary:
We develop a new framework for evaluating higher-loop integrals for scalar fields on $S^D$. Starting from a Schwinger-parametric form for general higher-loop integrals on the sphere, it combines total-derivative identities with edge-contraction relations into a coupled linear system. To illustrate our method, we solve for all vacuum graphs through two loops. This includes, in particular, the two-loop three-melon, for which we construct the analytic solution for general $D$ and obtain explicit dimensionally regularized results at all integer dimensions.
