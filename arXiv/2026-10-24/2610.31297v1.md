# Exact stress-tensor generators distinguish integrable continuations

## authors:
#H_Babaei-Aghbolagh
#Song_He
#Zahra_Pezhman

## summary:
The same leading $T\bar T$ interaction can coexist with sharply different strong-field responses, even within causal, classically integrable sigma models. We reconstruct exact autonomous stress-tensor generators for prescribed one-scale Courant--Hilbert families in two dimensions. Separating the invariant stress scale from a signed trace ratio reduces reconstruction to one scalar inversion on each invertible physical branch. The resulting profile determines both the deformation law and the relation between local energy density and pressure. For generalized Born--Infeld and logarithmic models, the leading interactions coincide, yet pressure saturates in the former and grows without bound in the latter. Their exact generators are respectively quadratic and nonpolynomial, the latter involving Lambert $W_{-1}$. A regular Legendre transformation gives the same profile in the auxiliary potential formulation. The flows commute locally with root-$T\bar T$ within a common smooth branch and retain a common classical Lax form. Exact reconstruction thus quantifies the nonlinear freedom left by leading-order matching.
