# A Comment on Local Hypercube Inequalities

## authors:
#Keyou_Zhuo
#Tian-Shun_Chen
#Kilar_Zhang

## summary:
This comment gives dimension-independent analytic proofs of the local inequalities arising in the odd- and even-dimensional constructions of higher-dimensional partition charge functions. The prior works established these inequalities by exhaustive computation in low dimensions and tested them numerically in selected higher dimensions.
