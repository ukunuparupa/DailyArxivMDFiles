# Explicit soft scale symmetry breaking regulators: the Higgs mass and the consistency of scale symmetry breaking

## authors:
#A_R_Vieira

## summary:
In this work, we search for explicit regulators that do not spuriously break scale invariance starting from conditions established by implicit regularization. There is a family of regulator functions that obey the conditions and lead to soft breaking terms of the dilatation current. This family of functions is shown to be related with the Laguerre polynomials. We apply this approach by revisiting quadratic divergences in the context of the naturalness problem of the Standard Model.
