# Quantum field-theoretical description of solar neutrino oscillations

## authors:
#Vadim_Egorov
#Igor_Volobuev

## summary:
A quantum field-theoretical description of neutrino oscillations in matter in adiabatic approximation is developed. Estimates of the oscillation coherence lengths have been made for several solar neutrino production and detection processes. It has been shown that, within the framework of the developed approach, the results of solar neutrino detection experiments can be correctly described in terms of neutrino mass eigenstates without taking oscillations into account.
