# AdS$_4$ Celestial Symmetries: from Ambitwistor charges to CFT$_3$ Light-Ray Operators

## authors:
#Alex_Goodenbour
#Adam_Kmec
#Lionel_Mason
#Romain_Ruzziconi

## summary:
The ${L}_Λw_{1+\infty}$ algebra is the cosmological-constant deformation of the celestial $Lw_{1+\infty}$ symmetry algebra of gravity, providing an infinite-dimensional extension of the three-dimensional conformal algebra at the AdS$_4$ boundary. We construct the Hamiltonian charges associated with these symmetries from boundary ambitwistor-space data, using LeBrun's Heaven on Earth correspondence, and translate them to spacetime via the Penrose transform. We relate the resulting expressions to light-ray operators in the boundary CFT$_3$, including ANEC operators, their conformal descendants, and their commutators.
