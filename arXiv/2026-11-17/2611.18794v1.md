# Nonperturbative lattice simulation of the nucleation rate in thermal first-order phase transitions

## authors:
#Jing_Zhou
#Ligong_Bian

## summary:
We present a nonperturbative lattice study of vacuum decay through bubble nucleation in a three-dimensional real scalar field theory at finite temperature. The vacuum decay rate is computed by combining an expanded-ensemble multicanonical hybrid Monte Carlo approach, which samples the critical bubble configurations, with real-time Langevin dynamics, which follows the subsequent bubble evolution. We extrapolate the nucleation rate to the continuum limit and compare it with the corresponding one-loop perturbative prediction, finding a discrepancy of about \(8\%\).
