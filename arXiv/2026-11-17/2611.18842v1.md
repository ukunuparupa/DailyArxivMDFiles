# Skyrmions coupled to the sigma meson

## authors:
#Christoph_Adam
#Carlos_Naya
#Thomas_Winyard

## summary:
We propose a geometric coupling of the Skyrme model of nuclei to the scalar--isoscalar sigma meson by identifying the sigma field with the radial length of the chiral four-vector while preserving the topological protection of the baryon number. Restricting to the $B=1$ hedgehog ansatz, an asymptotic analysis of the field equations shows that the in-vacuum sigma mass $m_σ$ is replaced inside the nucleon by an effective in-medium mass $m_{σ,{\rm eff}} = 2m_π$. Full numerical integration via a double-shooting method and parameter optimization demonstrate that including the sigma meson leads to a remarkable improvement in the description of nucleon observables and to a pion decay constant very close to its physical value.
