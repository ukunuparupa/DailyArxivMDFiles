# On the Spontaneous symmetry breaking of Conformal Gravity to Weyl and Einstein Gravities

## authors:
#Partha_Nandi
#Gerasimos_Kouniatalis
#Stelios_Stefas
#George_Zoupanos

## summary:
Does the special-conformal connection remain an auxiliary field when a spontaneously broken conformal gauge theory is promoted to a fully dynamical Yang-Mills-Higgs theory, and under what conditions does its elimination yield Weyl gravity? We address these questions within an $\mathrm{SO}(2,4)$ gauge-theoretic formulation of gravity. Revisiting the gravitational sector selected by spontaneous symmetry breaking through an adjoint $15$-plet, we retain the special-conformal connection as an independent field and demonstrate that, on a source-free projected branch, its algebraic equation of motion determines it in terms of the Schouten tensor. Eliminating this connection yields the four-dimensional Weyl-squared action, up to the Euler density, without imposing the Schouten relation as an additional geometrical constraint. We then extend the analysis to the standard local Yang-Mills-Higgs theory and show that the special-conformal connection remains auxiliary, owing to the structure of the invariant bilinear form of $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$, which prevents the appearance of an independent kinetic operator. The additional gauge and scalar sectors can modify its algebraic solution, so the exact Weyl reduction is restricted to the source-free branch. We further investigate the linearised spectrum of the reduced Weyl theory around de Sitter spacetime, identifying massless and partially massless spin-2 branches, with the latter occurring at $m_{\mathrm{PM}}^2=2Λ/3$. Finally, we discuss the subsequent symmetry breaking induced by an antisymmetric scalar $6$-plet and its role in connecting the Weyl-gravity phase to Einstein gravity.
