# Unruh-induced decoherence and entanglement degradation in Ramsey interferometry

## authors:
#E_D_C_Brandão
#O_P_de_Sá_Neto
#F_C_E_Lima
#H_A_S_Costa

## summary:
We propose a modified Ramsey interferometric protocol to probe decoherence associated with the Unruh effect through fringe visibility. The scheme employs Unruh-DeWitt detectors coupled to a massless scalar field in the Minkowski vacuum through an interaction that induces pure dephasing without transitions between their internal energy levels. For a uniformly accelerated detector with finite spatial extent, an analytical dephasing function separates inertial vacuum fluctuations from the thermal Unruh contribution. When the thermal contribution dominates, fringe visibility decays exponentially with a rate proportional to the proper acceleration. The bipartite extension considers two detectors initially prepared in a Bell state, one inertial and the other uniformly accelerated, while retaining correlations mediated by the scalar field. In particular, we find that the joint fringe visibility coincides exactly with concurrence when the cross-term contribution disappears. This suggests that the entanglement degradation can be quantified directly from the interference contrast.
