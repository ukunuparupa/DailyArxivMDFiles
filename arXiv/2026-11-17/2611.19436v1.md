# $Q$-operators, cluster mutations, and flavored Toda chain

## authors:
#Saebyeok_Jeong
#Taro_Kimura
#Norton_Lee

## summary:
We construct Baxter $Q$-operators for a family of quantum cluster integrable systems, that we call the flavored Toda chains, associated with quivers $\mathcal{Q}_N[F]$, where $F\subseteq\{2,\dots,N\}$ specifies data of $\vert F \vert$ additional nodes compared to $\mathcal{Q}_N[\varnothing]$ for the ordinary $N$-particle open $q$-deformed Toda chain. A distinguished sequence of quantum cluster mutations yields an ordered product of Faddeev's quantum dilogarithms. We prove that these operators form a commuting family and that their shifted quotients are transfer matrices whose coefficients are commuting Hamiltonians. We identify these transfer matrices with matrix elements of products of explicit local Lax matrices of rank two. Local cluster transformations move flavor insertions along the chain while preserving their ordered flavor parameters and the transfer matrix together with the canonical transformation. We also derive integral kernels for the $Q$-operators and evaluate an associated Fourier transform as a factorized scalar $Q$-function. Finally, we relate the quivers to the BPS quivers of five-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=1$ gauge theories in the decoupling limit and identify the normalized scalar $Q$-function with the perturbative contribution of a canonical codimension-two defect wrapping a squashed three-sphere.
