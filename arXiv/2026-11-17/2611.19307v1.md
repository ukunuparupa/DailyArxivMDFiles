# Holographic Entanglement after a Global Quench in Noncommutative Yang-Mills Theory

## authors:
#Elahe_Hassanpour
#Komeil_Babaei_Velni
#M_Reza_Mohammadi_Mozaffar

## summary:
The time evolution of holographic entanglement entropy and mixed-state correlations following a global quench in four-dimensional N=4 noncommutative Yang-Mills theory is investigated using a Vaidya geometry. For strip-shaped regions extended along a noncommutative direction, two distinct early-time growth regimes emerge, depending on the relation between the shell-crossing and noncommutative scales: logarithmic growth, with a coefficient proportional to the injected energy density and the square of the noncommutative scale, followed by quadratic growth. For sufficiently wide strips, an intermediate linear-growth regime appears, governed by an entanglement velocity that increases with noncommutativity. The approach to equilibrium is also examined, revealing the role of the butterfly velocity in the continuous evolution of extremal surfaces, while competing branches can modify the physical saturation time. For two disjoint strips, the leading width-independent logarithmic contribution cancels in the mutual information, whereas the entanglement wedge cross section exhibits an intermediate linear-growth regime governed by the same entanglement velocity, provided the connected entanglement wedge remains dominant.
