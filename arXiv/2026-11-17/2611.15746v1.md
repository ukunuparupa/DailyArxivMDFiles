# Relaxation of Quantum Observables in a 4D Black Hole that forms from Collapse of a Null Shell

## authors:
#Shohreh_Gholizadeh_Siahmazgi
#Anıl_Zenginoğlu
#Paul_R_Anderson
#Alessandro_Fabbri

## summary:
The vacuum polarization and luminosity are computed in a four-dimensional spacetime where a spherically symmetric null shell contracts to form a black hole. This is done by computing the contribution of the spherically symmetric modes of a massless minimally coupled scalar field to the quantities $\la φ^2 \ra_{\rm in} - \la φ^2 \ra_U$ and $L_{\rm in} - L_U$. Here the subscript ``in'' denotes the {\it in} vacuum state for the null shell spacetime and the subscript ``U'' denotes the Unruh state in Schwarzschild spacetime. The computation is done outside the null shell and outside the event horizon where the spacetime geometry is the Schwarzschild geometry. It is found that, as expected, these differences approach zero at late times. However, they do so significantly more slowly than in the two-dimensional case where there is no scattering of the mode functions. In both cases, the difference is dominated by a quasinormal mode at intermediate times and a tail term at late times that decays quartically in time. For the luminosity difference, an analytic calculation verifies the late-time result for the tail term.
