# Renormalization Group Flow from 4d Chern-Simons Theory

## authors:
#Meer_Ashwinkumar
#Masahito_Yamazaki

## summary:
The four-dimensional Chern-Simons theory coupled with surface defects is known to realize two-dimensional integrable quantum field theories (QFTs). We discuss the renormalization group (RG) flow of such two-dimensional integrable QFTs from first-principles Feynman diagram computations intrinsically in the four-dimensional framework. We first derive the one-loop RG flow of two-dimensional QFTs realized by the four-dimensional Chern-Simons theory on $Σ\times C$ coupled with the order surface defects supported on $Σ$. Here, the affine Kac-Moody symmetry of the defects underlies the one-loop RG flow. We then derive the one-loop RG flow of the principal chiral model (PCM) without Wess-Zumino term, which is realized using disorder surface defects, within the Batalin-Vilkovisky formalism. We obtain the nontrivial one-loop RG flow of the PCM, reproducing its known asymptotically-free beta function, using two complementary methods: by explicitly evaluating one-loop Feynman amplitudes, and by computing an analogue of analytic torsion for four-dimensional Chern-Simons theory.
