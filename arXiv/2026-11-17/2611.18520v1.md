# The Spectral Anatomy of Complex-Time Singularities

## authors:
#Samuel_Valach
#Mile_Vrbica

## summary:
The properties of the bulk spacetime in a holographic theory may be probed by the analytic structure of the boundary correlator $G(t,x)$. In this letter, we analyse the singularities of $G(t,x)$ by deriving them from Fourier space. We show that the quasinormal modes are characterised by two distinct momentum scaling regimes. The first regime is defined by a finite $k/ω^γ$ for some characteristic exponent $γ$ which is extracted from the singularity, and probes $G(t,0)$, reproducing the bouncing geodesic analysis. The second regime is defined by a finite $k/ω$, and probes the complexified bulk away from the singularity, which allows us to study the singularities of $G(t,x\neq 0)$. We extract coefficients and exponents of the singularities for general spacetime dimensions, and allow for more exotic interiors. The heavy reliance of our computation on quasinormal modes opens the doors to a similar analysis being done on spacetimes that are accessible only numerically.
