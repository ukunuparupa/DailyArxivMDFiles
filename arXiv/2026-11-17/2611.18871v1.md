# Beyond the Onsager matrix: All-order resummation of relativistic particle and energy diffusion

## authors:
#Lorenzo_Gavassino

## summary:
We study the coupled diffusion of particle number and energy carried by ultrarelativistic probe particles propagating through a material medium. The probes undergo elastic scattering, which randomizes their direction, and inelastic scattering, which exchanges energy with the medium while preserving particle number. We compute exactly the full $2\times2$ frequency- and wave-number-dependent diffusivity matrix, resumming the constitutive gradient expansion to all orders. In a particularly natural Onsager-normalized basis of conserved densities, the resummed matrix is found to be diagonal at arbitrary frequency and wave number, even though particle and energy transport remain coupled and the conventional Onsager matrix has nonzero thermoelectric cross coefficients. Beyond the hydrodynamic limit, the resummed diffusivities resolve microscopic relaxation timescales and the ballistic kinetic continuum that are invisible to the ordinary Onsager coefficients.
