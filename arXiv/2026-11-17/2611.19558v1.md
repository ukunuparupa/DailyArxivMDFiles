# A proof of Nakamura's conjecture

## authors:
#Arsen_R_Melikyan
#Aleksandr_Pinzul

## summary:
We prove Nakamura's conjecture on the relation between the 2D Toda model and solutions of the Ernst equation, using the Pfaffian solutions obtained in our previous work arXiv:2610.27420.
