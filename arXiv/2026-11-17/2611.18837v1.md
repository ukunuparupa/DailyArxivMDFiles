# Generalised global symmetries of 6d SCFTs on a circle from the blow-up equations

## authors:
#William_Harding
#Noppadol_Mekareeya

## summary:
We study 6d $(1,0)$ SCFTs on a circle, possibly twisted, through the blow-up equations of their 5d descriptions. For every theory we consider, we construct the explicit map between the 6d and 5d frames, including the ratio $r$ of the Kaluza--Klein (KK) mass to the instanton mass, which depends on the frame and not only on the twist. Our main result is that the higher-form symmetries of these theories, their mixed anomalies and 2-groups can all be read off from the fluxes through the exceptional curve of the blown-up $\mathbb{C}^2$. The flux sectors are the 't$~$Hooft sectors of the 5d 1-form symmetries. On the tensor fluxes, the 6d Green--Schwarz couplings fix the weights, whose fractional exponents we read as the mixed anomalies of the 6d 2-form symmetry with the KK $\mathrm{U}(1)$, gravity, flavour and $\mathrm{SU}(2)_R$. The fluxes forced in each sector decide between a 2-group and a mixed anomaly. For instance, the twisted frames $\mathfrak{su}(4)_{0,4}+4Λ^2$ carry a non-split $\mathbb{Z}_4$ 2-group, while, in $\mathfrak{su}(4)_2+4Λ^2$ and $\mathfrak{su}(4)_6+2Λ^2$, the instanton symmetry is locked to the flavour centre and only a mixed anomaly remains. The same method applies to the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ gauging of two, three and four copies of the $E_1$ theory, which describes, respectively, $\mathrm{SU}(4)_0$, pure $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ and the untwisted $\mathfrak{so}(8)$ KK theory. There, the fluxes exhibit the extensions of 1-form symmetries inherited from the $E_1$ 2-groups, identify the states that break each accidental 1-form symmetry and the background to which it is locked, and give the 5d 2-group to which the $S_3$-invariant 6d 3-group reduces.
