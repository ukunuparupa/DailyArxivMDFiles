# Black holes in Myrzakulov $F(R,T)$ gravity

## authors:
#Alexandre_Landry
#Ratbay_Myrzakulov

## summary:
We investigate vacuum black-hole solutions in metric-affine Myrzakulov $F(R,T)$ gravity (MG-I), where $R$ is the affine Ricci scalar and $T$ is a quadratic torsion invariant, not the trace of the matter energy-momentum tensor. For the polynomial model $F(R,T)=αR+βT+γT^3$, with $α\neq0$, we first vary the metric and all components of the independent affine connection, and only then specialize to metric-compatible geometry with totally antisymmetric torsion. Nonzero axial torsion admits an exceptional branch $α+β+3γT_*^2=0$, on which the metric satisfies the Einstein equations with effective cosmological parameter $Λ_*=γT_*^3/α$. We establish a local existence result and construct torsionful Schwarzschild-(anti-)de Sitter representatives that are smooth through a selected future Killing horizon. Within the spherically symmetric axial ansatz, we characterize Ricci-symmetric affine connections by the condition $(fV-U)'=0$ and compare a constant-$W=fV-U$ family with a constant-$U$ family. Although these connections share the same metric and torsion scalar, their affine curvature invariants generally differ. Independent coordinate-level curvature contractions and component tests of the unrestricted metric and connection variations corroborate the identities used in the construction; these checks complement the analytic variational derivation. Finally, we identify null directions of the restricted axial quadratic form and distinguish this degeneracy from a complete metric-affine stability analysis. Our conclusions are local and do not establish global extendibility, physical inequivalence of all torsion profiles, or dynamical stability.
