# The fate of hidden symmetries under magnetic Harrison transformations

## authors:
#Shoulong_Li
#Fangkang_Yu
#Hong-Da_Lyu

## summary:
Solution-generating transformations construct new geometries from known seeds, raising the question of whether and to what extent the resulting solutions inherit the hidden symmetries underlying the seeds' integrability and separability. We have established universal perturbative inheritance laws for CKTs and massless scalar separability directly at the level of magnetic Harrison transformations, encompassing both magnetization of Carter-type seeds and demagnetization of conformal Carter-type seeds. In both magnetized Kerr and demagnetized Kerr-Bertotti-Robinson, this perturbative inheritance coexists with the loss of exact Carter-type separability, while seed-connected conformal Killing-Yano structures are already obstructed at the same perturbative order. These results reveal a hierarchy in the perturbative survival of hidden symmetries and provide a framework for investigating their inheritance in more general theoretical settings and under other solution-generating transformations.
