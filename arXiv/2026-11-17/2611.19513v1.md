# Local field shifts and the Gribov-Zwanziger horizon functional

## authors:
#Daniel_Guimaraes_Tedesco

## summary:
We study a Maggiore-Schaden-like re-centering of the Gribov-Zwanziger action using a smooth compactly supported profile. At fixed background and finite cutoff, under the stated coercivity and contour hypotheses, this transformation preserves the original horizon functional with no residual dependence on the profile. The extension to gauge-field integration requires additional domination assumptions. Near a self-adjoint first Gribov horizon, the response depends on both the lowest Faddeev-Popov eigenvalue and its source weight. We extend this visibility analysis to non-self-adjoint compressions, separating source darkness from truncation-induced shielding, and recall continuum transverse $SU(2)$ examples. Transporting the composite insertion preserves the regulated Gaussian and resolvent structure. A separate free-space tree-level check reproduces the auxiliary-mass denominator of the RGZ propagator, without determining condensate dynamics
