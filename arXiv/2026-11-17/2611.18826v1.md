# Schwarzschild scattering from CFT

## authors:
#Paolo_Arnaudo
#Benjamin_Withers

## summary:
We show that retarded CFT correlators on $\mathbb{R}\times \mathbb{S}^2$ in a state described by the small black hole in AdS$_4$ can be used to derive asymptotically flat Schwarzschild partial-wave S-matrix elements, including its spectrum of quasinormal modes. This is achieved by identifying a class of operators for which the dual bulk fields behave as plane waves near the AdS boundary for sufficiently high frequencies. For such operators there is a simple analytic relation between CFT correlators and reflection coefficients which behaves gracefully in the flat space limit, $L\to \infty$. These include spin-1, spin-2, and conformally coupled scalars. We provide a simple toy model that captures how dissipative QNMs can appear for short times inside Hermitian systems, closely resembling the small black hole in AdS.
