# Classification of Conformal Ising/Tricritical Ising Interfaces Preserving $\mathcal{W}_3^{\mathbb{Z}_2}$ Symmetry

## authors:
#Bufan_Zheng

## summary:
We classify conformal interfaces between the Ising and tricritical Ising models that preserve the chiral algebra $\mathcal{W}_3^{\mathbb{Z}_2}$. Using the folding trick, we formulate the problem in terms of boundary states satisfying the Cardy condition and determine the unique NIM-rep compatible with the bulk partition function. We obtain a complete set of 24 fundamental boundary states, of which 18 correspond to factorized interfaces preserving the full product Virasoro symmetry, while the remaining six break this symmetry. We construct these six interfaces explicitly and determine their annulus partition functions. They are related by topological defect line dressings, and one coincides with the RG domain wall proposed by Gaiotto.
