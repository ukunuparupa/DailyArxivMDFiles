# Quadratic Sum Rules for Scalar Amplitudes

## authors:
#Karol_Kampf
#Jiří_Novotný
#David_Podivín
#Jonah_Stalknecht

## summary:
Kleiss--Kuijf and Bern--Carrasco--Johansson relations, and their string monodromy generalizations, have long provided some of the most powerful and useful constraints on partial amplitudes. Motivated by their success, we uncover a new class of quadratic-sum rules that connect scalar amplitudes at different multiplicities. Combining recursion, kinematic shifts, and symbolic regression, we establish them for the nonlinear sigma model and its string-inspired extensions. We then extend these relations to diagonal elements of the inverse Kawai--Lewellen--Tye matrix and subsequently to planar scalar theories with nonderivative interactions and general propagators.
