# Asymptotic stability of axisymmetric perturbations of near-horizon extremal Kerr with a Schwarzian-deformed throat

## authors:
#Marcela_Cárdenas
#Benjamín_Cuevas-Tapia
#Claudio_Muñoz
#Jessica_Trespalacios
#Hanne_Van_Den_Bosch

## summary:
We study the asymptotic stability of axisymmetric gravitational perturbations of the near-horizon geometries of (near-)extremal Kerr black holes and consider the family of backgrounds in which the AdS$_{2}$ factor of NHEK is deformed by a Schwarzian mode. Requiring the Killing parameter to be local in the background makes the Gelfand--Dikii polynomials natural solutions of the Killing equation, and that locality requirement brings stationary solutions of the Korteweg-de Vries equation. The hierarchy selects a family of backgrounds, which remain $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})\times \mathfrak{u}(1)$ throughout them: the first level gives NHEK and near-NHEK, the second, solitons that depart from one of these geometries and return to it. We linearize Einstein's equations around this family, where the axisymmetric sector reduces to a wave equation on the nearly-AdS$_{2}$ base, and analyze this equation by energy and virial methods. For NHEK we obtain a conserved energy, an integrated local energy estimate and asymptotic stability in a weighted norm; for a nonconstant Schwarzian the energy becomes a Lyapunov functional whose monotonicity is dictated by the sign of its derivative, yielding uniform bounds for integrable ones, and mild local decay along the KdV soliton. We also find a monotone horizon-localized norm, which is not of Aretakis type, showing that stability properties are directly sensitive to the near-horizon structure encoded in the Schwarzian background. We compute the associated Regge--Teitelboim charges at a late-time corner of the throat using a radial ADM foliation of the spacetime.
