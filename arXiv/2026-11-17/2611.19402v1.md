# A Stable Vector Bounce

## authors:
#Nils_A_Nilsson

## summary:
We construct a geodesically complete bounce in cubic generalised Proca theory with a free canonical scalar as the matter field. The temporal vector evolves along its algebraic constraint and supports the transition from contraction to expansion while matter obeys the null energy condition. This evolution permits nonzero derivative mixing at the bounce. The kinetic coefficients are positive at every finite time for all nonzero comoving wavenumbers, and short-wavelength perturbations are stable throughout the solution. Following the linear equations to both infinite-time limits also shows that local perturbations are bounded for all nonzero comoving wavenumbers.
