# Stochastic gauge fixing from the holographic renormalization group

## authors:
#Soner_Albayrak

## summary:
We extend the dictionary between stochastic quantization and holographic Wilsonian renormalization to Yang-Mills theory in four-dimensional anti-de Sitter space at tree level. The radial component of the bulk gauge field is Zwanziger's gauge-fixing function; radial gauge is Parisi-Wu quantization, whose random walk of gauge modes is the longitudinal bulk propagator. We obtain the triple- and quadruple-trace couplings of gluons at finite cutoff in closed form. On transverse data, their dependence on the bulk gauge resides in a single zero mode, which drops out of the flat-space limit.
