# Large-$N$ expansion in SU($N$) Yang-Mills-like theories

## authors:
#Marco_Bochicchio
#Mauro_Papinutto
#Francesco_Scardino

## summary:
It has been known for fifty years that bare U($N$) pure Yang-Mills (YM) theory admits the large-$N$ 't Hooft expansion for the $n$-point connected correlators of gauge-invariant single-trace operators, where the corresponding Feynman diagrams are topologically classified by the sum over the genus $g$ of $n$-punctured smooth orientable closed Riemann surfaces. In fact, 't Hooft expansion extends verbatim to U($N$) YM-like theories whose Lagrangians only involve fields valued in the U($N$) Lie algebra - including supersymmetric (SUSY) extensions of pure YM theory such as $\mathcal{N}=1,4$ SUSY YM. The aim of the present paper is to systematically work out the topology of the large-$N$ expansion in SU($N$) - as opposed to U($N$) - YM-like theories, where a number of subtleties inevitably arise. Indeed, since the U($1$) inside the U($N$) YM-like theories decouples, the large-$N$ U($N$) and SU($N$) expansions should either essentially coincide or intrinsically differ - in the latter case necessarily by extra - possibly singular - topologies, as the 't Hooft expansion in U($N$) YM-like theories already exhausts all the smooth topologies. In fact, we demonstrate that 't Hooft smooth topologies are actually generically incomplete in the large-$N$ expansion of both the SU($N$) and renormalized U($N$) YM-like theories, where renormalization inevitably separates the SU($N$) sector that is interacting from the U($1$) sector that is free. Specifically, we interpret the extra topologies that arise in SU($N$) theories for correlators of higher-twist quasi-partonic operators as singular pinched closed surfaces, thus extending our previous investigation of correlators of twist-$2$ operators for which extra pinched tori arise.
