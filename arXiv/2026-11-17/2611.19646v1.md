# A Numerical Approach to Quantization

## authors:

## summary:
We introduce a numerical approach that simultaneously searches for matrix quantizations and compatible Poisson structures on classical geometric surfaces. Our method identifies irreducible Hermitian matrix representations and a trainable Poisson multiplier by minimizing a loss function that penalizes violations of the Casimir and commutation constraints. Through numerical investigations and subsequent theoretical analysis, we establish a no-go theorem showing that, for Weyl quantized fuzzy spheres with nondegenerate axial spectrum, the matrix dimension cannot exceed the degree of the nonconstant polynomial axial Poisson multiplier. We further extend our approach to nonpolynomial Poisson multipliers, demonstrating its versatility and potential to drive novel discoveries. Although here we focus on Weyl quantized fuzzy spheres, the framework is extensible to general geometric manifolds and quantization schemes. Our approach also provides a practical starting point for investigating Schild string theory and the quantization of spacetime histories.
