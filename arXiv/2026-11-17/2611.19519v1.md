# Confinement--Higgs Complementarity Viewed through Relative Quantum Reference Frames

## authors:
#Kei-Ichi_Kondo

## summary:
We formulate confinement--Higgs complementarity in terms of the relative orientation between two independently constructed quantum reference frames (QRFs): one derived from the gauge field and the other from the scalar field. Exact continuum and lattice identities give a relational interpretation of the scalar--color observables used in recent gauge-fixing-free $SU(2)$ simulations. In particular, we provide a QRF interpretation of the additional gauge-invariant nonlocal separation line obtained from the Cho--Duan--Ge (CDG) reduction reported in lattice simulations of the radially fixed fundamental $SU(2)$ gauge--scalar model. We explain why such a candidate nonlocal boundary is compatible with Fradkin--Shenker continuity and Osterwalder--Seiler analyticity for the appropriate class of local gauge-invariant observables. Separately, we show that a Wilson-rooted control observable admits an exact results at zero plaquette coupling and that it does not cause such a nonlocal separation line.
