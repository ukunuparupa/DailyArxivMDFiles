# Non-Topological Chiral Anomaly: The Chiral Magnetoelectric Effect

## authors:
#Rémy_Larue
#Amaury_Marchon
#Jérémie_Quevillon
#Diego_Saviot

## summary:
We derive the chiral anomaly in a thermal medium and show that, in addition to the well-established topological piece, the anomaly acquires a previously overlooked non-topological contribution, relevant for non-gauged axial symmetries. Remarkably, this modification of the continuity equation is independent of the electromagnetic fields, thereby giving rise to novel transport effects in relativistic hydrodynamics. We further propose an experimentally accessible new effect in Weyl semimetals which we call the Chiral Magnetoelectric Effect: an electric field proportional to the magnetic field is generated by the non-topological anomaly, even in the absence of an externally applied electric field. Beyond this concrete prediction, our results establish a broader hydrodynamic framework for non-topological chiral anomalies, opening the door to a range of previously unexplored anomaly-induced transport phenomena across condensed-matter and high-energy systems.
