# Conditional Principle of Least Action: Gauge Algebra and Noether's Second Theorem

## authors:
#V_A_Abakumova
#S_L_Lyakhovich

## summary:
We consider the problem of a conditional extremum of an action within a class of fields constrained by differential equations. For this setup, we establish the interconnection between the gauge symmetry algebra of the equations constraining the admissible fields and that of the action restricted to this class of fields. Based on this relation between the two gauge algebras, we derive an extension of Noether's second theorem and its converse. The general method is illustrated by the example of linearized gravity with the Einstein-Hilbert action and the class of admissible metrics restricted by the Cotton equation.
