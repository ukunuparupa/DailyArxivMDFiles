# Extremal non-rotating black holes have no fermionic Love

## authors:
#Xiankai_Pang
#Yu_Tian
#Hongbao_Zhang

## summary:
The static tidal Love numbers (TLNs) of $4$-dimensional black holes vanish for bosonic perturbations but are generically nonzero for fermions, with rare exceptions. In this paper, we show that for static, spherically symmetric black holes, fermionic TLNs vanish if and only if the black hole is extremal, in the sense that its horizon is degenerate. This follows from a closed formula for the static fermionic TLN of any asymptotically flat black hole, obtained by solving the static massless Dirac equation exactly on an arbitrary such spacetime and imposing regularity at the horizon. As applications, we analyze the Simpson--Visser regular black hole and the loop-quantum-gravity remnant black holes, whose extremal configurations lead to vanishing fermionic yet nonvanishing bosonic TLNs.
