# Non-abelian uni-vector deformations in gauged supergravities and non-abelian Einstein-Maxwell theories

## authors:
#Kirill_Gubarev
#Konstantin_Sovit

## summary:
We construct non-abelian uni-vector deformations of solutions in non-abelian Einstein\--Maxwell theories and gauged supergravities, obtained as Scherk--Schwarz reductions of general relativity and double field theory, respectively. We provide examples of deformed backgrounds for both cases. We show that non-abelian deformations in Einstein--Maxwell theories can be presented as coordinate transformations in the parent theory, general relativity, extending the result of arXiv:2508.09637 for abelian uni-vector deformations.
