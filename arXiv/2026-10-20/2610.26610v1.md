# The $A_3$ Narain family over $\mathbb{Z}_4$

## authors:
#Rajae_Sammani
#Abdellah_Sebbar

## summary:
The code $\{000,220,202,022\}\subset\mathbb{Z}_4^3$ realizes the split $A_3$ root-weight gluing construction as ordinary Construction A, preserving the Lorentzian pairing and the positive energy form. For two hyperbolic pairs of $A_3$ blocks, the twenty-two even self-dual gluing codes form seven block-monomial classes. Explicit metric and $B$-field representatives and theta series distinguish exactly seven T-duality classes in this family at central charge six; the one-pair family has two classes at central charge three. For arbitrary $d$, the chiral root system on either side is $A_1^{3m}$, where $m$ counts the words $(2e_i,2e_i)$ in the gluing code. Exactly one two-pair class has no zero-$B$ T-duality frame. Intersections of gluing codes determine finite shift orbifolds between the backgrounds, with the target code specifying the twisted-sector projections.
