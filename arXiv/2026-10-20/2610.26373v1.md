# The NLL/NLO$^+$ QCD spectrum from Higgs-plus-charm correlations at the LHC

## authors:
#Francesco_Giovanni_Celiberto
#Francesca_Lonigro

## summary:
We investigate the high-energy behavior of observables sensitive to the production of a Higgs boson in association with a singly charmed hadron at the $\sqrt{s}=14$ TeV LHC, with light-flavored hadrons considered for comparison. Our reference formalism is the hybrid factorization at NLL/NLO$^+$, where the standard collinear picture is enhanced by the resummation of energy logarithms at the next-to-leading level and beyond. We present a prime phenomenological application of the next-to-leading correction to the singly off-shell Higgs emission function, as implemented in the multimodular JETHAD interface, a core element to test the stability of our high-energy distributions under energy-scale variations and to assess the weight of missing higher-order uncertainties. The resulting natural stability traces our path towards precision studies of Higgs production within high-energy next-to-leading-order QCD.
