# Equilibrium relativistic spin magnetohydrodynamics from chiral kinetic theory

## authors:
#Gopal_Mistry
#Amaresh_Jaiswal

## summary:
We develop a microscopic and thermodynamically consistent framework for relativistic equilibrium spin magnetohydrodynamics of massless chiral fermions, directly from first-order chiral kinetic theory. Starting from the covariant Wigner function, we recast the chiral kinetic equation as a pure streaming equation on a magnetically shifted mass shell, incorporating both the magnetic-moment energy and the Berry-curvature phase-space measure. This formulation yields a kinetic $H$-theorem and an entropy current with vanishing divergence in global equilibrium. We construct the equilibrium distribution for an arbitrary spin frame and derive the associated currents, energy--momentum tensor, spin tensor, entropy current, and frame-independent spin polarization, including the magnetic splitting between particles and antiparticles. Thermodynamic consistency further determines the second-order magnetovortical responses, revealing a relativistic Barnett magnetization, a temperature- and chemical-potential-independent anomalous charge density, and rotation-induced density corrections that reproduce exact field-theory results. Our framework provides a unified microscopic foundation for relativistic equilibrium spin magnetohydrodynamics and establishes a direct link between quantum kinetic theory, spin polarization, magnetization, and anomalous transport.
