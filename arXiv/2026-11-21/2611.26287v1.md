# Black holes and 2-group symmetry from blackfolds in minimal supergravity

## authors:
#Jay_Armas
#Giorgos_Batzios
#Pedro_Vicente_Marto
#Chiara_Toldo

## summary:
We investigate the black hole phase diagram of five-dimensional minimal supergravity using the blackfold approach and the framework of generalized symmetries. Starting from supergravity coupled to probe sources, we derive the 2-group (non-)conservation laws, construct the equilibrium action for a two-dimensional 2-group fluid, and compute the associated global conserved charges. We then match the thermodynamics of the general non-extremal black string in the ungauged theory to that of this fluid, organizing the blackfold effective theory and its solutions in terms of symmetry invariants. In the non-extremal ungauged sector, we perturbatively construct new black rings, Saturns, di-rings, and bi-rings, including a black Saturn with seven independent parameters. Taking appropriate extremal limits, we identify several extremal branches, some previously unexplored and others anticipated, as well as BPS branches that approximate known supersymmetric families. Finally, we apply the same framework to construct extremal black rings in AdS$_5$, identifying three regular branches that are promising candidates for an exact completion.
