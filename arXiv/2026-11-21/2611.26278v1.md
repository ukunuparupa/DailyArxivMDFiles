# Exact Spherical Branes and Deformations: BMN, IKKT and $\mathcal{N}=2^\ast$

## authors:
#Cameron_Gibson

## summary:
We present a unified analytic solution to the BPS equations of spherical Dp-branes for $1\leq p \leq 5$ and give the corresponding type II uplifts in closed form. These are holographically dual to maximally supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory on $S^{p+1}$. The change of variables that makes the BPS equations integrable also allows us to analytically calculate a universal result for the holographic Wilson loop and obtain UV asymptotic behaviour previously determined numerically. The same change of variables extends naturally, with suitable modifications, to the cases $p=0$ and $p=-1$. We obtain a 1-parameter family of D0-brane solutions and show how the IR conditions lead to a singular limit of the integration constant. We also solve the D(-1)-brane BPS equations and show the resulting Euclidean uplift fails regularity conditions. Separately, we obtain an explicit representation of the one-ball electrostatic solution of polarised IKKT, which lets us express multipole moments and holographic observables using elliptic invariants. Finally, we analytically solve the BPS equations of the holographic dual of $\mathcal{N}=2^\ast$ supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory on $S^4$, present the corresponding type IIB uplift and calculate the holographic Wilson loop.
