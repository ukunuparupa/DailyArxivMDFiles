# Aggregating soft-photon recoil with a positive finite-step sampler

## authors:
#Nina_Elkina
#Alexander_Pukhov

## summary:
We model stochastic radiation reaction by aggregating soft photons into one positive recoil, retaining the local no-emission probability and two loss moments of the crossed-field QED kernel. Unlike Gaussian diffusion, the sampler preserves one-sided energy loss. Analytical synchrotron moments expose a finite-step cooling bias, which state feedback reduces. Laser and ion-channel tests probe excessive-loss risk and directed radiation spectra, providing a practical route to stochastic particle simulations without resolving individual soft emissions.
