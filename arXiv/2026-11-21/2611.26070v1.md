# Galaxy Bias and Soft Limits from Inflation

## authors:
#Charalampos_Nikolis
#Henrique_Rubira

## summary:
Primordial non-Gaussianity (PNG) leaves an imprint on large-scale structure through a scale-dependent galaxy bias, and properly incorporating the effects of higher order PNG into the bias expansion requires a consistent renormalization framework that extends beyond the squeezed limit of the bispectrum. We develop the multi-loop renormalization of bias operators in the presence of non-vanishing primordial n-point functions of arbitrary order, including stochastic contributions. We show that galaxy bias renormalization is determined by the multi-soft limits of primordial correlators and bias kernels. We provide explicit computations for one- and two-loop renormalization of deterministic operators up to fifth order in the bias expansion. Finally, we derive the renormalization group equations for the bias parameters at one and two loops.
