# Full-Color Two-loop Four-point Form Factor in N=4 SYM with Global CK-duality

## authors:
#Zhiming_Cai
#Yuanhong_Guo
#Gang_Yang

## summary:
We determine the full-color two-loop four-point MHV form factor of the stress-tensor supermultiplet in N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory. Using color-kinematics (CK) duality and unitarity cuts, we construct complete one- and two-loop integrands manifesting global off-shell CK duality without explicit $μ^2$ dependence. Integration-by-parts reduction to uniform-transcendentality master integrals yields purely rational numbers multiplying simple kinematic prefactors, manifesting maximal transcendentality. Expressing the form factor in terms of pentagon functions, we verify full-color infrared-pole cancellation, extract the finite remainder, and obtain new non-planar results for the two-loop triple-collinear splitting function and double-soft current.
