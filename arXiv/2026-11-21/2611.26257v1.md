# Fast extraction of universal data from temporal spectrum: Applications to Haagerup CFT and monopole scattering

## authors:
#Tokiro_Numasawa
#Boris_De_Vos
#Masataka_Watanabe
#Atsushi_Ueda

## summary:
Extracting precise universal data on conformal field theory (CFT) on the lattice traditionally requires large-scale simulations of the ground states and excitations of critical systems. Here, we propose an alternative accurate and fast algorithm, where we read off the CFT spectrum from the transfer matrix spectrum of an infinite matrix product state after a short imaginary-time evolution. We apply this method to extract the charge-resolved spectrum of a Haagerup CFT to higher levels and obtain a central charge $c = 2.137(27)$. Moreover, we show that the Maldacena--Ludwig monopole boundary can be realized as the ground state of the Fidkowski--Kitaev Hamiltonian after space-time rotation.
