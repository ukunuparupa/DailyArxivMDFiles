# Weyl-induced ultrasoft renormalization of the static QCD potential

## authors:
#Michael_Wilson

## summary:
We derive the leading curvature-dependent ultrasoft anomalous dimension of the static color-singlet potential in potential nonrelativistic QCD. In a Ricci-flat background the electric Weyl tensor introduces a $t^{-2}$ term in the short-time chromoelectric correlator. The resulting dipole self-energy is ultraviolet divergent at order $α_s^2$ in the curvature sector, two orders earlier than the flat-space contribution. For $W=C_{μανβ}r^μu^αr^νu^β$ and $V_s^{(W)}=c_W W/r$, we obtain $ν\,\partial_νc_W=C_F C_Aα_s^2/(3π)$. We calculate the compensating soft infrared pole and the finite one-loop matching coefficient in an optical dimensional subtraction scheme. A direct physical-metric fermion calculation reproduces the finite light-flavor term. We also exhibit the cancellation of the leading regulated infrared overlap and the compensating transformation of soft and ultrasoft constants under an evanescent curvature rescaling. In the perturbative adiabatic regime the anomalous dimension fixes the coefficient of a curvature-dependent $α_s^2\lnα_s$ term in the static energy.
