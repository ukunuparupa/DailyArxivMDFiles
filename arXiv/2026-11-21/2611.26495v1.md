# Inhomogeneous Cutoff Regularization of Loops in Cosmological Correlators

## authors:
#Jacopo_Fumagalli
#Keisuke_Inomata

## summary:
We revisit the use of cutoff regularization in computing loop corrections to cosmological correlators. We argue that a cutoff corresponding to a fixed physical scale must be defined with respect to the local background geometry. In the presence of superhorizon perturbations, this is naturally implemented within the separate universe picture, where different local patches are regulated at the same physical scale. Expanding this cutoff in the long-wavelength background generates a series of regulator-induced contributions to loop amplitudes, which admit a diagrammatic interpretation as long-mode insertions on the corresponding loop contractions. Our cutoff prescription preserves residual spatial dilatations and volume-preserving anisotropic rescalings. It is therefore particularly relevant for loop correlators with soft external modes. We illustrate the mechanism by computing the contributions to the one-loop scalar and tensor power spectra on superhorizon scales. In both cases, the additional corrections induced by the inhomogeneous cutoff cancel the ultraviolet boundary terms arising in the ordinary diagrams. These calculations establish, within cutoff regularization, the late-time constancy of both power spectra and clarify its physical origin. We then investigate the squeezed one-loop scalar bispectrum and show that the additional cutoff contributions are essential for satisfying the consistency relation at finite cutoff. Finally, we discuss the role of the inhomogeneous cutoff in the late-time constancy of the one-loop bispectrum.
