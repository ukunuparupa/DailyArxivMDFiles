# All-Order Linearized Hydrodynamics of Charged Holographic Plasmas

## summary:
We investigate linearized all-order transport in a finite-density holographic plasma described by five-dimensional Einstein-Maxwell-Chern-Simons theory. By retaining the full frequency- and momentum-dependence of the fluid/gravity equations, we determine generalized shear, diffusion, higher-gradient, and chiral-vortical transport functions together with their real-time memory kernels. The different channels exhibit a clear hierarchy of temporal response: the leading shear and diffusion sectors respond promptly, whereas the higher-gradient sectors develop only after a finite buildup time. Remarkably, the chiral-vortical response also shows a finite buildup time despite entering at first order in the hydrodynamic derivative expansion, demonstrating that derivative order does not by itself determine the temporal organization of transport. Toward the near-extremal regime, long-time memory becomes increasingly persistent across several channels. These results reveal how finite density reshapes nonlocal transport and relaxation in strongly coupled holographic plasmas.
