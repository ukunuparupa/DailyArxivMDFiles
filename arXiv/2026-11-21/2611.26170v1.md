# Cardinal RG=GR: Phase-Space Cardinality and the Cosmological Constant and Hierarchy Problems

## authors:
#Laurent_Freidel
#Jerzy_Kowalski-Glikman
#Robert_G_Leigh
#Djordje_Minic

## summary:
The effective field theory approach constructs the low-energy theory by integrating out fluctuations above an ultraviolet scale which raises the cosmological constant and hierarchy problems. We investigate an alternative prescription in which the quantum theory is restricted to a finite accessible region of phase space, characterized by a dimensionless cardinality $\mathcal N$, rather than by independently prescribed spacetime and momentum scales. At fixed cardinality these scales are related. For Euclidean de Sitter space, the stationarity condition for the gravitational action can then be written as a stationarity condition with respect to the momentum scale, a relation which we call Cardinal RG=GR. We demonstrate the relation between $\mathcal N$ and the Gibbons-Hawking entropy of de Sitter space, compute the cosmological constant proportional to ${1}/{\sqrt{\mathcal N}}$, show that it is radiatively stable and apply the same prescription to demonstrate the radiative stability of the mass parameter for a simple interacting scalar field model, thereby illuminating the resolution of the hierarchy problem. We also establish a precise limit, involving gravitational data, in which the conventional results of Quantum Field Theory and the Renormalization Group are recovered.
