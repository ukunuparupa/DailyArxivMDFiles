# Notes on non-equilibrium thermodynamics of Robinson-Trautman spacetimes

## authors:
#Glenn_Barnich

## summary:
Robinson-Trautman spacetimes are determined by a parabolic heat-type evolution equation of fourth order for a scalar field on future null infinity. This equation may be re-written as an energy balance for an asymptotically time-like observer. In turn, it becomes a first law in the context of non-equilibrium thermodynamics if the non-linear memory is interpreted as heat. This leads to a local version of temperature and a manifestly increasing entropy density from the viewpoint of the environment.
