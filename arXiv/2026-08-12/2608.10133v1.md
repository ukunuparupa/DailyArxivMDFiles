# Towards a Proof of Mass Gap in 3d Yang-Mills Theory

## authors:
#V_P_Nair

## summary:
We give the outlines of a proof for the existence of a mass gap for the 3d Yang-Mills theory in terms of an inequality on the eigenvalues of the Laplacian on the gauge-invariant configuration space.
