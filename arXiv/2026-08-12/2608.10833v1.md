# False vacuum decay around black holes: calculation from first principles in the (3+1)-dimensional case

## authors:
#Ratmir_Gazizov
#Dmitry_Gorbunov
#Dmitry_Levkov

## summary:
In $(3+1)$ spacetime dimensions, we numerically calculate the suppression exponent of the false vacuum decay probability in the presence of a thermal bath and a black hole.
