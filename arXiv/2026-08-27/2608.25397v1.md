# Topological String Blowup Equations via Stable Pairs

## authors:
#Lutian_Zhao

## summary:
For the local Hirzebruch threefolds \(Y_\ell=\operatorname{Tot}_{\mathbb F_\ell}K_{\mathbb F_\ell}\), \(0\leq\ell\leq2\), we prove blowup equations for the two-variable, torus-equivariant symmetrized \(K\)-theoretic stable-pair series. After division by the fibre-class contribution, four-chart localization is identified coefficientwise with the equivariant Euler-characteristic series of \((\det\mathcal V)^\ell\) on moduli spaces of framed rank-two sheaves, with \(\mathcal V\) tautological. The framed-sheaf blowup formulas then give the unity and vanishing equations; for \(\ell=2\) this is an identity of localized indices. We state separately the two conjectures needed for the conditional local \(\mathbb P^2\) equations, the general Huang--Sun--Wang conjecture, and a rational-elliptic specialization involving the \(E_8\) lattice.
