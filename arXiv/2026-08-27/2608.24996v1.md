# Genus 4 Supermoduli Space Is Not Projected

## authors:
#Ron_Donagi
#Simone_Noja

## summary:
The supermoduli stack ${\mathfrak M}_g$, parametrizing smooth unpunctured super Riemann surfaces of genus $g$, is known to be non-projected for $g \ge 5$. We extend this result to the even-spin component of ${\mathfrak M}_4$. This is done by exhibiting an appropriate compact curve in the spin moduli space ${\mathcal S\mathcal M}_4^{+}$, and computing the restriction to it of the class obstructing the projectedness of ${\mathfrak M}_4^{+}$. This restriction turns out to be non-zero, proving that ${\mathfrak M}_4^{+}$ is non-projected.
