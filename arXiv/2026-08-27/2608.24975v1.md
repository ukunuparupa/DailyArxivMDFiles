# Emergence of Kastor-Traschen spacetime from a nonlinear Dirac system

## authors:
#Daisuke_Ida

## summary:
We establish a uniqueness theorem in four-dimensional spacetime, demonstrating that a nonlinear spinor system naturally constrains the background geometry to the Kastor-Traschen spacetime, which describes dynamical, cosmic multi-black-holes. By analyzing a nonlinear field equation where the super-covariant derivative of a Dirac spinor is sourced by intrinsic fermion currents, we evaluate its integrability under two physical requirements: a phase-locking condition enforcing a real hermitian product $\overlineψ_R ψ_L$, and a pure-electric source condition. We show that the field equation uniquely compels the spinor to be an eigenvector of the super-covariant Dirac operator, with the eigenvalue dynamically identified as the Hubble constant of the de Sitter background. These results provide a novel geometric mechanism wherein both the cosmological constant and dynamical black hole configurations spontaneously emerge from fermion condensates.
