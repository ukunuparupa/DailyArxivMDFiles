# Quasimaps to Nakajima varieties as critical loci

## authors:
#Spencer_Tamagni

## summary:
$X$ is a Nakajima quiver variety. In this note we report the observation that the space parameterizing quasimaps from $\mathbb{P}^1$ to $X$, sending infinity to a given point, may be presented globally as the critical locus of an explicit function.
