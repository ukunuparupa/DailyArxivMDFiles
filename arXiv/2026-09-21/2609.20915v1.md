# A Type IIB Dualifold

## authors:
#Zihni_Kaan_Baykara
#Cumrun_Vafa

## summary:
We present evidence that ten-dimensional type IIB string theory orbifolded by the $\mathbb{Z}_3$ duality symmetry at the strong-coupling fixed point $τ=\exp(2πi/3)$ defines a consistent theory. Motivated by its F-theory dual description and its duality with M-theory in nine dimensions and type IIA string theory in eight dimensions, we argue that the resulting theory is a ten-dimensional chiral non-supersymmetric theory with a discrete gauge symmetry whose bosonic quotient is the automorphism group of the Narain lattice $Γ^{2,2}(A_2)$ ($S_3 \times S_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$). We further identify the ten-dimensional tachyon fields with the corresponding Narain moduli. Gauge anomalies cancel nontrivially through a topological Green-Schwarz mechanism involving a $\mathbb{Z}_3$-valued two-form gauge symmetry: the ten-dimensional lift of the familiar zero-form orbifold quantum symmetry in the eight-dimensional type IIA description.
