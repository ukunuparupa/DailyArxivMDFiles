# A Split-Signature Type IIB Background on $\mathrm{AdS}_{5,\mathbb C}$

## authors:
#Brenno_Carlini_Vallilo

## summary:
We construct a maximally supersymmetric Type IIB background in signature $(5,5)$ on the real ten-manifold underlying complex $\mathrm{AdS}_5$. The spacetime is diffeomorphic to $T^*S^5$ and carries the invariant metric $G=\mathrm i(\bar q-q)$, where $q$ is the holomorphic constant-curvature metric. The real five-form $F_5=(4\mathrm i/L)(Ω-\barΩ)$, built from its normalized volume form, is self-dual, and its stress tensor supports this scalar-flat, non-Einstein metric. We derive these fields from the most general $\mathrm{SL}(4,\mathbb C)$-invariant metric and five-form ansatz: self-duality and the Einstein equation fix their phases up to the overall scale, orientation, and flux sign. The fields obey the reality conditions of the untwisted $\mathrm{IIB}_5$ theory. The holomorphic coframe makes the supercovariant curvature cancellation explicit, and flat transport gives thirty-two global real Killing spinors. Their Killing superalgebra is the factor-exchanging real form of $\mathfrak{psl}(4|4,\mathbb C)$, and we identify the corresponding supercoset.
