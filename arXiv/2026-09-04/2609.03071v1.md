# The trans-Planckian problem and gravitational interactions

## authors:
#Ted_Jacobson

## summary:
The strong gravitational interaction of counter-propagating quantum field modes is of foundational importance to the trans-Planckian problem of black hole horizons. This article, in memory of Renaud Parentani, is primarily an exposition of Renaud's attempt, using perturbation theory, a large $N$ approximation, and spherical reduction, to capture a mechanism by which those interactions might quench the trans-Planckian near-horizon quantum field correlations. Before giving a detailed account of Renaud's calculation, the trans-Planckian problem is briefly introduced, and the paper concludes with a discussion of related issues and questions.
