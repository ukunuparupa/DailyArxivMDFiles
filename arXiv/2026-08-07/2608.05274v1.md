# Relative entropy of entanglement of Haar random states

## authors:
#Takato_Mori
#Beni_Yoshida

## summary:
We determine the relative entropy of entanglement of a bipartite mixed state $ρ_{AB}$ obtained by tracing out one subsystem of a tripartite Haar-random pure state $|ψ\rangle_{ABC}$, finding $E_R(ρ_{AB})=\log\frac{d_Ad_B}{\max(d_A,d_B,d_C)}+O(1)$. Equivalently, the relative entropy of entanglement nearly saturates the smaller of the entanglement of formation $E_F(ρ_{AB})$ and the mutual information $I(A:B)$. The upper bound is achieved by an explicit separable state obtained through one-sided Schmidt dephasing, which is therefore approximately closest.
