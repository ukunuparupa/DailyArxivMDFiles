# AMSB in Truly Confining Gauge Theories

## authors:
#Riku_Ishikawa
#Hitoshi_Murayama
#Shota_Saito

## summary:
We study deformation of supersymmetric $t$-confining (truly confininig) gauge theories with small anomaly mediation of supersymmetry breaking. We identify breaking pattern of global symmetries in the theories. These results can be compared in priciple to lattice simulation of non-supersymmetric theories (except for one of the cases where the theory is pseudoreal and chiral).
