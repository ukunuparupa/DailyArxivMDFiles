# Point-Like Charges and Dirichlet Boundary Conditions in a Nonlocal Scalar Field Theory

## authors:
#L_H_C_Borges
#C_Filgueiras
#F_A_Barone
#A_A_Nogueira

## summary:
In this paper, we investigate novel classical non-local effects in scalar field theory arising from the presence of external sources and a material boundary. Specifically, we consider a model in which the standard Klein-Gordon field theory is modified by a non-local term. First, we analyze the interaction between stationary field sources modeled as point-like scalar charges. In specific configurations, the resulting interparticle potential reduces to the Cornell form. Then, we examine the model in the presence of a Dirichlet plane, computing both the scalar field propagator and the interaction force between the Dirichlet plane and a point-like scalar charge. Additionally, we demonstrate the failure of the image method in this non-local context and compare our results with those obtained in non-local electrodynamics and in the standard Klein-Gordon field theory.
