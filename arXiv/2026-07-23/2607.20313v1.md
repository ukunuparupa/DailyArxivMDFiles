# Resolution of Gauss' law in the maximal Abelian gauge

## authors:
#D_R_Junior
#H_Reinhardt

## summary:
We resolve Gauss' law in the maximal Abelian gauge supplemented by the Coulomb gauge for the Abelian gauge field and derive the gauge-fixed Hamiltonian of QCD.
