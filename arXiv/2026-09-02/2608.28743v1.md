# OPE and correlation functions in a generally covariant form

## authors:
#Arpit_Das
#Anatoly_Konechny
#Naveen_Balaji_Umasankar

## summary:
In this paper we consider some general aspects of Euclidean conformal field theories on curved spaces of dimension $D\ge 3$. We first look at the OPE of scalar primary fields on conformally flat spaces. Extending the results of \cite{Konechny:2026bqg}, we give a general construction of the descendants' contributions to the OPE in the scalar channel. We then discuss CFTs on non-conformally flat spaces in the ambient space formalism. We investigate the short-distance behaviour of three-point functions on such spaces using the general ansatz proposed by Parisini, Skenderis, and Withers \cite{Parisini:2022wkb, Parisini:2023nbd}. We find that some additional corrections need to be added to the ansatz to ensure the existence of a local covariant OPE.
