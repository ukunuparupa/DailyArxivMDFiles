# Gravitational Waves from Reheating beyond Instantaneous Thermalization

## authors:
#Kyohei_Mukaida
#Tenta_Tsuji

## summary:
We study gravitational-wave production during perturbative reheating without assuming instantaneous thermalization of the inflaton decay products. The injected energetic particles thermalize through an in-medium cascade of nearly collinear splittings subject to the Landau-Pomeranchuk-Migdal effect and are ultimately isotropized by elastic scatterings near the thermal scale. The non-thermal hard population present before complete thermalization produces an additional gravitational-wave component through hard-soft scatterings, with $Ω_{\mathrm{GW}}\propto f^{1/2}$ below the injection-scale turnover. The same isotropization erases the directional information underlying the vacuum $1/k$ bremsstrahlung soft pole, changing the deep-infrared spectrum from $Ω_{\mathrm{GW}}\propto f$ to $Ω_{\mathrm{GW}}\propto f^3$.
