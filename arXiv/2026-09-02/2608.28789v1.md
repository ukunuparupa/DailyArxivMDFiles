# Acceleration and Rotation in Three Dimensions

## authors:
#Cameron_R_D_Bunney
#Ruth_Gregory
#Robert_B_Mann

## summary:
We present a new family of exact solutions representing accelerating, rotating, point particle and black hole solutions in three-dimensional Einstein gravity. We investigate their geometric properties and the global embedding in three dimensional anti-de Sitter space. Our results extend the catalogue of exact solutions in three-dimensional Einstein gravity and provide a useful setting for the study of black hole thermodynamics, asymptotic symmetries, and holography.
