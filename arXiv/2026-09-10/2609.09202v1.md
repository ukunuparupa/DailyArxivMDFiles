# On the analytic continuation and matching of threshold singularities from Vacuum Amplitudes

## authors:
#Selomit_Ramírez-Uribe
#Prasanna_K_Dhani
#German_F_R_Sborlini
#Germán_Rodrigo

## summary:
We provide a detailed discussion for all kinematical configurations of the analytic continuation to negative values of initial-state on-shell energies used to generate interferences of scattering amplitudes from vacuum amplitudes in the Loop-Tree Duality. We also extend previous discussions on the matching of threshold singularities and introduce angular averaging as an efficient strategy to achieve a local cancellation when the matching of threshold singularities from vacuum amplitudes is constrained by flavour.
