# Wess-Zumino gauge for analytic prepotentials of off-shell $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity: bosonic sector

## authors:
#Nikita_Zaigraev
#Julia_Zernina

## summary:
We present a comprehensive study of the Wess-Zumino gauge for the analytic prepotentials of the off-shell $\mathcal{N}=2$ Weyl and $\mathcal{N}=2$ Einstein supergravities (the version with a non-linear tensor compensator). The field redefinitions, the full transformation laws, and the gauge superparameters that preserve the Wess-Zumino gauge are derived. As a concrete application of the obtained results, we examine the component structure of the free hypermultiplet action coupled to $\mathcal{N}=2$ supergravity. In this work, we restrict our analysis strictly to the bosonic sector.
