# The Saito determinant for extended affine Weyl discriminant strata

## authors:
#Andrea_Brini
#Karoline_van_Gemst

## summary:
We investigate the form of the Saito metric on quotients of the reflection representation of an extended affine Weyl group, restricted to an arbitrary stratum of its discriminant. We compute the Saito determinant for all strata and Dynkin types and show that it is divisible by a product of q-analogues of restricted roots for the associated hyperplane arrangement. Our results simultaneously generalise the factorisation in the Weyl denominator formula to higher codimension strata, and provide q-analogue refinements of results of Antoniou-Feigin-Strachan for Coxeter groups.
