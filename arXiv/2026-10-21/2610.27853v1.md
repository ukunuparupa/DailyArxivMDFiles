# Free-field realizations of $\mathcal{N} = 1$ supersymmetric $\mathcal{W}$-algebras associated with Lie superalgebras $B (n - 1 , n)$ and $B (n , n)$

## authors:
#Naozumi_Tanabe

## summary:
We extend the recursive construction of free-field realizations proposed by Ide, Ito, and Miyazaki to the $\mathcal{N} = 1$ supersymmetric $\mathcal{W}$-algebras $\mathcal{SW} B (n - 1 , n)$ and $\mathcal{SW} B (n , n)$, associated with the Lie superalgebras $B (n - 1 , n)$ and $B (n , n)$, respectively. We obtain explicit free-field realizations of $\mathcal{SW} B (0 , 1)$, $\mathcal{SW} B (1 , 1)$, $\mathcal{SW} B (1 , 2)$, and $\mathcal{SW} B (2 , 2)$. In particular, the realizations of $\mathcal{SW} B (1 , 2)$ and $\mathcal{SW} B (2 , 2)$ are new. Using these realizations, we compute central charges, conformal weights, and eigenvalues of the generating currents on free-field highest-weight states, and construct the local integrals of motion $I_1$, $I_3$, and $I_5$.
