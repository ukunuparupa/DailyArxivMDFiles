# Gravitational baryogenesis in asymptotically safe gravity

## authors:
#Rong-Jia_Yang

## summary:
Gravitational baryogenesis in asymptotically safe gravity is considered. Two baryogenesis interactions are explored and their effects on the baryon-to-entropy ratio are discussed during the radiation era. The model's parameters are constrained with the current observational data, indicating these constructions may provide viable explanations for the observed baryon asymmetry.
