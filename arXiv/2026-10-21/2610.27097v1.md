# Mapping AdS$_4$ quantum gravity via the Bethe Ansatz formula

## authors:
#Cheng_Peng
#Xuao_Zhang
#Ziruo_Zhang

## summary:
The topologically twisted index of 3d ADHM theory can be written as a sum over solutions to the Bethe Ansatz Equations (BAEs) of the XXZ-type model constructed from quantum toroidal $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}_1$. Via a novel numerical algorithm, we solve for all $p(N)$ physical solutions at $N\leq 21$, where $p(N)$ denotes the number of integer partitions of $N$. Surveying these solutions, we find that in addition to the known "1-cut" solution dual to dyonic black holes, where the gauge holonomies are distributed along a single curve in the complex plane, there are "dressed" 1-cut solutions dual to combined configurations of the black hole and M2-branes, $m$-cut solutions dual to $\mathbb{Z}_m$ orbifolds of the black hole, a discrete family of solutions interpolating between the 1-cut and 3-cut solutions, together with other exotic solutions, at each value of $N$. The free energy of the interpolating solutions cannot be matched with the on-shell action of any known bulk solution. The $1,2,3$-cut solutions and interpolating solutions all have a well-defined large $N$ limit, and their leading order distribution of gauge holonomies can be computed analytically. Whenever possible, we fit the contributions of the solutions to the index to all orders in the $1/N$ expansion with high-precision numerics, and extract the leading non-perturbative correction. With exceptions, these are matched with the results of equivariant localization in the bulk, and the action of wrapped M2-branes respectively.
