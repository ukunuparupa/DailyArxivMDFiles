# Deconfinement on $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ and Axion Domain Walls

## authors:
#Paolo_Bilisco
#Raffaele_Tito_D_Agnolo
#Arthur_Platschorre
#Pablo_Sesma

## summary:
We investigate the nontrivial dynamics on $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls and compute their tension in quantum chromodynamics (QCD) compactified on a circle with $N$ colors and $F < N$ massless fundamental flavors. In the process, we obtain insights on the $η'$ meson's potential in a regime where it is heavy and comparable in mass to other QCD resonances, as in Nature. We employ a center-stabilized compactification of QCD on a small circle of size $L$ with color-flavor twisted boundary conditions for the quarks. We demonstrate that probe particles in color representations of $N$-ality $N/\mathrm{gcd}(N,F)$ are deconfined on the $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls whenever $N$ and $F$ are not coprime. This is reminiscent of deconfinement on axion domain walls in pure Yang--Mills. Finally, in the double scaling limit $N\rightarrow \infty$ keeping $NL$ and $F$ fixed, the infrared spectrum spans an emergent dimension. We establish that the $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls are dual to electrostatic charge distributions in this emergent dimension. This duality allows us to compute their tension analytically and find a different tension scaling at large-$N$ between $\mathbb{Z}_{F}$ domain walls in QCD and axion domain walls in pure Yang--Mills, in center-stabilized compactifications.
