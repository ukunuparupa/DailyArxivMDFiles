# Surface defects on lens space and transfer matrices

## authors:
#Kazunobu_Maruyoshi
#Toshihiro_Ota
#Masahito_Yamazaki

## summary:
We study surface defects in four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2$ theories of class $\mathcal{S}$ and $\mathcal{N}=1$ theories of class $\mathcal{S}_k$ placed on $L(r,1)\times S^1$. The lens space index depends on the fugacities $p$ and $q$, and the effect of the defects on it is captured by difference operators acting on the fugacities and holonomies of flavor symmetries. For theories of type $A_1$ we determine in closed form the difference operators for the basic defects, labeled by $p^r$, $q^r$ and $pq$, and show that for odd $r$ there exist additional elementary defects, whose operators shift the holonomy by one half modulo $r$. All the operators commute, and the elementary operators generate the others. For even $r$ the same holds once the holonomy is extended to a twisted sector. We show that the elementary operators are traces of $L$-operators whose entries generate a Sklyanin algebra, with nome $q^r$ and shift $pq$. The lens space enters only through the parameters of the algebra and through its representation, which is realized on a doubled holonomy label. The $p$- and $q$-type algebras form a lens space version of the elliptic modular double. For class $\mathcal{S}_k$ theories we propose, and test in several ways, that the defect operators are transfer matrices of inhomogeneous spin chains built from these $L$-operators. Throughout, we use the TQFT structure of the lens space index, which follows from S-duality, both to fix and to check the operators.
