# AdS$_2$ from Type IIA on weak G$_2$ manifolds

## authors:
#Niall_T_Macpherson
#Achilleas_Passias

## summary:
We present and study supersymmetric warped ${\rm AdS}_2 \times {\rm M}_7 \times I$ solutions of Type IIA supergravity, where M$_7$ is a weak G$_2$ holonomy manifold. Locally, the solutions are characterized by a cubic polynomial. We distinguish two branches, Branch 1 and Branch 2, and analyze their global properties, taking flux quantization into account. At the endpoints of the interval $I$ they exhibit either OF1-plane or O8/D8 type of singularities with Branch 1 allowing also for a Spin(7) cone. In the case of zero Romans mass, Branch 1 uplifts to eleven dimensions to ${\rm AdS}_4 \times {\rm M}_7$ whereas Branch 2 uplifts to warped ${\rm AdS}_3 \times {\rm M}_7 \times I$ with OM2-plane type of singularities.
