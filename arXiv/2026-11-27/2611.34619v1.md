# The all-loop spectrum of GGK strings

## authors:
#Marius_de_Leeuw
#Andrea_Fontanella
#Cameron_Gibson
#Juan_Miguel_Nieto_García

## summary:
We study the non-perturbative spectrum of the non-relativistic limit of string theory in AdS$_5\times$S$^5$, also known as GGK theory. In light-cone gauge, we show that the bosonic spectrum on a number of vacua is described at all-loops by free massive and massless scalar fields in AdS$_2$, confirming a conjecture by de Leeuw, Fontanella and Nieto-García, arXiv:2403.09563. In static gauge, we obtain the same result for any classical string vacuum, and including fermions. We compute the quantum correction to the classical energy, and show that it vanishes once we adopt the regularised Hurwitz series. We conclude that the quantum energy spectrum built upon any GGK string consists solely of its classical vacuum energy.
