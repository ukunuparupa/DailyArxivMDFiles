# Measuring the flavor composition of high-energy neutrinos from the Milky Way

## authors:
#Mauricio_Bustamante

## summary:
The origin of the Milky Way's high-energy neutrinos is debated. Their flavor composition reflects how they are made and could help settle it, but remains to be measured. We show that IceCube and KM3NeT, which see the inner Galaxy in complementary ways from opposite hemispheres, could start resolving its flavor composition in about a decade. With upcoming telescopes, they would infer the $ν_e$ fraction at the sources to within 27%. A first comparison of Galactic and extragalactic flavor compositions could reveal strong magnetic fields in extragalactic sources or neutrino decay.
