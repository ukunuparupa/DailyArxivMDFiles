# Logarithmic scalar soft factorization from matching conditions

## authors:
#Matías_Briceño
#Hernán_A_González
#Oriana_Labrin

## summary:
Logarithmic soft theorems in QED and gravity can be understood as conservation laws associated with asymptotic symmetries. In contrast, for a massless scalar, no gauge symmetry is available to organize this relation. Despite that, we show that the logarithmic soft factorization for a massless scalar coupled to massive fermions through a Yukawa interaction follows from the asymptotic field equations and matching conditions at spatial infinity. From the first subleading logarithmic fall-off, we construct a conserved surface integral whose boundary values satisfy the antipodal map between past and future null infinity. At future null infinity, the field equations relate this quantity to the $1/u$ tail of the scalar radiation, while at past null infinity, for scattering states without incoming scalar radiation, it is carried by a non-radiative logarithm of the incoming particles. We derive the classical tail from the particle trajectories and obtain the hard charge from the asymptotic Dirac current. The particular solution of the leading scalar field gives the classical contribution, while its homogeneous component gives the quantum contribution. The resulting Ward identity reproduces the logarithmic soft coefficient of the one-loop scalar emission (up to a zero mode on the sphere), including its classical and quantum parts.
