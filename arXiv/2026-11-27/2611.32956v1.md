# Positive Geometry of Oriented Matroid Strata

## authors:
#Arne_Kuhrs
#Artyom_Lisitsyn

## summary:
We study the positive geometry of Grassmannians whose boundary is a union of Plücker divisors. For the full Plücker boundary the regions are closures of realization spaces of uniform oriented matroids. In rank three we show that the pair $(\mathrm{Gr}_{3,n},Z_{3,n})$ is a positive arrangement in the sense of Sturmfels and Telen for $n\le 9$ points and compute its combinatorial rank from point counts of the configuration space $\mathcal{X}_{3,n}$ over finite fields. For seven points the canonical forms of the regions span all logarithmic forms. For eight points they do not, because the Möbius-Kantor configuration is not realizable over the reals. This determines for seven and eight points whether the integrands of Cachazo, Early and Zhang for the generalized biadjoint amplitudes of Cachazo, Early, Guevara and Mizera span the space of logarithmic forms. From ten points on, the genus is positive because a K3 surface appears in the boundary. For smaller boundaries and arbitrary rank we give a recursion that adds one point at a time and preserves the genus and the combinatorial rank. It recovers the positive Grassmannian and shows that realization spaces of stacked polytopes are regions of positive arrangements of combinatorial rank one.
