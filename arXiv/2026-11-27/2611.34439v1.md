# Inverted Pendulum in the Sky: Floquet Stabilization of Cosmological Axions

## authors:
#Tokiro_Numasawa
#Masahito_Yamazaki

## summary:
Many cosmological scenarios require a scalar field to sit near the top of its potential, which usually requires a fine-tuned initial condition. We ask whether the hilltop of an axion potential can be dynamically stabilized by a rapidly oscillating coupling, as for the inverted pendulum of Kapitza, and to what extent this can reduce the fine-tuning. Using Floquet theory, we determine the stability window of the hilltop and analyze several realizations: an oscillating coupling, a spinning or oscillating second field, and a gyroscopic analogue, an inverted spinning top realized by a sigma model on a squashed three-sphere. In an expanding Universe the stabilized phase lasts for a number of $e$-folds logarithmic in the hierarchy of scales, independently of the cosmological background. Classifying the stabilizers by how their effective frequency redshifts, we show that in these realizations the hilltop cannot both attract the field and leave it frozen by the Hubble friction during radiation or matter domination, and that during inflation the attraction is limited by the Hubble friction and the energy budget. Thus the simple realizations studied here do not generically remove the hilltop fine-tuning. They can instead delay the onset of the oscillation and enhance the relic abundance as $n_φ/n_{φ,{\rm std}}\propto(\tilde m/H_c)^{3/2}$ in radiation domination. We discuss the implications for axion dark matter, inflation, quintessence and curvatons.
