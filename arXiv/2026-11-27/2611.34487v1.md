# Superconformal Index Identities for Dual Gauge Theories

## authors:
#Min-xin_Huang
#Gao-fu_Ren
#Xin_Wang

## summary:
We study superconformal index identities for three classes of dual four-dimensional gauge theories. For $\mathcal N=4$ super-Yang--Mills theory with gauge groups $SO(2n+1)$ and $USp(2n)$, we prove at every rank the equality of flavored Schur indices predicted by $S$-duality, using Rains's diagonal trace transformation. For Kutasov--Schwimmer duality with adjoint superpotential $\textrm{Tr} X^{k+1}$, we prove the index identity between $SU(k+1)$ and $SU(k-1)$ with two flavors at independent elliptic nomes $p,q$, in the ultraviolet assignment $R(Q)=0$. On the locus $p=q^k$, we reduce the identity to that of ordinary supersymmetric QCD whenever the specialized expressions are generically regular. Finally, we test the dualities conjectured by Cho and Song between quiver and single-group descriptions of four families of Argyres--Douglas theories. For the rank-one $H_1$ theory we prove equality of the two full flavored indices, and for each family at arbitrary rank we evaluate the generalized Coulomb index in both descriptions, obtaining the product formula conjectured by Buican and Jiang.
