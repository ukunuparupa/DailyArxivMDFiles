# Spectral reciprocity for reductive groups

## authors:
#Baizhou_Jin
#Zichang_Wang

## summary:
For every finite subgroup $Γ\subset\operatorname{SU}(2)$, every connected compact or connected complex reductive group $G$, and every finite central subgroup $B\subset Z(G)$, we construct a degree-preserving linear Fourier isomorphism between the polynomial class modules of $(G,B)$ and $((G/B)^\vee,\widehat B)$. The transform exchanges central twists with characters of the actual grades and preserves the invariant polynomial contributions of the full centralizers. In degree zero it yields central-coefficient reciprocity under the Poincaré pairing on $S^3/Γ$.  In particular, $Γ$ admits equally many conjugacy classes of homomorphisms into $G$ and its Langlands dual, which verifies a conjecture of Kojima and Tachikawa.
