# Distribution of the ratio of the two smallest eigenphases of the Jacobi unitary ensemble

## authors:
#Shinsuke_M_Nishigaki

## summary:
We compute the joint distribution of the two smallest eigenvalues of the Jacobi unitary ensemble and the distribution of their ratio. We use a framework for calculating Janossy densities based on the Tracy-Widom system of ordinary differential equations. For the cases studied here, the leading finite-N correction to the ratio distribution, relative to its universal Bessel-kernel limit, is O(N^{-4}). This behaviour reflects a nontrivial cancellation of lower-order corrections present in the Jacobi kernel. Eigenphase ratios may thus reveal subtle finite-size effects in quantum-chaotic systems with particle-hole or chiral symmetry. As an exploratory arithmetic application, we compare the ratio distribution for the two lowest zeros of quadratic Dirichlet L-functions with the hard-edge limit for Sp(N). The data indicate that the N_{eff}^{-1} term vanishes, leaving an O(N_{eff}^{-2}) correction.
