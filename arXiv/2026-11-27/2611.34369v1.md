# Robin--Hadamard states for unstable field in half-Minkowski spacetime

## authors:
#Bruno_S_Felipe
#João_P_M_Pitelli
#Claudio_Dappiaggi

## summary:
We investigate the quantization of a real massless scalar field in four-dimensional half-Minkowski spacetime subject to an attractive Robin boundary condition. We show that the unstable modes, required for classical spectral completeness, are also essential to recover the canonical commutation relations (CCR) of the complete quantum field. By specifying the Cauchy covariances of the unstable sector, we construct a family of positive quasifree states compatible with the conservation of total energy. Within this family, positivity is incompatible with stationarity, so that the resulting states necessarily break the time-translation symmetry of the background. We also obtain a closed position-space representation of the two-point function for the chosen stable vacuum sector, including the contribution fixed by the CCR in the unstable sector. The resulting expression exhibits the direct and reflected singularities, including Robin-dependent logarithmic terms. Since the remaining unstable contribution is smooth, all states in the constructed family share the same singularity structure, consistent with the Robin--Hadamard framework.
