# Power-law tunneling in the Razavy hyperbolic double well: Heun connection formulas and their resonances

## authors:
#Jie_Ren
#Xuanzhuang_Wu

## summary:
We study nonperturbative level splitting in the symmetric Razavy hyperbolic double well, relating its double-confluent Heun connection problem to exact WKB and quasi-exact solvability. In the weak-coupling limit the wells separate logarithmically and approach two Morse systems. Reflection factorizes the two-ended spectral condition into separate even and odd quantization conditions. For each normalizable Morse level with noninteger decay exponent, the irregular-block connection formula yields the leading splitting with its full gamma-function coefficient, two analytic fluctuation corrections, and the leading logarithmic three-traversal contribution. The noninteger power of the coupling follows from the logarithmic growth of the barrier action. We express the classical periods in terms of complete elliptic integrals and derive the first quantum-period correction. At integer exponent, a convergent reduction onto the two resonant Floquet modes resolves the singular fixed-order expansion into two local spectral branches and reproduces the known finite-spin tunneling gap.
