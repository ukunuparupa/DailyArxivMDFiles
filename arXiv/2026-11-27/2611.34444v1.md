# Black hole entropy from angular holonomies of Sine-Gordon soliton lattices in AdS$_3$

## authors:
#Marcela_Cárdenas
#Francisco_Correa
#Nicolás_Salazar

## summary:
We construct boundary conditions for AdS$_3$ gravity that realize Sine-Gordon dynamics. For a periodic soliton lattice at fixed winding, we relate the angular holonomy to the exact acoustic dispersion by continuing the Bloch momentum through the zero-frequency band edge associated with rigid translation of the lattice. For suitable embedding angles, the stable vacuum has a regular BTZ geometry. When only one chiral sector carries winding, we construct a family whose asymptotic region extends to a BTZ black hole. As the spectral parameter of the winding sector tends to the corresponding band edge, the black hole approaches extremality. The leading correction to the extremal entropy is linear in the Hawking temperature defined with respect to laboratory time, with a coefficient inversely proportional to the sound velocity of the lattice. The same inverse-velocity dependence appears in the low-temperature entropy of one-dimensional quantum fluids.
