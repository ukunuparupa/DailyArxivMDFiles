# Character expansion of the Itzykson-Zuber pair correlator

## authors:
#Alexei_Morozov
#Hasib_Sifat

## summary:
We revisit a long-standing problem of correlation functions with the Harish-Chandra-Itzykson-Zuber measure. The immediate goal is to reproduce the long-known answer for pair correlator in the form of character expansion - a sum over Schur polynomials with carefully adjusted coefficients. The infinite sum can be folded up into exponentials with the help of the Cauchy identity. What matters are the coefficients, which we express in terms of projectors, made from Cherednik operators. Development of character expansion methods for unitary integrals, which are much more difficult than the Hermitian ones, gives a hope for further generalizations - up to arbitrary correlators with non-trivial measures.
