# The Zero Mode Constraint in the Dirac-Bergmann Analysis of Kadomtsev-Petviashvili Type Equations

## authors:
#Ali_Pazarci

## summary:
We derive the Hamiltonian formulation of the Kadomtsev-Petviashvili (KP) equation from a singular Lagrangian by means of the Dirac-Bergmann algorithm. The Lagrangian contains an auxiliary field, which leads to an irregular secondary constraint. On the branch that reproduces the KP equation, all constraints are second class, and the Dirac brackets give the known Hamiltonian and Poisson operator. We confirm these results by the Faddeev-Jackiw method. Then, we consider fields periodic in the first spatial coordinate. In this case, the zero modes of the derivative operator lead to an additional secondary constraint on the mean of the potential. This constraint replaces the inverse derivative by its zero mean counterpart, and the resulting Hamiltonian is in accordance with the one of Reiman and Semenov-Tyan-Shanskii, up to normalization conventions. For decaying fields, the same condition is necessary for rapidly decreasing potentials to remain rapidly decreasing. We show that this constraint appears for a general class of KP-type equations. In addition, we classify the smooth solutions of the auxiliary-field action. We also relate our results to an existing quantization of the KP equation, for which the potential Lagrangian provides an action.
