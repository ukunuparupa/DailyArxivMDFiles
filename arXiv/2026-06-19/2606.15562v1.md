# Quantum Corrections to Page Curve of Charged Near-AdS$_2$ Black Holes

## authors:
#Zi-Qing_Xiao

## summary:
We study the evaporation and Page curve of charged near-AdS$_2$ black holes coupled to a non-gravitating bath at fixed temperature and chemical potential. The low-energy dynamics is governed by the Schwarzian reparametrization mode together with a $U(1)$ phase mode. We average the boundary energy and the outgoing flux over these two soft modes and obtain corrected balance equations for the temperature and chemical potential. We then use the corrected background to calculate the no-island and island entropies and the Page time shifts. We find that the two soft sectors affect the Page transition in our low-temperature semiclassical regime. The $U(1)$ phase mode correction delays the Page transition, while the Schwarzian correction tends to move it earlier. The total Page time shift is therefore determined by the competition between the Schwarzian and $U(1)$ sectors.
