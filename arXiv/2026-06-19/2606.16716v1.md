# Effective Lifshitz-Born-Infeld black holes from general covariance breaking

## authors:
#D_C_Moreira
#A_S_Lemos
#F_A_Brito

## summary:
In this work we present an effective Lifshitz black hole solution with Born-Infeld electrodynamics and explore some of its properties. We discuss the mechanism for capturing the solution, achieved through diffeomorphism invariance breaking, study the emergent causal structure, and analyze aspects of critical behavior and local stability in the associated thermodynamics.
