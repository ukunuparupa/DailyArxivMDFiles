# Cosmological Vacuum Decays from Schwinger-Keldysh Formalism

## authors:
#Zi-Yan_Yuwen

## summary:
In this work, we establish a systematic framework to describe the vacuum decays in a radiation-dominated FLRW universe from the Schwinger-Keldysh formalism. By splitting the phase transition field $Φ$ into the mean field $φ$ and the short-wavelength modes $σ$ and tracing over the latter as the environment, we obtain a classical Langevin-type equation-of-motion for the mean field $φ$, where the quantum effects are encoded in a non-Markov memory kernel and a non-Gaussian noise. As a phenomenological example, we consider a polynomial potential and study the structure of the memory kernel as well as the correlation functions of the noise term. With a less restrictive scale split by allowing $φ$ to carry spatial dependence, further extensions remain possible to describe the whole dynamics of cosmological first-order phase transitions via numerical simulations.
