# Worldsheet skeins and block decomposition of quivers

## authors:

#Piotr_Kucharski

#Dmitry_Noshchenko
#Ramadevi_Pichai

## summary:
Following the knot-quiver correspondence, we investigate quiver counterparts of the HOMFLYPT skein relations by interpreting link invariants in terms of holomorphic worldsheets and their multicover contributions. Extending this framework to more general worldsheet geometries, we conjecture that, up to quiver equivalence, the adjacency matrix of the quiver associated with any link in a skein triple admits a block decomposition whose diagonal blocks are quiver adjacency matrices associated with the other two links. We verify this conjecture in numerous examples.
