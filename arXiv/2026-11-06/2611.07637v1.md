# Topological Characterization of Adinkra Heights

## authors:
#Amanda_E_Francis
#S_James_Gates
#Digvijay_Roy_Varier
#Ursula_A_Whitcher

## summary:
Adinkras are highly structured bipartite graphs that encapsulate 1D supersymmetry representations. We classify hypercube Adinkra heights with one, two, three or four edge colors using a graph-theoretic notion of equivalence. For the 990 distinct height functions in the case of four edge colors, we show that these classes match modified twisted character classes bijectively and provide a finer classification than spectral Height Yielding Matrix Numbers (HYMNs). We also relate our classification to the physics of the two-dimensional chiral and twisted chiral supermultiplets.
