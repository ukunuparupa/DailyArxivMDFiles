# Enhancing Gravitational Waves from Global Strings via a First-Order Phase Transition

## authors:
#Yongtao_Jia
#Ligong_Bian
#Huai-Ke_Guo

## summary:
We perform the first (3+1)-dimensional lattice simulations of a cosmological first-order phase transition (PT) in which true-vacuum bubbles with random phases nucleate, expand, and collide, producing a network of global strings. We find that the string network enters the scaling regime, and the coefficient of the logarithmic growth is smaller than the value obtained for a second-order PT. From the axion spectrum, we derive the dark-matter relic abundance and the dark-radiation bound from big bang nucleosynthesis on the axion decay constant $f_a$ and mass $m_a$. The ratio of the gravitational wave emission rate to the axion emission rate is found to be about two orders of magnitude larger than in the second-order case, and the resulting gravitational wave spectrum can be probed by pulsar timing arrays as well as by space- and ground-based interferometers.
