# Running Couplings in CP Violating Abelian Theories

## authors:
#Joshua_Newey
#John_Terning
#Christopher_B_Verhaaren

## summary:
We calculate the one- and two-loop running couplings for a massless photon coupled to dyons, including CP violating couplings. The electric and magnetic source vertices in the most general theory form a real coupling matrix $C$, while a current-current interaction depends on $C^T C$ and the quantized determinant $\det C$. These determine, respectively, the continuously running Coulombic interactions and the fixed antisymmetric Dirac--Schwinger--Zwanziger charge quantization condition which also determines pairwise helicity. The running preserves $\det C$, showing that the electric and magnetic couplings run inversely while every pairwise helicity remains constant. We comment on how the running of the CP-odd coupling $θ$ relates to physical CP violation, which also depends on the spectrum, mass phases, and threshold corrections. We also show that an invertible field redefinition moves the CP-odd coupling between the current source terms and the photon kinetic terms; these are two descriptions of the same local interaction. Finally, we show that the result agrees with the Argyres--Douglas $β$ function ansatz.
