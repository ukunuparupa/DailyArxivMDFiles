# Asymptotic safety is not generic

## authors:
#Francesco_Del_Porro
#Francesco_Ferrarin
#Alessia_Platania

## summary:
Asymptotically safe quantum gravity proposes a non-perturbative ultraviolet completion of gravity in which the high-energy behavior is controlled by an ultraviolet fixed point---the Reuter fixed point---for which there is robust evidence from a wide range of settings and truncations. The existence of asymptotic safety requires non-trivial zeros of beta functions, raising the question of how generic such fixed-point solutions are. In this work, we tackle this issue by investigating chiral perturbation theories. We focus on SU(2) and SU(3) chiral models, which share key features with gravity such as perturbative non-renormalizability or nonlinear interactions, but which are known to admit a different ultraviolet completion via new degrees of freedom. Using the functional renormalization group, we study these theories within a quartic-derivative truncation and find no evidence for an interacting fixed point. This result suggests that asymptotic safety is not generic, but rather a feature pertaining to certain special systems.
