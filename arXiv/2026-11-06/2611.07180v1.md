# In light of the Henneaux-Teitelboim action

## authors:
#Andreas_Gustavsson

## summary:
We construct an action similar to the Henneaux-Teitelboim action on a six dimensional Lorentzian manifold where the time direction is chosen to be lightlike and generated by a conformal lightlike Killing vector $U$. We show that such an action exists only if there is another commuting light-like conformal Killing vector $V$ with properties that can be realized by the Dirac currents of two conformal Killing spinors. We extend the action to the $(1,0)$ supersymmetric tensor multiplet.
