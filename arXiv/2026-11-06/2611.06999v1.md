# Matrix Theory from Holography II: Beyond Counting

## authors:
#Shota_Komatsu
#Eunwoo_Lee
#Chintan_Patel

## summary:
Extending our earlier realization of Matrix Theory for M-theory within AdS/CFT, we propose and test a new triality among a large-charge sector of three-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ ADHM theory, the BMN matrix model, and lightlike-compactified M-theory on the maximally supersymmetric pp-wave. In an appropriate triple-scaling limit, the superconformal index of ADHM theory becomes the grand-canonical index of the BMN model, with magnetic-flux sectors corresponding to fuzzy-sphere vacua. But the real interest of this new setup is that we can go beyond counting states. Using supersymmetric localization, we show that correlation functions in topological subsectors agree as well, including their nontrivial dependence on the BMN coupling. In each magnetic-flux sector, both the localization measure and the operator insertions can be matched precisely with those of the corresponding BMN vacuum. Our results also point to a deeper connection between monopole bubbling in ADHM theory and instantons in the BMN model. For minuscule monopole charges, ADHM correlators map directly to topological correlators in the trivial BMN vacuum. For non-minuscule charges, monopole bubbling produces additional screened sectors, whose relative weights reproduce the classical actions of BMN instantons. Mirror symmetry offers another perspective on this structure, mapping the monopole operators to Higgs-branch ``winding" operators in the mirror necklace theory. Finally, we discuss a type IIB counterpart, connecting a large-charge sector of four-dimensional $\mathbb Z_K$-orbifold quiver theories, the DLCQ of type IIB string theory on the pp-wave, and level-one mass-deformed ABJM theory on $\mathbb R_t\times T^2$. As a first test, we show that the superconformal index of the orbifold theory reproduces the vacuum degeneracy of the mass-deformed ABJM theory.
