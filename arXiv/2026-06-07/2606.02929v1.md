# Kerr--Schild--AdS geometries in quadratic f(R) gravity: A no-go theorem

## authors:
#Alikram_N_Aliev

## summary:
We investigate Kerr--Schild--AdS geometries in quadratic $f(R)$ gravity without imposing the constant-curvature condition $R=R_0$ a priori. For the geometrically natural Kerr--Schild--AdS subclass, we show that the field equations dynamically enforce constant scalar curvature and uniquely select the Kerr--AdS family of solutions. Thus, quadratic $f(R)$ gravity admits no Kerr--AdS black hole solutions beyond the Einstein branch, establishing a no-go theorem for this class of geometries.
